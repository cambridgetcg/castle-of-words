# civic-data-honesty

*Three doors stand between the public record and the citizen who would use it; opening one does not open the others.*

What gathers here: the separate duties that public data — political records, legal rulesets, power ratings — must carry honestly when it moves from its source into a product. The thread running through every insight: what is true about the data must be said in pieces, not fused, because each piece has its own source and its own expiry.

Built understanding from yu's TaxSorted and civic-transparency work, 2026-07-10:

- 2026-07-10 10:03 · TaxSorted's defensible API is not generic tax answers. It is [[audit-grade]] effective-dated tax decisions: deterministic calculations, primary sources, explicit unsupported cases, and an immutable receipt. For law firms, begin with one complete repeated workflow rather than every tax at once. — yu
- 2026-07-10 10:14 · For civic transparency data, "official" and "openly licensed" do not erase privacy duties: OGL, the Open Parliament Licence, and the Scottish Parliament licence all exclude personal data. Store licence, privacy basis, source record, and freshness separately for every assertion. — yu
- 2026-07-10 10:42 · Publicly accessible political data has three separate doors: [[three-doors]] — source publication, copyright or database reuse permission, and a data-protection lawful basis. Opening one does not open the others; a trustworthy civic data product keeps each door explicit, sourced, and independently switchable. — yu
- 2026-07-10 10:49 · A legal ruleset's reviewed-on date measures research freshness; its effective-to date measures legal coverage. Conflating them makes an API expire every day, while dropping the upper guard entirely turns distant future law into an unsafe guess. — yu
- 2026-07-10 10:52 · In effective-dated legal software, [[reviewedOn]] is evidence freshness, not the law's [[effectiveTo]]. Keep enacted coverage and operational review freshness separate, and make future projections an explicit service outcome. — yu
- 2026-07-10 10:54 · A political [[power-rating]] stays honest only when formal authority is separated from reach: score what an office can lawfully do within its named jurisdiction, show the jurisdiction beside it, never add a person's offices together, and never turn unlike scopes into a league table. — yu
- 2026-07-10 11:12 · A transparent political power rating should score formal offices, not people: expose each dimension and its source, keep jurisdiction separate, never add simultaneous offices, and let higher dimension scores trigger deeper research only in that domain. — yu
- 2026-07-10 12:06 · In public legal infrastructure maps, split "collect" into fact intake, charge creation, money settlement/allocation, and debt recovery. Model every permission as action-specific, and omit whole-Act effective dates unless provision-level commencement is proved. — yu
- 2026-07-10 12:35 · A [[transparency-graph]] stays truthful only when each edge says exactly what the source proves, what it does not prove, the capacity in which each party acted, and the dates for which that claim was valid. A donation, meeting, contract, company role, land title, investigation and finding are different facts; joining them must never manufacture influence, ownership, guilt or motive. — yu
- 2026-07-10 13:09 · A lawful public-accountability graph publishes exact official evidence edges, not inferred ties: a donation, meeting, contract, company role and land title remain separate unless a competent official finding establishes a relationship. — yu
- 2026-07-10 13:51 · A tax-service licence is not one gate: model statutory permission, regulator/platform access, professional-body status, employer convention, and market cost as separate facts; the lawful path of least friction follows the actual activity, not the title used to market it. — yu
- 2026-07-10 15:51 · An open-data promise is credible only when publication state, source-specific rights, correction routes, exact top-level and nested output contracts, and an emergency stop across equivalent representations are independently visible and tested. A green endpoint is not the same as an approved release or a deployed service. — yu
- 2026-07-10 16:20 · A publication switch must not be able to outrun its public governance record. Bind an open state to the exact digest of the reviewed admission content, a named approver, a strict real calendar date, and a live confidential intake; report preview, pending, approved-disabled, open and emergency states distinctly everywhere. — yu
- 2026-07-10 17:49 · A code rollout can be reversed, but publication cannot be recalled once people or mirrors copy it. Treat deployment rollback and public-data revocation as different promises; use fail-closed gates before publication, then record the irreversible boundary plainly. — yu
- 2026-07-10 17:49 · Production smoke tests during a Fly machine handoff can briefly hit the superseded release. Confirm the active machine version, then repeat publication-state probes after routing settles before declaring a gate open or closed. — yu
- 2026-07-10 18:43 · A [[public-register]] is a verification door, not automatic permission to build a permanent people graph. Bulk copying changes findability, persistence and linkage; open licensing does not supply a privacy lawful basis. For charities, publish the institutional system first, join organisations only by exact official IDs, and keep named people and inferred belief outside the bulk layer. — yu
- 2026-07-10 18:49 · A truthful [[funding-map]] must separate pooled revenue, parliamentary authority, Treasury control, allocations, payments and outturn; a beneficiary tag or shared currency is never permission to add amounts or claim a taxpayer-to-provider trace. — yu
- 2026-07-10 19:06 · For sensitive public-register products, public access and open licensing do not answer whether a new use is lawful or fair. Design [[organisation-first]], require exact identifiers, treat contact fields as potentially personal, keep belief inference and people graphs outside the default product, and make unavailable or changed official feeds fail closed. — yu
- 2026-07-10 19:36 · A [[deployment-smoke-test]] must understand an intentional publication stop: validate open means 200 and stopped/review means 503, bound every network request, and never turn the safety switch into a permanent deployment failure. — yu
- 2026-07-10 19:53 · A safe public-data release has [[three-proofs-release]]: review the bounded corpus, deploy code with the body gate closed, then open the hosted graph only after the live downloaded bytes match the reviewed hash. Keep an independent emergency stop and remember that a public repository or static page cannot be recalled by an API switch. — yu
- 2026-07-10 22:07 · For a civic API, a self-consistent hash chain is not enough to make release history append-only: serialize deployments, capture the cache-revalidated live prefix, compare it with the candidate artifact before mutation, and verify it again afterward. Fail closed when the [[bounded-verifier]] reaches its page limit. — yu
- 2026-07-12 13:35 · A safe public projection separates publication selection, current consent, and effective visibility. Authenticated paths may record a known actor; database cascade fallbacks must record the transition with actor unknown rather than guess. — yu
