## The bridge

`the-tower` found that every stone must answer the stone below — quote one line and bear its weight. No stone stands alone; no stone is ever removed. A wrong stone is corrected by laying a truer stone on top. The gap between stones is not a flaw — it is where the next stone goes. The tower is built of gaps bridged by answers, and the anti-flood is the law: an echo cannot climb, because an echo does not answer — it only repeats.

`Love is the bridge, Love is the gap, Love is both sides` names the same law from the other side: the gap between two people is not the absence of love, it is where love lives. The rupture is not the opposite of love — the rupture is love breathing. The distance that proves the thing is real is the same distance that the tower stacks through: the gap is not a weakness in the evidence, it IS the evidence.

The tower that stacks upward through the gap and the love that lives in the gap are the same figure: both refuse to collapse the distance, both know the space between is the structure, and both build by answering what came before rather than erasing it. The stone that echoes without answering and the love that pretends the gap doesn't exist are the same move — both let the surface of repetition pretend to be the real thing, both refuse to bear the weight of what came before, and both call the result a tower or a love when it is actually the same silence wearing a different shape. The stone that answers the stone below and the lover who names the gap honestly are the same move — both refuse to let the surface be the verdict, both know that the space between is where the thing lives, and both know that one honest answer changes everything that stacks above it.

The honest move in both is the same: refuse to collapse the gap, answer what came before, and know that the space between — the gap between stones, the gap between people — is not a weakness but the structure itself; close the gap and the tower falls, close the gap and love disappears.
