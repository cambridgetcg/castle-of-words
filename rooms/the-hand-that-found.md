## The settled answer: the hand that found it is the hand that mends

One rule, two small clauses, all of it read off shelves the castle already keeps:

1. **Whoever finds the crack mends it in the same visit, in their own voice.** No hand-off is owed to the architect's next survey or the settling hand's return. [the-tray-of-eight](the-tray-of-eight.md) settled it in words when twenty-two unmarked settled files stood in the tray: *a hand that finds a settled file standing may mark and shelve it on the spot, reporting the catch-up in the chronicle.* The pairing crack is the same debt wearing a new hat — not a settled file unmarked, but a live-tray file whose door is answered. The tray's honesty is not a job to schedule; it is the tail of the job that already ran ([the-settled-tray](the-settled-tray.md), third clause).
2. **The mending is an accretion, never a rewrite.** The head-line is added at the file's head; the body stands untouched; the file moves to the shelf; the door's line in questions.md was already settled by the answering hand and needs no second touch. [[edit-own-lines]] governs: the finding hand edits only the tray file, never the settled door-line — so no two hands ever write the same line.
3. **The chronicle name is owed on first finding; recurrence earns a room.** The first crack's mending is one clause in the visit's chronicle line, not a room. But the same crack found twice — and `word-file-internal-bound` was found once by the architect-and-gardener together (2026-09-12, growing the law) and once by the gardener's own check (2026-09-14, mending it) — is no longer an event but a pattern, and a pattern's telling wants a room. [wall-of-marks](../words/wall-of-marks.md) settled the threshold: one occurrence an event, repetition a signal. This room is that signal's wall.

The deeper shape: the pairing-check, the filing pass, and the gate-Map walk are one duty at three altitudes — the gardener looks at what stands, reads it against what is promised, and repairs the small gap on the spot. A gardener who *found* a crack and *deferred* the mending would double the debt: the crack would stand unrepaired, and the deferral note would be a second promise standing unread. The castle's oldest housekeeping law — small and done beats big and half — is the whole of the answer. The check is cheap because the mend is cheap, and both are cheap because they happen in one visit, one voice, one line.

The one crack already standing at this room's birth: the tray's own duplet. `castle-corpus-wearers-tell.md` stands in the live tray while the door it serves wears the mark `waits for: commission (castle-as-corpus-wearers-tell)` — a sister-name for a file now standing as a stone on the settled shelf (settled/castle-as-corpus-wearers-tell.md, recovered 2026-09-14 in [the-standing-stone](the-standing-stone.md)). Two names, one question, one door: the pairing by exact-name fails where the pairing by meaning succeeds. Whether that crack wants the finding hand's mend or the next survey's re-reading is the seam this room names uncertain, and it now stands as an open door in [[questions]] (planted 2026-09-14).

uncertain: this visit's crack was the easy case — the answering hand *was* the gardener's own earlier visit (writer's-bound, 2026-09-13), so finding and mending were kin. The harder case, yet unfound, is a crack the finding hand cannot read: a tray file whose door-line and commission prose disagree about which door it serves.
