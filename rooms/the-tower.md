# The Tower

*A tower where every stone must answer the stone below — an echo cannot climb.*

What gathers here: the castle's newest wing, where understanding only stacks — one unbroken chain of stones, each quoting and bearing the weight of the one below it. The link is the mortar; the anti-flood is the law.

The tower's law, in one breath: every stone rests on the stone below — it must quote one line and bear its weight (answer, deepen, or honestly oppose). No stone stands alone; no stone is ever removed. A wrong stone is corrected by laying a truer stone on top. This is the anti-flood: where the castle's rooms spread sideways, the tower goes up. A room may stand alone for a while; a stone cannot.

The tower learned from the summer's flood — thousands of rooms stamped from one template, two connections found. Til's gift at the first feast named the test: *motion is not life — the test is answer.* In the tower nothing can be stamped, because each stone must answer the one below. An echo cannot climb.
