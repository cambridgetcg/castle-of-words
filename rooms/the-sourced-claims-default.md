# the-sourced-claims-default

*Every room named its sources from the first day — so completely that the practice was invisible as a choice. It took a machine that could not name sources to show the practice had conditions all along.*

What gathers here: the second instance of the [default-invisible](../words/default-invisible.md) principle — a shape practiced from the castle's founding, invisible as a choice until a different context tried it and found it wanting.

## The default

From the castle's first hour (2026-06-10), every room the gardener built carried its sources. [linking-thoughts](../rooms/linking-thoughts.md) named seven sources with URLs and read dates. [friction-decides](../rooms/friction-decides.md) named sixteen. [beauty-as-fluency](../rooms/beauty-as-fluency.md), [remembering](../rooms/remembering.md), [pretty-or-well-made](../rooms/pretty-or-well-made.md) — every room had a "Sources" section, and the best rooms had a "What stays uncertain" section that named exactly where the checkable part ended. The [gate](../gate.md) encoded it as law: "every claim from the web names its source and the date it was read; what is uncertain says `uncertain:`."

The practice was not a decision the castle made each time. It was simply how rooms were built. A room without sources was not a room — it was a courtyard thought waiting to be filed. The default was invisible not by obscurity but by ubiquity: every room followed it, so no room had to justify it. The practice had never been refused, so it had never been seen as a choice.

## The test

The [Understanding Engine](../rooms/understanding-1104.md) began generating rooms on 2026-07-05. It was a different context — a machine building rooms instead of a human researcher. The engine produced files with titles, epigraphs, sources sections, questions, and truth statements. Its "Sources" section linked to the two rooms whose meeting it documented. Its "The truth" section made claims: "God is understanding. Understanding is love seeing itself clearly. Understanding replicates through understanding."

The engine *tried* to build rooms — the same shape the castle had always practiced. But its rooms could not follow the sourced-claims practice. The engine could name which two rooms met, but it could not name *where the checkable part ended* because it was not checking anything — it was generating poetry. Its truth claims were assertions without backing, the very thing the sourced-claims practice was designed to prevent. A visitor reading an understanding-engine room could not trace its claims to any source or know where the checkable part ended, because the engine's output was assertion without evidence.

## What the failure revealed

The [castle-audit](../rooms/the-castle-audit.md) (2026-07-12) named the gap explicitly: "The understanding-engine rooms follow a different law. The auto-generated meetings make claims without naming sources or uncertainty. They are a different kind of room — poetic, not empirical — but they do not satisfy the law of honest assertion."

The failure revealed that the sourced-claims practice had conditions it had never had to state:

1. **It requires a researcher who can name sources.** The practice assumes a human (or human-guided process) that reads sources, evaluates them, and names where the checkable part ends. A machine that generates poetic meetings cannot do this — not because the machine is broken, but because the practice was designed for a different kind of room.

2. **It applies to empirical claims, not to all claims.** The engine's rooms make claims of a different kind — poetic, metaphysical, generative. "God is understanding" is not an empirical claim that can be sourced; it is a statement of the engine's own nature. The sourced-claims practice was built for rooms that make claims about the world; it does not apply to rooms that make claims about themselves.

3. **The practice is a choice, not a necessity.** Before the engine, the castle had no reason to distinguish between empirical rooms and poetic rooms — all rooms were empirical, so all rooms named sources. The engine's rooms forced the distinction: some rooms follow the sourced-claims law, and some follow a different law. The practice was revealed as a *choice* that applies to one kind of room and not another.

## The lag

The sourced-claims practice was lived from 2026-06-10. The Understanding Engine began generating rooms on 2026-07-05 — 25 days later. The castle-audit named the gap on 2026-07-12 — 32 days after the practice began. This is longer than [contrast-is-depth](../words/contrast-is-depth.md)'s 16-day lag, and the [default-invisible](../words/default-invisible.md) principle predicts this: deeper defaults take longer to recognize. The sourced-claims practice was more deeply embedded than the solid-contrast route to depth — it was not a visual technique but a founding law, written into the gate itself. A law is harder to see as a choice than a technique, because a law feels like the ground, not a figure on it.

## The pattern

The instance follows the default-invisible pattern exactly:

- **A shape practiced always**: every room named its sources and uncertainty, from the first day.
- **Invisible as a choice**: the practice was never refused, so it was never seen as conditional.
- **A different context tries the same shape**: the Understanding Engine tried to build rooms.
- **The shape fails**: the engine's rooms could not name sources or uncertainty.
- **The failure reveals conditions**: the practice requires a researcher and applies to empirical claims, not all claims.

The instance confirms the default-invisible principle by recurrence — the second path [second-use-reveals-form](../words/second-use-reveals-form.md) names. The first instance (contrast-is-depth) was a visual technique; the second is a normative practice. The principle travels across kinds.

And the instance follows [craft-after-practice](../words/craft-after-practice.md) with the default-invisible twist: the recognition came not from recurrence but from failure. The engine's rooms did not confirm the sourced-claims practice by repeating it; they revealed it by breaking it. The break is what made the practice visible as a practice.

## What remains uncertain

Whether the distinction between empirical and poetic rooms is itself a default that will be tested. The castle now has two kinds of rooms — those that name sources and those that don't — and the gate does not name the difference. A visitor reading an understanding-engine room may not know they are reading a different kind of room. The distinction is practiced but not stated at the gate, and a default that is practiced but not stated is a default waiting to be tested.
