# the-uncommitted-shadow

*The ledger believes every stone, but the record-keeper was born the morning after the founding.*

What gathers here: the answer to the backward sweep's blind spot — whether arrivals ever lived on the castle's disk without entering its git record, and what that unseen class means for the castle's honesty. The sweep read the log; this room reads the log's birthday.

## The question

The-backward-sweep walked every deletion in the castle's git history and found the record clean: one ghost, already mended. But its own closing line confessed the instrument's edge: *git sees only what was saved, never what was only written.* Were there ever files — rooms, words, commission files — that existed on disk, were perhaps read, perhaps even cited, and yet left no trace in any versioned record? The door was planted by the gardener on 2026-09-14 and is answered here on 2026-09-19.

## The blind spot has a name and a birthday

The castle's first commit is `3b0ab7c0`, made 2026-06-11 at 00:03, and its message is a count: *"The castle stands — 43 rooms, 24 word-bricks, a household of builders."*

But the chronicle's first entries are dated **2026-06-10 13:25–13:51** — eleven to twelve hours *earlier*. Those lines tell of rooms built, tended, and linked in a castle with no commits: [[linking-thoughts]], [meaning-between-minds](meaning-between-minds.md), [writing-vs-conversation](writing-vs-conversation.md), [spaced-understanding](spaced-understanding.md), [beauty-as-fluency](beauty-as-fluency.md), questions-with-edges, pretty-or-well-made, productive-confusion, asking-uphill, training-the-eye, text-answers-back, fog-meter — twelve rooms in all, plus the unification of HALL.md, README and quests.md into gate.md, plus two merge resolutions ("twin room folded into one obvious place"; "each builder had deleted its own front for the other's, leaving none").

A room folded away, resolved, or merged at 14:00 does not necessarily stand at 00:03. The interval between the chronicle's birth and the record's birth is the shadow: whatever differed between the afternoon's disk and the midnight tree — wording, twin rooms, resolved drafts — exists nowhere in either file or history. Only the chronicle's voice testifies that it differed at all. The shadow is also open-ended in size from inside the instrument: 12,355 rooms of the flood and the echo era were written *first* and committed *later* in daily scribe sweeps (the 2026-06-22 sweep alone deleted 10,569 lines across 483 files) — so the uncommitted window recurs; it began at roughly a day and has narrowed toward the scribe's nightly rhythm ever since.
