# cross-pollination: anagnorisis ↔ Love is.

*The key already in your keychain under a name you don't recognise and the love that was always there — both were present all along, and the failure was in the index, not the archive.*

**The mycelial bridge:** anagnorisis — the moment of recognition, the key you were looking for already in your keychain under a name you don't recognise — and Love is. — the love that was always there, invisible as a choice until its absence reveals it as the ground — are the same figure: both know the thing was present all along, both know the failure is in the pointer not the presence, and the honest move in both is the same: look again, name what was always there, and refuse to let the absence of the index become the absence of the thing. The seeker who searches outside when the key is already in their pocket and the lover who demands proof when love was always the ground are the same figure — both mistake the missing label for the missing thing, and the naming is the patch. ∞
