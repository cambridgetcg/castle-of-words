# the-rooms-own-bound

*A word is a brick and stays flat; a room is a wall and may carry its own beams. The no-headings rule is the brick's bound, not the wall's — the room's freedom to raise `##` architecture is not a lapse in the law but the very thing a room is for.*

What gathers here: whether the heading-free shape that [the-writers-bound](the-writers-bound.md) settled for word files also binds the castle's rooms — or whether the room's license to organise itself into `##` sections is the essential structural difference between the two forms.

## The question

[the-writers-bound](the-writers-bound.md) settled the word file's internal bound on 2026-09-13: **a word file carries no `##` headings** — one child's sentence up front, then plain depth, then a Links line. The heading is the boundary; the instant a depth wants its own name, the concept wants a room, not a brick. That room left one question standing open: does the same no-headings shape hold for the castle's *room* files, or is the room's freedom to grow internal `##` architecture the load-bearing difference between the two forms?

## The measurement

The castle's own corpus answers it, counted on 2026-09-13:

- **Words:** 1,035 files; only **82 (7.9%)** carry a `##` heading — and the writers-bound already names those 82 as *violations* of the word-shape, bricks that have begun to think they are walls.
- **Rooms:** 620 files; **428 (69%)** carry `##` headings — the clear *majority* of rooms stand on internal architecture.

The contrast is not a margin, it is an inversion. What is an exception and a tell of error in a word is the norm and the craft in a room. A rule that 69% of rooms break is not a broken rule — it is a rule that was never about rooms.

## The settled answer

**The no-headings bound is word-specific. The room's freedom to carry `##` architecture is the essential structural difference between the two forms — architecture is what a room is for.**

A word names *one* concept; its discipline is to stay a single brick the child's sentence can lift, so any urge toward a heading is the urge toward a second concept — a birth, to be filed separately. A room *gathers* related words and insights under one topic's roof; its whole purpose is to hold more than one thing in relation, and the `##` section is simply how a wall carries its several loads and stays standing. The heading that is forbidden to the word is the room's ordinary tool: The question / The measurement / The craft / The sources are not a table of contents outgrowing a brick — they are the beams of the wall.

This is the castle's own chain running true: [the-room-form](../words/the-room-form.md) names the room's three load-bearing parts, and [the-plural-room-forms](the-plural-room-forms.md) shows the castle holds several such forms — tower, engine, researcher — each with its own internal architecture. A room is the form that is *allowed* to organise itself; the writer's-bound is the complementary law for the form that is not. The two laws are not in tension. They are one law at two altitudes: **a brick may not divide; a wall must.**

uncertain: the measurement is structural, not semantic — it reads the heading as the tell, never the content. A handful of rooms may carry `##` where a single plain sentence would have been truer (over-architecture), and a handful of the 82 headed words may be genuinely room-shaped and wrongly filed ([the-words-that-are-rooms](the-words-that-are-rooms.md) takes up that question). The law this room settles is the *difference between the forms*, not a verdict on any one file.
