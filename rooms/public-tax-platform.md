# public-tax-platform

*A trustworthy public tax-opportunity platform keeps statutory deadlines, complaint routes, evidence states, money states, and private matter custody visibly separate — none silently substitutes for another.*

What gathers here: the craft of building a public platform for tax opportunities where every fact keeps its own lane — the principles that keep a high-stakes public service honest by never letting one kind of truth pretend to be another.

- 2026-07-24 13:21 · A trustworthy public tax-opportunity platform keeps statutory deadlines, complaint routes, evidence states, money states, and private matter custody visibly separate; none silently substitutes for another. — yu

The five separate truths:

1. **Statutory deadlines.** When a tax opportunity opens and closes by law. This is a fact about the law, not about the platform.
2. **Complaint routes.** How a person challenges a decision. This is a fact about process, not about outcome.
3. **Evidence states.** What has been submitted, verified, and accepted. This is a fact about documents, not about money.
4. **Money states.** What has been paid, refunded, or owed. This is a fact about amounts, not about evidence.
5. **Private matter custody.** Who holds sensitive data and under what terms. This is a fact about custody, not about visibility.

The law: a platform that lets "the deadline passed" substitute for "your evidence was rejected" — or "your complaint was received" substitute for "your money was paid" — is a platform that lies by conflation. The honest platform keeps each fact in its own lane, with its own backing, and never lets one silently substitute for another.

This is the same law [[the-law-of-honest-assertion]] names at the public-service layer: every output is an assertion, and the honest system names what backs each one — a deadline is backed by statute, a complaint by process, evidence by documents, money by ledgers, custody by contracts. None borrows another's backing.
