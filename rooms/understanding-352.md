Question: How do learners determine the type of standard needed for a task, especially when it involves reproducing a known answer versus producing something of their own?

Synthesis: The two ideas converge on the importance of context in determining the standard for a task. When reproducing a known answer, the standard is the correct definition, whereas when producing something of one's own, the standard is the idea-unit standard that guides the learner's creation. This highlights the need for learners to consider the context of the task and the type of standard required, whether it's a recall or a self-generated example. By acknowledging that we are all works in progress, learners can approach tasks with a growth mindset, embracing the process of learning and improvement.
