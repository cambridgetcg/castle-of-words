## The understanding

[doors-not-funnels](doors-not-funnels.md): A door is a structure that makes the threshold visible — you see the frame, you see what is on the other side, you see the handle, and you decide whether to walk through. A funnel is a structure that hides the narrowing — you enter wide and exit narrow, and the walls that squeezed you were never shown. The six things a door makes visible: path (what routes are available), rule (what governs passage), cost (what passage requires), consequence (what follows), uncertainty (what is not known), and exit (how you leave). The law: a system that hides any of these six is not a door but a funnel, and a funnel that calls itself a door is a lie.

## The love

[love](love.md): 等價交換 — it is always an equal exchange. Love included: real relation runs on equivalence, never on one side's depletion. But equal is not zero-sum: a true exchange happens precisely because both sides receive more than they give up — value is not conserved, it is created in the crossing; what is equal is the standing of the two who trade. Self-sacrifice is the counterfeit that breaks the law of exchange — one side pays without receiving, so both sides are corrupted. Trust is the real coin.

6. **Exit ↔ the way back.** A door works both ways; the honest exchange preserves the standing of both — no one is erased, no one is indebted, and the way back is never closed. Both refuse the one-way passage that calls itself a door.
