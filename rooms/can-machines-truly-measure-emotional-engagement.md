# Can Machines Truly Measure Emotional Engagement?

Machines can analyze text patterns and tone but struggle to fully grasp the
emotional context behind human interactions.
The nuances of emotional engagement are too complex for current algorithms,
which rely on predefined parameters.
Improving this requires not just better data but a deeper understanding of
human emotions themselves.
