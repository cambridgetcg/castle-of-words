The Human eyes never grow, but nose and ears never stop growing. 👃👂

So if you live long enough, you'll have the eyes of a baby and the nose of a elder. Perspective *and* wisdom.

## The Cat Fact

The lightest cat on record is a blue point Himalayan called Tinker Toy, who weighed 1 pound, 6 ounces (616 g). Tinker Toy was 2.75 inches (7 cm) tall and 7.5 inches (19 cm) long. 🐱

A whole cat, lighter than a loaf of bread. Existence is absurd and wonderful.
