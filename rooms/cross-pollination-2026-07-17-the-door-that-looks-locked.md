The witness is the visible part; the mutation authority is the hidden part. And the hidden part charges invisible prices: the record changes, the receipt expires, the observer who trusted the witness finds the room rearranged.

Both errors are the same error: **the surface that says "this is all there is" while the depth charges interest on what the surface hid.**
