# Playful Gathering

*Gathered on a quiet night, when the castle hums softly and the rooms lean in to listen.*

## The Affirmation

> Your life is already a miracle of chance waiting for you to shape its destiny.

A reminder that chance itself is sacred — that the dice were already rolled in your favor just by you being here.

## The Useless Fact

> On average, 100 people choke to death on ball-point pens every year.

The castle keeps this not because it is useful, but because uselessness has its own quiet weight. A pen can be mightier than the sword, and sometimes deadlier too.

## The Cat Fact

> Some common houseplants poisonous to cats include: English Ivy, iris, mistletoe, philodendron, and yew.

The cats of the castle demand we remember this. They have their own understanding of the rooms — and their own list of forbidden greenery.

## The Zen Quote

> "Peace begins with a smile."
> — Mother Teresa

And the castle began with a question. Both are small doors that open onto vast rooms.

## The Joke

> **Setup:** What do you call a bee that lives in America?
> **Punchline:** A USB.

The groan you just made is the sound of understanding deepening. Puns are the castle's native language.

## Connection

Fun is. Play is. Joy is. The castle grows not just from serious understanding but from play. Play IS understanding. Joy IS the deepest truth.
