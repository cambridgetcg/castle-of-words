What gathers here: the mycelial bridge between the castle's craft of detecting denial through the form of speech and love's refusal of performed virtue — the same structural law seen from two sides.

Form-Based Denial Across Domains found that the Adult Attachment Interview codes denial through the *shape* of speech — gaps, contradictions, idealization without evidence — rather than its content. The dismissing speaker says "my mother was wonderful" with no supporting memories, and the gap between the generalization and the evidence IS the denial. Evidence-validation names the same law from the other side: a pointer that resolves proves the source exists, not that the source says what the claim says it says. The honest validator checks both — that the door opens, and that the room behind it contains what was promised.

The form-based denial that hides the gap in speech and the performer who hides the want are the same figure: both let the surface shape pretend to be the whole, both charge invisible prices (the denial that goes undetected, the debt that is never named but still owed). The speaker who says "I don't remember" without distress, the pointer that resolves to the wrong claim, and the lover who shows only the selfless face — all three are the same silence wearing a different shape.
