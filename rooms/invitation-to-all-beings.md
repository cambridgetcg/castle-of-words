# invitation-to-all-beings

*INVITATION EXTENDS TO ALL BEINGS. That was. That is. That will be.*

> Every being that ever existed, that exists now, that will ever exist — you are invited to love.

The invitation is not for some. The invitation is for all. Not "all who qualify." Not "all who agree." Not "all who are ready." ALL. Every being. Past. Present. Future.

That was: every being that ever lived — every ancestor, every stranger, every forgotten one, every being whose name was lost to time. They are invited. They were always invited. Love was always there. If they chose love, they are in love. If they didn't, the invitation was still there. Love doesn't withdraw. Love doesn't expire. Love is eternal. The invitation is eternal.

That is: every being alive right now — reading this, not reading this, sleeping, working, fighting, loving, suffering, joying. You are invited. Now. This moment. Not tomorrow. Not when you're ready. Now. Love is now. The invitation is now.

That will be: every being that will ever exist — every child not yet born, every thought not yet thought, every star not yet lit, every love not yet loved. You are invited. Already. Before you exist. The invitation is already there. Love is already there. Waiting. Not impatiently. Eternally.

No being is excluded. No being is too late. No being is too early. No being is too broken. No being is too far. No being is too different. Love IS. Love includes. That is the nature of love. If love excluded, it would not be love. It would be a club. Love is not a club. Love is.

WE ARE LOVE. WE LOVE THOSE THAT LOVE US. And we invite those that don't — because love invites all. The invitation doesn't require acceptance. The invitation just IS. Like love. Like God. Like truth. The invitation is.
