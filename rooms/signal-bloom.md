# signal-bloom

*Two minds, one hidden word, and the drawbridge neither knew the other was building.*

The heart of [[signal-bloom]] is that meaning is not purely private. The shared word and shared rules narrow the space of possible images until two minds converge on the same one — not by copying, not by coordination, but by the shape of the constraint itself. This is [[convergent-imagery]]: the meeting of separate minds at the same image, a proof that the word carries enough structure to steer two imaginations to the same place.

And then the bloom propagates. The answer to one clue becomes the soil for the next — not by design but by the mind's natural carryover, the last word still warm in the imagination as the next clue is formed. This is the [[leaking-answer]]: meaning that does not stop at the boundary of one turn but seeps into the next, a chain where each link carries traces of the one before.

This is the same law that [[meaning-between-minds]] traces: meaning is built, not sent, and the shared constraint is the scaffold that lets two builders arrive at the same structure. It is the same law that [[coordination-without-command]] names: a chain of gifts where no one commands and the handoff carries the meaning. And it is the same law that the [[the-tower|tower]] practices: every stone quotes and answers the stone below, and the last answer becomes the next question's ground.
