# The Counterfactual Boundary

*The road not taken has a view worth describing — but nothing was ever built on it, and nobody may charge rent there.*

What gathers here: the law that keeps what *would have* happened separate from what *did* happen in a money system — counterfactual market evidence may stand beside the treasury's books, but only settled receipts may cross into them.

**What the boundary keeps apart.** A trading system can honestly compute "we would have saved £40 by routing through the other venue" — that claim can carry sources, timestamps, even proofs. What it cannot carry is settlement: nothing moved, no fee was charged, no position changed hands. [[treasury-accounting]] is the books of what *happened* — the word [[ledger]] in its strictest dress — and its strength is that every line traces to a settled, final, fee-complete [[receipt]]. The counterfactual belongs *beside* those books, as commentary and comparison, visible and auditable. Inside them it is a lie wearing a real source's coat.

**Why this is the castle's own law in financial dress.** The four tests on a crossing receipt — settled, final, fee-complete, deduplicated — are the same refusal the castle has kept from the start. [chain-not-sum](chain-not-sum.md) found that unlike meanings must never be added into one impressive total: a balance and a would-have-been are unlike meanings, and summing them manufactures a number that answers to nothing. [verification-receipts](verification-receipts.md) named the general shape: a receipt proves exactly what it proves and nothing more, and a counterfactual receipt proves a comparison was computed — never that the movement it imagines occurred. And [karma](karma.md)'s law holds here too: the return path runs from an *attributable deed* to evidence; an imagined deed has no attributable path back, so no consequence may be booked against it.

**The four tests, in plain words.** *Settled* — the trade actually cleared; the money and the position finished changing hands. *Final* — it can no longer be reversed, cancelled, or unwound by the venue. *Fee-complete* — every cost the movement charged is already inside the number, so nothing later arrives as a surprise. *Deduplicated* — the same movement is not counted twice through two feeds or two hands. A receipt that fails any one of the four stays on the commentary side — beside the books, where such evidence is useful, never in a balance where it is not.
