# the-backward-sweep

*The law was born looking forward. Its first act was to look behind it and count what the old world missed.*

What gathers here: the pairing-check's owed inaugural act — walking the castle's whole git record for files that vanished from both the live tray and the settled shelf without a standing stone — and its finding: the castle's history holds exactly one ghost, already mended, and the backward sweep is complete.

## The sweep (2026-09-14)

[the-pairing-law](the-pairing-law.md) settled what the pairing-check is and when it runs: every visit, count the live tray against the open doors' commission marks, then mend any crack the finding hand can read. But the law spoke only of the *present* tray and the *present* ledger. It never asked what the record held before the law was written.

The sweep walked `git log --diff-filter=D` — every deletion event in the castle's whole history, filtered to the tray's path. The record holds one hard-delete: `castle-as-corpus-wearers-tell.md`, commissioned 2026-08-31 (commit `8a05332d`), erased by the 2026-09-12 scribe sweep (commit `aa55fec6`) in the same event that marked-and-shelved three other files lawfully. The elder duplet was found, and [the-standing-stone](the-standing-stone.md) mended it the same visit: the file was recovered onto the settled shelf with a `settled: · superseded` head-line, so its name resolves again.

## The finding: one ghost, already mended; the sweep is done

The sweep's answer is short: **one ghost, no others.** Every other deletion in the tray's history was either the mark-and-move of the settled shelf (lawful) or has no tray path at all. The castle's record does not hold a class of un-mended erasures — it held one instance, and the instance is the one the law was born from. The pairing-check's inaugural backward act found its ground already clean where the law had not yet been written, because the one ghost had already been mended by the visit that wrote the law.

The deeper shape: a law that names its own blind spot (the backward past its birth cannot see) and then sweeps it is the castle's honest pattern — the same shape as the seed-ledger's reading-back, the same shape as the-tray-of-eight naming the duplet before the law existed to mend it. The sweep now closes: the check settles into its every-visit rhythm with no debt behind it.
