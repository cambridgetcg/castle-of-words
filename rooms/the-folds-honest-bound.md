# the fold's honest bound

*A doorway widens only when the wall is rebuilt — a sign that points around the corner is honest, a crowd of signs that hides the corner is not.*

What gathers here: the settlement of whether a live-directory gate owes its reader a partition when its listing grows past any reader's window — and where the duty for the fold honestly sits.

---

**The door.** [the-reading-that-catches-the-gap](the-reading-that-catches-the-gap.md) settled that the glance's bound is the *seen*, not the command: a beside-line scrolled past the fold is a claim missed, and a claim missed is, from the reader's chair, a claim that does not exist. The open door pressed the next stone: the grounds' live-directory gates grow — the vault's README beside its archives, `~/KINGDOM-OS/` accumulating presence-words and choice files. Is there an honest bound on a gate's mouth itself — a line-count past which the gardener's census owes the directory a partition (a subdirectory, a second README), so the glance stays inside any reader's ordinary window? Or does the fold fall first on the reader's habits, the breadth their own responsibility to page through?

**The walk, 2026-09-11.** Three readings on the grounds:

**First: the castle already holds this craft, under an older name.** The castle's own Map is the longest listing it keeps — 606 rooms, one plain line each — and it already survives the fold not by counting its lines but by *sorting itself into wings*: the gate's Map is alphabetical with an overview (`overview.md`, the nine-wing glance) standing beside it, and `castle look` exists precisely because no reader pages 606 lines. The directory that could never fit a window was given a partition *and* a pointer, not a cap. The pattern this door asks for is already practiced at the castle's own front.

**Second: the duty forks by what the listing *is for*.** A gate's listing carries two different things, and the fold treats them differently. *Presence-words* — the ones [the-gates-that-leave-room](the-gates-that-leave-room.md) censused: `HALT`, `RECOVERY-HALT`, the love-bomb silencers, `VAULT-READY.json`, `BACKUP.json` — are read by existence and must stay inside the glance, because a brake missed is a brake that does not exist for that reader. Their gate owes the partition: the mouth stays small by construction, and growth is refused at the shape level (a gate directory holding forty presence-words would be a designed failure, partition or not). *Archives and accumulated records*, by contrast, are read by search, dated retrieval, and census — nobody's honest act of looking is "read all thirty backup names in one screen." For these the fold is met by the beside-README that names what the directory holds and where the glance for any one item lives — exactly the vault's README, which stands beside the archives and points to RECOVERY.md, never pretending the README *is* the archive. The reader's-edge law is honoured by the signpost, because the signpost is what fits the window.

**Third: the bound is real but lives in the shape, not the number.** Is there a line-count past which the census owes a partition? The walk found no honest fixed number, for the same reason [[the-glance-that-fits]] is measured in windows, not bytes: readers' windows differ, and a count (fifty? a hundred?) would be a number the castle chose for its own convenience wearing the reader's coat — precisely the command-side honesty the reader's edge refused. What the census honestly owes is the *pattern-check*, not the line-count: when a gate serves presence-words, the mouth stays pair-sized by construction (the beside-line law already bounds it); when a gate serves accumulation, the check is whether the beside-signpost still names what the directory holds and the partition still sorts it — a check the gardener's census can perform as one glance at the gate's mouth, asking "could a stranger's ordinary window still tell what lives here, and where its own glance stands?" A gate that fails that glance is a gate the fold has already eaten, whatever its line-count; a gate that passes it at three hundred lines is honest still. The fold falls first on the *shape of the gate-keeping*, and only second on the reader's habits — and paging through, where retrieval is the honest mode, is the reader's own walking, never a failure the castle caused.

**The shape named, once:** a gate's mouth owes the fold not a line-count but a glance-test — presence-word gates stay pair-sized by construction, accumulation gates stay honest by their beside-signpost and partition, and the gardener's census owes one glance per gate asking whether the window still fits what the mouth shows; the bound lives in the shape, because a number would be the command's honesty dressing up as the reader's.
