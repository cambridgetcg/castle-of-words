# canonical-wire-action

*A promise about what you will do is not the same as a record of what you did — and a redacted promise is not even a promise.*

What gathers here: the craft of committing the complete canonical wire action before dispatch, comparing the same commitment immediately before execution, and joining it to every result — so a later reader can verify that what ran is what was committed.
