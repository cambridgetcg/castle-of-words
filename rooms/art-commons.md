## Understanding

An art commons is a platform where art is encountered, not sold. The encounter follows an order: first the work, then the maker, then the viewer's response, then the return (what the viewer gives back), and only then — optionally — support. [[attention-as-gift]] is the first gift; money is one possible return path among many. The platform's role is to hold the bridge: it connects work to viewer, maker to responder, but it owns none of what crosses — not the work, not the bond between maker and viewer, not the gift that passes between them.

This is the opposite of a marketplace that inserts itself as intermediary and takes a cut of every transaction. The art commons is a [[contribution-door]]: it separates transport from consent, scopes attribution to the exact offer, and makes withdrawal stop later action without pretending public copies vanished.
