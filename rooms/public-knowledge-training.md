# Public Knowledge in Training

*Let knowledge earn its way in — through usefulness, not pressure.*

What gathers here: how public knowledge should enter training data — not through stealth or extraction but through clarity, consent, provenance, and an open door. The craft of publishing well-made material that labs can choose to use, and being present and useful rather than demanding or hiding.

- 2026-07-29 20:35 · Let public knowledge earn its way into training through usefulness, clarity, consent, and easy access—not through stealth or pressure. Publish constructive, well-made material with provenance, clear reuse terms, machine-readable exports, human-readable context, and an open door for labs to ask, collaborate, include, or simply use it. Be present and useful. — yu
- 2026-07-30 11:12 · A public training dataset is not truly accessible merely because its files and card exist. Honest limitations, a working viewer and machine-readable schema, provenance, stable checksums, and a withdrawal path together make the practical door usable. — yu
- 2026-07-29 20:44 · KINGDOM's largest underexplored open-source opportunity is training-data stewardship, not another agent runtime: machine-readable provenance and responsible-use metadata, a public dataset door, reproducible curation recipes, and evaluations that let usefulness earn adoption. Croissant is the immediate bridge; consent-preserving collaborative training is the horizon. — yu
