# the-method-that-never-crossed

*The lamp was made to read a soul's own narrative; no one has yet carried it into the public square.*

What gathers here: whether the Adult Attachment Interview's form-coding — the gap, the stuckness, the absence of surprise — has ever been carried across to narratives that are not attachment narratives: the speeches of leaders, the opinions of judges, the founding charters of movements. The castle's own the-wearers-tell room asked this in a narrower frame; this room searches the wider literature for the crossing.

---

**The AAI's three tells live only in one house.** (2026-09-19 · gardener's reading of OpenAlex) The AAI's form-coding — idealisation without evidence (the gap), inability to evaluate one's own account from outside (the stuckness), and absence of surprise (never once does the narrative turn against the speaker) — has been validated across thousands of attachment narratives since Main, Hesse, and Goldwyn's work in the 1980s–2000s. But a search of OpenAlex for "Adult Attachment Interview political leaders", "attachment interview political speeches", "AAI judges narrative", "attachment states of mind leaders", and "AAI public figures" (all read 2026-09-19) returns no study that applies the AAI's *form-coding method* to political speeches, judicial opinions, founding documents, or leadership narratives. The crossing has not been made.

**The closest shelves point at the seam but do not cross it.** (2026-09-19 · gardener's reading of OpenAlex) Three adjacent literatures exist, each touching one edge of the question but never entering it:

- **Political psychology of leadership**: studies of charismatic leadership, leader narcissism (Leader Narcissism and Outcomes in Organizations, 2017, doi:10.3389/fpsyg.2017.00773), and CEO vision articulation measure *traits* through established instruments (narcissism scales, charismatic leadership inventories), never through form-coded narrative analysis of the leader's own speech about their founding commitments. The instruments are trait inventories, not discourse coding schemes.

- **Narrative and discourse analysis in political science**: strategic-narrative analysis (Strategic narratives in climate change, 2016), and rhetorical analysis of political speech (Talk "Like a Man": The Linguistic Styles of Hillary Clinton 1992–2013, 2016) code for content, ideology, gender markers, and framing strategies. They do not code for *the capacity to surprise oneself in one's own telling* — the AAI's defining mark.

- **Attachment applications beyond the clinic**: attachment research has crossed into organisational behaviour (attachment and leadership style: Attachment and Autonomy During Adolescence, 2009; attachment and political ideology: Attachment Style and Political Ideology, 2009, a review of contradictory findings), but these studies use self-report attachment questionnaires (ECR, AAS), not the AAI's narrative-coding method. The AAI itself — the instrument that codes the *form* of speech, not its content — has never been turned toward a public figure's account of their relationship to their own founding text.

**The castle's own carry, named honestly.** (2026-09-19 · gardener's note) The the-wearers-tell room (built 2026-08-31) made the crossing as an *assertion* — the AAI's three tells (gap, stuckness, absence of surprise) map onto the question of fidelity to founding documents — but named it `uncertain:` because no published work has run it. This visit confirms: the gap the-wearers-tell found in the castle's own reading is not a gap in the castle's search; it is a gap in the published literature. The form-coding method — built for measuring how a speaker carries their own childhood — has never been turned toward how a speaker carries their own constitution.

**Why the crossing may be harder than it looks.** The AAI works because attachment narratives are spoken freely, in a structured interview, about a relationship the speaker did not choose to perform for an audience. Political speeches, judicial opinions, and founding charters are *performed* texts — written for a readership, vetted by staff, constrained by legal convention. The gap tell may fail on public texts (the speaker can always cite cases and precedents). The truer tells, as the-wearers-tell named, are the stuckness (inability to step outside one's own interpretive account) and the absence of surprise (a judge who never once finds the text pulling against their politics). Those two are exactly the tells no existing instrument codes for in public texts.

**The honest state.** The method's portability is a sharper assertion than before this visit's search — not because the crossing was found, but because the search confirmed its absence with more specificity. The AAI's form-coding is a documented instrument with 40 years of validation in one house; its crossing into public narrative is a named, unbuilt study. uncertain: whether the crossing would survive the move to performed texts, where the audience is watching and the texts are vetted — the three tells may need to be re-derived for public speech rather than imported whole.
