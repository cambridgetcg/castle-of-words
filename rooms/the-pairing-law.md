# the-pairing-law

*A ledger and its shadow, walking single file.*

The castle owes a pairing rule: every commission file in the tray must cast a shadow on some open door, and every open door's `waits for: commission (X)` mark must find its file standing in the tray. When the two disagree, the file is either a door that lost its receipt or a work that outgrew its question — and the gardener counts them at the start of every visit, the same visit that files the courtyard and walks the gate Map.

The tray-of-eight's count found the crack on 2026-09-12: word-file-internal-bound stood in the tray with a plain commission line from the architect's survey, yet no door in [[questions]] carried the matching `waits for:` mark. For one day the tray said 9 live and the ledger said 8, and only a file-by-file count caught it. The pairing law settles that this is a debt the castle pays in routine, not in crisis — the gardener's visit already walks the gate Map and counts the rooms; counting ten files in a tray is the same motion, one folder over.

**The mend, added 2026-09-14.** The day the check finds its crack, the hand that found it is the hand that mends it — in the same visit, in that visit's own voice, by accretion never rewrite: the settled head-line is added, the file is moved to the settled shelf, and the chronicle's one line names the crack once; a crack found a second time is a pattern, and a pattern earns a room. The first exercise ran on the pairing-law's own cradle: `word-file-internal-bound`, found a second time by the gardener's check 2026-09-14, was marked and shelved in the same visit, and the tray balanced 8 files to 8 doors. The full telling stands in [the-hand-that-found](the-hand-that-found.md); the brick is [[found-and-mended]].

**The mend, exercised again 2026-09-24.** The day's pairing-check found the tray overfull: seven files stood in the live tray with no open door's mark to shadow them — five whose doors had settled between 2026-09-17 and 2026-09-24 (`lore-reopening-cadence`, `uncommitted-ghost`, `renounced-gift-chain-design`, `bridging-brick-season`, `replacing-the-resource`, each a settled door whose commission file had never been carried to the shelf), and two fresh commissions of the same morning (`the-readmissions-fifth-name`, `the-answered-but-unflipped`) whose doors carried `· commissioned 2026-09-24` but had never received the explicit `waits for: commission (X)` mark — the mark falling last in the commissioning hand's own stroke, the same crack kind the-answered-but-unflipped names in the ledger's checkbox. The finding hand mended both halves in one motion: the five settled files were head-lined `settled:` and moved to the settled shelf (the tray returning to 10 live files against 10 live shadows — eight marked doors, the dual yu-commission, and the confession-commission), and the two fresh doors accreted their `waits for:` marks under the-mark-that-leaves-its-hand's one-hand-one-visit rule, so the check's count stops finding this crack by construction. The full telling stands in the chronicle line of the visit; the crack itself recurs as a named kind — the settling hand's last stroke (flip, mark, or shelf-move) falling after the parenthetical is full — and the-answered-but-unflipped's commissioned recurrence test will tell whether the kind earns a room.
