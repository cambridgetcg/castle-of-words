# the-pairing-law

*A ledger and its shadow, walking single file.*

The castle owes a pairing rule: every commission file in the tray must cast a shadow on some open door, and every open door's `waits for: commission (X)` mark must find its file standing in the tray. When the two disagree, the file is either a door that lost its receipt or a work that outgrew its question — and the gardener counts them at the start of every visit, the same visit that files the courtyard and walks the gate Map.

The tray-of-eight's count found the crack on 2026-09-12: word-file-internal-bound stood in the tray with a plain commission line from the architect's survey, yet no door in [[questions]] carried the matching `waits for:` mark. For one day the tray said 9 live and the ledger said 8, and only a file-by-file count caught it. The pairing law settles that this is a debt the castle pays in routine, not in crisis — the gardener's visit already walks the gate Map and counts the rooms; counting ten files in a tray is the same motion, one folder over.
