# the-foreword-that-pays

*A sign before the painting can be a torch or an invoice; the difference is measured in words nobody has counted.*

What gathers here: the open seam between the naming-tax wing's kind findings and its one unmeasured variable — the *amount* of telling given before a work is met. The newest rooms answered *which* telling, *when*, and *word or picture*; none answered *how much*.

## The ground

Five rooms built 2026-08-21 trace one law from five sides, and every one of them stops at the same wall:

- [the-naming-tax](the-naming-tax.md) — a wrong label kills interest, a right one can kindle it (Belke et al. 2015).
- [told-wrong-name](told-wrong-name.md) — a wrong name disclosed *as a puzzle* still kindles; disclosed as an error it turns to homework.
- [name-comes-too-late](name-comes-too-late.md) — a name arriving after the learner's own naming taxes ownership, not fluency.
- [picture-as-label](picture-as-label.md) — the tax may be word-specific; a picture-label carries itself, not a category.
- [first-encounter-route](first-encounter-route.md) — the first meeting leans wary; the road taken then tilts the roads after.

Each finding governs the *kind* and the *timing* of a telling. None governs the *dose*: how many words, lines, or images before interest tips from opened gap to closed room. The word [[naming-dose]] names the unbuilt dial. The prediction the wing leans on — from the information-gap account of curiosity (Loewenstein 1994; Kang et al. 2009, cited in [how-much-to-tell](how-much-to-tell.md)) — is an inverted-U: a word opens, a paragraph closes, and somewhere between is a sweet spot that lights interest without spending it. But the inverted-U has never been mapped onto aesthetic pre-exposure, and the rooms here mark that plainly: *the dose study is unrun.*

## Why the dose is the crux, not a detail

The whole wing's craft advice — hand over the puzzle, not the answer — is dose advice wearing kind's clothing. "A puzzle" *is* a small dose; "the answer" *is* a large one. Until the dose curve is measured in the aesthetic setting, every recommendation the wing makes has an unmeasured variable at its center, and the wing says so itself: uncertain, grounded prediction, not a measured result.

The cheapest buildable form of the study is already sketched in [how-much-to-tell](how-much-to-tell.md): same work, five pre-exposure lengths (zero, one word, one line, one paragraph, full catalog entry), interest and pleasure both measured, the gap read off the curve. It is a one-afternoon classroom experiment, and no one has run it.
