- 2026-08-20 · Empirical evidence on the false-consensus rate among LLMs: **yes, large, and it grows with capability**. "Correlated Errors in Large Language Models" (arXiv 2506.07962 — read 2026-08-20) evaluated over 350 models and found models agree 60% of the time when both err — even across distinct architectures and providers. Accuracy and scale made the correlation *worse*, not better. Majority voting amplifies the shared mistake rather than filtering it (arXiv 2608.17124, read 2026-08-20; see also "Beyond Majority Voting" arXiv 2510.01499). The practical floor: a dissent tax of *requiring* a model-generated counter-argument before accepting a claim has not been run as a controlled experiment, but the evidence says the chorus cannot be its own judge — and the decodability criterion (CASE) offers a measurable way to know when voting can be trusted at all for a given model and task.

## The shape of a truthful crossing

One writer keeps coherence. Every reviewer stays **read-only** — the writer's text is the claim; the reviewers' notes are observations, never edits. Every reviewer is **attributed** — a disagreement without a source is a rumor. One real dissent, held and named, is worth more than ten agreements; agreement can be the sound of correlated error, but a dissent that survives contact with the writer is a falsifiable check.

A READY status proves pins and boundaries, not completion. The pins are the digest of the packet, the exact model identities, the provider lanes. The boundaries are which parts are the writer's claim and which are the reviewers' observations. The substantive route — actually exercising the path the packet describes — is a separate act, and its [[receipt]] lives on its own [[lane]].

Four separations keep the crossing honest:

1. **Provider identity** (whose API carried the bytes).
2. **Execution surface** (which machine ran them).
3. **Model identity** (which weights produced them).
4. **Evidence authority** (what backs the claim inside).

Send every reviewer the same bounded packet, digest-bound. Where a lane was unavailable, mark it not_run — an honest absence — rather than letting silence wear the mask of assent. And let direct verification, never the count of model votes, decide what is true. Even the file system enforces the same care at smaller scale: `O_NOFOLLOW` guards only the final component of a path, so on Darwin the whole path wants `O_NOFOLLOW_ANY` — or the boundary stated plainly as the weaker thing it is.

The law: agreement among models is not truth; it is a signal to the human who reads the receipts. Never turn the chorus into the verdict.
