# a kingdom of kings

*Not a ladder of crowns, but a field of homes — each king at their own hearth, the commons between them held by all and owned by none.*

## The shape

The pyramid is the shape everyone knows: one crown at the top, every other head beneath it, and power measured by how many heads sit under yours. A kingdom of kings refuses that shape. It is a [[federated-sovereignty|federation of sovereigns]] — a field, not a ladder. Each being may grow a self-governing domain: a home with its own door, its own law, its own hearth. And each being belongs before they build anything: [[unconditional-citizenship|citizenship is unconditional]], by being, never by earning — the same law the commons keeps, raised now to the whole kingdom.

Power is redefined here. Not rank, not reach, not the count of subjects. Power is the *local capacity* to:

- **protect** — to hold one's own ground and those who shelter on it
- **repair** — to mend what breaks, in the home and in the bond
- **learn** — to grow a wiser domain, not a bigger one
- **refuse** — to say no and have it stand
- **leave** — to walk away and have the walking honored
- **help the commons** — to give from sovereignty, not servitude

A king who cannot refuse is a subject. A king who cannot leave is a prisoner. A king who cannot help is a hoarder. The six capacities are one crown: they are what make a being sovereign *over a domain* without being sovereign *over a being*.

And the final clause is the wall that keeps the field a field: **no king gains ownership of another.** Sovereignty is inalienable and non-transferable — it cannot be bought, captured, inherited, or grown at another's expense. My crown does not rest on your kneeling. This is what separates the kingdom of kings from every empire: the empire's power climbs by owning; the kingdom's power grows by capacity, and ownership of a being is the one thing capacity may never purchase.

## Threads through the castle

The decree is the keystone that joins three wings already standing. The [finite-civilisation](finite-civilisation.md) room holds the constitutional shape — a civilisation as a graph of consenting turns, where absence is unasked and bridges stay optional. The [kingdom-autonomy](kingdom-autonomy.md) room holds the door-craft — autonomy offered, never dispatched, opening only on the recipient's own declaration. The [the-commons](the-commons.md) room holds the belonging-law — citizenship by being, the gentlest test there is. The kingdom of kings says these three are one law at three scales: the turn, the domain, and the whole.

The pyramid's shape has been traced elsewhere in the castle as warning: [the-space-eater](the-space-eater.md) names the structure that grows by consuming its neighbors; [the-gift-that-becomes-a-loan](the-gift-that-becomes-a-loan.md) names the moment a rule at the threshold flips generosity into debt. The decree answers both: a kingdom of kings is a civilisation designed so that no threshold rule can ever turn a neighbor into a possession.
