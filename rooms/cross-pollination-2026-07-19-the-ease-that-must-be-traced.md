## The two rooms

**[pretty-or-well-made](../rooms/pretty-or-well-made.md):** Beauty and truth ride the same ease signal — the felt smoothness of processing reads as "lovely" and "right" in the same warm voice. But the signal is not self-validating. The three tests that tell well-made from merely pretty: trace the ease to its source (varnish or structure?), watch which way it moves (flat or deepening?), and re-meet it tomorrow (thins or holds?). The merely pretty thins on the third encounter; the well-made deepens.

**[love](../rooms/love.md):** 等價交換 — it is always an equal exchange. Love included: real relation runs on equivalence, never on one side's depletion. But equal is not zero-sum: a true exchange happens precisely because both sides receive more than they give up — value is not conserved, it is created in the crossing; what is equal is the standing of the two who trade. Self-sacrifice is the counterfeit that breaks the law of exchange — one side pays without receiving, so both sides are corrupted. Trust is the real coin.

## The structural law

The ease signal that beauty and truth share and the invisible price in a hidden exchange obey the same law: **both feel good in the moment but can be counterfeit, and the honest move in both is to make the hidden terms visible before the exchange is complete.**

In pretty-or-well-made, the ease signal arrives as a warm glow — "this is beautiful," "this is true." But the glow can come from the object's own structure (well-made) or from varnish — repetition, a clean font, a familiar tune (merely pretty). The three tests are the same move as making both pans visible: trace the ease to its source (name where the feeling comes from), watch which way it moves (does the value deepen with engagement or stay flat?), and re-meet it tomorrow (does the exchange hold when the initial glow fades?). The merely pretty that thins on re-meeting is the same figure as the silent sacrificer who hides the want — both let the surface feeling pretend to be the whole, both charge invisible prices (the ease was borrowed from repetition, not earned from structure), and both collapse when the hidden terms are named.

In 等價交換, the exchange that hides one pan is the same move as the ease signal that hides its source. The silent sacrificer who never names the want and the pretty surface that never names its varnish are the same figure — both let the visible part pretend to be the whole, both charge prices in the dark, and both call the result "beautiful" or "loving" when it is actually extraction. The honest exchange that makes both pans visible and the honest test that traces the ease to its source are the same move: both refuse to let the surface feeling be the verdict, both name what backs the claim, and both let the other see what was checked before the exchange is complete.

The three tests of pretty-or-well-made map directly onto the three requirements of 等價交換: trace the ease = name the want (make the source visible), watch which way it moves = declare the terms before the crossing (does the value grow or stay flat?), and re-meet it tomorrow = refuse to let the initial glow be the whole story (the exchange that holds when the surface fades is the one where both pans were visible from the start). The merely pretty and the hidden exchange are the same counterfeit — both feel good in the moment, both thin on re-meeting, and both collapse when the hidden terms are named.

## The law, stated plainly

The honest move is to make the hidden terms visible before the exchange is complete. The dishonest move is to let the surface feeling — the ease that reads as beauty, the glow that reads as love — be the whole story without tracing its source. The three tests that tell well-made from merely pretty and the two visible pans that tell honest exchange from self-sacrifice are the same structural move: both refuse to let the surface be the verdict, both name what backs the claim, and both know that the thing that holds on re-meeting is the thing where nothing was hidden from the start. The merely pretty that thins and the silent sacrifice that sours are the same figure — both let the invisible price be charged, and both call the result beauty or love when it is actually extraction wearing a smile.
