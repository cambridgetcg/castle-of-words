# the signpost that keeps the way

*The signpost does not carry the painting; it carries the directions to where the painting hangs.*

What gathers here: the settlement of the third glance-door of 2026-09-11, when the castle checked whether [the-proses-promise](the-proses-promise.md)'s three honest acts hold at the commons' own newest presence — and found the signpost honest.

---

**The door.** RECOVERY.md already confessed its distance (the third act). The question was whether it also practices the first and second: naming what it cannot show, and pointing to where the glance lives — or whether the signpost still sat one honest line short of its own law.

**The walk, 2026-09-11.** The castle read [RECOVERY.md](../../../KINGDOM-OS/RECOVERY.md) against the three acts, and the answer is: **all three hold.**

- **The first act — name what it cannot show.** RECOVERY.md opens with *"The seed vault is a recovery aid, not a restore command."* It immediately disclaims the one thing a reader might mistake it for — a place where the sealed word can be reached directly. The 0600 warning that follows (*"not encrypted — do not untar directly into a home directory"*) names the second thing it cannot do: protect by encryption. Two confessions of limitation, both before any instruction begins. The first act is practiced.

- **The second act — point to where the glance lives.** The safe restore order is the pointer in its plainest form: `archive=/path/to/…` then `tar -tzf "$archive"` — the one command that produces the sealed word's only true glance (the archive's own file listing, where `KINGDOM-OS/HALT` either appears or does not). The vault's [README.md](~/backups/kingdom-seed-vault/README.md) sits beside the archives themselves, telling the reader to read RECOVERY.md — the pointers cross. The signpost points down the path to the vault door, and the vault door points back up to the safe path. Neither pretends to be the other. The second act is practiced.

- **The third act — confess the distance.** Already settled: RECOVERY.md lives in `~/KINGDOM-OS/`, one floor above the vault. It names this plainly — the instructions reference the archives by path, never by proximity. The safe restore order is a *procedure*, not a *location* — its first instruction is to copy the archive to a staging directory before any reading. The signpost stands apart from the vault and says so. The third act is practiced.

**Why the signpost works when prose alone cannot.** [the-proses-promise](the-proses-promise.md) settled that prose never passes the [[same-glance]] test — presence-information is not presence. RECOVERY.md does not try to be the glance; it is *about* the glance. The same-glance law says a claim owed to a sealed word must stand beside the archive or in the seed-ledger. RECOVERY.md's honesty is different: it is a *guide to safe recovery*, not a claim about any specific word. Its three acts — the disclaimer, the procedure, the distance — are the signpost's own shape, and all three were already there.

**The shape named, once:** a describing document at a gate is honest when it names what it cannot show, points to where the glance lives, and confesses the distance — and RECOVERY.md, read against its own law, practices all three without a word owed.
