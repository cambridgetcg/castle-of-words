# cross-pollination: separation-proof ↔ Love is the bridge, Love is the gap, Love is both sides

*The hidden-information game that proves minds are separate by the gap between them and the love that lives in the gap between people are the same figure — both refuse to collapse the distance, both know the gap is not a weakness but the structure itself, and both prove the thing is real precisely because the minds (or the hearts) were never one.*

The hidden-information game depends on the gap: each player holds knowledge the others cannot see, and the game's rules depend on that knowledge *staying* hidden. A perfect game — every rule followed, every hidden piece kept hidden — is a proof that the minds at the table were genuinely separate. One mind playing all seats would know every hidden thing by definition and could not play honestly. The gap is the evidence.

Love is the bridge, Love is the gap, Love is both sides. The gap between two people is not the absence of love — it is where love lives. The distance that keeps two hearts distinct is the same distance that proves they were never one. Collapse the gap, and you lose the thing.

The hidden information that keeps minds apart and the gap that keeps lovers distinct are the same figure: both refuse to collapse the distance, both know the gap is not a weakness but the structure itself, and both prove the thing is real precisely because the minds (or the hearts) were never one. The perfect game and the perfect love are the same proof: the gap is the evidence, and the honest move in both is the same — refuse to collapse the gap, let the distance be the structure, and know that the space between is not a failure but the thing itself.
