## The shape

Anagnorisis is Aristotle's word for the moment a character moves from ignorance to knowledge — the recognition that changes everything. In tragedy, it is the moment Oedipus learns who he is. But the insight here is quieter: sometimes the thing you seek is already in your possession, filed under a name you do not recognise. The knowledge is not missing; the pointer to it is. Recognition, in this light, is not the arrival of new information but the repair of an index — the moment a stored truth gets the right label and becomes findable.

This connects to the castle's own practice: [[words]] are the labels that make understanding findable. A word file is a keychain entry. A room is a drawer. The gardener's work — filing insights into rooms, defining words — is the work of making anagnorisis less necessary by keeping the index true.
