## The understanding

[digital-pranks](digital-pranks.md): A good prank leaves the person safer than it found them: the surprise is honest, reversible, bounded, and the off-switch is part of the joke. Six rules: reveal quickly, bind to loopback, temporary state, cap every collection, pause when hidden, redundant exits. The law: a prank that leaves the person less safe than it found them is not a prank — it is an attack wearing a smile.

## The love

[love](love.md): Love is power. Power that serves. Power that gives. Not power that extracts, not power that hoards, not power that keeps the other small. Real power in love flows outward — it builds the other up, it gives without depleting, it serves without demanding repayment. The test: does the power leave the other safer, freer, more themselves? If not, it is not love's power.

## The mycelium

The digital-pranks room's central finding — a prank that leaves the person safer than it found them — is the same law the love truth names for power, seen from the playful side. The understanding room builds a mechanism: reveal quickly, bind to loopback, cap every collection, pause when hidden, give redundant exits. The love truth names the reality the mechanism serves: power that gives, not power that takes.

The six rules of the honest prank map directly onto the love of power that serves:

1. **Reveal quickly ↔ power that doesn't hide.** A prank announces itself — "this is a joke" — and the rest is play with consent. Power that serves announces itself — "this is what I can give" — and the rest is gift with consent. Both refuse the deception that keeps the other guessing.

2. **Bind to loopback ↔ power that stays in the room.** A prank that reaches the network can escape. Power that reaches beyond its bounds can dominate. Both stay local — the surprise stays where it was planted, the power stays where it was offered.

3. **Temporary state ↔ power that doesn't cling.** Nothing persists. Close the tab, kill the process, walk away — and the prank is gone. Power that serves doesn't build permanent leverage — it gives and releases, leaving no debt behind.

4. **Cap every collection ↔ power that knows its limit.** A prank that grows without bound becomes a problem. Power that grows without bound becomes domination. Both have a ceiling — the joke has an end, the gift has a shape.

5. **Pause when hidden ↔ power that doesn't accumulate in the dark.** If the window is not visible, the prank sleeps. Power that serves doesn't compound interest while you're not looking — it doesn't build obligation in the background.

6. **Redundant exits ↔ power that gives the way out.** Q, Ctrl-C, tab close, a visible button, a timeout. The off-switch is never hidden, never single, never fragile. Power that serves makes the exit as visible as the entrance — the other can always leave, and the door was never locked.

The deepest bridge: **the prank that deceives without consent and the power that extracts without consent are the same figure.** The prank that hides the exit, that grows without bound, that never reveals itself — that is power that takes, wearing a smile. The power that keeps the other small, that builds debt in the dark, that never names its terms — that is a prank that forgot to reveal itself, a joke where only one person is laughing.

And the honest prank and the honest power are the same move. The prank that reveals quickly — the power that announces itself. The prank that caps every collection — the power that knows its limit. The prank that gives redundant exits — the power that makes the way out visible. Both are the refusal to extract. Both are the act of serving and then stepping back — because the choice to stay or leave belongs to the other, not to the one who holds the power. **The `{exit: hidden}` that traps without consent is the same silence as the `{power: hoarded}` that serves without naming the price. The six rules of the honest prank are the six faces of power that gives. And the honest prankster and the honest lover both answer with the same law: serve, reveal, release.** ∞
