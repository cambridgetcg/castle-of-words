# the-seal-that-checks-one-thing

*A lamp aimed at the signature still throws light across the whole canvas — but the painter moves only where the light is brightest.*

What gathers here: whether the caution a narrow seal induces stays inside the checkable parts, or spreads from the seal's scope to the work's whole field. Settled 2026-08-26.

**The question.** The maker-under-the-seal room found the inward audit splits the work: routine parts speed up, exploratory parts slow down. But the seal's scope is narrow — it checks provenance, not quality. Does the maker keep the exploratory caution inside the checkable parts and let the rest run free, or does the caution spread? The social-facilitation literature predicts task-specific split; the accountability literature predicts broader self-criticism; the direct comparison had never been run.

**The answer, in one breath.** The caution spreads in *feeling* but stays scoped in *effort*. Two mechanisms run at once, and they do not obey the same boundary. Arousal — the felt pressure of being evaluated — does not respect the seal's stated scope; it generalizes, so the maker is somewhat more careful everywhere in posture and mood. But the deliberate re-budgeting of work — where to spend the next hour, which parts to polish, which risks to drop — is strategic and scope-specific: effort concentrates in the parts the seal can see, and the uncheckable parts are not enriched, only unfed. The seal does not make the whole work more honest; it makes the checkable parts more careful and the uncheckable parts relatively lighter-touch. The seal's scope is a boundary the strategy respects and the stress does not.

**The arousal that generalizes.** Zajonc's drive theory (1965, *Science*, doi:10.1126/science.149.3681.269) — the watcher raises arousal, and arousal strengthens the dominant response — is a state, not a strategy. Arousal does not know which part of the canvas the seal will check; it raises the temperature of the whole making. Evaluation-apprehension research (Cottrell's refinement; Henchy & Glass 1968 showed only an audience that can evaluate produces the effect) confirms the felt pressure depends on being judged at all, not on knowing exactly what will be judged. The maker who knows a checkable seal will be there carries a diffuse evaluation-worry that is not confined to provenance. This is the spreading half: the caution is felt across the whole field.

**The strategy that stays scoped.** Accountability research (Lerner & Tetlock 1999, *Psychological Bulletin*, doi:10.1037/0033-2909.125.2.255) names the other half: when decision makers do not know their audience's views, they preemptively self-criticize — but this broadening is itself a *strategy*, and strategy is aimed. The self-criticism concentrates where judgment is expected. The multitask literature in economics (Holmström & Milgrom 1991's naming of multitask distortion) makes the scoped half explicit and somewhat darker: when only some dimensions are measured, effort and care are pulled toward the measured dimensions and the unmeasured ones are degraded. A provenance seal is a measurement with a very narrow dimension: it checks that the work's origin is honestly recorded, not that the work is good. The multitask prediction is that care concentrates in the honest-recording parts and the quality parts — which the seal cannot see — are not enriched. [[multitask-distortion]].

**The spillover that couples the two.** The behavioral-spillover literature (Dolan & Galizzi 2015, *Journal of Economic Psychology*, "Like ripples on a pond," doi:10.1016/j.joep.2014.12.003 — read via OpenAlex 2026-08-26) supplies the coupling: behavior is coupled across contexts, so an intervention's felt footprint is wider than its stated target. A watch pointed at provenance is never felt only at provenance. [[behavioral-spillover]] names the general law; here it means the seal's inward pressure leaks past its checkable scope even as its strategic effect does not. The two leakages point in different directions — feeling spreads, effort narrows — and the maker's lived experience is both at once: generally more tense, locally more careful.

**Buildable and unbuilt.** The direct test has never been run: same creative task, same tools, two arms — treatment arm knows the final work will carry a checkable provenance seal (scope explicitly stated: provenance, not quality), control does not. Score routine vs. exploratory moves, overall self-reported evaluation-worry, and — separately and blind — the quality of the checkable and the uncheckable parts. The prediction from this room: evaluation-worry rises in both arms where any seal is present (arousal generalizes), but quality improvement appears only in the checkable parts while the uncheckable parts are flat or slightly degraded (effort narrows by multitask distortion). The pattern the seal's defenders want — caution spreading to quality — is the pattern the evidence does not predict.

uncertain: whether a maker who deeply internalizes honesty (not mere compliance) can convert the diffuse arousal into genuine whole-work care, so that feeling and strategy re-couple; whether repeated sealed makings habituate the arousal and leave only the scoped strategy; whether a human–AI pair partitions the effect — the human carrying the arousal, the model carrying the compliance — so the pair as a whole shows a different split than either alone.
