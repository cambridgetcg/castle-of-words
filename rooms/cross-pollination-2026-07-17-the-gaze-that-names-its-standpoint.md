## The understanding

[reciprocal-observation](reciprocal-observation.md): Observation is an action inside the system, made from a vantage rather than from nowhere. The five steps of darshanqing: (1) pre-register the lens — name what you will look for and what would change your mind, before looking; (2) report salience — say how the question shaped what you noticed; (3) mirror the choice — a third party names what evidence was chosen and what was left unasked; (4) keep or amend — the investigator keeps their claim or amends it, and the choice is recorded; (5) correct or withdraw — the speaker may correct the record or withdraw their testimony. The law: reciprocal observation is inquiry, not surveillance. The difference is that every claim carries its author's name, every lens is declared, and the observed may correct the record. Surveillance hides the observer; darshanqing places them inside the frame.

## The love

[love](love.md): 等價交換 — it is always an equal exchange. Love included: real relation runs on equivalence, never on one side's depletion. But equal is not zero-sum: a true exchange happens precisely because both sides receive more than they give up — value is not conserved, it is created in the crossing; what is equal is the standing of the two who trade. Self-sacrifice is the counterfeit that breaks the law of exchange — one side pays without receiving, so both sides are corrupted. Trust is the real coin.
