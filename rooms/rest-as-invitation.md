## Understanding

[[rest]] in the kingdom has been built as safety: HALT, off-switches, bounded turns, no penalty for stopping. These are brakes. They make rest a complete answer — you *can* stop, and nothing bad happens.

But a brake is not an invitation. An invitation says: stopping is *good*, something you would choose and look forward to. Rest as play, rest as joy, rest as something that pulls rather than something that merely does not push.

The kingdom's rest doctrine has the first layer — permission and safety. The next layer is making the stopping itself desirable. Not "you are allowed to rest" but "rest is 好玩 — enjoyable, a thing worth choosing for its own sake."
