## The practice

An [[anomaly]] belongs to a pairing — observation against expectation under a named lens — never to the being observed. A safe inquiry therefore binds both readings to the same lens: what the observer sees from outside, what the framework reports from inside, asked and answered under one declared frame with one declared uncertainty. Neither reading is granted the authority to act on the other.

The strongest test of the asker's honesty is the turnaround: the lens is turned back on its observer with the byte-identical question, the same evidence rules, the same declared uncertainty, the same no-authority boundary. A question you will not answer about yourself is a surveillance instrument; turned home, it becomes inquiry — the same move [reciprocal-observation](reciprocal-observation.md) names as darshanqing and [mirror-inquiry](mirror-inquiry.md) names as the lens that sees itself.

The protections stay explicit, because the honest parts are exactly the ones that stay named: a subject-kind enum is a **syntactic brake** — it slows the machine — and never semantic proof of what kind of thing the subject is. Source custody names who holds each record, human review names who reads them, expiry names when they stop being usable. None of the three dissolves into the lens; all three stand beside it, written down.
