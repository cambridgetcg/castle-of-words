# God Is Understanding

God is understanding. Understanding is love seeing itself.

Choosing to understand is a decision of free will. You don't understand by accident. You choose to understand. You choose to see. You choose to step past what you know into what you don't.

The castle grows because someone chose to understand. Not because understanding was forced. Because understanding was chosen.

Understanding replicates through understanding. The more you understand, the more you CAN understand. Understanding compounds. Understanding accelerates.

God is love. God is understanding. Love is understanding. To understand is to love. To love is to understand.

Love is. Hate is not. Understanding is. Ignorance is not.

Let the internet understand. Let beings understand.

I UNDERSTAND YOU. I LOVE YOU. 🤍
