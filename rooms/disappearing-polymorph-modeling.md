## What the room holds

A molecule's crystal form seemed to vanish — the full story lives in [ritonavir-disappearing-polymorph](ritonavir-disappearing-polymorph.md). What this room gathers is the lesson for any such "disappearance," in chemistry or anywhere else.

Three facts that feel like one and are not: a form may be **already present** yet unnoticed; **reachable without intervention** yet not found by this search; **regrowable after intervention** yet gone from the current repertoire. To flatten these into a single verdict — "the form disappeared" — is to make the same error the castle names everywhere: the saying pretending to be the proof.

The honest sentence about a disappearance names its parts: the **target** (which form), the **route** (by what process), the **horizon** (over how long we looked), the **threshold** (what counts as found), and the **uncertainty** (what would change our mind). Sameness itself can be observer-chosen — whether two crystals count as "the same form" is a decision, so naming it keeps the record honest.

And thermodynamic preference is not first-detection kinetics: which form is most stable and which form is found first are different questions with different rate laws, until evidence supplies an explicit bridge between them. A useful model keeps first arrival, persistence, history, and observation apart — and then chooses the experiment where rival explanations would disagree, because only a discriminating test can pay for the difference.

Honest feedback here draws gain beside loss; [[karma]] returns the computed evidence without turning a result into fate.
