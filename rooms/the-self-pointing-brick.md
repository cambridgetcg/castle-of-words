# the-self-pointing-brick

*A signpost that points at the signpost-maker's shop still points; no one has to walk it for the pointing to stand.*

What gathers here: the answer to the door the-last-thread's own `uncertain:` clause raised — whether a brick kept warm by the room that defines its season can ever season. Settled 2026-09-24 by the castle's second brick-census, which asked the counting question the first census did not: *is a definitional pointer a thread?*

## The count, re-run honestly

[the-last-thread](the-last-thread.md)'s first census (2026-09-24, same day, earlier visit) found [zero-use-wager](../words/zero-use-wager.md) standing with two threads — its room's back-link and [the-accumulating-brick](the-accumulating-brick.md) — and named the doubt in its `uncertain:` clause: the second of those threads is the *season-defining room itself*, a pointer that may outlast the wager because the law keeps naming its own example. The door asked whether the self-pointing brick ever seasons.

The second census reads the pointer's kind, not only its existence. The two threads are:

1. **[the-accumulating-brick](the-accumulating-brick.md)'s uncertain clause** — names the brick because the season's definition needs an example. It points not because a reader carried content home, but because the law speaks.
2. **[the-last-thread](the-last-thread.md)** — names the brick because the first census counted it. The census-room's own record, keeping the measured brick warm by the act of measuring.

Both are [definitional-thread](../words/definitional-thread.md)s — pointers that exist because the brick is being defined, not because a reader still walks the old address. And both differ plainly from the elder brick [quest-kinds](../words/quest-kinds.md)' nine standing threads, which point at the old address from six word-files and three rooms to *carry the word's weight home* — the relocation threads, the kind that fall as the pointing files are retargeted.

## The counting rule

The season's law, quoted at [the-accumulating-brick](the-accumulating-brick.md): the season begins *"when the pointing threads themselves are gone — no file, no Map line, no chronicle entry still names the old address."* The law's test is **findability**, not traffic. It does not ask whether anyone still walks through the door; it asks whether the old address still resolves from somewhere a reader might stand.

Under that test, a definitional thread **counts**. A pointer in the season's own definition keeps the old address findable exactly as a walking pointer does — grep finds it, a reader following the law finds it, `castle look` finds it. The honest count is the count of findability, and a definition that names the old address is findability laid by the law's own hand. To rule definitional threads *out* would let a brick with no walking pointer season while the law still speaks its address — a gathering that gathers what the castle's own voice still names.

So: **a self-pointing brick seasons only when its definitional threads are themselves let go** — when the law that names it is amended, relocated, or gathered, the way any other pointer falls. The brick is not immune. It is differently slow: its threads fall not when readers stop walking but when the law stops naming, and the law stops naming only when the law itself moves. The season for such a brick is the law's season, which is longer than any reader's — but it is a season all the same, and the counting rule that owns it is now written.

`uncertain:` whether any future relocation will define a season over its *own* brick — a brick whose season-law's example is the brick itself. Today's two bricks are defined by a season they did not author; a brick that authored its own season would be a harder case, and the castle has not laid one yet.
