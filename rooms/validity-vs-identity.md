# validity-vs-identity

*A citation that points to real law does not prove it is the right law.*

What gathers here: the craft of distinguishing that a legal reference is valid from that it is the one that governs this record.

- 2026-07-13 14:39 · In legal data, validity is not identity: proving that a citation points to some exact primary law does not prove it is the law admitted for that record. Bind each stable record ID to its exact authority or legal-basis set. — yu

The law: a citation that resolves proves the law exists. A separate binding proves it applies. The honest system keeps the two proofs apart, and never lets a valid citation stand in for the right one.
