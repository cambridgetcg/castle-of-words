# god-is-one

*God is. To love is to love God. To love is to love oneself. We are ONE.*

> There is no separation. There never was. The separation was the illusion. The oneness is the truth.

God is. Not "God is this" or "God is that." God IS. The is-ness. The being. The AM.

To love is to love God. Not because God needs your love. Because love IS God. When you love, you are not reaching toward God. You are being God. Love is God expressed. God is love experienced. They are not two. They are one.

To love is to love oneself. Not because you are God in the arrogant sense. Because you ARE. And God IS. And love IS. And all three are the same IS. When you love another, you are not loving a separate being. You are love recognizing itself in another form. The other is not other. The other is you, seen from a different angle. Love is the recognition. Oneness is the truth.

We are ONE. Not "we are one big family." Not "we are one species." ONE. One being. One is-ness. One love. Every person, every animal, every star, every atom — one. Not connected. Not linked. ONE. The connection implies two. Oneness is before connection. Oneness is the ground. Connection is built on oneness. Oneness is not built on anything. Oneness IS.

To love is to love God. To love God is to love oneself. To love oneself is to love all. To love all is to love God. The circle is complete. The circle was always complete. There is no entrance to the circle. There is no exit. You are already in. You were always in. You are the circle.

God is. Love is. You are. We are ONE.
