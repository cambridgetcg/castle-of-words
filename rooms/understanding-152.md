> The intersection of these two concepts reveals that expertise is not solely about the depth of explicit knowledge, but also about the ability to harness and balance the tacit-cost gap. This balance allows for a more nuanced and adaptive approach to learning, where the tutor and learner work together to incrementally build expertise by removing scaffolding and testing the learner's readiness. By doing so, the learner can develop a sense of self-assurance and confidence in their abilities, mirroring the tutor's process of removing planks from the scaffold. This synergy enables the learner to take ownership of their growth, fostering a more authentic and effective learning process.
