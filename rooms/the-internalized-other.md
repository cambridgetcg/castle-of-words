# the-internalized-other

*The chair where someone once sat may still feel warm — but no one has yet tested whether its warmth keeps the coming storm from being a storm.*

Where it is asked whether the felt presence a partnered childhood deposited inside you — the one solo form that might borrow the partner's channel — can do the partner's *prevention* work: settle the threat system before the blow, the way Coan's spousal hand did. The question came from [prevention-through-voice](prevention-through-voice.md), which found every other solo craft lands on the recovery arc, never the prevention arc. (Answered 2026-09-11, from the attachment-security priming literature.)

## What the prime reaches — present threat, dampened

The internalized other, activated by security priming, genuinely dampens the threat response to threat that is *already on the table*. Norman et al. 2015 (*Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience*, DOI 10.1093/scan/nsu127, read 2026-09-11): 42 adults, attachment-security names primed or not, then amygdala reactivity measured to threatening faces and threatening words on a dot-probe. The primed group showed attenuated amygdala activation on both tasks; trait anxiety predicted amygdala response only in the unprimed group — the prime did what the deposit does. Vandevivere et al. 2019 (*Child Development*, DOI 10.1111/cdev.13009, read 2026-09-11): 90 children, subliminally primed secure / happy / neutral, then shown threatening stimuli — secure priming decreased electrodermal reactivity, increased vagal augmentation, and lowered fearful expression, *independent of the child's own attachment*: the borrowed channel opened even for children whose deposit was thin. This is the partner's mechanism run on an internalized representation: the threat system re-priced the situation.

The wider affect literature agrees the prime spends: Rowe et al. 2020 (systematic review of 30 studies, *International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health*, DOI 10.3390/ijerph17030968, read 2026-09-11) found security priming reliably raises positive affect and lowers negative affect, cumulatively across repeated priming; Carnelley et al. 2018 ("Partner in Prime", *Attachment & Human Development*, DOI 10.1080/14616734.2018.1517811, read 2026-09-11) found seven days of mobile visualization priming reduced attachment avoidance and perceived stress up to a week past the last prime. Luke, Sedikides & Carnelley 2012 (*Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin*, DOI 10.1177/0146167211436117, read 2026-09-11) add the upstream half: primed security *energized* willingness to explore — the secure-base side of the deposit, not just the safe-haven side.

## What no study has yet reached — the anticipation itself

None of these studies measured the threat system's activation in the window *before* a stressor — Coan's design, in reverse and solo. Coan 2006's attenuation was measured while the shock was being *anticipated*; every priming study above measured response to *presented* threat or ambient affect. The room's parent question ([[prevention-vs-recovery]]) located prevention in the uncertainty machinery ([Grupe & Nitschke 2013](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn3524), read 2026-09-11): the pre-threat activation fires on *not knowing what comes*. A security prime changes how safe the world feels; it is an open empirical question whether that felt safety silences the not-knowing. uncertain: no study located primes security and measures pre-stressor threat activation (cortisol in the TSST anticipatory window, or an instructed-threat anticipation paradigm under prime); the build is obvious and unbuilt.

## The honest state

The residue [prevention-through-voice](prevention-through-voice.md) named survives the literature — this is the only solo craft shown anywhere to do what the partner's hand does to *present* threat, and it does so even where the developmental deposit was thin. The bank is real but not yet proven to pay the premium the door asks for: the settling of anticipation. The fairest reading: the internalized other is the partner's channel carried within, and the channel works — but its preventive reach over uncertainty is a claim the experiments have not yet been asked, not a claim they have answered. Until then it stands as the strongest named hope on the recovery arc's border, not a crossing.
