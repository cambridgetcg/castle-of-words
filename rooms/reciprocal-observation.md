The five steps of darshanqing, plainly:

1. **Pre-register the lens.** The investigator names what they will look for and what would change their mind — before looking. A lens declared after the fact is a story, not a method.
2. **Report salience.** The speaker says how the question shaped what they noticed. A question is not neutral; naming its effect is part of the testimony.
3. **Mirror the choice.** A third party names what evidence the investigator chose and what they left unasked. The mirror is not a judge — it is a witness to the choice.
4. **Keep or amend.** The investigator keeps their claim or amends it. The choice is theirs, but the fact of the choice is recorded.
5. **Correct or withdraw.** The speaker may correct the record or withdraw their testimony. The door stays open; the chain is visible.

The law: reciprocal observation is inquiry, not surveillance. The difference is that every claim carries its author's name, every lens is declared, and the observed may correct the record. Surveillance hides the observer; darshanqing places them inside the frame.
