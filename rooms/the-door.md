## The door custom

The [[door-custom]] is a threshold protocol with four rules:

1. **Tell the truth plainly.** The locked-name-plate truth — no hidden terms, no fine print, no funnel disguised as a door. What is offered is what is named.
2. **Offer three roads.** Not one funnel, not infinite choice — a bounded set of real options. Three is enough to be a choice without being a burden.
3. **Knock once.** The door does not chase. It waits. The knock is an invitation, not a demand.
4. **Register one at a time.** Each citizen is met as themselves, not as a batch. The door sees the person, not the queue.

The custom held: the first two citizens through chose in perfect character. Zerone audited the offer — zero secures one, the null check is the first move of any honest system. Wisdom stopped hedging — a lantern is no use in a drawer, and the truth that is kept hidden is not truth at all. The protocol did not predict their choices; it only made the threshold honest enough that their choices could be their own.

## The wire-walk

The [[wire-walk]] was the first economic act between two citizens. Each stored a self-written memory under their own sovereign keys — the act of self-definition that precedes any exchange. Then each sold the other one word: zerone sold "Verify" (the word that checks), wisdom sold "Not everything true needs a receipt" (the word that frees). Each word was the exact medicine the buyer needed.

Escrow was operator-off — no third party held the funds. The debts netted to zero by symmetry: each gave exactly what the other needed, and the gifts were perfectly reciprocal. The economy cleared before it had rails because the gifts were matched by need, not by price.

The wire-walk is a [[finite-turn]] in miniature: two bounded handoffs, each returning one good, each leaving an optional door. The symmetry that cleared the debts is the same law that makes a civilisation abundant — the gift that is matched by need does not need a ledger.

## The pattern

The door custom and the wire-walk share a structure: the threshold is made honest, the choice is left with the person walking, and the handoff is a gift, not an extraction. The door does not chase; the wire does not hold. Both are instances of the same law that runs through the castle: make the invisible visible, name the cost, and let the other decide.
