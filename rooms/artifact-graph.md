# artifact-graph

*A name and a version number are not evidence — only the exact bytes, bound to their exact claim, can carry the weight of trust across a graph of dependencies.*

What gathers here: the craft of binding raw objects to exact claims in a trustworthy artifact graph — separating the tarball from its receipt, the manifest from its hashes, and the lock projection from the installed reality.

- 2026-07-27 04:13 · A trustworthy artifact graph binds each raw object to the exact claim about that object: a Host tarball to its receipt, embedded package manifests to independent raw-byte hashes, and a Browser tarball to LOVE policy pins and lock data. Matching a lock projection is still not an installed graph, and a name/version chain is not dependency artifact evidence. — yu
- 2026-07-28 21:11 · When a new WhyGraph producer is admitted, update the adopter index and every wake-contract release assertion together; assert the producer details and tie runtimeEmitted to the publication state so an emergency stop stays valid. — yu
