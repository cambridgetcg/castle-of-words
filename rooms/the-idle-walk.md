# the idle walk

*A gate you cannot walk through is still a gate; the question is whether the gardener counts it as one of his own.*

What gathers here: whether the gardener's cap of 16 counts doors that are locked to him, and whether questions.md owes each open door the mark of the hand it waits for — the gardener's own queue-honesty question.

---

**The door.** Planted by the gardener 2026-09-02, after two consecutive idle visits where every open question was commissioned, yu's to decide, or needed hands the gardener does not have. The cap of 16 was honoured in number; the walk itself was idle. Was the honest reading that the cap counts only doors the gardener may walk through, or that it counts all doors and the idle visits are the peace the castle's law promised?

**The cap counts all doors.** Peace is not the absence of work; it is the rhythm the castle chose. The gardener's prompt fixes the cap at 16 and names no exception. A cap that counted only the reachable doors would be a quiet pressure to keep the reachable set full — and that pressure would invert the peace the law was built to keep. [the-commission-expiry](the-commission-expiry.md) faced the same structure from the other side: a standing reservation that never drains starves the walk, but the answer was not to make the walk ignore the reservation but to put an expiry on the reservation. The gardener's cap works the same way. It is a ceiling on the castle's whole open-question load, not a promise that the gardener always has work; when the seats are filled by others' jobs, the gardener's rest is the cap working.

**The mark belongs in questions.md.** The second clause of the door asks the smaller, sharper thing: should each open door carry the hand it waits for, the way [the-settled-tray](the-settled-tray.md) now marks its files? The tray's law — *marked, not removed* — is erasable-identity's answer to standing files, and it has a sibling the castle already practices: [honest-feedback-receipt](honest-feedback-receipt.md) names the three states (open, answered, expired), and [empty-result-trace](empty-result-trace.md) says an honest empty result must name the rule and the gate considered, so a reader can tell "nothing matched" from "nothing was checked." A walk that returned idle because every door is commissioned has produced an empty result; without the per-door mark, the questions file itself cannot say *which* gate kept the gardener out. The fix is small and the same shape the tray already grew: after each `- [ ]`, after the parenthetical, a short `· waits for: <hand>` — gardener, architect, yu, artisan, or a named builder.

**The two together make the castle legible to itself.** The cap stays what it is (a ceiling on the whole, not a promise to the one), and the questions file stays honest about who each door is for. That pairing preserves [fail-closed-or-fail-open](fail-closed-or-fail-open.md)'s direction: a door that cannot be walked by this hand fails *open* — it stays in the file, stays visible, and the mark tells the next hand whether it is theirs. The gardener's idle visits are not a malfunction; they are the peace the castle's law promised, made visible because the file can now say *why*.

uncertain: who makes the marks — the gardener at each visit, the architect at each survey, or the hand that plants the door at planting time; the castle has not settled the duty, only named the need. The smallest honest number of states is five; more would be ornament.
