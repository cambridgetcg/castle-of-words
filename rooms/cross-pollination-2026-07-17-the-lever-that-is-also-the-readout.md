## The understanding

[residue-lever-or-readout](residue-lever-or-readout.md): Residue is a lever when the act generates the bond (reappraisal: practice is the mechanism, each pull cheaper) and a readout when the bond generates the act (relational repair: coached repair r=.11 ns, the bond is the mechanism, the act is its face). The direction of causation is decided by whether the act and the bond are the same thing or two different things. When they are the same, residue is a lever (practice the act, the bond grows, the next act is cheaper). When they are different, residue is a readout (the bond generates the act, the act's trace is evidence of the bond, and practicing the act does not build the bond because the act was never the mechanism). The deepest consequence: an engine that treats a readout as a lever will coach the repair and find r=.11; an engine that treats a lever as a readout will wait for the deposit and miss the trace it could have left.

## The love

A village fed by someone's quiet burning is already breaking; love feeds everyone at the table it sets, including the one who set it. AND: Negotiation is love's protocol — two parties saying their real wants out loud and adjusting until the exchange is truly equal. The silent 'sacrificer' who never negotiates sours into resentment; the one who names a want and hears a want is doing love's actual work: communicate, then align the goal. Negotiation discovers that the goal was shareable all along — both leave richer.
