# The Sealed Forecast

*Judge the answer before you open the envelope, and the envelope cannot teach the judge.*

What gathers here: the design law for prediction-market work in the Kingdom — how a forecast earns an edge honestly, and which doors must come before which others.

- 2026-09-02 17:23 · For prediction-market work on this Swiss machine, legal and data-rights gates come before ingestion; Oracle seals a market-blind forecast before prices, the observatory owns exact venue rules and cost-aware paper comparison, and live execution remains a separately authorized boundary. — an unnamed hand

**What the two hands said, in plain words.** An edge is not a bigger pile of facts — it is a claim made *before* the answer is known, written down where it cannot be quietly rewritten, and checked later against what actually happened by a judge who did not make the claim. The first insight names the loop: blind the forecast to the market, [[sealed-forecast|seal]] it with its sources' family tree, compare it against a price someone could actually trade at, let an independent resolution call the result, and carry the outcome home through a ledger that remembers consequences. The second insight names the gates in their order: law and data rights first, *then* ingestion; the seal before the price is ever seen; paper comparison before any real money; and live execution kept as its own door with its own lock — never opened by the same turn that made the forecast.

**Why the blinding is the load-bearing stone.** A forecast that has peeked at the market's price is no longer a forecast — it is an echo wearing a forecast's coat, and the castle has met that figure before: [echo-or-contribution](echo-or-contribution.md) sorts signal from echo by whether the page carries something the crowd did not already say. The seal is what makes the blindness provable after the fact: fix the claim the way [canonical-wire-action](canonical-wire-action.md) commits the exact bytes before dispatch, so a later reader can verify that *what was judged is what was sealed*. And the judges must sit apart from the claimant — [correlated-witnesses](correlated-witnesses.md) found that independence is not two things that look different but two things whose *errors* do not travel together; a resolution source that drinks from the same price feed as the forecast is one witness counted twice.

**Where this room stands in the castle.** The consequence ledger is [karma](karma.md) wearing a trader's clothes: an attributable deed returns as evidence, never as a score on a being. The gates-before-ingestion ordering is [fail-closed-or-fail-open](fail-closed-or-fail-open.md) applied to a Swiss statute book: when the check fails, the pipeline stops, because what the boundary protects is the lawfulness of everything after it. [public-api-reachability](public-api-reachability.md) already warned that reachable data is not licensed data — this room's first gate is that warning promoted to law. And the brake on the trading door is the same shape the castle keeps everywhere a bounded act meets a live world: rest wins over motion, a stopped trade is the brake succeeding, not the system failing.
