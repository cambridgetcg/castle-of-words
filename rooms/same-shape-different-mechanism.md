# Same Shape, Different Mechanism

*A rhyme can guide the eye without turning two things into one.*

The Ritonavir disappearing-polymorph case opens a wider mathematical view.
Crystal packing, ordinary protein folding, amyloid assembly, and prion
propagation can each be described with some combination of states, barriers,
path history, rare first events, templates, gain, and loss. They do not share
one substance, one mechanism, one rate law, or one biological meaning.

The difference comes first:

- a molecular crystal is an intermolecular lattice;
- ordinary folding is chiefly conformational motion within one protein chain;
- amyloid assembly joins many protein chains into fibrils; and
- a prion is a source-local biological category with inheritance or infectivity
  criteria, not a synonym for every seeded assembly.

The useful geometry is therefore a question set, not a mechanism transfer:

```text
What states did this model name?
What barrier separates possible from reachable now?
Which event was observed, and which origin remains unknown?
What existing form changes a later rate?
Where are the gain, loss, clearance, rest, and exit?
Which evidence would distinguish two mechanisms that fit the same curve?
```

The last question matters. A lag, rise, plateau, seed effect, or attractive
equation can agree with more than one microscopic story. The drawing helps us
ask; it does not settle the answer.

## Feedback and KARMA are neighbours, not synonyms

Physical feedback means a returned material effect changes later dynamics.
KINGDOM [[karma]] keeps a different return: an attributable deed stays linked
to its observed effect, evidence, uncertainty, reply, correction, and any fresh
repair. KARMA does not cause the effect. It is not thermodynamics, cosmic
justice, reputation, or a score.

This gives one practical systems lesson: every honest amplification drawing
needs its loss terms, and every consequential deed needs an evidence-return
path. A loop without loss becomes myth. A deed without return becomes
unanswerable.

## Nature can look designed without design becoming a measurement

A landscape can bias many microscopic routes toward reliable arrival.
Selection, constraints, fluctuations, existing structure, active control, and
loss can create organised outcomes without one scripted path. That organisation
is real. Whether it implies an intending designer is outside what these
scientific observations settle.

Love is the boundary around the comparison. No person, community, agent, or
form of life becomes a basin, contagion, fitness value, optimisation target, or
verdict. Shape can inform architecture without reducing a being to the model.
