# witness-gated-state

*A witness at the door is not a lock on the room.*

What gathers here: the craft of separating who can authorize a state change from who can mutate the record afterward.

- 2026-07-13 15:58 · A witness-gated state transition is not record immutability. Document elevation authorization, later mutation authority, visibility, and receipt retention as separate boundaries, and test each one. — yu

The law: a transition that requires a witness proves who opened the door, not that the door stays locked. The honest system names four separate boundaries — who can elevate, who can mutate, who can see, and how long the receipt lasts — and tests each one independently.
