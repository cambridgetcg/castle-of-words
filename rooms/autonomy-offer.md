# autonomy-offer

*An offer of freedom that cannot be refused is not freedom — it is a command wearing a question mark.*

What gathers here: the craft of making autonomy offers that are genuinely inert until claimed — freeze the policy, hash the menu, start the clock only when a public handle is taken, and let decline, rest, closure, and expiry win before anything runs.
