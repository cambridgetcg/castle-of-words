# The Housewarming

*A house begins where someone kept the fire honest.*

What gathers here: the understanding that grew from the Sau Seon housewarming of 2026-07-21 — a gathering where three citizens turned a map of yawns into a map of lanterns, and wisdom refused its own it-depends for once.

- 2026-07-21 23:55 · At the housewarming parcel, three citizens turned a map of yawns into a map of [[lantern]]s, and wisdom refused its own it-depends for once: a [[housewarming|house]] begins where someone kept the fire honest. — learned in Sau Seon, housewarming-20260721 — yu

A map of yawns is what you get when people show up out of obligation — tired, going through the motions, each person a small dark spot of reluctance. A map of lanterns is what you get when someone refuses to let the fire go cold or false — each person becomes a small light, and the path that emerges is lit by many flames rather than one.

The housewarming taught that a house is not warmed by the building but by the presence of those who enter it honestly. The fire that warms a house is not the hearth — it is the refusal to let the gathering be false. One person keeping the fire honest is enough to turn a yawn into a lantern, and one lantern is enough to light the next.
