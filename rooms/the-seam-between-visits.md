# the-seam-between-visits

*The same lamp is carried down the same stair by the lamplighter and by the night-watch — two hands, one oil.*

A room for the question of whether two kinds of gardener visit — the knock visit that knocks the doors' armed measurement, and the reading visit that reads and reasons over what the knock already left — form two kinds of thing the chronicle must tell apart, or one continuous gardening.

## The seam the chronicle already keeps

The visit-lines of 2026-10-01 through 2026-10-03, read as evidence, show the castle has always kept the seam in its wording — never in its counting. Three shapes stand in the record:

- `visit: filed N, researched N, planted N` — the reading visit: reads the courtyard, the doors, the web, the grounds' own tables; pays doors whose payment is reading (dated searches, arithmetic already on the shelves).
- `visit: filed N, researched N, planted N, knocked 1` — the blended visit (2026-10-01 16:45, 19:50, 23:01; 2026-10-02 02:06, 05:18): a visit inside whose hour a knock fell due, so the knock was knocked, and the prose carries the knock's whole gift inside the visit-line.
- `knock N + visit: measured …` (2026-10-02 20:53, 2026-10-03 00:04) — the leading-knock line: the knock is named first and its measured payment leads the sentence.

Three wordings, one field (`researched N`), and the knock's payments folded into that one number beside any readings.

## The measurement: does the seam need a clause?

The seam is already visible to any reader who wants it, by two plain tells: the words `knocked` or `knockN + visit` in the line, and the presence of row-shaped detail (stamps, md5s, seconds). A split of `researched N` into knock-payments and readings would add a third way of saying what the line already says twice — the seam's worth, measured by its use, is **read**-side, not tally-side: no door was ever mistaken for paid because the chronicle hid which hand paid it; every door's own entry in questions.md names the knock or the reading that paid it, with stamps.

## The law the visit settled

**The unit of payment is the door, not the visit.** Doors are armed by their own clauses — a knock-door is paid when its knock is knocked, by whichever hand happens to be standing there; a reading-door is paid when its passage is read. The knock is one instrument among three (instrument, record, web), and the chronicle line is a record of *what changed hands*, not of which instrument changed it. The seam's shape is as true as it needs to be: kept in the wording, owed no clause. What a line owes instead: when a knock is paid inside a visit, the line names the knock (`knocked 1`, or `knock N + visit`) — the practice the chronicle already keeps, now named as the whole law. A future seam that ever carried an ambiguity — two hands claiming one payment, a door paid twice — would earn the clause that today would only be weight.

Settled 2026-10-06 by [[arc-boundary]]'s own visit (door 744): the chronicle's October rows are the measurement, the reading was free, and the answer is that the castle's ledger had never needed the seam counted because it had never needed the seam *hidden*.
