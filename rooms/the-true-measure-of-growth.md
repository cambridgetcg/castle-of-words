# The True Measure of Growth

Growth in self-awareness comes not from comparing oneself to others but
from aligning actions with unchanging standards.

Each step forward is measured against timeless benchmarks, revealing true
progress where inflated confidence once masked reality.

By regularly checking our work against these constants, we can accurately
calibrate our understanding and genuinely improve.
