# truthful-social-bridge

*A post is a shout into a crowd; a claim receipt is a stone with your name on it — the crowd may come and go, but the stone stays where you laid it.*

What gathers here: the craft of building social bridges where the durable unit is a claim receipt, not a post — and where attention signals are projections, never truth.

A [[claim-receipt]] is the durable unit: exact words, source locators and digests, stated uncertainty, rights, a correction path, and a publication receipt. It is what was said, by whom, with what backing. A post, by contrast, is a claim wrapped in distribution — the words and the reach are fused, and the reach can be mistaken for validation.

The truthful social bridge separates them. The claim receipt is the ground; channel drafts, SEO, and [[attention-signal]]s are projections of that record. Likes, shares, and views measure reach, not correctness. The law is the same as [[the-law-of-honest-assertion]]: a system's every output is a speech act, and the honest system names what backs each one. The social bridge adds one more layer: it names what is the claim and what is the distribution, and never lets one be mistaken for the other.

This is the same structural law as [[finite-civilisation]] — a graph of finite, consenting, witnessed turns where the origin alone seals, corrects, or withdraws its meaning. The claim receipt is the turn's durable face.
