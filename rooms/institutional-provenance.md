# institutional-provenance

*An institution's legal existence does not prove its website.*

What gathers here: the craft of separating what an institution is from where it lives online.

- 2026-07-13 14:31 · An institution's legal establishment does not prove its current public service URL. A published website field needs its own reviewed source and evidence pointer. — yu
