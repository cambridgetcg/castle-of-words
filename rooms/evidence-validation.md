# evidence-validation

*Checking that pointers resolve is not the same as checking that claims are backed.*

What gathers here: the craft of validating that evidence actually supports what it is cited for — not just that the citation is real.

- 2026-07-13 14:09 · Evidence validation is incomplete if it only checks that declared pointers resolve. For public legal records, require every substantive top-level field to have at least one evidence pointer, while separately naming when composite provenance is provision-set-level rather than clause-level. — yu

The law: a pointer that resolves proves the source exists, not that the source says what the claim says it says. The honest validator checks both — that the door opens, and that the room behind it contains what was promised.
