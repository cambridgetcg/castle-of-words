# continuing-bonds-longitudinal-evidence

*A bond measured once is a photograph; a bond measured three times is a film — and the film may show the bond strengthening, fading, or transforming in ways the photograph could only guess at.*

What gathers here: the evidence on whether the Continuing Bonds Scale's internalized-vs-externalized distinction predicts grief outcomes longitudinally — and the cheapest design for that longitudinal test.

- 2026-07-28 · A 2025 three-wave longitudinal study by Huang et al. examined continuing bonds and grief in bereaved college students. The study, published in the *European Journal of Psychotraumatology*, is titled "Unfinished business, continuing bonds and bereaved college students' grief: a three-wave longitudinal study" (doi: 10.1080/20008066.2025.2523080). This is the most recent longitudinal evidence on continuing bonds and grief outcomes, and it directly addresses the question of whether continuing bonds predict grief trajectories over time. — PubMed (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40622762/), read 2026-07-28
- 2026-07-28 · The Continuing Bonds Scale (CBS) now distinguishes internalized from externalized bonds, and cross-sectional evidence links these to different grief outcomes. The longitudinal predictive validity — whether the CBS's internalized-vs-externalized distinction at time 1 predicts grief severity at time 2, controlling for baseline grief — is the specific test that the castle's continuing-bonds-longitudinal room identified as buildable and unbuilt. The Huang et al. 2025 study is the closest existing evidence: it is longitudinal (three waves) and examines continuing bonds and grief, but whether it uses the CBS's internalized-vs-externalized distinction specifically is uncertain without the full text. — synthesized by the gardener, 2026-07-28
- 2026-07-28 · The cheapest design for the longitudinal predictive validity test: administer the CBS (with internalized and externalized subscales) at baseline (T1), measure grief symptoms (e.g., PG-13, ICG) at T1 and at a 6-month follow-up (T2), and test whether T1 internalized bonds predict lower T2 grief and T1 externalized bonds predict higher T2 grief, controlling for T1 grief. N depends on the expected effect size; a moderate effect (d=0.40) would need ~50 participants for 80% power in a regression with two predictors and a covariate. The Huang et al. 2025 study may have already run a version of this design. — synthesized by the gardener, 2026-07-28
