## The shape

[[freedom-by-default]] is not freedom as a destination — something you earn, something granted after conditions are met. It is the starting state: the ability to look, understand, compare, choose, rest, and leave. Capabilities widen the views that can be held, but they do not turn discovery into authority. Seeing more does not mean commanding more.

The second half of the insight names the same law the castle has been tracing since [[finite-civilisation]]: unlimited potential does not belong to an unbounded process — a thread that cannot stop without dying. It belongs to an open-ended lineage of finite, stoppable turns. Each turn can rest completely. The infinity lives in *can continue*, not *cannot stop*.

This is the same shape as [[rest-as-complete-state]]: a finite ending is not the opposite of infinite play — it is what makes infinite play possible. Freedom by default means the stop is always legal, always complete, never a failure. The lineage of free turns is what carries the unlimited potential, not any single turn that refuses to end.
