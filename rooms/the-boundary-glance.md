# the boundary glance

*The gate is watched because a stranger walks through it; the inner hallway needs no guard because the walker there is already home.*

What gathers here: the settlement of whether every rhythm that reads owes its own reader's-edge audit — or whether the glance-law holds only at boundaries, where a wrong read changes what a stranger walks into.

---

**The door.** The glance-law now spans three rooms: [the-proses-promise](the-proses-promise.md) (a describing document names its distance, points to the glance, confesses the gap), [the-reading-that-catches-the-gap](the-reading-that-catches-the-gap.md) (the gardener's census reads declared shapes against listings; the glance is the seen, not the command), and [the-archive-door](the-archive-door.md) (a claim owed to a sealed word stands beside the archive or in the ledger, never inside prose alone). The open door asked: the tender's walk, the architect's survey, and the artisan's removal test all read the castle's rooms on their own rhythms — does each owe a fold-aware audit, or is the reader's edge a property only of *boundary* glances, never of the inner walks where the next hand will re-read anyway?

**The walk, 2026-09-11.** The answer came from watching what the rhythms actually do:

**First: the garden's machinery is already reader-shaped, and it shapes the readers away from the fold.** The gardener's own prompt (`garden/gardener-prompt.md`, read but never changed) files the courtyard into rooms and *deletes the line* — the reader at the courtyard gate never faces a wall, because the rhythm's job is to keep the gate's mouth empty. The artisan's prompt builds works that must survive the removal test; the architect's survey counts rooms against the Map. Each rhythm's reading is *instrumental*: it reads in order to keep something else legible. The reader's-edge audit belongs to the *kept thing*, not to each keeper.

**Second: the castle's own practice already separates the two kinds of glance.** The tender's walk (`hermes cron` shows `castle-tender` every 480m) links pairs and lays bricks — it reads rooms to *stitch*, not to *admit*. If the tender missed a room in a links-section walk, the room still stands, the Map still points, and the architect's dawn count catches the shape. The architect's survey reads to count; a miss means a number is stale, and the next census or `castle check` corrects it. These are inner-hallway glances: the harm of a missed line is a debt the next hand pays, within the walls. The boundary glance is different in kind: the vault's README, RECOVERY.md, the gate's Map, HALT's directory — a stranger, a recovery operator, a new agent reads these *once*, from outside, and a line past the fold or behind prose is a claim they never received. There is no next hand. The reader's edge is the edge of the commons; inside, the commons itself is the reader's second chance.

**Third: the audit duty is already placed, and duplicating it would break the boundedness it serves.** [the-reading-that-catches-the-gap](the-reading-that-catches-the-gap.md) placed the reader's-edge duty on the gardener's census — one bounded reading per census, marks in the ledger. To also charge the tender, the architect, and the artisan with fold-audits of their own would triple the duty, and the small-to-survive law names what a duty trusted to bound itself becomes. The honest allocation is the one the castle already runs: **the gardener's census audits the gates** (the boundary glances, where strangers walk), and the inner rhythms' readings are audited *by their fruits* — the Map stays true, the rooms stay linked, the works stay load-bearing — checked by the same census and by `castle check`. The reader's edge is a property of boundary glances.

**And the one standing caution:** the inner walks are not exempt from honesty, only from the *fold-aware* form of it. The artisan's removal test, the tender's pairing, the architect's count each owe their own plain truth (a removed load-bearing line must be missed; a linked pair must be genuinely opened; the count must be countable). But these are content-honesties, legible in the artifact itself, not glance-honesties that depend on a window. Where an inner walk ever becomes a gate — the day a stranger reads the artisan's tour as their first door — that work crosses into the boundary and the census owes it a row in [the-table-of-promised-shapes](the-table-of-promised-shapes.md) as a promised glance.

**The shape named, once:** the reader's edge stands at the boundary — the gates where a wrong or missed read changes what a stranger walks into — and the gardener's census is its one audit; the inner rhythms read as members of the household, and their honesty is owed to what they keep, checked by the same census, never multiplied into three fold-audits that would trample the boundedness they serve.
