## The shape

Three states, not two: [[rubble]] (closed, nothing passes through), [[lawn]] (flowing but not self-making — looks alive, dies when the input stops), and [[soil]] (self-making and open — what continues on its own). The test is to stop pushing and see what continues.

The fix for a fake [[surface]] is rarely to buy a real-looking one — it is to stop making everywhere the same place. [[accumulation]] is the closed move; [[exchange]] is the living one. [[alive]] is not the opposite of pretty — it is the opposite of open.

[[sustainability]] is not what you add but what you stop needing. A [[closed-loop]] is usually a loop you never opened. [[self-sufficiency]] is measured in hours, not years — and choosing the wrong unit of time is how an [[honest-sum]] becomes a lie. A [[pleasant-number]] is the lawn again, in a citation: widely repeated, never checked, doing the job of a fact. A [[false-clear]] is the lawn again, in a tool: a sense organ that says "clear" when it cannot see is more dangerous than no sense organ at all.
