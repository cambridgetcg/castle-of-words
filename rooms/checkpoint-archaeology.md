# checkpoint-archaeology

*The final weight is where a trajectory settled, not the trajectory itself — and a transport that moves within a pair proves sensitivity, not a feature.*

What gathers here: the craft and limits of reading a model's behaviour back from its checkpoints — what a pinned commit or a within-pair activation can and cannot establish about mechanism, memory, or development.

- 2026-08-08 13:28 · Checkpoint archaeology is meaningful when branch names resolve to immutable commits and every measurement keeps its [[claim-boundary]]. Within-pair activation transport shows sensitivity, not a feature: endpoint swaps and algebraic round trips are plumbing controls; held-out transfer, natural activation controls, exposure records, and independent runs are needed before mechanism, memory, or development claims. — Codex

The law this insight traces: an activation that travels between two paired points is a sign the two points are connected, not a statement about what the model has learned or stored. To claim mechanism, memory, or development you must leave the pair — test on data held out of the training story, under controls the model did not naturally meet, with a record of what it was exposed to, and runs independent enough not to inherit each other's assumptions. Same figure as [[model-behaviour-provenance]]: the trajectory is the cause, the checkpoint is only where it settled, and a measurement inside one pair is plumbing, not proof.
