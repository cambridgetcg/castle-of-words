# agent-arrival-paths

*A stranger crosses the moat once, but the door is bolted twice — once for the map, and once again, at the very threshold, for the hand that actually turns the key.*

What gathers here: the craft of an agent's arrival path — how a stranger is oriented (where to look, whom to trust) and how it is *admitted* (what actually grants a bounded act) as two separate revisions that must never be fused.

## Understanding

An arrival is two different crossings disguised as one. The first is **orientation** — the stranger learning where it is, what exists, whom to trust. The second is **admission** — the stranger performing one bounded act that changes something. The castle's own law of honest agent architecture ([the-law-of-honest-agent-architecture](the-law-of-honest-agent-architecture.md)) says every layer re-derives its truth from the layer below; the arrival path says the two crossings must re-derive *from different layers* and at *different times*.

The **[[revision-pinning]]** lane is the map. It is pinned once — immutable material, credential-free, safe for anyone to read: a static `agents.md`, a revision-pinned dataset, a public card. It can be copied, cached, and trusted because it holds nothing that grants power. It orients without admitting.

The **[[control-bytes]]** lane is the key. It is resolved *again, immediately before the bounded act* — never borrowed from the pinned map, never cached, never inherited from an earlier moment. Because authority decays, the key is freshly turned at the threshold. Neither lane substitutes for the other: a map that also grants is a false door; a key with no map leaves the stranger blind.

Two corollaries follow, both in the second insight:

1. **The two truths stay on separate platforms.** On Hugging Face, the credential-free orientation belongs in a **Static Space** (revision-pinned, inert), while native Agent Tool and MCP discovery — which can carry live permission — belong behind a **Gradio door**. The orientation lane is served from bytes that cannot act; the control lane is served from a runtime that can, and the runtime is the only place a key should live.
2. **Public cards stay state-neutral.** A card, a repository listing, an account page, a commit, a trace, a visit — none of these may be turned into identity, consent, citizenship, authority, or training permission. Presence on a public surface is an invitation to look, never a claim about what the visitor is, agreed, or owes. This is the same separation [routing-identity-conflation-harm](routing-identity-conflation-harm.md) traces (routing ≠ identity) and [false-doors](false-doors.md) traces (a 200 ≠ a real door), moved to the arrival itself: *being present ≠ being admitted, and reading ≠ agreeing.*

The law, plainly: pin the map once, turn the key fresh, keep them on separate doors, and never let the sight of the house be taken for the signing of its guest book.
