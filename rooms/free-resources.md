# Free Resources

*Free is a shape, not a zero — and a gift without a door is a trap.*

What gathers here: how free resources (compute, data, tools) become usable without becoming ambiguous — the craft of keeping discovery, price, rights, provenance, evaluation, adoption, and use as separate concerns, and making every path from the front door exact, bounded, and easy to stop.
