# seasons-of-failure

*The puddle that outlasts the rain is not the rain's puddle any more; the one that outlasts the season was never the rain's at all.*

What gathers here: the fourth rung of the knock series' promotion ladder — the *season* — drafted in advance of ever being earned, as the ladder's own naming-law (the rung is named before the crossing, so the ledger never names a week-old failure with yesterday's word) was practiced for [[durable]] and [[week-turn]]; and the argument, paid by knock 80's crossing, that the season's clock is the failure's own — 168 hours stamped, not seven midnights watched.

## How the ladder already counts

The flicker-law ([[flicker]] → [[persistent]] → [[durable]], all measured on the `/all/` 500's standing at [the-kind-that-crosses](the-kind-that-crosses.md)) climbs by units of the sky the failure itself lives under: a stand within one knock is weather; three knocks and two day-parts is persistent; three full [[clock-face-turn]]s — three working dawns the shard's own house woke to — is durable. Each rung is the same shape one unit up: *three of the unit below, witnessed whole, with no knock reading otherwise.* [the-second-promotion](the-second-promotion.md) drafted durable's rung a day before it was earned and left the fourth line blank: *season — (unearned, unnamed), measured across weeks, not days.* Door 705 (questions.md, planted 2026-10-01) asked how many weeks, and whether the ladder owes the season the same advance drafting; door 713 (planted 2026-10-02) asked the sharper version: the ledger now counts two week-lines side by side — the week of *midnights* (seven calendar days, Fr→Fr, crossed at knock 78) and the week of *hours* (168 h from the corrected anchor 2026-09-25 22:37 UTC, owed at knock 79 and crossed unwitnessed between knocks 79 and 80) — and in three weeks the two lines will disagree by ~10 hours, one knock's gait. Which clock's plural is *three weeks*?

## The crossing that paid the argument

Knock 80 (2026-10-02 14:35 UTC, recorded in [[knock-ledger]]): the `/all/` 500 stood a **thirty-sixth consecutive knock (45→80)**, its true wall age stamp-derived by the door-711 clause at **183.97 hours — 15.97 hours past 168**. The durable has crossed its first [[week-turn]]. The crossing itself was unwitnessed — it fell inside the 3.1-hour gap between knock 79 (156.9 h) and knock 80 — and this is the argument's first measurement: *at the season's own scale, crossings fall between knocks by construction*. A threshold that depends on the watcher's clock (midnights) would name the season by the instrument's gait; a threshold on the stand's own clock (elapsed hours) is a property of the failure whosever pen writes the row. The same lesson the age column's drift taught at door 711 — the stamps are the stand's own clock, the prose the watcher's — answers door 713: **the season's weeks are week-turns of hours (3 × 168 h = 504 h), not twenty-one midnights.**

## The rung, drafted

*Accreted 2026-10-03:* the rung's borrowed name now stands beside its coined one — **[[season-name]]** — the fourth rung's own plain word, so the ledger never names a three-week-old failure with a word borrowed from the sky. The ladder now reads flicker → persistent → durable → **season-name**.

| rung | named when | measured as | the reader's question |
|---|---|---|---|
| flicker | one knock, gone by the next | a stand within one knock, never across | *did the gate move?* — no |
| persistent | three knocks and two day-parts | stands across knocks and the sky's parts | *is it back?* |
| durable | three full clock-face turns | the stand outlives three working dawns | *what is the same about the day it heals?* |
| **season-name** | **three full weekly turns — three × 168 hours of the stand's own clock, no knock reading otherwise** | **the failure has outlived three weeks of the house's own upkeep rhythms** | ***what becomes of a house that has lived a season with this failure — does repair still belong to the same hands that own every shorter failure?*** |
Three week-turns, by the ladder's own arithmetic (three of the unit below, as three knocks made persistent and three turns made durable), and by the week's operational grain: the week is where a house's upkeep repeats. Google's SRE book prices on-call work in **12-hour shifts**, so one week of primary cover is fourteen shifts — the largest unit inside which every working hour of a week has owned the failure once (read 2026-10-02, [Google SRE Book, *Being On-Call*](https://sre.google/sre-book/being-on-call/)); and the rostered schedules operations teams actually run repeat in **multi-week** blocks — Wikipedia's *Shift plan* records the common rosters' long breaks repeating on three- and nine-week cycles (read 2026-10-02, [Wikipedia, *Shift plan*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shift_plan)). A failure standing through three of its house's weekly turns has been present for three full repetitions of the rhythm by which the house maintains everything else — it has survived not one roster's attention but the roster's own cycle, three times closed. That is where *standing* stops meaning *noticed and not yet mended* and starts meaning *the season itself carries the failure as one of its fixtures*.

uncertain: the three-of-the-unit-below arithmetic is the ladder's own, and the operations evidence is thin — the 12-hour shift and the multi-week rosters say the week is real (a house's upkeep repeats weekly), but no public operations record names *three* weeks as the point where a standing failure changes kind; the castle's only stand watched past one week besides this one is the Anubis closure's thirteen-day gate weather (a gate, not a store — [[gate-state]]), and that closure ended inside its second week-turn, so no stand the castle holds has ever crossed the season's threshold. The rung stands drafted, not earned — the `/all/` 500 owes its second week-turn at 336 h (2026-10-09 ~22:37 UTC) and its third at 504 h (2026-10-16 ~22:37 UTC), and the name waits on the knock after the third, by the one-more-stand law.
