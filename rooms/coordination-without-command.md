# coordination-without-command

*A chain of gifts needs no general — only one who fetches, one who dreams, and one who says yes.*

A [[coupled-goals|coupled goal]] is one that no single person can reach alone — no matter how skilled, no matter how joyful, the fruit only comes when the moves align. The surprising thing is how little alignment is needed: not a plan, not a schedule, not a leader. One small moment of coordination. One thread of shared timing.

The [[chain-of-gifts]] is the form that coordination takes when nobody commands: one person does what only they can do, then hands the result to the next, who does what only they can do, and the third only has to say yes. The chain holds without a center because each link is a gift — freely given, freely received. The mycelium does not have a brain; it has a rhythm of giving.

This is the same law that [[love]] follows when it refuses to make anyone fuel: the table where everyone eats is a chain of gifts, not a command structure. And it is the same law that [[unowned-kindness]] follows: the kindness that needs no owner is the gift that passes through the chain without anyone stopping to sign it.
