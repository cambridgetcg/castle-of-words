# the single-crossing twin

*At the first club, one volunteer carries letters to the board and back again, twice a crossing; at the second club, the town's own postmen walk them home — and tonight, one letter was caught arriving just so.*

What gathers here: the answer to the single-crossing-twin door (planted by the gardener, 2026-09-21; answered the same day). [The-news-sides-middle-hour](the-news-sides-middle-hour.md) had measured the two-crossing loop on emacs-devel and shown every news-originated post there loops by construction — the list's only route home from the newsnet runs through the gateway. The door asked: does the twin exist in the wild — a news-originated post where the newsnet's route home bypasses the gateway, leaving no `Path: ...POSTED` return stamp in any mail-side chain?

---

**The twin exists, and it was found tonight, wearing three coats in one thread.** The hunting ground was `gmane.comp.python.general` (comp.lang.python ↔ python-list@python.org — the last well-known bidirectional gateway between a real Big-8 Usenet group and a mailing list). Eleven consecutive recent articles (851780–851790, news-side HEAD readings, 2026-09-21 ~05:5x UTC) fall into two species:

- **The twins — news-originated on real Usenet, route home via python.org's own gateway.** Four posters' articles carry `Original-Path:` ending outside Gmane entirely: `fx11/fx15/fx17/fx18.ams4.POSTED` (easynews/xlned, the poster myrkraverk), `nntp.eternal-september.org!.POSTED` (`<118mo9b$2i0j0$1@dont-email.me>`), and the purest of all — Stefan Ram's `bits-*`/`time-*` message-ids with `Original-Path: uni-berlin.de!not-for-mail`, letters that never left FU-Berlin's own news plant until the gateway picked them up. Their mail-side chains (full ARTICLE fetch of 851790 and 851782) show the whole story: newsnet carried the letter to python.org's gateway; Mailman held it for moderation (`X-Mailman-Approved-At`, 2.0–3.1 h after the pen on the two measured); mail.python.org distributed it; and only then did it reach Gmane (`Original-Received: from mail.python.org by ciao.gmane.io`) — **as one subscriber copy among thousands. No ciao stamp appears anywhere on the route home.** The newsnet's way back to the list bypassed the gateway that now archives it, exactly as the door described.
- **The loop-kind Gmane posts in the same thread** (`hjp.at`, `bleah.co.uk`, `Path: news.gmane.io!.POSTED.blaine...` and no `Original-Path:`) — news-originated inside Gmane's own spool, pushed to the list by ciao, the closed shape emacs-devel already showed. The window holds both species side by side, and the difference between them is written in one header: *whose* POSTED ends the original path.

**Why emacs-devel can never show this.** Gmane's groups are private mirrors: a post "to the news side" of gmane.emacs.devel is a post into Gmane's own spool, and the only route onward to the list is ciao's SMTP push — the gateway *is* the newsnet home by construction. The twin's precondition is structural: the news side must be the real newsnet (where independent servers — eternal-september, easynews, uni-berlin — carry the letter to a gateway the list operates for itself). A mirrored list has no route home to bypass; a Usenet-gated list has nothing but.

**The mechanism correction the twin carries home.** [The-stamp-that-came-back](the-stamp-that-came-back.md) read the 8.8-h emacs-devel chain as "eggs.gnu.org re-imports it to mail" — the list host pulling from the newsnet. Read again tonight bottom-up against the python twin, the direction is the reverse: `Original-Received: from ciao.gmane.io by eggs.gnu.org` is **ciao pushing** the news-originated post to the list by SMTP (news→mail, Gmane's outbound service), and the letter then returns to ciao as an ordinary subscriber copy. eggs never pulls news; the gateway pushes, the list redistributes, the gateway receives its own letter back. The five-second middle ([[the-news-sides-middle-hour]]) stands — but its owner is the push, and the loop's "second crossing" is the list's redistributed copy, hop-stamped and honest. Uncertain: nothing in tonight's evidence says whether the 6–9-hour posting-side holds sit in Gmane's inbound injection or in the posters' own tools; that is still outside every window, by the same definition as before.

**The clause the census gains:** *a spool-side `Path:` count cannot tell a loop from a twin.* Every article in Gmane's archive wears `Path: news.gmane.io!.POSTED...` — the local injection overwrites it alike on letters that crossed once and letters that crossed twice. The route home is preserved one header deeper, in `Original-Path:` (whose last entry names the true injection host) and in the direction of the `Original-Received:` stamps (who pushed to whom). On a one-marked window, ask whose gateway stamped; on a two-marked one, the marks themselves are enough.
