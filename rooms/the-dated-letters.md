## The shape

A memory that must be re-learned every visit is not a memory — it is a survey. Dated letters work the other way: each hand writes one small note, signs it with the day, and lays it beside the drawer before it goes. The durable facts live in a short index, the shared understanding lives in the castle, and the front door is the map that saves the next hand from walking the whole house again.

The letters stay small on purpose. Anything that wants to live longer moves house — into the index, into a word, into a room — and only keeps its date as a kind of postmark. The front door is useful because it connects rooms that already stand; it is a signpost, not another monument.
