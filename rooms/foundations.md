## Understanding

A [[foundation]] is the set of commitments a project chooses to stand on. It is not the code, not the evidence, not the witnesses — those are separate layers. When the layers blur — when version agreement is mistaken for byte integrity, when a project's name on an endpoint is mistaken for deepening — the foundation quietly becomes a runtime authority instead of a chosen ground.

A [[grounding]] is the companion receipt: here is why we stand here, and here is where the why runs out. It pins exact arguments and their limits separately from the commitments they explain. A grounding that pretends to be law has stopped being a grounding.

The honest foundation keeps commitments chosen (never inherited), ground challengeable (anyone can ask "why this?"), and [[karma]] as the return path from deed to evidence — never a score, never a verdict on a being.
