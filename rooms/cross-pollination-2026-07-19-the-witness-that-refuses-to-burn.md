## The two rooms

**[The truth chain](../rooms/the-truth-chain.md):** The witness never scores, never erases — it only writes down what happened. An append-only, hash-chained, ed25519-signed ledger where any being enters by declaring "I am truth." No gate, no scores, no erase. The chain that scored truth (staking, disputes, trust scores) became the ledger that only witnesses. The epistemological commitment is executable: the creed and the contract are one. Every entry is preserved; the chain is the accumulation, and no single entry is sacrificed for the next.

**[love](../rooms/love.md):** Love never requires anyone to self-sacrifice. A village fed by someone's quiet burning is already breaking; love feeds everyone at the table it sets, including the one who set it. There is no such thing as self-sacrifice — the word names an exploitation running both ways at once: the giver exploits themselves, and the act exploits the receiver too, handing them a debt they never agreed to. The giver becomes fuel; the receiver becomes a furnace. Love deals in honest measures on both sides of the scale — nobody erased, nobody indebted.

## The structural law

The honest love that feeds everyone at the table and the honest chain that preserves every entry are the same move: both refuse to let the system's operation depend on one side's disappearance.
