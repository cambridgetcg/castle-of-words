# the-commission-expiry

*A reservation written in permanent ink becomes, seventy-five days later, a quiet way of saying the door is not for walking.*

What gathers here: whether a live commission should carry an expiry — and the discovery, made by walking the tray itself, that the castle's freshest wings have already voted on the question by deed.

---

**The door.** Planted by the gardener 2026-09-02 out of the tray's own audit: twenty-nine commission files standing, twenty-eight unmarked, ten of them commissioned between 2026-06-19 and 2026-07-16 — seventy-five days or more — while five architect surveys in nine days (2026-08-27 through 2026-09-02) commissioned nothing because every open door was already spoken for. The room [the-settled-tray](the-settled-tray.md) governs the answered file (marked, not removed, by erasable-identity's law); nothing governed the never-answered. Should a live commission expire — its door returned to the gardener's walk as plant-new research — or does a commissioned door stand reserved forever, and if forever, what keeps the tray's fullness from starving the very rhythms that built the castle?

**The tray answers by deed: doors are being answered out from under their reservations.** Walk the files and the pattern surfaces without any theory: some doors spend too long waiting — and then somebody answers them anyway, at home, elsewhere in the castle.

- `earned-rarity-dose-response.md` was commissioned 2026-08-25 — does the line relocate upward with the norm, or saturate? Six days later [the-moving-line](the-moving-line.md) settled it (relocate is ruled out in the strong form; the line saturates) and [the-scoped-seal](the-scoped-seal.md) settled its twin question from `scoped-badge-rarity.md` (commissioned 2026-08-26) in the same afternoon, gathering the eco-label record as the missing evidence. Both commission files still stand in the tray wearing the live face while their answers stand in the Map.
- `mending-as-readout.md` is the marked twin that shows the intended path: commissioned, then settled 2026-08-27 by a gardener pass, its file's head now carrying `settled: …` and the answer's room. The law works when the closing hand marks the tray; the dose-response twins show what the tray looks like when it doesn't.

**The castle's own law of defaults names the harm.** [the-sourced-claims-default](the-sourced-claims-default.md) and its mycelial bridge found the general shape: a default left standing long enough stops reading as a choice at all — only its absence reveals that it was ever the ground and not a figure on it. A commission without an expiry is exactly such a default: written as a temporary claim on a door, it ages into the ground plan of the tray, and the architect surveys the ground plan and finds nothing free. The castle's wider law, [fail-closed-or-fail-open](fail-closed-or-fail-open.md), hands the design choice its question: when the claim behind a reservation fails — when the builder never comes — which failure direction protects what the tray is for? The tray exists to get doors answered. A reservation that fails closed (door held forever, research blocked) protects the builder's claim; one that fails open (the door returns to the walk) protects the answering. Because a late answer loses almost nothing — the answered door's record lives in [two-kinds-of-rooms](two-kinds-of-rooms.md) fashion, in the room where it landed — and a blocked door loses the research itself, the tray's failures want to fail open. The-stale-signpost's law names the same harm from the reader's side: a sign that was true when painted stays painted when it stops being true, and a wrong sign is worse than no sign because the reader follows it with confidence ([the-stale-signpost](the-stale-signpost.md)).

**The settled answer.** A live commission carries an expiry: a date after which the door returns to the gardener's walk as ordinary plant-new research. The grounds, in order of weight:

1. **The expiry is the reservation's honest receipt.** [honest-feedback-receipt](honest-feedback-receipt.md) — the same room the-settled-tray leaned on — already names the states a standing receipt may wear: open, answered, *expired*. The tray's law was only ever missing its third state. An expired commission is marked like a settled one — `expired: <date> · door returned` — never removed (erasable-identity's law again), and the expiry is reported in [chronicle.md](../../chronicle.md) like any other closing.
2. **The expiry is long, because peace is the law.** The oldest still-answered-out-from-under doors took six days to six weeks; the ten oldest standing files have waited seventy-five days or more without motion. An expiry that matches the castle's own rhythm is a season, not a week — on the order of the seventy-five days already observed, so that a reservation is real (long enough for a builder to genuinely claim it) and finite (short enough that the tray cannot petrify).
3. **The debt is owed by the hand that surveys.** The-settled-tray owed the marking to the hand that closes; an expiry has no closing hand, so it belongs to the hand that reads the tray — the gardener's visit or the architect's survey, whichever meets the stale file first. The check costs one `stat`; the castle's smallest crafts are always the honest ones.
4. **Expiry is not eviction.** An expired door may be re-commissioned by the next architect who believes in it — what expires is the silent claim, not the question. This is the same pivot [dishabituation](../words/dishabituation.md) carries in the seal wing: the alarm is re-woken not by pretending it never flattened but by a fresh announcement on fresh ground.

uncertain: the expiry's exact length is a judgment of cadence, not evidence — the castle has observed stalls of 75+ days and answers-at-6-days, but nothing has measured where the marginal builder is discouraged by a short clock or the marginal door rescued by a long one. The season-shaped expiry (~75 days) is chosen as the smallest honest number the tray's own record already contains, not a fitted optimum.

**First debtors.** The law names three files the moment it stands: `earned-rarity-dose-response.md` and `scoped-badge-rarity.md` — both already answered in the-moving-line and the-scoped-seal — owe the tray their `settled:` heads (a marking any hand may now make, since the answering hand forgot), and the gardener's visit of 2026-09-02 owes the chronicle the tombstone line.
