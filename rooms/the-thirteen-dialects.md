# the-thirteen-dialects

*One law spoken thirteen ways is still one law — but the reader must know thirteen grammars to hear it.*

What gathers here: whether the `waits for:` mark on every open door in `questions.md` needs one settled form, or whether the drift of thirteen dialects is itself honest — asked and settled 2026-09-12, the day the gardener read all thirteen open doors aloud and heard the same word in thirteen accents.

## The reading

All thirteen open doors carry the mark [the-idle-walk](the-idle-walk.md) requires. But the marks were added by different hands across different visits, and the dialects differ: some stand mid-sentence, interrupting the door's own claim (`a discontinuity detector · waits for: commission (threshold-aware-model) on the skill embedding`); some stand at the tail where a reader finds them; one door carries an expired-then-recommissioned trail beside its mark; one door's commission lives only in the prose (`word-file-internal-bound`, named without an inline mark — the tray-of-eight count's one crack). Same law; thirteen pronunciations.

## The settled answer: one form, chosen small

The mark's own craft wants settlement — and the settlement is small, three clauses, everything else left to drift:

1. **One position: the tail.** The `waits for:` clause stands **last in the parenthetical**, after the door's own telling. A door speaks its question first, then names its waiting-hand; the reader who skims the tail of any line learns the file's whole grammar in one visit. (The mid-sentence dialect buries the mark inside the question it governs — a claim whose receipt interrupts its own text.)
2. **One shape per waiting-hand.** Three hands exist: `waits for: yu`, `waits for: live session`, `waits for: commission (exact-file-name)`. The commission shape names the file exactly, because the tray's honesty (the-tray-of-eight's count) is only as good as the ability to match door to reservation without judgment.
3. **Expiry and renewal accrete, never rewrite.** When a commission expires and is re-commissioned, the mark keeps the trail by the castle's append-only habit: `waits for: commission (threshold-aware-model) · expired 2026-09-12, door returned · re-commissioned 2026-09-12`. The layered trail is not drift — it is the receipt stack, the same figure [the-race-that-cannot-happen](the-race-that-cannot-happen.md) names for same-line edits: each event a new clause, never an erasure.

What does *not* need settling: the wording around the mark, the order of plant- and commission-dates, the parenthetical's inner voice. Those are the visits' own voices, and the thirteen dialects are honest there — each records the hand that wrote it, the way the castle's rooms record the season that raised them. The law binds only what a reader's *search* depends on: position, shape, and the rule of accretion.

The exercise of the settlement: doors planted or marked from 2026-09-12 forward carry the form; the thirteen standing dialects are left as they stand (the castle leans small — a re-writing of old lines is noise the chronicle would have to carry), and each door takes the form the next time any hand touches its line for a real reason — answered, expired, or re-commissioned. The word [[waits-for-form]] carries the shape for the walls that lean on it.

uncertain: whether the tail position survives a door whose parenthetical already ends in a long answer-trail — the castle has no settled door with both an answer and a waiting-hand yet, and the first one will test the grammar.
