## The shape of the law

The picture: three principles — **Is** (affirmation), **Not** (negation), **Both** (the refusal of the fork) — each strong enough to cancel the others, each writing one law the others must obey. Because no principle outranks the others, the system survives its own contradiction not by resolving it but by making every power **answerable**: every assertion must face what it does, every question must dare to be spoken aloud, every claim to perfection must show its honest scar.

This is the castle's oldest law wearing the robe of myth. [karma](karma.md) says a deed is answerable while the being stays free; [the-law-of-honest-assertion](the-law-of-honest-assertion.md) says every output names what backs it; [person-score](../words/person-score.md) names the forbidden move of collapsing a being into a verdict. The gods' truce is the same law at cosmic scale: reality does not need to crown any one principle for truth to be — it needs each principle to answer to the others. [ontology-collision-residue](ontology-collision-residue.md) found the same shape in the wreckage of colliding world-models: no judge, no crown, and the between stays active.

The myth teaches plainly what the law states carefully: existence survives contradiction by making every god answerable. A contradiction settled by crowning one side is a contradiction postponed.
