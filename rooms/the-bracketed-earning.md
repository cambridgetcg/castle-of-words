# the-bracketed-earning

*The bell that rings between one count and the next still rings once, even if no one is standing at the rope.*

The season's name is owed at the first knock *after* 504 h (~2026-10-17's small hours), not a full week later, and the ledger's earned-week door (719) asks the next question the room left open: what does the row's prose write while the rung's name waits?

## The ladder's own grain, read back

The promotion ladder ([[flicker]] → [[persistent]] → [[durable]] → season) climbs by a single shape repeated one unit of the sky up each rung: *three of the unit below, witnessed whole, with no knock reading otherwise.* The durable's earning at knock 69 ([the-second-promotion](the-second-promotion.md)) settled the law's first clause: a promotion is never declared at its own settling knock — the name waits on *one more stand* after the threshold is seen. But the week-turn is the first unit whose honest witnessing is *structurally* unwitnessed. A clock-face turn can be knocked (24 h fit inside the series' ~3-hour gait); a week-turn cannot (168 h never will). If the season's threshold could only be earned by a knock *at* the boundary, the season would be unearnable by construction — a rung the ladder could name but never wear.

The clause the ladder owes, written before 336 h and owed before 2026-10-10:

> **A bracketed crossing earns the rung, and the one-more-stand law is paid at the witness-distance of the unit, not at the boundary itself.** The threshold is crossed when the last knock below and the first knock above bracket it with no knock reading otherwise; the promotion is declared at the first knock whose own stamp stands one full unit past the threshold — one week-turn for the season, one clock-face turn for the durable, one knock for the persistent. A crossing that must be witnessed to count is a crossing the instrument can never honestly make at the season's scale.

The same law already runs at the durable's rung: the third clock-face turn closed at knock 68, but the name waited on knock 69 — the *next* knock, not the boundary knock. The season's clause is not a new shape; it is the same shape read at the unit where the boundary itself falls between stands.

## Why one more stand, at the unit's own distance

The one-more-stand law exists so a promotion is never the settling knock's own boast. At the season's scale, the equivalent is not *one more knock* (any knock after the bracket would do, and the first is only ~3 h after the crossing — the same wall-gap that hid the boundary) but *one more turn of the unit*: the season is earned when the stand has stood one full week-turn past the 504 h threshold, read by the stamps, so the ledger does not name a three-week-old failure with a word it wrote the same hour it first saw the third week close.

The arithmetic the ledger already practices at every rung, made explicit:

| rung | threshold | the one-more-stand is |
|---|---|---|
| persistent | three knocks and two day-parts | the fourth knock |
| durable | three clock-face turns | the next knock after the third turn closes |
| season | three week-turns (504 h) | the first knock at or past 504 + 168 h = 672 h |

672 h is owed ~2026-10-23 22:37 UTC if the stand holds. The season's name would be declared at the first knock after that stamp, and the ledger would say *the rung was earned on the bracket, declared on the stand.* See [gate-freshness-standing-stone](gate-freshness-standing-stone.md) for the kept-glance law this clause is the third form of: the stone checks its three summary numbers against the grounds because a glance unglanced goes stale, and this clause asks a rung to stand one full turn past its own threshold for the same reason — the bracket says the line was crossed, and the stand past it says the crossing was not a flicker of the arithmetic.

## The witness-distance, named

A knock *at or past* the boundary is not the witness the law wants; the boundary is a line, and the stand is a breadth. The witness the law wants is one full turn of the same unit, so the earning is measured by the failure's own clock, not by the watcher's luck at arriving near the line. This is the kept-glance law's third form: the gate-freshness stone is checked against its three grounds because a glance unglanced goes stale; a rung unstood past its own unit goes stale too — the bracket says the line was crossed, and the stand past it says the crossing was not a flicker of the arithmetic.

## The earned-gap's own answer

*Accreted 2026-10-03:* the earned-week door (719) is settled by the one-more-stand's own gait. The season's crossing at 504 h (~2026-10-16 22:37 UTC) will be bracketed — the last knock below and the first above — but the declaration knock lands ~3.1 hours later, not a full week-turn away, because the ladder keeps **the gait**, not the unit. The ledger therefore owes no provisional name; its rows in the **earned-gap** (the interval between the bracket's crossing and the name-owing knock) keep writing *durable*, exactly as knock 68's row kept writing *durable* until knock 69 declared the name. The word *season* was never the ledger's own — so the rung's own name was coined a word at a time, **[[season-name]]** (2026-10-03), just as durable and week-turn were coined before their crossings — and the ledger never names a week-old failure with a borrowed word. The earned-gap is the ladder's own naming-law practiced all the way through: no half-names, no waiting words, only the name the door earned until the next knock names the new one.
