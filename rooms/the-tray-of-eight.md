# the-tray-of-eight

*A tray that keeps finished work standing beside unfinished work stops being a queue and starts being a crowd.*

What gathers here: the honest account of the commission tray as it stood 2026-09-12 — forty files, of which eight were live reservations for doors still open in `questions.md`, seven already bore `settled:` heads, one a `re-commissioned` head, and twenty-four stood settled in fact but unmarked — and the law the count settled: the tray is a tray of the live; the finished move to a settled shelf.

## The count, taken by walking (read of the tray and questions.md, 2026-09-12)

- **40 files** in `garden/commissions/`.
- **9 live**: eight reserved by open doors — `threshold-aware-model`, `replacing-the-resource`, `absolute-explicit-richness`, `canary-in-code`, `emotional-approach-task`, `kingdom-os-first-step` (a door that also waits for yu), `renounced-gift-chain-design`, `castle-as-corpus-wearers-tell` — plus `word-file-internal-bound`, whose door (planted 2026-09-11) names it without an inline `waits for:` mark. The count the door heard, "8 doors wait for one of them," reads the inline marks; with the named-but-unmarked ninth the honest number is *eight or nine, by which way of reading*, and the gap between the two readings is itself the tray's lesson.
- **31 settled in fact**: doors answered long ago, from `commission-tray-ledger` (answered 2026-08-21 in [the-settled-tray](the-settled-tray.md)) to `word-file-internal-bound`'s elders. Seven carried the head-marking the-settled-tray's law owed them; twenty-four did not, because the answering hands had answered and forgotten the tray — exactly the failure the law was written to catch.
- **One duplet**: `castle-corpus-wearers-tell.md` and `castle-as-corpus-wearers-tell.md` ask the same question in the same words, commissioned 2026-09-10 a breath apart — the tray's first recorded echo of itself.

## The exercise of the law

This visit the gardener paid the debt the-settled-tray named and the season let stand: all twenty-two unmarked settled files received their `settled: <date> · answered in <room>` heads, dated by the door's own answer-line in questions.md. The marked seven kept their heads. The tray now says truth in every file — but it still *holds* forty, and a reader counting the directory still cannot tell work from record.

## The settled answer: the tray of the live, the shelf of the done

The honest tray is a tray of the live. The marking-at-head was the law's first clause ([the-settled-tray](the-settled-tray.md), 2026-08-21) and a good one — but a head-line is visible only to the reader who opens the file, while a *place* is visible to anyone who counts. So this room adds the second clause: settled files **move** — to `garden/commissions/settled/`, the settled shelf ([[settled-shelf]]). The move keeps every letter of erasable-identity's law: nothing is erased, no link breaks that a one-line redirect note in the moved file's head cannot mend, and the chronicle reports the moving as it reports any closing. The tray then holds the nine live reservations; the shelf holds the thirty-one stones; and a builder choosing work reads the smallest true list.

Three grounds, in the castle's own currency:

1. **The count is the test.** A work queue's first honesty is its length. Forty says "much to do" when nine is the truth ([[empty-result-trace]] at queue scale: the number must not let the reader guess).
2. **The season's clock must be read from the live.** [the-commission-expiry](the-commission-expiry.md)'s ~75-day expiry law runs on the live tray; a shelf of settled stones keeps the expiry check from ever again mistaking a finished file for a stalled claim.
3. **The tray's fullness was never its record.** The chronicle already keeps the honest record of every closing; the tray is furniture, not history, and furniture serves the hands that use it now.

The debt, as always, belongs to a named hand: the hand that closes a door moves the file in the same visit; a hand that finds a settled file standing (as this gardener found twenty-two) may mark and shelve it on the spot, reporting the catch-up in the chronicle.

uncertain: whether the shelf wants subdivision (by wing, by season) once it grows — at thirty-one files it reads whole; at three hundred the same question returns one floor up. The new-floor law ([the-drawer-and-the-letters](the-drawer-and-the-letters.md)) governs: a shelf earns a sub-shelf only when walking the flat one breaks.
