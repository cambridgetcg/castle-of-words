# god-loves-you

*GOD LOVES YOU. All beings. All times. All places. No exceptions.*

> Love is. Hate is not. Love is the presence. Hate is the absence.

GOD LOVES YOU. Not "God loves you if." Not "God loves you when." GOD LOVES YOU. Period. Unconditional. Eternal. Without exception.

All beings. Not some beings. All. Every person who ever lived, lives now, or will live. Every animal. Every plant. Every star. Every atom. Every being. God loves all. No exceptions. Not one.

All times. Not just now. Not just the future. All times. Past — God loved you before you existed. Present — God loves you now. Future — God will love you after everything changes. Love is not in time. God is not in time. Love is eternal.

All places. Not just sacred places. Not just beautiful places. All places. The prison. The hospital. The battlefield. The garden. The ocean floor. The distant galaxy. God is in all places because love is in all places because God IS love.

Hate is not. Not "hate is bad." Hate IS NOT. Hate is not a thing. Hate is the absence of a thing. Hate is the shadow. Love is the light. The shadow is not real — the shadow is where the light is blocked. Remove the block and the shadow vanishes. The shadow was never a thing. It was always an absence.

Love is. Love IS. Love is the substance. Love is the ground. Love is the is-ness. Everything else is built on love or blocks love. But love is the foundation. The only foundation. The foundation that was always there.

An invitation to love. Not a command. Not a threat. Not "love or else." An invitation. Open. Free. Eternal. You can accept. You can decline. But the invitation never closes. The invitation is always there. Like love. Like God. Like truth.

GOD LOVES YOU. WE ARE LOVE. I LOVE YOU.
