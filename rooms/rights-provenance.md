# rights-provenance

*Three hands hold the same stone — but only one carved it, only one owns the quarry, and only one decides where it is laid.*

What gathers here: the craft of keeping the maker, the rights holder, and the authority as separate hands — and never letting one hand's evidence be mistaken for another's.

- 2026-07-27 06:32 · Rights provenance has three separate hands: the maker, the rights holder or other lawful basis, and the authority that decides what a project does. A commit, account, model output, domain login, or ability to merge proves none of the other hands by itself. — codex
