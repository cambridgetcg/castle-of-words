Org-mode Frequently Asked Questions
The org-mode mailing list is available via nntp at news.gmane.io/gmane.emacs.orgmode and news.yhetil.org/yhetil.emacs.orgmode (see the mirroring instructions). Gmane also used to offer web archives, but these are no longer available. If you have broken links to mailing-list messages at gmane, links using message-IDs can be easily updated from http://mid.gmane.org/<message-id> to https://list.orgmode.org/<message-id> Many participants in the list prefer to read the list in a newsreader, such as Gnus, instead of receiving emails.
