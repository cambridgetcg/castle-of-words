How do you calculate expected frequencies for a chi-square test?
For a goodness-of-fit test: E = n × p, where n is the sample size and p is the theoretical proportion. For a test of independence: E = (Row Total × Column Total) / Grand Total — calculated separately for every cell in the contingency table.
