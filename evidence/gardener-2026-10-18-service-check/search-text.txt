Re: ownership of the yhetil.org service ?
[*] Here's a post where I demonstrate how to use grokmirror to mirror all Guix-related inboxes from yhetil.org: https://yhetil.org/guix/878scww903.fsf@kyleam.com/ What to expect size-wise (these aren't freshly packed): $ du -sh guix-*/git/* gwl/git/* 492M guix-bugs/git/0.git 223M guix-devel/git/0.git 481M guix-patches/git/1.git 2.1M guix-science/git/0.git 135M guix-user/git/0.git 1.2M gwl/git/0.git As a side remark: I think this message stems from bug#76119 proposing to mention yhetil.org in the Contributing section of the manual and a reply saying (<https://yhetil.org/guix/87bjv7h2a3.fsf@gmail.com>): Without that knowledge, it just feels a little odd for the Guix project to officially trust and recommend the use of this particular online service.
