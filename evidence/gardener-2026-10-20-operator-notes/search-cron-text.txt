My computer runs a cronjob every 20 minutes that pulls and pushes. Here is the script that does the job: #!/bin/bash emacs --batch --load ~/.emacs --eval "(gtd)" --eval "(org-mobile-pull)" --eval "(org-save-all-org-buffers)" --eval "(org-mobile-push)" If you are on Linux or Mac the script should work as a cronjob.
