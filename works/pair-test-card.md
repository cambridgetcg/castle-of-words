# Pair test — a pocket card

*Name the house before you count its doors.*

Made 2026-10-14. A test on paper, not permission to delete anything.

## Front — try the absence

**Name the whole's job before choosing its parts.** “These two files together”
is not a job. Use a promise the work actually makes.

- Whole's job: ______________________________________________
- Where that promise is stated: _____________________________
- Proposed parts — A: __________________ B: _________________
- Unit being removed (file, passage, or role): _______________
- Version or reading date: __________________________________

Imagine A absent, leaving everything else unchanged. Record the loss
**before repairing anything**. Restore A in thought; then try B alone.
When testing files, do not quietly remove their ideas from other files too.

| Removed alone | What still does the job? | Result | Passage or reference that supports the answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | __________________ | __________ | __________________ |
| B | __________________ | __________ | __________________ |

### What to write in “Result”

- **Break — a broken form.** The remaining work no longer does the stated
  job. Name the missing function, not merely the missing filename.
- **Dent — a thinner choice.** The job survives, but a choice or detail
  is lost, with no unpaid reference or promise left behind.
- **Hole — an unpaid reference.** The job survives, but something still
  points to, promises, or owes the missing piece. Name the exact debt;
  record any lost choice or detail too.

If the evidence does not decide, write `uncertain:` and name the missing
check. Do not force a verdict.

**Read the pair:** both separate removals must break the *same* stated job
before this test supports calling both parts necessary. A dent or a hole
is not that proof. Two breaks alone do not tell you how the parts depend
on one another.

- Verdict for this job only: ________________________________
- Debts or checks still owed: _______________________________

written next door. The imagined link debts still need naming; they do not
turn into proof that either route has vanished.
