**Two passes are not a ranking.** They can show more than one way to do this
job; they do not show which is better or whether either was chosen deliberately.
Without a record of changed understanding, do not claim a newly noticed default.

Missing evidence is not a failure result. If you change the job or pass rule,
keep the old result and name the new comparison. Stop at the evidence you have;
this slip grants no permission to start a test or an endless watch.

## Back — one imaginary hall

**Everything about this scene is invented:** the hall, keeper, desks, origins
and moment of recognition. The two notes below were made for this illustration.
No visitor used them; no real discovery or reader test is being reported.

**Shared job:** put the agreed activity-to-desk assignments into a note.
**Pass rule:** in the text shown, quiet reading points to the window desk and
conversation points to the door desk, with no conflicting assignment.
This checks the note's contents, not whether a visitor finds the right desk.

**Supposed unnoticed default:** the imaginary keeper thinks those assignments
must be written as sentences. That earlier belief is part of the fiction,
not a recovered record.
