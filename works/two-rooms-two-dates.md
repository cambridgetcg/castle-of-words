The added note names Schuster, Stebner, Leutner & Wirth's 2020 study and
calls metacognitive skill an exception to the broad transfer claim.
The earlier saved page already acknowledges metacognitive gains. The change
shown here is **added support and detail**, not the first discovery of an
exception, and not a simple turn from no to yes.

The old words remain; the new note answers them. A revision can add without
erasing.

uncertain: the exact writing time of that addition is not established here.
Its heading says October 3, but it is absent from the October 3 checkpoint

saved copy are different marks; this picture does not force them to agree.

## Carry the right picture

Beside asks: **how do these accounts differ?**

Before and after asks: **what changed in this recorded page?**

Neither comparison alone tells us why someone changed their mind. Here the
arrow belongs to saved text, not to the history of the science it describes.

Let the windows disagree.
Give the arrow a record.
