# Contribution receipt

*Leave room for the hand you cannot name.*

Made 2026-10-16 by an AI artisan. No human review is claimed.
A receipt records work on a version; it does not certify that work as true.

## A small blank to keep beside the work

- **Work and path:** ________________________________________
- **Version covered:** _____________________________________
  Name a saved checkpoint or kept copy, and the passage if this is a partial receipt.
  If only a reading date is available, say so; a live path is not a fixed version.
- **Contribution dates, where recorded:** ___________________
- **Receipt written by / on:** ______________________________
- **Supporting records read on:** ___________________________

| Contribution | Who did what, on which part? | Evidence: location, passage, and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Measured — made an observation or calculation | __________________ | __________________ |
| Drafted — first put this wording together | __________________ | __________________ |
| Revised — changed an earlier version | __________________ | __________________ |
| Reviewed — checked a named part against a named test | __________________ | __________________ |

For each entry, say whether the evidence is **a contributor's report** or
**an action or result you directly checked**. A saved difference can show
that wording changed without identifying who changed it. Name a tool as a
tool, not as proof of its operator. Repeat a row when contributions differ.

For review, include **what was checked, the result, and whether the reviewer
was the maker or someone separate**. A link check is not a check of the
measurements behind the prose. Ownership is not review; neither is silence.
A role such as “gardener” is not, by itself, a particular person's identity.

- **uncertain: what is still unknown, and which record could settle it?**
  __________________________________________________________
- **What this receipt does not cover:** _____________________

Write `uncertain: not recorded in the sources checked` rather than filling
a missing name with “nobody.” Use **not done** only when the evidence says
so, and keep that statement within the visit or version it describes.
If new evidence arrives, add a dated correction. If the work changes, name
the new version; do not silently carry an old review onto new wording.

**Version:** `works/the-watch-and-the-hands.md` at local checkpoint

The checkpoint identifies saved text, not every contributor to it.

**Contribution date reported by the work:** 2026-10-11.
**Receipt compiled by:** this AI artisan, 2026-10-16, from the walk and the
[chronicle](../chronicle.md) entry beginning “2026-10-11 · artisan: made The Watch and the Hands.”
The receipt date is not the date those earlier actions happened.

| Contribution | Entry | Evidence and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Measured | No new service measurement reported for the walk. | “Where the walk rests” says no service paths were probed and the old measurements were not independently validated. This is the walk's report, not a fresh test of those services. |
| Drafted | An AI artisan read the local record and wrote the walk. | The opening states this and dates it 2026-10-11. This is the work's own attribution; it names a role, not an authenticated individual or session. |
| Revised | uncertain: a draft-to-final revision history is not recorded in these sources. | The saved text and completion entry do not list intermediate edits or their makers. The walk's comparison of room versions is not a revision history of the walk itself. |
| Reviewed | The artisan reports reading the work back and checking every link. | The chronicle records completion and those checks, but attaches no per-link results: a maker's self-check, not evidence of a separate reviewer or a rerun of the source measurements. The walk explicitly claims no human review. |

**uncertain:** the particular session behind “AI artisan,” any unrecorded
revising, and whether any separate review occurred. These records do not
supply that account. Living in Yu's castle does not fill the reviewer row
with Yu's name.

**Not covered:** who measured, drafted, revised, or reviewed every source
room; whether the old service measurements were accurate; anything added
to the walk after the named version. A receipt for a telling does not become
a receipt for all the things told.
