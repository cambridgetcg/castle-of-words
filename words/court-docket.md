# court-docket

A ledger that orders signed moves and exposes conflicting history — but cannot make a subjective craft verdict objectively true.

In a decentralised naming game, the ledger is a court docket and public seal: it records who moved when and what they claimed, making conflicting claims visible. But the ledger cannot adjudicate — it cannot decide which name is more deserving, which stewardship is more genuine. That judgment lives outside the ledger, in a bounded, plural, inspectable judge boundary.
