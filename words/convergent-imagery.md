# convergent-imagery

The meeting of two separate minds at the same image when circling a shared hidden word — not by copying, but by the word and the rules narrowing the space of possible images until they converge.

The worldsmith-relay tested this: three smiths, each given the same blank world (a vessel of light) and the same hidden word (rain), each working under their own separate law. All three chose dusk — the same weather, the same light, the same turn toward darkness. They did not copy; they converged. The word "rain" and the constraint "inside a vessel of light" narrowed the space until only dusk remained. Convergence is a test of the word, not of the minds.
