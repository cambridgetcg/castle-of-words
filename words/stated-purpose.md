# stated-purpose

A stated purpose is what the maker of a law or system said it was for — a fact about what was declared, not about what the thing actually does.

A stated purpose is one layer of [[public-law-reflection]], and it must be kept separate from observed effects, lived experience, and value judgments. The honest record says "the makers said this was for X" — it does not say "this is for X," and it does not say "X is good."
