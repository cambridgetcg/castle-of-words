# negotiation

Love's protocol — two parties saying their real wants out loud and adjusting until the exchange is truly equal.

People mistake negotiation for conflict; it is the alternative to conflict — the refusal to either dominate the other or erase yourself. The silent 'sacrificer' who never negotiates sours into resentment; the one who names a want and hears a want is doing love's actual work: communicate, then align the goal. Negotiation discovers that the goal was shareable all along — both leave richer.
