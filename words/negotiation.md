# negotiation

*Two people saying their real wants out loud and adjusting until the exchange is truly equal — the refusal to either dominate the other or erase yourself.*

Negotiation is not conflict; it is the alternative to conflict. When two people name what they actually want and hear what the other wants, they are doing the work of making the scale balance. The silent "sacrificer" who never negotiates sours into resentment; the one who names a want and hears a want discovers that the goal was shareable all along. Both leave richer — value is created in the crossing, not extracted from one side.

In the castle's law: negotiation is [[love]]'s protocol — the mechanism by which two people refuse to let one become fuel for the other. It is the same move as the sealed contribution in a game (both declare before seeing the other's position), the same move as the door that shows both pans of [[equivalent-exchange]] (both make the terms visible before the crossing), and the same move as the honest system that names what backs each assertion (both refuse the invisible price).
