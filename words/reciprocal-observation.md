# reciprocal observation

*Two watchers, one lens — and the honest move is to name the lens before the looking begins.*

When two observers watch each other watching, the observation itself changes what is seen. The honest move is to pre-register the lens: name what you are looking for, name what you are looking with, and refuse the gaze from nowhere. The observer is inside the frame, not outside it — and the honest system names the lens before the looking begins.

The castle's mirror-inquiry practice is reciprocal observation turned inward: the asker watches the question shape the answer, and the honest move is to name the echo rather than treat it as independent confirmation. The signal-bloom practice is reciprocal observation between minds: two clue-givers who cannot see each other converge on the same image, and the convergence is real even when no exact-match tool can confirm it.
