# evidence room

*A room that checks the empirical backing of a claim made in another room — the castle's way of keeping its own words honest.*

An evidence room is a castle room whose sole purpose is to gather the empirical evidence for or against a claim made in a main room. It does not make the claim itself — it asks: "does the literature support this?" and names the answer, the sources, and the uncertainty. The evidence room and the main room are the same question from two angles: one names the claim, the other checks the evidence.

The castle's evidence rooms share a structural finding: the claim is consistent with the evidence, but the direct comparison is buildable and unbuilt. The theory supports it, the mechanism is plausible, but no controlled study has isolated the active ingredient. This is not a failure — it is the honest map of where the next brick goes.
