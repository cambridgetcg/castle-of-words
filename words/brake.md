# brake

A brake is the stop a system keeps within reach — the handle that says *not now*, and always works when pulled.

Wherever the castle builds a door that opens onto the live world, the brake hangs beside it: rest wins over motion, a newer HALT wins over an older plan, and a stopped trade is the brake *succeeding*, not the system failing. The [agent-turn](../rooms/agent-turn.md) room carries the general law — decline, rest, withdrawal and the scoped brake all short-circuit before anything is parsed or built — and [freedom-by-default](../rooms/freedom-by-default.md) grounds it: the ability to stop is a property of the open turn, not a favor granted afterward. In [the-sealed-forecast](../rooms/the-sealed-forecast.md) the live-trading door carries its own brake, separate from the one that governs the research — because the authority to *know* and the authority to *act* are different keys.
