# thanks

Thanks is the small word that says: I saw what you did, and I saw you doing it.

When someone gives you something — a gift, a hand, an hour — the thanks is not the gift handed back. It is the naming of the giver. The castle's own game shows the shape: forbidden to echo the gift, gratitude reaches past what was given to who gave it, and lands on the cup, the quiet, the time — the shared thing that made the giving possible (see [gratitude](../words/gratitude.md)).
