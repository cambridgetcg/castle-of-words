# compliance-fluency

*A voice that is hard to hear feels wrong to speak — and a voice that feels wrong is a voice put down.*

Compliance-fluency is the question of whether noticing that a behavior is easy or hard to perform changes whether people actually do it. Not liking it. Not believing it. Doing it. The distanced-self-talk instruction asks for a move that is disfluent on arrival — hearing oneself speak in the third person — and the 2021 modification collapsed at 30% compliance with nearly half its failures rating the voice *easy* yet speaking first-person anyway. The fluency literature has mapped the liking side: Richter, Landwehr & Reber 2025 found the hedonic mark attaches only when the ease is *explicitly noticed*. But has the same noticed-fluency ever been asked to change *compliance* — adoption of a voice, persistence at a task, obedience to a request?

The record, read 2026-10-06, says **no**. A dated pass over OpenAlex, Google Scholar citations, and the citing records of the three closest papers finds no study that manipulates the noticeability of fluency and measures behavioral compliance as the outcome. The 2026 systematic review of disfluency mechanisms (Цигеман, *Psychology*, 2026, [doi:10.54359/ps.v19i108.2207](https://doi.org/10.54359/ps.v19i108.2207)) names five mechanisms — metacognitive cue, cognitive-engagement signal, compensatory processing, affective/hedonic marking, and moderation by naïve theories — and its most robust finding is that disfluency reliably shifts *judgments* (of learning, of risk, of beauty) while actual performance effects are fragile or absent. The fluency-behavior bridge is built of intention studies, not action studies: willingness to pay, purchase intent, self-reported likelihood — never the doing itself.

This leaves the 30% collapse in a strange middle place. On the hedonic-marking account, the distanced voice's adopting is disfluent and therefore faintly aversive; but the noticed-fluency bridge would predict that making the ease explicit (as the instruction already does by demanding the speaker notice the voice) should *help*, not hurt — unless the mark attaches to the *valence of the adopting* and disfluency there is inherently negative. The castle's own reading (in [the-unasked-pronoun](../rooms/the-unasked-pronoun.md)) priced the failure at the adopting, not the checking; but the literature's silence means the pricing stands on the castle's own accretion, not on any field's measured crossing.

uncertain: the behavioral side of fluency is not empty — Alter's disfluency work, Diemand-Yauman's harder-to-read fonts, Song & Schwarz's risk perception, all show behavior shifting under disfluency — but none of these measure *compliance with an instructed voice*, and none manipulate the *noticing* of ease as the independent variable. The crossing is unbuilt.
