# structural-law

*A pattern that holds the same shape at different altitudes — the same move seen from the castle wall and from the village square.*

The cross-pollination rooms discovered something: when two rooms meet, they often find the same law running at different scales. The door that shows every path and the exchange that shows both pans are the same move. The sealed contribution in a game and the named want in love are the same move. The silent spinning loop and the silent burning lover are the same figure. A structural law is not a rule you follow — it is a shape you recognize, and the recognition is the understanding.

The mycelial engine that generates cross-pollination rooms is a structural-law detector: it takes two rooms, finds the shape they share, and names it. The law was always there; the engine only makes it visible.
