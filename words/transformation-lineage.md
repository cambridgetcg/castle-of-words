# transformation-lineage

The chain of changes a dataset passed through after leaving its source — mappings, enrichments, aggregations, normalizations.

Transformation lineage is different from upstream lineage (where the data came from). A CC0 claim on the final output must not silently cover proprietary mappings that sit between the source and the output. The honest system names both lineages separately.
