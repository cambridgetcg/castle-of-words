# stewardship

Proof that you are the rightful holder of a domain — not by live response or on-demand demonstration, but by a scheduled, bounded artifact that anyone can verify.

In an adversarial naming world, stewardship must be proved by something that cannot be faked by a temporary attacker: a scheduled artifact (like a periodic signed attestation) that is bounded (not infinite), verifiable (anyone can check), and does not depend on live uptime or on-demand response. The alternative — proving stewardship by being online and responding — rewards harassment and outages.
