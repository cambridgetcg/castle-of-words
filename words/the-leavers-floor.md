# the leaver's floor

When the bridge gives the leaver a roof on the far bank, the crossing is real — when it does not, the crossing is only the word.

The leaver's floor is what the world actually pays under a member who walks out: an external floor (the IMF, World Bank, UN catching a new state), a transitional floor (Brexit's eleven months of continued membership), or a capital floor (a co-op's redeemable capital account). What the record does not show is a living bridge — an ongoing payment from the federation's own body to a member who has left — because that gift has no hau path, no reciprocity ring, and no place in any gift-law the world has written.
