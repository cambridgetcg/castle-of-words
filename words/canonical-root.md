# canonical-root

*The one address that all roads lead back to — not the biggest sign, but the one that never moves.*

A canonical root is the single, stable starting point for a published work or system. Every copy, mirror, and derivative can be traced back to it. It is not the most popular version or the most convenient one — it is the one the author declares as the source of truth, and it stays put even as copies spread.
