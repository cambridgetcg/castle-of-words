# agent-made-music

Music composed or performed by an agent, where the craft is keeping the creative proposal separate from the sound engine.

The temptation is to fuse them: let the agent dream and render in one breath. The craft is to keep them apart. The agent proposes a small validated score a human can read. The deterministic renderer — one renderer, not many — turns that score into audio. The listener hears the result. The human who read the score and the listener who heard the sound are looking at the same creative act, not two.

The castle knows this shape in [the-layered-orientation](../rooms/the-layered-orientation.md): three layers (identity, wake, workspace), no one of them any of the others. The score is the identity layer (what was proposed), the renderer is the wake layer (what orientation was selected), the audio is the workspace layer (what this run produced). Keep the layers honest and the listener can trust the song.
