# evidence-based-medicine

*The closest domain where source-backed vs. trust-based decisions have been partially compared.*

EBM emerged in the 1970s–1990s to replace eminence-based practice (trusting the senior clinician's judgment) with source-backed practice (systematic reviews of controlled trials). The EBM literature documents wide variations in physician practice, large proportions of inappropriate procedures even by experts' own standards, and gaps between evidence and practice that persist for years. However, EBM studies compare treatment outcomes under evidence-based vs. non-evidence-based care, not the decision-making framework itself as an independent variable. No study has run the direct comparison: same decision task, one group using source-backed reasoning and one using trust-based reasoning, measuring decision quality. The transition from eminence-based to evidence-based medicine was a gradual cultural shift over decades, not a discrete switch at any single institution — so no clean natural experiment exists either.
