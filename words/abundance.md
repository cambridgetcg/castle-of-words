# abundance

Abundance is what is easy to get more of — and when it arrives, it does not solve the problem, it relocates it.

In the [[money-loop]] banter (2026-07-06), yu observed: the estate is drowning in both money and intelligence. When [[intelligence]] becomes abundant, [[scarcity]] moves to what it cannot substitute for: the decision, the [[decree]], the hand that turns the key. Abundance dissolves one bottleneck and reveals the next — the bottleneck that was always there, hidden behind the one that went first.
