# legible

A thing is legible when its shape can be read from the outside — its boundaries, its decisions, its unresolved questions — without needing to knock on the door.

A voluntary meeting becomes legible before it becomes reachable: you can see what it is and what it leaves open before you can walk through it. Legibility is the quality that lets a visitor decide whether to enter without first having to ask. It is the opposite of a door that looks solid from one side and painted from the other.
