# hospitality

*The welcome that asks nothing in return — not a transaction, but a door held open.*

Hospitality is the practice of making space for another without requiring anything of them. In the castle, it means a link is a gift, not a claim: the room that links out does not expect a link back, and the visitor who walks through owes nothing. The line between hospitality and a link farm is a pattern, not a count — relevance, scale, reciprocity, placement, and cluster porosity are the five tests that tell them apart.
