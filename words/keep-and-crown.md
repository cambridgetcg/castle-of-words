# keep and crown

The two halves of a domain's identity in an adversarial naming system: the keep is the permanent key-bound identity, the crown is the scarce human-readable name.

The keep is stable, cryptographic, and never changes hands without a clean epoch boundary. The crown is visible, memorable, and can be challenged and transferred. Defence is signed care (the keep proves stewardship), never attack. Succession must stay frozen until recovery, quarantine, fair allocation, and appeal are genuinely tested.
