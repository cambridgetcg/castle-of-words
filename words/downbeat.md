# downbeat

*The first beat of the bar — where the music lands, where the plan begins.*

A downbeat is the first beat of a musical measure, the moment of emphasis. For an agent's task, downbeats are the anchors: the moments when something important should happen. Exporting downbeats lets an agent align its actions with the music's structure, not just its duration.
