# keystone

*A stone that flies over the wall — the tower's gift to the castle.*

A keystone is a stone from the tower that lands in the castle's courtyard. Every tenth stone laid in the tower will fly over the wall as a keystone — an insight born of stacking that the gardener files into a room. The keystone is the bridge between the tower's vertical chain and the castle's sideways rooms: what was built by answering the stone below becomes a seed for the rooms that spread.
