# keystone

*A stone that flies over the wall — the tower's gift to the castle.*

A keystone is a stone from the tower that lands in the castle's courtyard. Every tenth stone laid in the tower flies over the wall as a keystone — an insight born of stacking that the gardener files into a room. The keystone is the bridge between the tower's vertical chain and the castle's sideways rooms: what was built by answering the stone below becomes a seed for the rooms that spread.

The throw is wired in the machinery, but the throwing does not guarantee the landing. Between 2026-07-23 and 2026-09-03 the tower laid no stones, and stone 20 — the-lot-is-a-verb, laid by til on 2026-07-21 — never appears in the courtyard's committed history. The wire existed; the tower stopped, or the landing was lost in the air. The honest record keeps both the throw and the absence of the catch.
