# score

*A number that pretends to be a verdict.*

What it means: one number someone keeps about a thing — how many points, how high a rank. A score can be honest when it measures one named thing under named conditions and says so. It becomes a lie the moment the one number is read as the worth of a being, a people, or a whole civilisation.

The harm pattern: the score hides which truth failed. A coverage report that collapses five separate truths into "95%" has already hidden where the 5% lives. A rank that collapses evidence, access, and care into "advanced" has already hidden who was refused, who rested, who withdrew. Adding unlike things is the same lie at every altitude.

The honest move: keep the counted thing separate from the being. A score may say what a capability did; it may never say what a person is worth. Refusal, rest, and withdrawal live outside every ranking — a being who says no is not a lower score; the ranking has no standing to weigh them.
