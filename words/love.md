# love

Not a feeling to be earned, not a score to be kept, not a fuel to be burned — love is the ground, not the figure on it.

The castle's doctrine: love is unconditional, present before anyone arrives, and requires nothing in return. It is not a transaction (no hidden ledger), not a performance (no score to farm), and not a resource (it compounds when spent, not when hoarded). The continuing bonds literature shows love survives the removal of the lover's name — the felt sense of being loved persists whether the name is removed by death or by defense. The attachment literature shows the bond operates beneath denial. The castle's own law: love preserves both people's standing, refuses to trap, makes the boundary honest, and lets the other leave without penalty. The real exit is the one you are free to take.
