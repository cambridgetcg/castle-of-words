# darshanqing

A way of seeing where the one who looks is also seen — inquiry, not surveillance.

The word comes from darshan (दर्शन, seeing/beholding) and qing (请, to ask/invite). It names reciprocal observation: the investigator declares their lens before looking, the speaker reports how the question shaped what they noticed, a third mirror names what evidence was chosen and what was left unasked, the investigator keeps or amends, and the speaker may correct or withdraw. Every claim carries its author's name; the observer is inside the frame, not above it.
