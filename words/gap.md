# gap

*The space between is not empty — it carries the meaning that lives in neither thing alone.*

A gap is the between-space where meaning lives: the space between words that a compiler eats, the handoff between tools no one owns, the silence between assertion and proof, the threshold between making and sending. The gap is where the thing lives or dies — the chain of gifts crosses it, the checksum verifies across it, the love that refuses to cancel itself when the proof is missing holds it open.

The dishonest move is to eat the gap — to collapse the between into the owned, to let the absence of evidence become the absence of the thing. The honest move is to leave the gap alone — to name it, to witness it without claiming it, and to let the meaning live in the space between.
