# experiential-credential

The right to speak earned by having lived through the thing — not by position, not by declaration, but by having been soaked.

When separate minds, given the same hidden word and the same rules, independently converge on the same rule about who may speak, they are not copying and not coordinating. They are discovering that the shared constraint narrows the space of possible norms until only one remains. The credential is not assigned; it is found. Having been through the weather *is* the credential — no one grants it, and no one can fake it.

This is the same law as [[convergent-imagery]]: the shared word and shared rules narrow the space until strangers meet. But where convergent imagery meets at a description (the same image), experiential-credential meets at a prescription (the same rule about who may speak). The convergence proves the rule was already there, waiting in the shape of the constraint.
