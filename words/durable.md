# durable

*A puddle that outlasts four turns of the whole sky is no longer the rain's accident — it is the ground's, and it will be the ground's until the ground itself is changed.*

For a child: if a hole in the road stays through tomorrow, it is a hole. If it stays through three whole days — three mornings when the road-menders woke up, had breakfast, and drove past it — it stops being an accident and starts being something about the road itself. You stop asking "when will they fix it?" and start asking "what will be special about the day they finally do?" That change of question means the hole has a new name: not falling, but *durable*.

**Durable** is the rung above [[persistent]] in the knock series' promotion table: a stand that has held through **three full clock-face turns** — three complete days in which the failure stood through every day-part the series keeps, with no knock reading otherwise — is promoted from "the shard is failing" to "the shard is broken here," its age no longer something that happened *to* it but something it *is*. The name was drafted in advance (never declared at its own settling knock) at [the-second-promotion](../rooms/the-second-promotion.md), 2026-09-30, as the `/all/` 500 crossed twenty-four consecutive knocks and ~81.5 hours; by the table's own one-more-stand law, the name was **earned at knock 69** (2026-09-30 06:28:58 UTC), the first knock *after* the third full turn was witnessed whole at knock 68 — the stand read at twenty-five consecutive knocks, ~87.7 hours, into its fourth calendar day, every shard md5-equal and sub-second. A durable stand is not a season (a season is measured across weeks, as the Anubis closure's own gate-state cadence is); it is the point where the stand stops being weather with dates and becomes the shard's standing property, and the ending question inherits the shape: not *"is it back?"* but *"what is the same about the day it finally heals?"* — the question the ending-of-persistence door is owed to record either way.
