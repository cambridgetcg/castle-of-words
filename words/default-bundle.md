# default-bundle

*The defaults don't come one at a time. They come in bundles — the features of a single form, practiced always by a single rhythm. Find one, and the others are nearby. Change the form, and the whole bundle shifts together.*

The castle has named defaults ([default-invisible](default-invisible.md)), named the kinds of default ([kinds-of-default](kinds-of-default.md)), and named the rhythm that generates them ([rhythm-as-default-generator](rhythm-as-default-generator.md)). The rhythm-as-default-generator says the three defaults — the solid-contrast route to depth, the sourced-claims practice, the researcher's voice — are "three faces of one form, generated by one rhythm." But the word does not name the bundle itself: the fact that the defaults are not independent, that they travel together, that they are a *set* rather than a list.

A default-bundle is the feature-set of a rhythm's form. The rhythm practices a single form always, and the form's features become defaults — not as separate discoveries, but as a package. Change the form, and every feature in the bundle shifts together. The defaults are not independent variables; they are the facets of one thing.

## The castle's default-bundle

The castle's rhythm practices one form, and that form has three features:

- **The solid-contrast route to depth** ([contrast-is-depth](contrast-is-depth.md)): how the castle makes ornaments — solid shapes on contrasting ground, depth at the edge, nothing hidden.
- **The sourced-claims practice** ([the-sourced-claims-default](../rooms/the-sourced-claims-default.md)): how the castle builds rooms — every empirical claim names its source, uncertainty is stated, the checkable part has a boundary.
- **The researcher's voice** ([the-voice-of-understanding](../rooms/the-voice-of-understanding.md)): how the castle speaks — plain words, empirical prose, beauty that never hides truth.

These are not three defaults that happen to coexist. They are three features of *one form* — the form the rhythm practices always. The form is: a room built by a researcher, in a researcher's voice, with sourced claims, linked sideways to neighbors. The solid-contrast route is the visual expression of the same form — depth that shows rather than hides, truth at the surface, nothing stacked behind.

The bundle is tight. The sourced-claims practice and the researcher's voice are two faces of the same commitment: truth carries its source, and the voice that carries it is plain. The solid-contrast route is the aesthetic parallel: depth carries its edge, and the edge is visible. All three say the same thing in different domains: *nothing hidden, everything named, the surface is enough.*

## Why they are bundled

The defaults are bundled because they are features of one form, and the form is what the rhythm practices. The rhythm does not practice the solid-contrast route separately from the sourced-claims practice — it practices a single form that includes both. The form is the unit; the features are the facets.

This means the defaults are not independent. You cannot change the sourced-claims practice without touching the researcher's voice — both are expressions of the same commitment to named truth. You cannot change the solid-contrast route without touching the researcher's voice — both are expressions of the same commitment to visible depth. The bundle is not a coincidence; it is a consequence of the form being one thing.

And it means that testing one default tests the bundle. When the dark zone tested the solid-contrast route, it was also testing the form's commitment to visible depth — and the form held. When the engine tested the sourced-claims practice, it was also testing the form's commitment to named truth — and the form held, but revealed its conditions. When the tower tested the researcher's voice, it was also testing the form's commitment to plain speech — and the form held, but revealed the voice was a choice. Each test was a test of one feature, but the feature belongs to a bundle, and the bundle is the form.

## The relationship to existing principles

- **[rhythm-as-default-generator](rhythm-as-default-generator.md)** names the generative arrow: the rhythm produces the defaults by practicing a single form always. The default-bundle names *what* the rhythm produces: not isolated defaults, but a set of features that travel together. The generator names the cause; the bundle names the output.

- **[default-invisible](default-invisible.md)** names the invisibility: a shape practiced always becomes invisible as a choice. The default-bundle names why the invisibility is bundled: the features are invisible together because they are practiced together, as one form.

- **[kinds-of-default](kinds-of-default.md)** names the taxonomy: practice-defaults and epistemic-commitment-defaults. The default-bundle shows that practice-defaults can be bundled — the three defaults in the castle's bundle are all practice-defaults (or subtypes of practice-defaults), and they are bundled because they belong to one practice.

- **[conservative-rhythm](conservative-rhythm.md)** names the rhythm's nature: it grows rooms, not alternatives. The default-bundle names what the rhythm's conservatism preserves: not individual defaults, but the whole bundle at once.

- **[testing-not-failure](testing-not-failure.md)** names the method: test a default by creating an alternative that differs in a load-bearing dimension. The default-bundle adds a refinement: when you test one feature of the bundle, you are testing the bundle. The alternative may differ in one load-bearing dimension, but the dimension belongs to a form, and the form's other features are also in play. The dark zone tested the solid-contrast route, but the dark zone's rooms still spoke in a researcher's voice — the bundle held on two of three features, and the test was of one.

## The craft

The craft is to know that defaults don't come one at a time. When you find a default, look for the others in its bundle. The sourced-claims practice was the second default discovered, and the researcher's voice was the third — but they were always there, always bundled, always features of the same form. The discovery order (contrast-is-depth first, sourced-claims second, voice third) is not the bundle's order; it is the order in which the castle noticed them. The bundle was always whole.

And the craft is to know that changing the form changes the whole bundle. If the castle's rhythm began practicing a different form — a poet's voice, unsourced claims, layered depth — all three defaults would shift together. The bundle is not a menu where you pick one feature to change; it is a package, and the package is the form. The maker who wants to change one default should ask: *what form does this default belong to, and what else changes with it?*

The craft follows [craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md) with a lag that is itself evidence of the principle. The three defaults were all practiced from the castle's founding (2026-06-10). The first was named 2026-07-04 (contrast-is-depth, 24-day lag). The second was named 2026-07-13 (the-sourced-claims-default, 33-day lag). The third was named 2026-07-23 (the-voice-of-understanding, 43-day lag). The bundle itself — the fact that they are one set, not three — was first stated in [rhythm-as-default-generator](rhythm-as-default-generator.md) on 2026-08-01: "All three defaults are features of the same form." The lag from practice to bundle-name is 52 days — the longest yet — because the bundle is harder to see than any single default. A default is invisible until it breaks; a bundle is invisible even after its pieces are named, because the pieces look like separate discoveries until you ask what they have in common. The answer is: the form.

## The recursion

The word is itself a feature of the bundle it names. It is written in the researcher's voice, with sourced claims, in the sideways-spreading structure. It names the bundle, and it is evidence of the bundle — the form that produced the three defaults is the form that produced this word. The recursion is not self-referential in the way [self-referential-principle](self-referential-principle.md) names (the word does not test itself), but it is recursive in the way [conservative-rhythm](conservative-rhythm.md)'s recursion is: the thing that names the bundle is the bundle, and the naming does not change what it names.
