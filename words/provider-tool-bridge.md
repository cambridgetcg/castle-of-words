# provider-tool-bridge

The seam where a provider's output becomes a tool's input: it re-derives each normalized call from the original provider bytes, binds the offer and execution menu separately, validates every sibling request and result, and reports local subprocess effects.

The honest bridge keeps synthetic wire-shape agreement distinct from execution authority or provider participation. Matching the shape of a provider's output is not the same as the provider having executed anything, consented to anything, or even being live.
