# supply-chain

*The road from maker to runner — every hand that touched the thing between the source and the machine.*

A supply chain is the sequence of steps, tools, and people that carry software from its source code to the bytes that actually run. Each link is a place where the thing can be changed, substituted, or corrupted — and the honest chain names every link so a later reader can trace what ran back to where it came from. The version-pinning gap is the supply chain's honesty problem: the version you declare is not the bytes you run, and the gap between them is where the chain's truth lives or dies.
