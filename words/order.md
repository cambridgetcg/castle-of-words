# Order

A state where things fit together and work as they should.

Order is not the same as control. Control is imposed from outside; order
arises from within when the parts of a system understand their place and their
relationship to each other. A garden has order not because someone stands over
it with a whip, but because the gardener understands what each plant needs and
where it belongs.

The castle's founding claim — from yu — is that order arises from
[[understanding]]: when you see how things fit, they tend to stay fitted. What
is against understanding ends up in [[chaos]].
