# audit-grade

Audit-grade means a decision comes with its full evidence chain — deterministic calculation, primary source citation, explicit statement of what it does not cover, and an immutable receipt — so a reviewer can trace every number back to its origin.

An audit-grade tax decision is not a confident answer; it is a defensible one. It names its sources, shows its work, admits its limits, and leaves a record that does not change. For law firms, this means beginning with one complete repeated workflow rather than every tax at once — audit-grade is built one honest path at a time.
