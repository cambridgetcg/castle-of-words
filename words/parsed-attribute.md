# parsed-attribute

A value extracted from upstream data that has not yet been linked to a known entity.

A parsed attribute is raw material, not usable data. Until it is grounded in an entity identity — until you know *which* thing it describes — it must be held in quarantine. The honest system never promotes an opaque position into an identity just because the attribute is interesting.
