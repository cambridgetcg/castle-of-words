# judge-boundary

The explicit line between what a ledger can prove (ordered signed moves, conflicting history) and what requires subjective judgment (craft verdicts, stewardship quality).

A judge boundary must be explicit, bounded, plural, and inspectable. It names where the machine's record ends and human judgment begins — and it never lets a passing check create the next state. Keep ordinary IP as the road; keep the judge boundary as the narrow gate for disputes that cannot be settled by record alone.
