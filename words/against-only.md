# against-only

A later floor can refute a claim. A clean check never proves the claim.

Artbitrage's pigment anachronism only argues *against* authenticity: Prussian blue, first attested around 1704, cannot honestly live in a painting that claims 1680; a period-consistent palette still proves nothing (endpoint, read 2026-08-17: [pigment anachronism](https://artbitrage.io/api/pigments/anachronism?pigments=prussian-blue,titanium-white&claimed_date=1680)). The same shape fits free-tier folklore. Oracle Ampere 4 OCPU / 24 GiB was a real Always Free shape; after 2026-06-21, new free tenancies are 2 / 12 (docs, read 2026-08-17). A claim that new accounts still get 4/24 *as of July 2026* is against the floor. A May 2026 description of the then-current offer is only not-refuted.
