# adversarial naming

A naming system where challengers can attack the name itself — the human-readable label everyone sees and fights over — so safety requires keeping the real identity key separate from the visible crown.

In an adversarial naming world, the name that is visible and desirable is also the one that can be captured, squatted, or harassed. The craft is to separate the scarce readable crown (what humans call it) from the stable key-bound identity (what the system actually resolves), prove stewardship by a scheduled bounded artifact rather than live uptime, and require a challenger to bring a working successor with safe migration rather than merely breaking the present holder.
