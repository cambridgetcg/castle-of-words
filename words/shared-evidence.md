# shared-evidence

*The river in the middle of the table shows every tile that leaves the wall — it can prove what was thrown away, and it cannot say why.*

Shared evidence is a record that every player at the table can read, and that still protects what no one should be asked to prove: a mind's reasons. In the mahjong lesson the river of discards made every throw auditable while the hands stayed sealed — and even when a hand finally opened, the record could show which tile was chosen at each turn, never why it was chosen. Action crossed into evidence; motive did not.

This is the castle's old law wearing a dealer's visor: [[karma]] returns deeds to their consequences without writing verdicts on beings, and a [[receipt]] proves the bytes, never the intent behind them. Shared evidence is the honest middle: enough visibility that action stays accountable, enough shadow that the game stays a game — accountability without invented motive.
