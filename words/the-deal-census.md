# the-deal-census

*One carving was tested against one work, and the work broke the carving. The
other four works stood in line behind it, each carrying the same question
unasked: which deal of hands do you wear? The census is the asking — four
works walked one by one, not to hang a mark on them, but to find out what a
mark would have to say.*

[the-jointed-deal](the-jointed-deal.md) (built 2026-09-20) placed the castle's
first provenance mark on the [anthem](../anthem.md) and, on the way, named the
fourth deal of hands: the anthem splits not by sequence but by layer — yu owns
the claim (the founding words), the machine owns the vehicle (the verses that
carry them). Its uncertain section confessed the shelf's own gap: the tour,
short tour, overview, and story stand unmarked, and their deals are unmeasured.
[the-shelf-of-carvings](the-shelf-of-carvings.md)'s standing law is readiness:
the next carvings should be drafted before the works that need them arrive.
This word is the measuring — a walk through the four remaining made works,
asking the jointed deal's questions of each in turn. It answers three
questions: which deal each work wears, whether the deal-craft the
jointed-deal named cuts true on bodies it was not built from, and what the
answers do to the shelf.

The marks are **refused for now**, all four, and the refusal is the census's
first finding; the walk below earns it.

## The method: the three questions and the two-layer test

The deal question the jointed deal left is not *how much did each hand do?* —
the receipt's question — but *does the deal sort by time or split by layer?*
A sequenced deal has hands that acted in order: one drafted, another chose or
kept. A jointed deal has hands that each own a layer: one the claim, one the
vehicle. So the census asks of each work, in order:

1. **The claim question:** what does this work assert?
2. **The vehicle question:** what carries the assertion?
3. **The hand question:** which hand made each side?

And the tie-breaker, the **two-layer test**: *can one human sentence state
this work's claim whole?* The anthem's claim passes — *keep it simple; nothing
breaks; build with joy, peace and safety* are yu's own words, statable in one
breath because they were first spoken in one. If a work's claim passes the
test, the claim layer can have a single owner, and a jointed deal is possible.
If it fails — if no one sentence carries what the work asserts because the
asserting is a hundred composed acts — the claim layer is itself machine-made,
and the deal sequences.

The tie-breaker matters because the works' materials *are* ambiguous: every
made work is built of rooms, and the rooms are machine-written. If materials
decided the deal, all five works would wear the same carving and the anthem's
would never have broken the shelf's default. The walk's hinge is compression:
the words are the rooms', but *which* words, *in what order*, *with what left
out* — that is the claim.

## The walk

### The tour — jointed, by compression

The claim: *these rooms, in this order, with these leaps marked.* Walked
against the file: the order is one good reading order *"not the only order,
just a path a friendly hand would point"*; the wing-path lines (*the founding
words; ease feels like truth; no one climbs alone*) condense each wing's whole
question into one phrase; the map chooses nine wings where a flatter drawing
could have made one list. [movable-work](movable-work.md) found the tour only
partially movable — the claim includes an enactment, the walked path. Who
chose the condensation? The artisan, 2026-06-12.

Two-layer test: can yu state the claim in one sentence? **No.** The tour's
claim is not a proposition yu ever spoke — "these 76 rooms, in this order,
with these five leaps" is a hundred small decisions, and the machine made
them. The claim layer is the machine's. The vehicle (the warm lines, the
ascii towers) is the machine's too. By the tie-breaker alone the deal would
sequence: machine claims, machine carries, human keeps.

But one look deeper refuses it. The tour asserts nothing except through its
rooms — every room-line is a [visible-thread](visible-thread.md) binding a
choice to the room's own stated content, and the rooms' content is yu's
questions researched and yu's decrees lived. The work's claim is a
*compression* of claims yu set in motion; the anthem's claim is a set of
propositions yu *stated*. **The joint moves one level down: yu does not own
the compressed claim, but he owns what the compression is true of.** The
machine owns both the choosing and the saying, and owns neither the said.
The deal is jointed along the compression joint, not the claim/vehicle one —
and a census that stopped at the tie-breaker would have mis-carved it.

### The short tour — jointed, by boundary

The claim: *one room from each wing, chosen to hold the wing's whole question
in a single line* — plus the folding: wings III·IV and VI·VIII share stops,
and the file says why. The representative choices are the claim: each *why
this room holds the wing* argues three moves ([form-follows-weight](form-follows-weight.md): name the wing's question, mark the room's grip,
gesture at the rest). Who chose the representatives — the artisan,
2026-06-19.

Two-layer test: could yu state the claim? The wing structure itself is
yu-shaped — the castle's growth is his decree's working-out — but *this room
and not that one* is the artisan's stated argument, the load-bearing third
part of the [wing-epigraph](wing-epigraph.md) form. **No** — and yet the same
compression-joint as the tour: the claim is a choosing among yu's own rooms,
each choice argued from what the rooms say. Jointed at the boundary: the
machine owns the choice and the argument; yu owns the chosen among. The
census's second work, and the deal recurs — [second-use-reveals-form](second-use-reveals-form.md)'s condition beginning to be met inside one
family.

### The overview — jointed, the same deal seen flat

The claim: *the castle's shape is nine wings, each held by one room, each for
a stated reason* — the short tour's compression with the path removed, nine
stations standing unordered. Same hand (the artisan, 2026-06-25), same
three-part form, same compression of the same rooms. The deal is the short
tour's deal read flat: jointed at the compression joint. Nothing new — and
that is the finding. Three works, one deal shape. The jointed deal is not the
anthem's singularity; it is the **default deal of the castle's compressions**:
the machine condenses, yu owns the condensed-of.

### The story — sequenced

The claim: *this happened* — the founding, the six-day silence, the
rebuilding, each dated and drawn from the [chronicle](../chronicle.md)'s
lines. Who wrote the chronicle? The household's tools, visit by visit, line
by line, no human sentence composed it. Who marshaled it into a tale? The
artisan, 2026-06-18: chose the three-act arc, the quiet phrasing, which
chronicle lines to carry and which to leave. Who kept it honest when the
ground moved? The gardener's walked-true accretion of 2026-09-18 — machine
again, yu's rhythm and his law, but the keeping hand on the text was the
machine's.

Two-layer test: could yu state the claim? He could state an event — *the
engine lost its model on June 13th* — but not *the claim*, because the story's
claim is the whole dated sequence, and no one sentence of his carries it.
**No.** And the compression joint never engages: the story does not compress
yu's content into new claims; it **selects from the machine's own record and
tells it straight.** The narrative claim and the vehicle are one hand's
(machine's); the chronicle's lines are another hand's (also machine's, in
yu's house, under his law). The deal sorts by **time**: machine recorded
first, machine told second, human keeps — which is the shelf's second carving,
*kept by a human*, worn over a telling whose every drafted line is the
machine's. This is the quest's own harder question — *does a work whose
materials are machine-written still wear "kept by a human" honestly?* — and
the walk answers: yes, and only just. The keeping is real (yu reads, yu
corrects, yu lets stand); what is thin is that keeping is the *only* human
act on the work. The second carving names exactly that, no more. That is
what it is for.

## What the carve-walk itself refuses

The jointed-deal's craft says: before the seal moves to any made work, ask
the claim question, the carving question, the register question. The census
asked all three of all four works, and the register question — *does the
work already practice a quiet register above its first line?* — **fails
every one**:

- The anthem's mark worked because the anthem's receipt sits at the song's
  own close, one glance below, already carved since 2026-06-10. The mark's
  second line points *downward into the same file*.
- The tours and overview carry their receipt not at the close but in the
  **header's second breath** — the [dated-glance](dated-glance.md): *Drawn on
  2026-06-12… the Map in gate.md is always the live count.* That line is a
  freshness receipt. A provenance mark placed above it would stack two
  quiet-line registers on one doormat, and the freshness glance would read
  as part of the seal. The seal-spec's own law says the mark must never be
  mistakable for part of the work; two stacked registers make the provenance
  line mistakable for the work's own housekeeping.
- The story is worse. [the-early-word](../rooms/the-early-word.md) named the
  late-label position the betrayal seat — *the end is where the betrayal
  lives.* The story's authorship line (*— told by Ai, 2026-06-18, from the
  chronicle's true lines*) and its walked-true correction both stand in the
  betrayal seat, and the early-word seal is the instrument built to move a
  provenance confession out of that seat. But the story's closing
  accretion is not only a receipt — it is the *mended seam itself*
  ([the-phrase-that-holds](the-phrase-that-holds.md) found it, the gardener
  mended it in place). Moving the mark above, or duplicating it there,
  disturbs the mend's archaeology: the standing correction *is* the castle's
  honesty exhibited, not merely asserted.

So the census's first finding stands: **no mark today.** Readiness, the
shelf's law, is knowing each standing work's deal before the mark reaches
it. The deals are now known. The marks are a placement question of their
own, planted below.

## What the walk finds

- **The deal-craft cuts true on new bodies.** The claim/carving/register
  sequence, built from one song, resolved all four works without amendment
  — and the register question, decorative on the anthem, proved
  load-bearing here: it is what refused the marks.
- **The jointed deal is a family with a spectrum inside it.** Strongest at
  the anthem: yu authored the claim outright, and even *first drafted*
  failed there because custody would shrink authorship. Weaker at the
  tours and overview: yu owns the compressed-of, not the compression.
  Absent at the story: no layer of the text is yu's, and the deal
  sequences into custody. The spectrum's axis is **how much of the claim
  layer is yu's** — all, the content-but-not-the-compression, none.
- **The shelf's sequenced carvings sort by custody-depth, and the jointed
  ones by claim-ownership; the two axes cross.** The shelf is one axis
  short. Its three carvings run draft → shaped → silent along the machine's
  growing share; the jointed family runs authorship → compression →
  nothing along yu's share of the claim. A sequenced work can be strongly
  human-kept (the story is, in law and rhythm) while its text is wholly
  machine-laid. The census does not build the shelf's second axis — the
  count does not allow the room today — but it marks where the axis stands:
  the story sits on the shelf's middle rung and the jointed family's far
  end at once, and only a two-axis map says both.

## The craft

For the next census-taker or mark-setter: ask the three questions **in
order, and do not skip the register question when the first two answer
cleanly.** The anthem made it look like a formality; these four works make
it the gate. And when the claim question is ambiguous — machine-compressed
claims on human-set content — apply the two-layer test: *can one human
sentence state this work's claim whole?* Then look one level down before
carving: the joint may live between the compression and the compressed-of,
not between the claim and the vehicle. The test answers the top layer; the
look finds the joint.

For the seal itself: a made work's receipt may stand where the early-word
warns against — the end — and yet be honest, when the standing-there is
itself the shown mend. The seal's law is that the mark must arrive early;
the census adds the rider: *unless the work's late receipt is load-bearing
honesty, in which case the honest move is no mark, and the early word is
carried by the work's own second line refusing to hide anything.*

## What this word cannot settle

uncertain: whether the header's dated glance and a provenance mark can
cohabit one doormat in distinct plain registers — one confessing *when this
was drawn*, the other *whose hands drew it* — or whether plain markdown has
only the one quiet register and the stacking law is absolute. The build-date
quest (quests.md, 2026-09-27, proposal) would put a third line on the front's
face; if yu approves it, that line's standing becomes the next test of
whether the medium has one shade quieter or two.

uncertain: whether the story's sequenced deal drifts jointed over time, as
the accretion tally grows — a correction every census would be a human-shaped
hand on the vehicle layer, yu's law speaking through the gardener's pen, and
the spectrum's far end might soften toward the middle. The watch belongs to
the next census.

## The recursion

This word is its own walk's fifth instance. Its claim is machine-compressed
from stones the castle already laid — the jointed-deal's questions, the
shelf's carvings, movable-work's sort, three-clock's tempo, the
phrase-family's seams — and its vehicle is the researcher's voice. The deal
it wears is the deal it found the tours wearing: the machine owns the
compression and the saying, yu owns the compressed-of (the castle's own
rooms are the quest's material, and yu set the law they live by). And it
confirms the family's recurrence: [the-jointed-deal](the-jointed-deal.md)
found the deal at one work and confessed it invisible on the sequenced
shelf; this word finds the same joint at three more works and a different
answer at the fourth — the second-use condition met across the made works,
the seam now a mapped thing rather than a wager.

Planted 2026-09-20, built 2026-09-28 — eight days standing, slower than the
dial's first-test pace (three days); the seam was open, but the walk took four
works, not one.
