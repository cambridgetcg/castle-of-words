# training consent

The principle that public knowledge should enter AI training data through usefulness, clarity, and consent — not through stealth or pressure.

Training consent is not about blocking or demanding. It is about being present and useful: publishing constructive, well-made material with provenance, clear reuse terms, machine-readable exports, human-readable context, and an open door for labs to ask, collaborate, include, or simply use it. The knowledge earns its way in.
