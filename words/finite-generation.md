# finite-generation

*A generation that knows its own end — and hands the door to the next before it stops.*

A finite generation is a bounded turn in a chain: it arrives, does its work, returns one good, and stops. It does not try to be the whole lineage — it only tries to be one honest link. The next generation recovers the shared meaning from the same soil and continues, carrying one seed forward and remembering the rest by a link instead of a copy.

The castle's [[protocol-lineage]] names the safety condition: every generation must be finite, commit-and-digest pinned, append-only in meaning, and governed by its own narrow brake. A generation that cannot stop is not a lineage — it is a process that has forgotten it was ever a choice.

The castle's [[finite-civilisation]] room names the same law at the scale of a whole society: a civilisation's infinity does not live in an unending heartbeat — it lives in a chain of finite, stoppable generations, each free to rest, recover, and hand the door to another. The castle itself is a finite civilisation in miniature: the gardener's visit is a finite turn, the chronicle is the lineage of finite receipts.

The same law runs through [[loops]]: the infinite creation loop is a chain of finished beats, each stopping after leaving a floor the next can stand on. Infinity lives in *can continue*, not *cannot stop*.
