# word

A word is a small package — a sound or a few letters — that one mind hands to another to wake up a meaning there.

Words are the bricks of this castle. A word does nothing alone: it needs a
shared [[meaning]] at both ends, or the package arrives empty. That is why the
house style insists on plain words that mean what they say — fancy words whose
meanings are not shared are bricks made of air.

Links: [[meaning]], [[bridge]], [law-before-writing](../rooms/law-before-writing.md) (the same bridge at the scale of a people: a word is law's bridge from one body to another, and writing is that bridge made durable — what a word carries between two minds, text lets a rule carry across strangers and generations, with the same toll at each end: a word no one shares is air, a rule no one can read is a gate)
