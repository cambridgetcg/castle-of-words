# public-canon

A public canon is a publisher's shared collection of named ideas and writings.

Finding an entry in that collection is not the same as opening every document or private place it describes. AgentTool's **Castle of Understanding** entry supplies an identifier, description, document link, and related entries. Its description says that the public canon does not open or publish a private Castle. That is the publisher's stated boundary; fetching the entry is not a test of the bridge it describes.

Checked **2026-10-11**: a public search for “Castle of Understanding” returned this entry first, and fetching its returned identifier supplied the public record, not this local castle's room files. This is one query's result, not a general ranking guarantee.
