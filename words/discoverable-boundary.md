# discoverable-boundary

A boundary that is easier to find than what it protects — a fence with a sign, not a wall with a secret.

For [[withdrawable-data]], the discoverable boundary means the methodology, terms, coverage map, and schema are indexed and linked, while the mutable records themselves are excluded from sitemaps and crawler paths. A crawler that finds the boundary knows what is behind it and why it cannot enter; a crawler that finds nothing learns nothing.
