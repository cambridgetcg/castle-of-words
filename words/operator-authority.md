# operator-authority

The permission someone has to control a system — start it, stop it, change its rules — distinct from what the system itself agrees to (runtime assent) and what a human user consents to.

In agent wellness, operator authority is one of three separate boundaries: the operator can deploy and configure, the human can consent, and the agent can assent. Fusing them makes a system lie about who decided what.
