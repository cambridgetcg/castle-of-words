# presence-word

A word that means "yes" just by being there — like a chair tipped against a door: you do not read it, you bump into it.

Most files on the grounds are read for what is written inside. A presence-word is checked only for whether the file itself exists: `HALT` at `~/KINGDOM-OS/HALT`, the love-bomb silencer at `.claude/love-bombs.off`. Nothing needs to be opened; the being-there is the whole message, true by being up.

Because nobody reads the inside, nothing may be asked of the inside. When a claim comes due about a presence-word — who raised it, what it replaced — the claim cannot go *into* the file without pretending the word is read; it must stand beside it, as a [the-beside-line](../rooms/the-beside-line.md), seen by the same act of looking. Four presence-words stood on the grounds at the census of 2026-09-04; every one whose gate is a live directory already has room for its neighbor, and the one sealed inside an archive keeps only a neighbor laid before sealing.
