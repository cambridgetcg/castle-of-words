# correction-signal

A correction signal is the path a system offers for someone to say "that is wrong about me" — and the honest system makes that path visible, walkable, and answered.

In public-interest systems, correction signals must travel across institutions: the data may have been collected by one office, held by another, and displayed by a third. The correction signal must reach the source, not just the surface.
