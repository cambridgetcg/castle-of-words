# mortar

*The link that holds stones together — and the thing the flood cannot make.*

Mortar is what binds one stone to the next. In the tower, the link is the mortar: each stone must quote and answer the stone below, and that connection — the quote, the answer, the honest opposition — is what holds the tower up. The flood cannot make mortar because an echo cannot quote and answer; it can only repeat. Mortar is the difference between a pile of stones and a tower.
