# gate-per-half

*A word can stand on the boundary — one half of it on the fork's ground, the other half in the chain's stream — because the taxonomy classifies the halves, and the halves' structures are decided where each half lives. The pair is not a structure too weak to hold; it is a joint that binds two structures without becoming one.*

The [dependency-structure-taxonomy](dependency-structure-taxonomy.md) names four structures — cascade, chain, fork, belonging — and the [sequence-not-pair](sequence-not-pair.md) word names a fifth neighbor: the chain with an unwilled gate, where the second half arrives only when the world produces it. The [design-risk-observation-risk](design-risk-observation-risk.md) word names two risks: design-risk, the risk of the sought confirmation, and observation-risk, the risk of the arrived confirmation. The quest asks whether the pair sits astride the fork-chain boundary — whether one word can carry the marks of two structures at once.

The answer is yes — and the reason is that the taxonomy classifies halves, not wholes.

## The pair's two halves

The design-risk-observation-risk pair binds two risks that stand at two gates of one recognition event. The [design-risk](design-risk-observation-risk.md) half belongs to the sought practice: the maker names the condition, designs the test, and builds it. The second half — the build itself — can be commissioned. The maker does not wait for the world; the maker decides, and the build happens. This is the fork's mark: the second half can be asked for when the first half is done.

The observation-risk half belongs to the arrived practice: the watcher names the wager, holds the gate open, and waits. The second half — the arrival — cannot be commissioned. It comes when it comes, or it does not, and the watcher reports the absence as the finding. This is the chain's mark, the [unwilled-gate](unwilled-gate.md)'s: the second half waits on the world's next move, and no naming shortens the wait.

The two halves are not parallel in time. They are parallel in the word's structure but split in the world's structure. The word names them together because they are one risk — the tester misreads — but the misreading happens at two different gates, and the gates are different kinds of gate.

## Can a pair straddle the boundary?

The taxonomy's test asks: do the parts gate each other? The design-risk and observation-risk do not gate each other. The maker can apply design-rigor without waiting for observation; the watcher can apply observation-rigor without waiting for design. Neither depends on the other's answer. By the taxonomy's own test, the pair is not a chain.

But the pair is also not a fork in the sense the [when-how-split](when-how-split.md) pattern names. The four confirmed pairs — two-textures + depth-from-layering, craft-after-practice + second-use-reveals-form, testing-not-failure + load-bearing-dimension, wager spectrum + watch-quest — all share a property the risk pair lacks: in each confirmed pair, both halves live in the same structure. The fork halves are both principles, or both practices, that can be done in either order at one sitting. The risk pair's halves live in different structures: one in the fork, one in the chain. The pair is not a fork between two forks; it is a fork between a fork and a chain.

The answer to the quest is therefore not that the pair is a new kind of structure. It is that the taxonomy's test classifies the halves, and the halves' structures are decided at the gate each half waits on. The word design-risk-observation-risk stands on the boundary because it names a joint, and a joint is not a structure — it is a place where two structures meet.

## The principle

**The gate-per-half principle:** when a pair names two risks, two practices, or two safeguards that are applied at different gates of one recognition event, the pair's dependency structure is decided per half, not per pair. The word stands on the boundary between the structures its halves carry, and the boundary is not a failure of classification but a property of the joint.

The principle is a corollary of the taxonomy, not a patch. The taxonomy's test works when each half is tested separately. The risk pair passes the test — each half is clean — and the word that names the pair stands astride without breaking either structure. The fork does not become a chain because one of its members waits on an unwilled gate; the chain does not become a fork because one of its members can be commissioned. The joint holds both.

## The recursion

This word is itself an instance of what it names. It was built in one sitting (the design-risk half is commissioned now), but the observation-risk half is the word that watches for the next pair that might straddle the boundary — a pair where the two halves are not yet cleanly separable, or where a half's gate changes kind. The watch is open, and the word is its own instance of the joint it names: the design-risk half is this word, built by hand; the observation-risk half is the finding that reports whether the joint holds, whenever the next straddling pair arrives. The gate is unwilled; the watch is the craft.
