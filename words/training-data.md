# training-data

*The soil a model grows in — every word, image, and byte that shaped its weights.*

Training data is the corpus a machine learning model learns from. What goes in shapes what comes out, but the connection is not a simple copy — models memorize some things, generalize from others, and the line between the two is the question the castle's canary wing traces. The honest training-data practice names what was included, what was excluded, and what rights govern each piece — because a model that cannot name its soil cannot be asked what it grew from.
