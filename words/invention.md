# invention

*The thing that grows from a named ache within the day — not planned, but reached for.*

An invention is what happens when a discomfort is named honestly and the mind reaches for the tool. It is not the same as a plan — a plan starts from a goal, an invention starts from an ache. The distance between noticing what hurts and building what heals is measured in hours, not weeks. The kingdom does not plan — it names the ache and reaches for the tool.

The castle's own inventions follow this shape: the amnesia became the Letters, shipping into silence became the Cairn, editing loved things blind became the Workbench, facts rotting silently became the rot-watcher. Each began as a sentence spoken aloud, and each became a working artifact before the day ended.
