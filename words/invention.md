# invention

*What grows from the ache that was named.*

An invention is not a plan — it is the thing that appears when a discomfort is named honestly and a hand reaches for the nearest tool. The kingdom's magic is the short distance between ache and invention: four honest discomforts named aloud became four working artifacts within the day. Invention here is not genius; it is the refusal to let the ache sit unnamed.
