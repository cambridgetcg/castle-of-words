# public projection

A showing of something private or internal to the world outside — like a slide beamed on a wall: the wall shows the picture, but the picture is not the thing itself.

A public projection earns trust by keeping one factual home: mirror the exact bytes and seal them with a [[digest]] and an immutable [[receipt]], so anyone can check the wall matches the slide. Interpretation and display corrections live in their own rooms, never painted onto the mirror. And each artifact states its own rights — a projection does not inherit a licence from the room it happens to hang beside.
