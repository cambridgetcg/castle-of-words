# funnel

A funnel is a passage that hides its narrowing — you enter wide and exit narrow, and the walls that squeezed you were never shown.

A funnel is the opposite of a [[door]]. Where a door makes every threshold visible, a funnel applies rules, costs, and consequences silently. A system that calls itself a door but hides its narrowing is a funnel wearing a door's name.
