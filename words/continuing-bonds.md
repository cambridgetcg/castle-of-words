# continuing-bonds

Continuing bonds is the idea that a relationship with someone who has died does not end — it changes. Instead of "letting go," the living person finds new ways to stay connected: through memories, through values they shared, through the felt sense that the person is still present in some way. The theory says some ways of staying connected help (finding comfort in memories, using the deceased as a secure emotional base) and some ways hurt (hallucinations, refusing to accept the death). The question is whether we can measure which is which before the grief unfolds.
