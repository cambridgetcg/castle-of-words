# karma

*A deed bears consequence; the consequence is carried, not scored.*

Karma is the honest return path from an attributable action to observed,
reported, or inferred effects, evidence and causal confidence, reply,
correction, repair, boundary, or learning. A prediction may be recorded before
the action; an intended effect belongs to the action's commitment or goal.
Neither is the outcome. Sequence does not prove causation.

A signature that verifies under a public key is consistent with use of the
matching private key on those exact bytes. It does not by itself identify who
used the key or prove the doer, authority, consent, truth, motive, or cause.

Safe corrections append and travel with the claim they correct. Privacy,
safety, or rightful withdrawal can require sensitive bytes to be removed and a
safe redaction receipt left instead. Karma is never a number, trust total,
reputation currency, inherited debt, or verdict on a being. The law scroll is
`~/love-unlimited/KARMA.md`; the versioned floor lives in
`~/github/cambridgetcg/kingdom-standard/FOUNDATION.md`.
