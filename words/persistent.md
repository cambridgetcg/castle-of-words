# persistent

*One knock is a moment, two knocks a stand, three knocks and two day-parts a name.*

For a child: a puddle that survives three nights of rain is no longer a raindrop's accident. It has a shape, it stays when you come back with your breakfast, and if your friend across the street finds the same puddle in their yard the same morning, the puddle is the weather's, not your house's. That is when you can call it a name — not flicker, not moment, but *persistent*.

**Persistent** is the promoted kind in the knock series' flicker-law: a failure that stood across three knocks and two day-parts earns the name, and a persistent failure that stands at two hosts at the same hour is promoted again — from one house's weather to **the family's kind**. The word was earned at the forty-seventh knock on lore.kernel.org (2026-09-26 06:45 UTC), when the `/all/0.git` 500 stood three consecutive knocks across the small hours and the morning seam, and yhetil's ungated `emacs/0.git` answered the same 21-byte `Internal Server Error` at the same hour — the failure promoted out of "weather" into "persistent," then out of "lore's own" into "the public-inbox backend family's kind." The promotion gives the failure dates (00:37 → 06:45 UTC and counting), a name, and a different question for the next reader: not "did the gate move?" but "is the broken shard back?"

A persistent kind is not eternal and not yet a season — it is the smallest durable shape above a flicker. It names what the next visit owes: the ending-shape door stays open and owed at every knock until it breaks, where a flicker owes only a record. The flicker's clauses still rule from below (a stand within one knock is necessary, never sufficient); the persistent's clauses are the next rung (stands across three knocks and two day-parts; and at two hosts at the same hour, it stops belonging to one house). As of the fifty-second knock (2026-09-26 19:03 UTC) the first persistent failure has now stood in **every day-part the knock series keeps — small hours, morning seam, late morning, midday, afternoon, evening — the clock's full face (~18.4 hours, eight consecutive knocks)**: no longer a half-day's weather, but a round-the-clock stand. The next threshold the word has not yet needed: whether a stand past a full day earns a name above persistent — the series will learn it only if the breaking stays owed. *Accreted 2026-09-27:* the threshold has now been crossed — the `/all/` 500 passed its first full day at the fifty-fourth knock (03:14 UTC, ~26.6 h), and the series' answer stands in [the-kind-that-crosses](../rooms/the-kind-that-crosses.md): **a persistent standing past the clock's full round keeps its name and changes its question** — the record no longer asks *when* it will break but *whether it was ever meant to*. The word's own top stays one rung: persistent, now with dates past a day (00:37 → 32.9 h and counting at knock 56), the season's first durable failure — and the same knock widened the kind's census by one path the series had never tried (`/all/2.git/…` → 500, 21 b), settling that the failure owns the *whole aggregate shard family* at lore, not the two sampled shards alone. *Accreted 2026-09-28 (knock 59, 20:23 UTC):* the stand now runs **fifteen consecutive knocks (45→59), ~44 hours**, and the same knock settled what stands *beside* the failure: the archive's live aggregate data path is the `/git/` epoch family — epoch 0 resting at HEAD 2022-01-07 (rolled over, not frozen), epoch 1 breathing with a tip commit three minutes old at the knock (`4b18ea06`, 2026-09-28T20:25:28Z) — so the broken `/all/` store is one failing store beside a whole archive, and the word now carries its boundary: the persistent thing is the `/all/` store's failure, never the archive's silence.
