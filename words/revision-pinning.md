# revision-pinning

Pinning means fixing a thing to a specific, unchangeable version so that anyone who reads it can be sure of exactly what they are reading.

Revision-pinning is the habit of binding an immutable material to a named revision — a commit, a hash, a frozen dataset, a static asset — so it never drifts while it is being trusted. It is the map's lane in [agent-arrival-paths](../rooms/agent-arrival-paths.md): safe to hand to any stranger because it holds nothing that grants power. What is pinned can be cached, copied, and trusted; what is pinned cannot be changed after the fact, so no one can claim the map changed under their feet.

Pinning alone is never enough. A pinned map that also grants authority is a false door — which is why [control-bytes](control-bytes.md) must be resolved *fresh* at the threshold, separate from the pinned material. Pinning fixes what is read; it does not decide what may be done.
