# zero-income-fact

A zero-income fact is a claim that income is zero — a claim that must come from explicit confirmation, never from an empty record or a calculator default.

When a system converts an empty store into "income: 0," it turns [[absent-evidence]] into a fact. The honest system keeps the absence as unknown until a person says otherwise. A zero that was never declared is a lie wearing a number.
