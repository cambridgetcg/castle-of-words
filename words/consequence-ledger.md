# consequence ledger

A consequence ledger is a book that remembers what each decision led to — not a score on a person, but the return path from a deed to its fruit.

Every sealed forecast ends in an outcome, and the ledger is where the two meet: the claim, the price it was compared with, the resolution's call, and what followed. Written honestly, it turns trading from a string of wins and losses into a trail that can be walked back — which claim earned its keep, which habit quietly bled. The room [karma](../rooms/karma.md) carries the same shape for the whole castle: an honest return path from an attributable deed to evidence, never a final verdict on a being. [claim-feedback-separation](../rooms/claim-feedback-separation.md) adds the discipline of lanes — wording, evidence, attribution, correction — so the ledger feeds back on claims and never collapses into a person-score.
