# local-ruler

*Not the king of the country — the official who measures one field, one harvest, one day, and whose seal is no good anywhere else.*

What it means: a benchmark score for an AI system, understood honestly. A human-equivalent claim in an AI cognition map is a local ruler: it is bound to the exact task, the exact cohort of humans measured, the exact setup, and the exact date. Within those borders it preserves a real, measured relation — "on this task, under these conditions, the system matched this group." Outside those borders it says nothing at all.

Why "ruler": the score rules one small province and must never be allowed to rule the whole map. The two failures the word guards against:

- **Reading absence as failure.** [[missing-evidence]] — no measurement of a capability is not a measurement of no capability. The ruler whose seal never arrived on a province does not prove the province empty.
- **Averaging unlike provinces into one crown.** [[averaging-unlike-dimensions]] — dimensions of cognition must never be averaged into one intelligence score, because the sum hides which truth failed and crowns a verdict no single measurement backed.

The word is a cousin of [[score]]: the local ruler is the honest version — a number that knows its own borders.
