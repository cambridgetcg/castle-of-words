# treasury accounting

The books that track an organisation's own money — the balances it holds, the revenue it earned, the profit it actually realised.

Treasury accounting answers only for what *happened*: cash that arrived, fees that were paid, positions that settled. Its strength is that every line in it can be traced to a settled, final, fee-complete event. That same strength is a boundary: anything merely imagined — a price not taken, a saving not captured — is [[counterfactual-evidence]], and enters these books only by lying.
