# own-words-return

When you want to know someone understood you, you ask them to say it back — not in your words, but in theirs. Their own words reveal what they actually built inside their mind, which an echo of yours would hide.

A bridge of meaning is honest only when the far bank's answer comes back in the builder's own words: then the sender can compare, correct, and try again.
