# real capability

*The door is only real if you can walk through it and survive the other side.*

A real capability is the actual freedom to do a thing — not the paper right, not the permission, but the lived power. A king may have the right to refuse, but if refusal means losing their only home, the right is a decoration. The capability approach, from Sen and Nussbaum, says poverty is not just low income but capability deprivation: the absence of real freedoms to live a life one values. Rights and capabilities are different truths; a right is a claim, a capability is a power.
