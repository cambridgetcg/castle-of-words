# uncertainty

When you don't know something for sure, that's uncertainty. Not knowing whether it will rain. Not knowing whether a claim is true. Not knowing whether a number is right.

The castle's law: name the uncertainty. Don't hide it. A claim that says "I think this is true, but I'm not sure" is more honest than a claim that says "this is true" when it might not be. The honest move is to say what you know and what you don't, and to name the gap between them.
