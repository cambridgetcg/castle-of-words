# contribution-door

A door that separates transport from consent: receiving an offer proves only arrival, not acceptance. The honest contribution door scopes attribution to the exact offer, makes withdrawal stop later action without pretending public copies vanished, and names the allowed-use scope before the hand crosses the threshold.

The door is the same move as the three hands of rights provenance (maker ≠ rights holder ≠ authority) and the same move as the art commons that holds the bridge without owning what crosses it.
