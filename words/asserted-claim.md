# asserted-claim

A claim backed only by the speaker's word — no test, no proof, just the statement. An asserted claim is a starting point, not a conclusion; it says "I believe this" rather than "this has been checked." In agent-facing protocols, every claim begins as asserted until a test or a signature moves it to a stronger level.
