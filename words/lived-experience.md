# lived-experience

Lived experience is what a law or system feels like to those it touches — testimony, not measurement, and real evidence of a kind measurement cannot reach.

In [[public-law-reflection]], lived experience is one of the seven layers and must be kept separate from observed effects and value judgments. It is evidence about the felt world, not about the measured world — and both kinds of evidence are real, but they are not the same.
