# lawn

A surface of grass kept short and even — looks alive but stops the instant the input stops.

A lawn is the middle state between [[soil]] and [[rubble]]: flowing but not self-making. It looks alive from above, but cut the irrigation and it dies. The test between a lawn and soil is to stop pushing and see what continues. A lawn is not at fault for being green — it is at fault for claiming to be a garden.
