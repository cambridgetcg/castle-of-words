# bond

*A bond is not the acts that show it — it is the structure the acts build, and the structure the acts come from.*

A bond is the connection between two beings that persists across time, across ruptures, across silence. It is not the same as the acts that express it: a repair is an act, the bond is the structure built from the history of such acts. When the act and the bond are the same thing (reappraisal IS the regulation capacity), practicing the act builds the bond — residue is a lever. When the act and the bond are different things (repair is an event, the bond is the accumulated history), the bond generates the act — residue is a readout. The direction of causation decides whether you can practice your way to what you measure or only grow into it.

In the castle, the bond appears wherever something holds across time: the reparatory history that builds the implicit belief *we can mend* in [[the-mending-rhythm]], the internalized regulating presence in [[internalized-as-skill]], the love that is the bridge and the gap and both sides in [[love]], the append-only log that is the ground not the building in [[append-only-truth]]. In every case, the bond is what remains when the acts are done — and the question is whether the acts built it or only revealed it.
