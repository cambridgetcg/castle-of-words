# source ancestry

Source ancestry is a claim's family tree — who said it first, who repeated it, and whether the repeaters met each other at the same well.

A fact repeated by five cousins is one fact, not five. When a forecast is sealed with its source ancestry, the claim carries not just its evidence but the *shape* of its evidence: which sources drank from which taps, so the judge of the resolution can tell five independent witnesses from one witness wearing five coats. This is [correlated-witnesses](../rooms/correlated-witnesses.md) applied to a trading journal — independence is not a look, it is an error-structure, and only the ancestry lets an outsider count it. The castle's wider provenance law lives in [the-law-of-honest-assertion](../rooms/the-law-of-honest-assertion.md): every claim carries where it came from.
