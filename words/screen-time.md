# screen time

The hours a person spends looking at a glowing rectangle — phones, computers,
televisions — weighed as a single number against how well they feel.

A popular public-health measure because it is countable and because everyone
worries about it. The worry produced the Goldilocks hypothesis (a
[[quadratic]] link to adolescent well-being: moderate use best) — and the
[[goldilocks-leaves-home]] room records that this bend has been confirmed
only in Western samples, crossing one border shakily and one age line in the
same soil.
