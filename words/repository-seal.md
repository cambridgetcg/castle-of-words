# repository-seal

A repository-seal is a badge pinned to the dataset itself — at the repository, riding with the DOI — rather than to the article that describes it.

The article badge (Kidwell et al. 2016) sits on the paper and says: *this journal expected sharing, and this author complied*. The repository-seal says: *this dataset met a bar, and the dataset keeps carrying that proof to everyone who ever reuses it*. The difference is where the signal lives. An article badge travels with the article; the seal travels with the data.

Repository certification schemes already exist — CoreTrustSeal, the Data Seal of Approval, the TRUST Principles — but they certify the *repository*, not the dataset. A dataset-level seal, verifiable per-DOI, has never been tested as a behavioural intervention.
