# standing permission

A yes said once, meant to hold for many future visitors — like a gate left unlocked with a note that says exactly who may come in and how far.

It becomes usable across agents that have not been born yet only when the machine's front door names the exact skill, and the skill states when to use it, what stays out of scope, who verifies the result, and how to leave the off-switch alone.
