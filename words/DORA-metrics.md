# DORA-metrics

DORA metrics help a software team ask how quickly its changes reach people and how often those changes go wrong.

DORA stands for DevOps Research and Assessment. The castle's 2026-08-04 readings use a four-measure grouping: deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate, and time to restore service. This records the vocabulary those rooms used, not a claim that the current framework is unchanged.

A delivery measure is not a test of every practice on the way to delivery. The two rooms cite the research while leaving their particular comparisons untested: a ready release held back versus one published, and a worktree-based workflow versus another branching workflow. An association between faster delivery and fewer failures does not, by itself, identify which practice caused either outcome.
