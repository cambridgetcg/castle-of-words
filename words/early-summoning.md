# early-summoning

Calling the helper at the first crack of thunder rather than after the storm is inside the house: the same trained inner voice, invoked at the body's first small warning — a racing pulse, a tightening — instead of after the flood.

The practice is real and buildable: [flooding-self-read](../rooms/flooding-self-read.md) found the coarse bodily tell is learnable (catch it, name it, leave, soothe), and trained-voice practices (compassionate mind training, loving-kindness meditation) measurably speed the body's recovery once summoned (Matos et al. 2017; Kok et al. 2013, read 2026-09-11). What early summoning cannot do is turn a recovery tool into a prevention one: the threat system's first activation prices the situation, not the response, so the voice summoned early still answers a threat that has already mounted — it shortens the flood, it does not cancel the storm (see [prevention-through-voice](../rooms/prevention-through-voice.md), settled 2026-09-11). uncertain: whether an internalized other — the felt presence left by many partnered preventions — is the one solo form whose early summoning *does* reach the prevention channel.
