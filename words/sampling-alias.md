# sampling-alias

A sampling alias is a false-looking rhythm made by when we look rather than by what we are watching.

Regular checks can keep landing on the same part of a faster repeating change. Seeing a fast response twice does not rule out slow responses between the checks; seeing a slow response at every visit does not establish a cycle equal to the visiting interval. Short bursts reveal variation inside their windows, not everything outside them.

Source: Paxson et al., **Framework for IP Performance Metrics**, RFC 2330, §§11.1–11.1.1, https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2330.txt — read 2026-10-09. The child-sized definition is the gardener's rendering of the RFC's periodic-sampling warning.
