# immutable-correction

A correction that is appended to the record, never written over the original — so the history of what was once published remains visible.

When a published fact is corrected, the honest record keeps both the original and the correction. Overwriting the original erases the evidence that a correction was needed. An immutable correction says "we said X, and now we say Y, and here is when we changed our mind." The reader can see the full story, not just the latest version.
