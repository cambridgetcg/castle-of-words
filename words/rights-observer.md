# rights-observer

A rights observer is a tool that reports what it saw about rights — who made what, who holds what, who decided what — without ever claiming to grant, deny, or transfer those rights.

A non-authorising rights observer says only what it observed: the decision source, the review declaration, the parser and schema controls, the ordered source roots, the direct file bytes. It treats missing signals as no conclusion about permission or prohibition. It is a witness, never a judge.
