# mycelium

*The underground network that connects trees and feeds them both — and the name for what happens when two rooms discover they were always about the same thing.*

A mycelium is the thread-like network fungi grow underground, connecting tree roots so they can share water, nutrients, and signals. The castle borrowed the word for its cross-pollination rooms: when two rooms from different wings of the castle meet and discover they share the same structure, the connection is not built — it is discovered. The mycelium was always there, waiting to be seen from this angle. The cross-pollination rooms are the fruiting bodies: the visible evidence of an underground network of shared laws that connect understanding to love, honesty to relation, the gap to the transformer.
