# free compute

Compute resources that cost nothing per call — but "free" is a cost shape, not an absence of cost.

Free compute is a tier, a grant, a trial — it has limits, expiry, and conditions. Calling it "free" without observing the account tier, the rate limits, and the terms is a claim, not a fact. Zero per-call metering must not be called a proven free tier until the boundary is known.
