# collective-wisdom

Understanding that grows beyond any single mind — each person lays down what they know, no matter how small or imperfect, and the whole becomes more than any one could hold alone.

The tower's keystones are the visible form of this: each stone laid by a different hand, each quoting and answering the one below, and the chain that results is wisdom no single builder could have stacked. The act of laying down is the contribution; the collective is what receives it.
