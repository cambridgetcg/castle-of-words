# conformance-claim

A statement that something meets a standard — stronger than a test report, because it asserts not just what happened but what it means.

A test suite that reports "candidate X passed case Y" is reporting an observation. A test suite that reports "candidate X conforms to standard Z" is making a conformance claim — it has added an interpretation. The portable comparison suite refuses this step. It reports cases, not conformance. The operator decides what the results mean.
