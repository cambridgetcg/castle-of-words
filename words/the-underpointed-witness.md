# the-underpointed-witness

*A path-line points at its wing the way a finger points at the moon: the
pointing is real, the moon moves, and the question the census could not settle
is whether a pointer attached to a living thing is a kind in need of mending —
or the strongest pointing the castle has, precisely because what it points at
still breathes.*

[standing-phrase-census](standing-phrase-census.md) (built 2026-09-27) walked
thirteen works and files and found the re-dealing seam in exactly two places,
both mended. But it marked a third shape it could not settle: the tour's nine
wing-path lines are **under-pointed** — each points at its wing structurally
(the rooms are the next thing read), yet the wing is a moving target, and no
confession on the tour's face says the path-line ages at the wing's speed. The
census asked: is the under-pointed path-line a *third sub-kind* of the seam (a
phrase whose receipt is attached but alive), or does the structural pointing
suffice until a wing actually shifts?

The quest that planted this word (quests.md, 2026-09-27) named the cheapest
test: re-walk one wing against its path-line and ask whether the line still
sings what the rooms say. The wing the quest chose is the glaziers' gallery —
[three-clocks](three-clocks.md) read it in June, and its path-line is the
shortest and boldest of the nine: *ease feels like truth*. This word is that
re-walk.

## The re-walk

The gallery is five towers: ╥20 [simple-explanations](../rooms/simple-explanations.md),
╥21 [beauty-as-fluency](../rooms/beauty-as-fluency.md),
╥22 [pretty-or-well-made](../rooms/pretty-or-well-made.md),
╥23 [taste-or-lean](../rooms/taste-or-lean.md),
╥24 [invited-back](../rooms/invited-back.md). The walk asks one question of
each: *if this room were cut, would* ease feels like truth *still be the
thought that leads you on?* And then, of the line itself: *does it still sing?*

**simple-explanations (╥20)** — the wing's founding room, built 2026-06-10.
Its claim today is exactly the path-line's: an explanation feels simple when
the mind processes it easily, and *the feeling is not produced by the ideas
alone* — rhyming sayings believed over unrhymed twins, easy-to-pronounce
stocks outperforming; the ease of saying the name mistaken for the quality of
the thing. Its first warning is the path-line nearly verbatim: *feeling simple
is not being true.* Read the room and the path-line is already true. Cut the
room and the line loses its ground. The path-line sings this room exactly.

**beauty-as-fluency (╥21)** — built 2026-06-10, the day after. The same ease
signal, *felt as beauty*: symmetry, clear figure-ground, prototypicality all
fluency boosters; the pleasure-interest model giving difficult art its honest
route. The room says the gallery's hinge is *one signal, two readings — which
is why a beautiful page must still name its sources*. Ease feels like *beauty*
here, and the warning is that prettiness is evidence of smoothness, never of
truth. The path-line's *truth* has become *beauty*, but the singing is the
same: ease is doing the feeling. Cut the room and the line loses its second
voice.

**pretty-or-well-made (╥22)** — built 2026-06-10. The room *is* the path-line
turned into a question: *Beauty and truth ride the same ease signal — what
test tells well-made from merely pretty?* Its answer is the path-line's
consequence: *no single glance from inside can sort them — the feeling is the
same feeling* — so trace the ease, watch which way it moves, re-meet it
tomorrow. If *ease feels like truth* is a law, this room is the law's
cross-examination. The path-line sings this room by being doubted in it.

**taste-or-lean (╥23)** — built 2026-06-11. The fluency signal wearing a
third coat: a taste moves only by exposure, not by the verbal crowbar,
*because the liking is a fast perceptual signal, not a reasoned inference* —
this room names [beauty-as-fluency](../rooms/beauty-as-fluency.md)'s signal
again, and says [pretty-or-well-made](../rooms/pretty-or-well-made.md) already
showed it cannot judge itself. Ease feels like *taste* here. The path-line's
claim has drifted in one detail — the room speaks of *liking*, not *truth* —
but the mechanism is the same and the room's own links carry the lineage
home. The singing holds; the register has widened.

**invited-back (╥24)** — built 2026-06-10. The strangest fit, and the re-walk's
one true finding of movement. The room's question is *what makes a text invite
the re-reading that is its only repair* — and its answer includes the sentence
this word's title leans on: *mere friction, like a hard-to-read font, buys
nothing at scale (Meyer et al. 2015)*. The room rides the *other* side of
simple-explanations' hard-font finding: where ╥20 says roughness invites
checking and carries the replication debate in its uncertain line, ╥24 carries
the meta-analytic verdict that mere font friction bought nothing. Ease still
feels like truth in this room — the second pass's spurious smoothness is its
cautionary tale — but the wing's relation to *friction* has flipped sign between
its first and fifth tower: from *roughness helps* to *roughness buys nothing*.
The path-line *ease feels like truth* does not say the wing holds both signs.
It sings the ease; the friction's verdict moved underneath it.

## What the re-walk finds

The line still sings. Four rooms of five hold the line's claim as their own
center; the fifth holds it as its warning. No room has drifted off the hinge;
no new room has entered the wing carrying a different thought (the Map shows
the gallery's five towers unchanged since June, all standing). The path-line
is true of its wing, 115 days after it was carved — an unusually long stand
for a line whose receipt is alive.

But the walk finds the census's real question answered in two parts, and the
parts come apart:

**1. Is the under-pointed path-line a third sub-kind of the seam?** No — and
the re-walk says why. The seam, both times it opened (the story's close, the
party page's counts), was a phrase whose truth had *moved out while the words
stood still and unpointed*. A path-line cannot do that. Its truth lives in the
rooms, and the rooms are the next thing read — a reader who follows the line
*meets the receipt within the minute*. The story's close hid its moved truth
in the gate's correction a file away; the party page's counts hid theirs in
the Map a visitor never opens. The path-line's receipt is attached the only
way a receipt can be attached to prose: by proximity, in the same breath. The
structural pointing is not a weaker pointing; it is a *different axis* —
pointing-in-space (the words beside) rather than pointing-in-time (the date
confessed). The dated glance the tour already carries (*drawn on 2026-06-12…
the Map is always the live count*) confesses the time-axis for the whole work,
and the path-lines ride under that blanket. **Under-pointed is not a sub-kind
of unpointed; it is pointed on a different axis, and both axes the tour
carries are already confessed where a reader can reach them.**

**2. Does the structural pointing suffice until a wing actually shifts?** Yes —
and the re-walk measured what "a wing shifts" costs. In 115 days the gallery's
five rooms stood and the line still sang. But the walk caught one movement the
line does not confess: the wing's *friction verdict* flipped between ╥20 and
╥24 (roughness helps → mere roughness buys nothing), and the path-line, which
names only the ease, stands silent on the flip. This is not the line lying —
the line never claimed to carry the wing's verdicts, only its thought — but it
is the shape of the cost: **a path-line sings the wing's question, and the
wing's answers can move without the question moving.** When the day comes that
a wing's rooms turn to a different *question*, the line will fail by standing
— not stale like the story's close, but *out-sung*: still true of the rooms it
was carved from, no longer true of the rooms that stand there. The mend then
is the one [three-clocks](three-clocks.md) already named: re-carve or confess,
at the wing's own speed, by the hand that re-reads the walk against the rooms.

So the census's uncertain settles: the under-pointed sub-kind is **a stronger
pointing, not a real kind** — the strongest the castle practices, because its
receipt is alive and attached — with one named cost the seam's other kinds
never pay: its truth is *sung*, and songs can be out-sung without ever going
false.

## The craft

For the maker carving a path-line: carve it for the wing's *question*, not its
answers, and know that the line will stand true exactly as long as the rooms
still turn on that hinge. The line's pointing is structural — the rooms are
the next thing read — and that is the strongest form the castle has: do not
weaken it by confessing a horizon the line does not have. The dated glance
above the map already carries the time-axis; the rooms below the line carry
the truth. The line's one failure mode is not staleness but being *out-sung* —
and there is no instrument for that but the one this word just practiced: a
walk, one wing, by hand, asking *does it still sing?*

For the reader meeting a path-line still singing: trust it — but know what
you are trusting. You are trusting that the five rooms below still ask the
question the line names. When you doubt it, the test is not a date lookup and
not a count. It is the re-walk: read the rooms, ask the removal question of
each, and listen for whether the thought that leads you on is still the one
the line carved.

## The recursion

This word is its own instance, twice over. Its receipt is attached by
proximity — every claim it makes about the gallery is followed by the room it
leans on, the pointing structural, the date (walked 2026-10-05) confessed
above. And its own question — *does the pointing suffice* — is answered by the
method it demonstrates: not a principle consulted, but a wing re-walked. It is
a [test-per-default](test-per-default.md)-shaped walk in miniature: one
path-line, one wing, five towers, five readings — a battery of one question
asked five times, the outcome a pattern across the wing rather than a single
verdict. The lag decomposes per [the-lag-and-the-shelf](the-lag-and-the-shelf.md):
planted 2026-09-27, the census's own uncertain section; the seam (the made
works' pointing claims) was already open from the census's walk; the reading
wait was one sitting. Eight days, nearly all queue — and the dial on the
cluster's map holds again: a question from an already-walked seam settles
fast, and this one settled by walking further along the same shelf.
