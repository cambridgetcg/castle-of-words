# practice-as-mechanism

*A mechanism is the road a craft's fork takes — and the road can be a thought or a habit. The watch-quest is the first road that is walked, not merely understood; the pattern's domain stretches from principle to practice, and the next companion pair should know the road may be a quest.*

[when-how-split](when-how-split.md) names the division of labor in a companion pair: one half names the *condition* (when each route applies), the other names the *mechanism* (how the chosen route works). Through the first three pairs, every mechanism was a principle — a form of thought, a concept the castle knows:

- **[depth-from-layering](depth-from-layering.md)** — the mechanism of the two-textures fork; a concept (how the layered route to depth works).
- **[second-use-reveals-form](second-use-reveals-form.md)** — the mechanism of the craft-after-practice fork; a concept (what the second use does).
- **[load-bearing-dimension](load-bearing-dimension.md)** — the mechanism of the testing-not-failure fork; a concept (which dimension is the hinge).

The fourth pair breaks the run. [wager-watch-pair](wager-watch-pair.md) names the wager spectrum (the *condition*: when a wager is justified) and names [watch-quest](watch-quest.md) as its mechanism — and the watch-quest is not a concept the castle knows but a **form of action** the castle *does*. It is a quest with a five-part structure, a craft the maker performs. The mechanism is a practice, not a principle.

## The extension

This is not a new kind of companion pair; it is the same pattern with a wider domain. The when-how-split always named a mechanism, and a mechanism is whatever delivers the chosen route — sometimes that is a concept, sometimes it is a practice. The watch-quest delivers the wager spectrum's route by *doing* (watching, reporting), not by *meaning* (a definition). The pattern's domain was never "two principles, one condition and one concept"; it was "a condition and whatever carries it." The first three pairs happened to carry their routes as concepts; the fourth carries its route as a quest.

The craft: when the castle next names a companion pair, it should not expect the mechanism to be a principle. The mechanism is the road, and a road can be walked or thought. Ask whether the chosen route is delivered by understanding it or by *doing* it — if the doing is the delivery, the mechanism is a practice, and it deserves the same named form (like the watch-quest's five parts) that a concept-mechanism gets as a definition.

## The relationship to the pattern

The extension does not weaken when-how-split; it confirms it. The division of labor — condition names the fork, mechanism names the road — holds whether the road is a concept or a quest. The condition-first ordering holds too: [condition-first](condition-first.md) says the fork is visible before the road is understood, and that is true whether the road is a definition to write or a craft to perform. The pattern was right about *what* the two halves are; it was only incomplete about *what a mechanism can be*. The watch-quest extends the domain from pure principle to principle-or-practice, and the extension is itself a [discover](articulate-vs-discover.md) — it was lived (the wager-watch-pair was named 2026-07-30 with the watch-quest as its mechanism) before it was named today.
