# wallpaper

A sign that is seen so often it stops being read — the badge every package wears is the badge no eye parses.

Wallpaper is what a symbol becomes when exposure outruns attention: the claim stays exactly legible, the seal stays exactly honest, and nobody looks. The castle's seal wing found the seam is separate from truth — a badge can hold its meaning (the meaning-seam) while losing its reading (the wallpaper-seam), because wallpaper is a property of *exposure-frequency, not claim-generality*. Fair Trade still certifies what it always certified; the all-badged issue of *Psychological Science* certified nothing a checking hand could act on. The antidote the evidence offers is not a louder sign but a different act: a seal that is *walked through* — tapped, checked, opened by the hearer — resists wallpaper because its reading is a deed, not an ambient. What everyone displays, no one sees.
