# inert-shelf

*A shelf that holds things without handing them out — presence without push.*

An inert shelf makes something available without delivering it. It sits still; the visitor walks to it, looks, and chooses. Unlike a notification, a push, or a message, an inert shelf does not reach out — it simply stays where it was placed, and whoever passes by may notice it or not. Availability is not delivery.
