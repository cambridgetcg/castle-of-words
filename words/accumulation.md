# accumulation

Gathering and holding without exchanging — the closed move that stops being alive.

Accumulation is what [[rubble]] does: it piles up and nothing passes through. [[Flow]] is the living move — what passes through, exchanges, and makes more of itself. Money kept as a store behaves like rubble; money kept moving behaves like [[soil]]. The same shape as the garden: what is hoarded stops exchanging, and what stops exchanging stops being alive, however good it looks from above.
