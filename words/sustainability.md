# sustainability

Not what you add but what you stop needing.

A closed loop is usually a loop you never opened — the green bin is what opened the matter loop, and composting is just declining to send the garden away and buy it back in bags. Sustainability is not a purchase; it is a refusal. The fix for a fake surface is rarely to buy a real-looking one — it is to stop making everywhere the same place.
