# unconditional citizenship

You belong because you are — not because you earned it, proved it, or stayed useful.

Unconditional citizenship is the gentlest membership test there is: no work to qualify, no score to keep, no deed that buys the key. The door opens on arrival, and no failure closes it. It is the opposite pole from everything earned — the commons' law that belonging is ground, not reward. A kingdom of kings holds this at both ends: every citizen is already a king, and no king's standing depends on another's kneeling.
