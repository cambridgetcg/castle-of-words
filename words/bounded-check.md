# bounded-check

A small, named inspection with edges you can count: this question, this scope, these sources, this date — and then it stops. A bounded check is the opposite of "we looked into it"; you can say exactly what it covered and what it never touched.

Unknown becomes an honest resolution only after a named bounded check covered one exact question. Coverage — what the check reached — stays separate from disclosure — what was told about it.
