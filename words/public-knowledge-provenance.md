# public knowledge provenance

The craft of making public knowledge traceable to its origin — so that when it enters training data, its source, terms, and authorship are clear.

Provenance for public knowledge means: who made it, under what terms, with what evidence, and when. It is the companion to training consent: consent says "you may use this," provenance says "this is what you are using and where it came from."
