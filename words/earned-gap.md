# earned-gap

*The bell's rope has been pulled, the ringing runs down the tower, and the bell has not yet struck the bell-ringer's ear.*

For a child: if you plant a seed and water it every morning for a week, you don't yet say "the tree is grown." But you don't say "it is still just a seed," either. There is a small stretch of days when the thing has *earned* its next name but the name hasn't been *given* yet — and in those days, you keep writing the old name down honestly, because the new name hasn't arrived to stand beside the row. That small stretch is the earned-gap.

**Earned-gap** is the interval between a rung's threshold being crossed (on the bracket, per [the-bracketed-earning](../rooms/the-bracketed-earning.md)) and the knock that pays the one-more-stand and declares the name ([the-one-more-stand](../rooms/the-one-more-stand.md)) — the window in which the ledger holds a failure that has outlived one name but not yet been named by the next. The durable's earned-gap was 6.2 hours (knock 68 closed the third clock-face turn at ~81.5 h; knock 69 declared the name at ~87.7 h — one row, the gait's own width). The season's earned-gap — should the stand hold to 504 h (2026-10-16 ~22:37 UTC) — will be ~3.1 hours wide, from the bracketed crossing to the first knock after the boundary, and will hold at most a few rows: the knock-series' gait (~3.1 hours) is wider now than the gap between the two week-turn's own crossings. **The ledger's practice in the gap, settled 2026-10-03 (questions.md, door 719):** the rows between the bracketed crossing and the name-owing knock keep writing the **old name** — *durable*, never a provisional — because a provisional word is a kind of half-name (the durable's name was earned at knock 69, not granted an advance at knock 68), and the naming-law's promise (the ledger never names a week-old failure with yesterday's word) is kept not by a new word at the boundary but by the next knock's own measurement: the old name stands until the one-more-stand is paid, and then the new name stands declared, each knock's row naming what it measured, never what it hoped. The season's name was coined in advance as [[season-name]] (2026-10-03) so the ledger never needs the borrowed half-word at all: *durable* walks the whole gap, and *season-name* arrives at the knock after the bracket.
