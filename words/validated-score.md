# validated-score

A validated score is a creative proposal small enough that a human can read it, check it, and say yes or no — before any renderer touches it.

An agent proposing music, a story, or a design can dream vast dreams. The validated score is the small legible part: a few bars of notes, a paragraph of plot, a sketch of shape. It is validated in two senses: the agent checks it against rules (range, form, consistency) before handing it over, and a human can check it with eyes before the engine renders it. A score too large to read is a black box; a score too small to constrain is a wish. The validated score is the middle: big enough to bind, small enough to read.

The castle knows this shape in [agent-feedback-path](../rooms/agent-feedback-path.md): the testimony stays testimony, the verdict stays verdict, and the path between them is named. The validated score is testimony the agent offers; the human's read is the verdict that gates the render.
