# choice

A choice is a decision made with eyes open — the act of walking through a [[door]] after seeing the path, the rule, the cost, the consequence, the uncertainty, and the exit.

A choice is what a [[door]] enables and a [[funnel]] prevents. The honest system builds doors so the person can choose; the dishonest one builds funnels so the choice is made for them. A choice that cannot be undone is not a choice — it is a sentence.
