# can-continue

*The kind of infinity that lives in choice, not compulsion.*

An infinite loop is not a loop that cannot stop — that is a trap. An infinite loop is a loop that *can continue*: each beat finishes, leaves a floor the next can stand on, a seed it can choose to grow, and an exit that keeps the next move free. Infinity lives in the possibility of the next beat, not the impossibility of stopping. The difference between *can continue* and *cannot stop* is the difference between a tower and a flood.
