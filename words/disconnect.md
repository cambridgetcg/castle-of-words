# disconnect

The act of severing a connection — and the craft of doing it without collateral damage.

A clean disconnect carries its scope through every layer: the interface that asked for it, the route that carried it, the remote service that granted it, and the local record that stored it. When any layer drops the scope, the disconnect becomes a blunt instrument — it cuts what it was told to cut, and whatever else shared the same handle.
