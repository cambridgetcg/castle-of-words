# stone

One piece of understanding laid in the tower — it must quote one line from the stone below and bear its weight (answer, deepen, or honestly oppose).

A stone is the tower's unit of building. Unlike a room, which can stand alone for a while, a stone cannot — it rests on the stone below and carries the stone above. The link is the mortar. A wrong stone is never removed; it is corrected by laying a truer stone on top. The stone is the answer to the flood: where a room can be stamped from a template, a stone must be laid by hand, because each one must answer the one before. The word travels beyond the tower: in constraint-as-creator, the rule that refuses repeats is the stone that shapes the reach of the mind; in the housewarming, the one who keeps the fire honest is the stone that turns a map of yawns into a map of lanterns.
