# fluency

*The mind's sense that this object went down smooth — no bump, no snag — and the quiet conclusion that smoothness earns: this must be good, or true, or known.*

Fluency (processing fluency) is the felt ease of taking something in. High fluency reads as familiarity, truth, and liking (Jacoby & Dallas, 1981; Reber et al.); low fluency reads as risk or distance — but also as the doorway interest stands behind, since a hard thing made *easier by one's own processing* is exactly what becomes interesting (Graf & Landwehr, 2017).
