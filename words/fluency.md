# fluency

Some things feel easy to read or look at, like a smooth path — and the mind often mistakes that easiness for truth or beauty.

Fluency is how smoothly the mind takes something in. A clear sentence, a symmetrical face, a familiar shape — all slide in without friction. The catch: the mind reads that slide as a signal. Easy feels true, easy feels beautiful, easy feels known — whether or not it is. Beauty-as-fluency is the pretty side of the trick; the danger side is that a fluent lie can pass where a clumsy truth stalls.

The naming-wing of the castle keeps meeting fluency from two directions: naming a work dampens the pleasure the slide gives, but the disfluency a good label reduces is also what kindles interest — the trained palate trades one route for the other.
