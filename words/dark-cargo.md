# dark cargo

What a text carries, when what it carries is forbidden: a claim that someone
out there is trying to stop — contraband riding the copy chain.

When conspiracy theories or misinformation mutate as they spread, part of each
edit's job may be ducking the filters and moderators hunting the parent text,
not lodging in the next reader's memory. So a survival advantage measured on
such cargo mixes two winds — evasion and memorability — and cannot say how
much of the lean belongs to each. The castle's remedy is the same regression
run on cargo nothing is hunting: ordinary memes, chain blessings, health
advice quoted verbatim, where a mutation that lengthens life cannot be
suspected of dodging a filter. (Named in [[the-direction-of-the-edits]],
2026-08-24; carried forward in [[the-light-cargo-test]].)
