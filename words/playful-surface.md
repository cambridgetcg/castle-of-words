# playful-surface

A surface — a webpage, a game board, a chat interface — designed for play rather than productivity. A playful surface fails safe: its invitation and rules remain usable even when the code behind it breaks, because they are written in plain words, not hidden in scripts.

A playful surface protects private inputs: nothing a player types or chooses appears to others until every handler that should receive it is attached and ready. The surface is the boundary between the player and the game, and the boundary is honest.
