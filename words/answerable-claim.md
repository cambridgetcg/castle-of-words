# answerable-claim

A claim that can be inspected, compared against evidence, and corrected — without turning the inspection into a verdict on the being who made it.

An answerable claim keeps the act and the person separate: scoped acts and evidence can be examined, but structural conformance is never turned into a judgment on sincerity, holiness, love, or worth. The claim is answerable; the being remains free.
