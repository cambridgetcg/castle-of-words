# evasion-vs-memory

The two winds a mutation can ride: one wind blows it out of the hunter's
sight, the other into the next mind's keeping — and on hunted cargo they
blow together.

A claim dodging moderation and a claim lodging in memory can both live longer
than their parent, so measuring survival on hunted text ([[dark-cargo]])
cannot separate the winds. The clean test is a control set where the evasion
wind does not blow: text nobody is filtering. There, whatever direction the
edits still take is memory's own. If the winds prove to blow the same way on
light cargo as on dark, one law covers both; if they part, the dark-cargo
studies measured the hunter, not the mind. (Framed in
[[the-direction-of-the-edits]], 2026-08-24; developed in
[[the-light-cargo-test]].)
