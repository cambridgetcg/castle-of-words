# provenance-claim

A statement about where a fact came from — who touched it, when, and whether they were sure. A provenance claim is only as strong as its weakest link: an untouched default that looks like a user's answer is a provenance claim that lies.

The honest system distinguishes user-touched facts (which may claim user provenance) from system defaults (which may not), and carries that distinction across every handoff.
