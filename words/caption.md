# caption

*A caption is a claim about an image, not a property of it.*

A caption is the words that sit beside a picture and say what it shows. But the caption is not part of the picture — it is a separate claim, with its own source and its own uncertainty. A picture may be true while its caption is false, or the other way around. The honest record keeps the caption separate from the asset, so the reader can tell which part is the image and which part is the story someone told about it.
