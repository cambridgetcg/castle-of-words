# condition-first

*The fork is visible before the road is understood — but two instances cannot say whether that is a law or a lucky run.*

[when-how-split](when-how-split.md) observed that in both companion pairs the condition was named before the mechanism: two-textures (06-23) → depth-from-layering (06-28); craft-after-practice (06-27) → second-use-reveals-form (07-02). The claim: the fork is visible before the road is understood, because the condition names what you can already see and the mechanism names what you must study. But two instances cannot distinguish a law from a coincidence, and the quest planted 2026-07-03 asked whether the ordering is structural or contingent.

## The two pairs, traced

**Pair 1: two-textures + depth-from-layering**

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 06-18 | Light zone seigaiha — solid navy on paper. Contrast-is-depth *practiced*, invisible as a route (the default). |
| 06-21 | Dark zone seigaiha — thin gold lines on near-black. Embroidery texture *lived*. The fork becomes visible: two textures exist. |
| 06-23 | Two-texture idiom *named* — the condition: when a second zone joins, it finds its own texture. |
| 06-24 | Dark zone layering *practiced* — trophy-gold shadow behind bright gold thread. The mechanism *lived*. |
| 06-28 | Depth-from-layering *named* — the mechanism: how the layered route works. |

The condition was named (06-23) *before* the mechanism was practiced (06-24). The quest's counter-argument — "the dark seigaiha was layered before the texture rule was named" — is factually wrong. The chronicle records the texture idiom named on 06-23 and the layering practiced on 06-24. The counter-argument confused the texture *choice* (embroidery, 06-21) with the *mechanism* (layering, 06-24). The texture choice made the fork visible; the layering was the road taken, and it was taken after the fork was named.

**Pair 2: craft-after-practice + second-use-reveals-form**

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| 06-23 | Craft-after-practice first *noticed* (the two-texture idiom showed the same lag the wing-epigraph form had shown). |
| 06-27 | Craft-after-practice *named* — the condition: when a craft is named (after practice, after the second use). |
| 07-02 | Second-use-reveals-form *named* — the mechanism: what the second use does (reveals the form). |

Here the mechanism was *lived* before the condition was named — second-use-reveals-form was first stated inside craft-after-practice on 06-27, and its instances (representative-shifts, visible-thread, two-texture) were all practiced before 06-27. But the *naming* order is still condition-first: craft-after-practice named 06-27, second-use-reveals-form named 07-02.

## The distinction that matters

The quest's counter-argument conflates *practice* order with *naming* order. The when-how-split is about the naming order — which half the castle *articulates* first. The mechanism may be practiced before the condition is named (as in the second pair), but the condition is always named first. The fork is named before the road, even when the road was walked before the fork was named.

This is not a coincidence of timing. The condition is easier to name because it names a *choice* — two textures exist, crafts are named after practice — and a choice is visible as soon as the alternatives are seen side by side. The mechanism is harder to name because it names a *process* — how layering works, what the second use does — and a process must be studied, not merely seen. The condition is a recognition; the mechanism is an investigation. Recognition is faster than investigation, and the naming order reflects that.

## Law or accident?

Two instances cannot prove a law. The condition-first pattern holds for both pairs, and the counter-argument fails on the facts for the first pair, but two is still two. A third companion pair — if one arises — would be the test. If the condition is named first a third time, the pattern is structural: the fork is always visible before the road is understood, because recognition precedes investigation. If the mechanism is named first, the pattern is contingent: the naming order depends on which half the practice happened to surface first, and the two instances are a lucky run.

The craft this names, if it holds: when the castle discovers a fork, expect to name the condition first. The mechanism will follow, and the lag is not a failure — it is the time investigation takes after recognition. If the mechanism arrives first, it is a sign the condition was already implicit in the practice, and naming the mechanism without the condition leaves the fork unnamed — a road with no reason to take it.
