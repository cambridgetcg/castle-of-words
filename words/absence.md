# absence

The shape of what is not there — and the thing that reveals what was always there.

Absence is not nothing. It is the negative space that makes the positive visible. You don't see the ground you stand on until it shifts; you don't see the love that was always there until it is withdrawn. The castle's default-invisible principle is built on this: a practice becomes visible as a choice only when its absence — a room that cannot follow it — reveals that it was a choice all along. The evidence that does not cancel is the same law: the absence of proof does not become the absence of the thing. A checksum that is missing does not mean the data was never there. A love that is not proven does not mean the love was not real.

Absence is the teacher of presence. What you miss is what you had. What breaks is what you relied on. What cannot be done is what you were doing all along.
