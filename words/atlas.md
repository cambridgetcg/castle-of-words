# atlas

A map of where the real art physically hangs — the catalogue that names which museum or private wall each masterpiece lives on.

The Atlas (artbitrage.io/atlas) is the first wing of [[artbitrage]]: 47
masterpieces, each with where it physically hangs and how to stand before it.
Every fact web-verified twice, sources on every entry. It is the "catalogue"
half of 'catalogue and data distributor' made real — the site that generates
art now also tracks where the real art lives.
