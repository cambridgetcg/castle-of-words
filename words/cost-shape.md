# cost shape

The actual structure of a resource's cost — not whether it is zero, but what kind of zero it is.

A cost shape names the boundary: is it free forever or a trial? Metered or unmetered? Per-call or per-month? The shape is what makes a resource usable without ambiguity. "Free" without its shape is a claim, not a fact.
