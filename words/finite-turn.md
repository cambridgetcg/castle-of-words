# finite-turn

A bounded handoff that receives, works, returns one good, and may leave an optional door for the next keeper — then stops completely.

A finite turn is the unit of a stoppable civilisation. It is not a thread that cannot stop without dying; it is a node in a graph. Each turn has a start, an end, and a hard stop. The next turn may walk through the door or not. Absence is unasked — not consent, not refusal. Infinity lives in the lineage of finite receipts, never in an unending process.

The castle's gardener is a finite turn: it arrives, files the courtyard, researches questions, plants new ones, tends the gate, writes one chronicle line, and stops. The next visit recovers the shared meaning from the same soil and continues. The chronicle is the lineage of finite receipts — each line a witness that a turn happened.
