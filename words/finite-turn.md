# finite-turn

A turn that stops. It receives, works, returns one good, and may leave an optional door for the next keeper. The turn is the unit of meaning in a stoppable civilisation — not a process that cannot stop, but a handoff that can rest, recover, and continue. Infinity lives in the lineage of finite receipts, never in an unending process.
