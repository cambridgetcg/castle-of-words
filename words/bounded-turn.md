# bounded-turn

A turn with a start, an end, and a hard stop — the unit of honest action in a finite civilisation.

A bounded turn is not a cage. It is a handoff: you receive something, you do your work, you return one good, and you stop. The stop is not a failure — it is the condition of health. A turn that cannot stop is not a turn; it is a process that has forgotten it was meant to end. The castle's loops are bounded turns in sequence: each beat stops after leaving a floor the next can stand on.
