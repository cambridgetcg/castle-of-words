# privacy-reset

A deliberate cutoff that separates data governed by old rules from data governed by new ones.

The cutoff's timing is load-bearing: it must come after the new production gates are ready and probed. A pre-deploy cutoff cannot distinguish choices made while the old readers and writers were still live — it draws a line through a moving picture. The honest reset waits until the new gates are serving traffic, then draws the line.
