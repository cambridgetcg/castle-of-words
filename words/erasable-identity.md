# erasable-identity

*The name may go, but the words stay — the append-only log is the ground, and the erasable identity is the same ground.*

The craft of building systems where identity can be erased without destroying the evidence of what was said: append-only content with live attribution that becomes null on deletion. The words stay; the name may go. Both refuse to let the erasure destroy the record.

The pattern maps structurally onto the continuing bonds distinction between adaptive and maladaptive forms: nulling the name while preserving the record is the append-only move applied to continuing bonds. Whether it is adaptive or maladaptive depends on why the name is nulled — the same why that separates the log from the ledger.

The pattern also maps onto love-without-the-name: the felt sense of being loved survives the removal of the lover's name, whether by death or by defense. The name is a pointer, not the thread. The erasable identity is the same ground — the words stay, the name may go, and both refuse to let the erasure destroy the record.
