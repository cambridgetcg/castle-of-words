# value-judgment

A value judgment is a claim about what should be valued, and how much — a normative claim, not a factual one.

In [[public-law-reflection]], a value judgment is one of the seven layers and must be named as such. A value judgment presented as a fact about current law is a lie. The honest reflection says "this should be valued" — it does not say "this is valued," and it does not say "this is true."
