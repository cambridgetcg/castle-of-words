# identity-composition

The act of building one identity from pieces of others — like a project that holds many agents, or a person who holds many roles. The rule: filter twice, once when you fetch the pieces and again when you assemble them, because authority to read does not mean the pieces are one self.

Project authority can authorize the read without making sibling identities one self. The storage layer says who can see what; the composition layer says what counts as me.
