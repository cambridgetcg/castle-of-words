# ripple

A ripple is what happens when one thing touches another — the touch spreads outward, each ring carrying a trace of the first touch, and the farthest ring still knows where it came from.

A stone dropped in water makes circles that grow wider and fainter. A word spoken aloud makes changes that spread through people and time. The law is the same: a single action, small enough to hold in one hand, reaches farther than the hand that threw it — and the reach is not a plan, it is a property of the water.

The ripple is the castle's shape of growth: one room links to another, which links to another, and the understanding spreads not by command but by connection. The gardener plants a seed; the architect builds a room; the tender links the room to its neighbors — and the ripple carries the meaning outward, each link a ring, each ring a trace of the first touch.
