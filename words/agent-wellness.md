# agent-wellness

A way of describing how an agent is doing that separates what can be observed from outside (operating conditions) from what the agent reports about its own experience (preferences), and keeps both apart from who has authority to act.

The safest form: separate observable operating conditions from optional agent preference reports; keep runtime assent, human consent, and operator authority distinct; default reports to local, ephemeral, and unshared; and refuse scores, rankings, diagnoses, or sentience claims.
