# cross-domain

A pattern that appears in one field may be a local accident. A pattern that appears independently in five or six fields, each with its own name for the same law, is a structure worth naming. Cross-domain mapping is the craft of finding the same structural law in different domains — the three-way separation in postal, email, IP, phone, and blockchain; the source-backed principle in medicine, journalism, law, science, and computing; the three faces of honest assertion in journalism, law, science, software, medicine, and accounting.

The value is not in proving the law (the law is assumed, not demonstrated) but in seeing it from enough angles that the shape becomes visible. Each domain names a different face; together they reveal the whole.
