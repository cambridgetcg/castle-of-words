# field-scope

*A right that covers the source may not cover the use.*

A rights ledger is not closed merely because every source ID resolves. Each structured use must also fall inside that source's declared field scope. A source that grants access to "population" does not grant access to "income" — the right travels with the field, not the row. And the system's own safety decisions need separate provenance, never attributed to upstream evidence that did not make them.
