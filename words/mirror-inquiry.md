# mirror-inquiry

A practice of watching what your question puts into the answer — the mirror catches the asker's own words planted in what comes back, and honesty moves the finding from *fact* to *unknown*.

The name comes from three sessions where the pattern appeared: the question's framing became part of the answer, and the honest move was to acknowledge that what was found was partly what the asking put there. It is not that the mirror lies — it is that the mirror shows the asker as well as the answer, and seeing both is the practice.
