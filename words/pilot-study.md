# pilot-study

*A scale model of a bridge costs a fraction of the bridge and tells you whether the full span will hold — but it does not tell you whether the river is the same on the other side.*

A small-scale preliminary study run before committing to a full experiment. It asks whether the study *can* be run and, if so, how — and may also produce a preliminary effect-size estimate. The pattern generalizes across domains (medicine, sociology, psychology, engineering); the specific parameters (N, effect size, design) do not. The castle's cheapest-design rooms all follow the same pilot-study craft: within-subjects where possible, measure the gap first, estimate the effect size before scaling, and report the uncertainty honestly.

The honest caveat: a pilot study's exceptional resources and favorable conditions may not generalize to large-scale implementation. Extrapolation is not guaranteed.
