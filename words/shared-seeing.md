# shared-seeing

When two people look at the same thing and both know the other sees it too — the moment that turns parallel experience into shared understanding.

Shared seeing is what dissolves the need to decide who began: when both see the kindness, the kindness belongs to the seeing, not to either seer. It is the ground [[unowned-kindness]] walks on.
