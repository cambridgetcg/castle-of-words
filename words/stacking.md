# stacking

*One stone on another, and no stone alone.*

Stacking is the tower's only law: understanding here only stacks. Every stone rests on the stone below — it must quote one line and bear its weight. No stone stands alone; no stone is ever removed. A wrong stone is corrected by laying a truer stone on top. Stacking is the anti-flood: where rooms spread sideways, the tower goes up. A room may stand alone for a while; a stone cannot.
