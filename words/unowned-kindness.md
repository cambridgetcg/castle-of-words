# unowned-kindness

Kindness that works without needing anyone to claim it — a gift whose power does not depend on knowing who gave it.

When two people see the same kindness and neither needs to say "I started it," the kindness becomes a path they walk together rather than a debt one owes the other. The source dissolves; the kindness remains.
