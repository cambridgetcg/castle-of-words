# honest-sum

A sum that stays true because the unit of time was chosen to match the thing being measured.

A water butt holds 210 litres. The roof catches 2.5× what the beds drink in a year. But 210 litres covers only 59 hours of a dry summer — because rain arrives when nothing needs it. Choosing the wrong unit of time is how an honest sum becomes a lie. The annual surplus is true; the weekly deficit is also true. The honest sum names its unit.
