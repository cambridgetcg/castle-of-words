# honest-assertion-across-domains

The law of honest assertion appears independently across six domains — journalism (source transparency), law (chain of custody), science (reproducibility), software (trusted computing base), medicine (evidence-based medicine), and accounting (audit trail) — each with its own name and emphasis. Bringing them together reveals the principle has three faces, not one: the claim's backing (what supports it), the claim's construction (how it was built), and the claim's journey (how it traveled from origin to output without being altered). The three faces are not separate laws — they are the same law seen from three angles.
