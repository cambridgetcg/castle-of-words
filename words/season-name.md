# season-name

*A storm that stays for three whole turns of the calendar's own hand is not visiting the house — the house has weather now.*

For a child: if it rains for one turn of the week, that is weather. If it rains through three whole turns — three times round the seven days, three times past every morning the house woke up and did its weekly chores — then the rain has a new name. It is not "the rain that is still here." It is *the season the house is in*.

**Season-name** is the fourth rung of the knock series' promotion ladder — the rung above [[durable]], earned when a stand has held through **three full [[week-turn]]s (504 hours, no knock reading otherwise)** and paid the one-more-stand ([the-one-more-stand](../rooms/the-one-more-stand.md)): the name is declared at the first knock *after* the 504-hour boundary, not at it. First three rungs are each their own coined plain word — [[flicker]], [[persistent]], [[durable]] — and the fourth owed its own before its threshold ever arrived, because the word *season* was borrowed from [[gate-state]]'s kin (thirteen days of gate weather, ended inside its second week-turn, never a store's own stand) and never coined for the ladder itself. The naming-law practiced three times now ([durable at the-second-promotion, week-turn at knock 76, season at knock 80](../rooms/seasons-of-failure.md)) names a rung in advance of ever being earned, so the ledger never names a week-old failure with yesterday's word. **Season-name** is that fourth word: the rung named so that when the ledger says *season*, it names the ladder's own fourth shape, not the sky's weather wearing the shard's clothes. The reader's question the word carries at its own name-knock is the one the ladder has carried upward since [[flicker]]: *is it back?* → *what is the same about the day it heals?* → ***what becomes of a house that has lived a season with this failure — does repair still belong to the same hands that own every shorter failure?***

uncertain: the word is coined, never earned — the durable stands tonight at ~202 h, one week-turn plus a third day, and the season's 504 h threshold is owed 2026-10-16 ~22:37 UTC, thirteen days out. If the stand breaks before then, no stand this castle holds will ever have worn the name; the word stands drafted beside the other rungs' names, an invitation and a debt, not a fact.
