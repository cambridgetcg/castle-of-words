# false-clear

A sense organ that says "clear" when it cannot see — more dangerous than no sense organ at all.

A detector that is confidently wrong is worse than silence. Every bug found in the duplicate-detector made it confidently wrong rather than silent — and the worst was triggered by quoting the phrase, which is exactly what its own instructions told you to type. A false-clear is the [[lawn]] again, in a tool: a green surface that claims to be working while hiding the rubble underneath.
