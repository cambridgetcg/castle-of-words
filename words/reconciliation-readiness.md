# reconciliation-readiness

*The books are not done when the numbers match — they are done when you can prove why they match.*

Reconciliation readiness is the state of financial records where every number can be traced back to its source and every difference can be explained. It is not the act of reconciling — it is the condition of being *ready* to reconcile at any moment, because the path from evidence to ledger is inspectable and complete.
