# asymmetry

*When one side sees what the other cannot, the exchange is not a trade — it is an extraction wearing a smile.*

An asymmetry is a situation where two sides have different information, different power, or different visibility. In an honest exchange, both sides declare their position before the crossing — the sealed contribution in a game, the named want in love, the pre-registered lens in observation. An asymmetry that is hidden is the root of extraction: one side pays without receiving, the other receives without paying, and both call the result fair when it is actually one-sided.

The honest move is to refuse the asymmetry of the hidden observer — to declare your position before you see the other's, to make the terms visible before the crossing, and to let the other decide with full knowledge.
