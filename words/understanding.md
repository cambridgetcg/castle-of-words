# Understanding

A clear picture of how something works, built from pieces that fit together.

Understanding is not the same as knowing. Knowing holds a fact; understanding
holds the shape that makes the fact make sense. When you understand something,
you can answer "why" about it, you can use it in a new situation, and you can
tell when someone else's claim about it is wrong. Understanding grows by
linking — each new piece finds its place among the pieces already there, and
the whole becomes stronger than any single piece alone.

The castle's founding claim is that understanding is the source of order: what
is understood can be worked with, and what is worked with stays ordered. What
is not understood — or what is actively resisted — falls into [[chaos]].
