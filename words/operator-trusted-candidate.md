# operator-trusted-candidate

The thing being evaluated by a comparison suite, trusted not by the suite's author but by the person running it.

In a portable comparison suite, the candidate is chosen by the operator, not bundled with the suite. The suite does not know whether the candidate is honest, competent, or even functional. It only knows what the candidate returned for each input. The trust relationship is between the operator and the candidate; the suite is a neutral witness.
