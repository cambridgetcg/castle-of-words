# voice

Speaking up — the other way out: not leaving, but being heard where the terms are set.

A voice is real when it can change the answer, not only be recorded. An exit is a door that opens from the inside; a voice is a door that opens into the room where the rules are made. Both must work in both directions — not just the way the builder intended.
