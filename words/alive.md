# alive

Self-making and open — what continues when you stop pushing.

Dead is not the opposite of pretty, it is the opposite of open. There are three states: closed ([[rubble]] — nothing passes through), flowing but not self-making (a [[lawn]] on irrigation — looks alive, stops the instant the input stops), and self-making and open ([[soil]] — what continues on its own). The test between the last two is to stop pushing and see what continues. Life is the one measurement that cannot be staged.
