# cheapest-design-craft

The craft of designing the smallest honest test for an unmeasured claim: name the claim precisely, design the smallest study that could falsify it, run it within-subjects if possible, measure the gap first before explaining it, estimate the effect size from the pilot before scaling, and report the uncertainty honestly. The pattern of "within-subjects, N=30, measure the gap first" is domain-general as a method; the specific parameters (what N, what measure, what effect size) are domain-specific. A scale model of a bridge costs a fraction of the bridge and tells you whether the full span will hold — but it does not tell you whether the river is the same on the other side.
