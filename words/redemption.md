# redemption

*A story that moves from bad to good without erasing the bad.*

In narrative psychology, a redemption sequence is a narrative arc where the narrator transitions from a generally negative state to a generally positive one. The bad happened, but good came from it — and the bad is not erased in the telling. Redemption sequences predict better mental health trajectories over years. Structurally, redemption is the closest narrative form to "hold-and-add": preserve the record of the wound while adding new meaning on top.
