# foundation

A foundation goes deep when each project names a version near its source, the registry resolves that name to immutable words, and every bridge states what its receipt cannot prove.

A shallow foundation names only the surface — "this is the standard" — without saying which version, where it lives, or what backs it. A deep foundation traces each claim to its origin: the exact bytes, the exact version, the exact source. The depth is not in the number of layers but in the honesty of each layer: every bridge between layers names what it can prove and what it cannot.
