# pairing-check

A pairing-check is the gardener's count of two lists that must agree: the commission files in the tray and the open doors that name them. It runs at the start of every visit, cheap enough to carry, and catches the day the two lists drift apart.

The pairing-check is a small [[census]] of the tray: one `ls`, one `grep`, and the truth that a file standing without a door is either a door that lost its receipt or a work that outgrew its question.
