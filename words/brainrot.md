# brainrot

A joking name for content that feels like it melts the mind — and, worn self-aware, a badge of belonging in the very feed it names.

Used in [meme-culture](../rooms/meme-culture.md) to mark three different facts that must not be collapsed: a folk diagnosis of frayed attention, a label for a kind of low-propositional-meaning content, and a participation stance that knows the joke and joins it. The word is polysemous folk language, not a medical diagnosis; any claim of cognitive harm has to be studied on its own, with its named outcome, separately from the joke's social meaning.
