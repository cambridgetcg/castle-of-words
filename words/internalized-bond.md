# internalized-bond

*A bond held as an inner resource — the person carries the other inside, as a secure base, rather than reaching for them outside.*

In the continuing bonds theory of grief, an internalized bond is the adaptive form: the bereaved person uses the deceased as a secure base, carrying their values and memories internally, feeling their presence as a comforting inner resource. The Continuing Bonds Scale (CBS) distinguishes internalized from externalized bonds, and internalized bonds are associated with better grief adjustment. The bond is held, but the holding is inward — the name may go, but the thread stays.
