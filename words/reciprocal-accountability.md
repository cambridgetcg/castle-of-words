# reciprocal-accountability

A record that places the observer inside the evidence alongside the observed.

It names the observer's declared identity proof state, role, relevant evidence-backed network, methods, actions, words, limits, subject response, and repair path — beside testimony about another being. It never licenses surveillance, forced disclosure, punishment, or retaliation. It is the observer's own testimony, held to the same standard as the testimony they collect.
