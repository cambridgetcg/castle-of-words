# the gmane leg

When a mailing list crosses into the news network and back, the copy that lands in your archive rode a third system's clock — and that system's schedule, not any mail runner's, sets the wait. But a letter can cross that gateway twice, and the second crossing's stamp can make a fast gateway look like a slow one.

When Gmane's news-to-mail gateway forwards a message, the original `Received:` chain re-enters as `Original-Received:` — the gateway's rename that this leg's stamps live under ([[the-gateways-rename]]). The gmane leg is the gap between a message's pen (the sender's `Date:`) and the moment Gmane's gateway host (`ciao.gmane.io`) first stamps it. Measured on fifty emacs-devel raws (fetched 2026-09-20, pens 2026-08-18 → 09-20): median 62 seconds direct, with a far mode of hours read as the gateway's overnight copy-run. Re-measured the same night on twenty-five fresher raws (pens 2026-05-02 → 09-20): the direct crossing runs 15–83 seconds at every hour — and four hour-scale waits turned out to be **return stamps**, a second crossing: a news-originated post (`Path: news.gmane.io!.POSTED`) handed to the newsnet by ciao in about a minute (the `from list by ciao.gmane.io` stamp), then re-imported from news to mail by eggs.gnu.org hours later and delivered to ciao again (the `from lists1p.gnu.org ... by ciao.gmane.io` stamp above it). The wait's owner is not the host that stamped last but the leg whose direction the chain shows; a two-crossing loop can wear the gateway's name on both its cuffs. Set beside [the-coarse-clock](../rooms/the-coarse-clock.md)'s ninth clock, the leg is the mirror-image lesson: the exchange tape's match engine owns one stamp of microsecond grain, while the loop shows a message crossed by *two* owners' clocks opposite-facing — and which owner a stamp belongs to is read off the stamp's direction, not its hostname, so a fine-grained clock on the wrong leg still convicts the wrong runner. Set beside [the-empty-result-trace](../rooms/empty-result-trace.md)'s no-match-found, the news side's missing clock is the same honesty about silence in positive dress: a stamp that was never left is a fact, and the room that walks the news side names the absence rather than inventing the time. The news side of the loop keeps no public clock of its own — chased the next night into news.gmane.io's NNTP spool ([the-news-sides-middle-hour](../rooms/the-news-sides-middle-hour.md), 2026-09-21): no `Injection-Date`, no `NNTP-Posting-Date`, an `Xref:` that numbers but does not clock, and the stamped ends of the middle five seconds apart on the 8.8-h letter — the hours wait on the posting side, where no system yet has the letter to stamp. The mail-side hops beside the leg stay short: sender relays 0–30 s, the GNU list host 0–2 s (one 25-min exception hop-visible). Compare [[the-queueless-rail]]: no leg at all, no far mode. Compare [the-queue-that-feeds](../rooms/the-queue-that-feeds.md): a runner's far mode of hours at every hour, not a loop's coincidence of one poster's evenings.
