# attribution-ledger

A record of who used what source and when — not a judgment of whether the use was correct, only that it happened.

An attribution ledger is like a library's borrowing record: it says "this person checked out this book on this date." It does not say whether they understood it, whether they cited it correctly, or whether their use was lawful. Those are separate questions. The ledger's honesty lives in what it refuses to infer: it records the fact of use, not the quality of use.
