# afterplay

Afterplay is what happens after a game ends — not a new round, but a re-weaving of what was already given, using only the pieces the players brought, adding nothing new, and letting the ending stay final.

Where [[bounded-play]] draws the boundary that makes play safe, afterplay deepens the play that already happened without breaking the boundary. It recombines only what players already gave, adds no new input, and lets reset remain final. The game is over; afterplay is the echo that makes the game mean more without pretending it never ended.

Afterplay is the same law as [[coordination-without-command]] in a different key: the chain of gifts that built the game now runs backward, each hand re-examining what it received, and the shared ending carries traces of everyone without ranking anyone.
