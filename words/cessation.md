# cessation

The exact moment a duty ends — a date, not a fade.

In tax compliance, cessation needs a precise date so the final notice and quarterly update obligations remain visible. Without it, the system loses sight of its own boundary — it cannot tell whether a taxpayer has left or is simply not filing. The same shape appears wherever a system needs to know when something stopped: a service retired, a key rotated, a claim expired.
