# emergency-information

Information designed for crisis: a separate, static shelf where the leave-now action comes first, every claim binds to reviewed exact bytes, and the exception lives inside the guide whose default advice could become dangerous.

Emergency information is not ordinary information with urgency added. It is a different kind of thing — the reader is scanning for escape, not browsing for understanding. The honest emergency page puts the exit first, works offline, names what is not live, and places warnings inside the old paths that could lead to harm.
