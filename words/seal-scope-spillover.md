# seal-scope-spillover

*The lamp that checks only the signature still throws light across the whole canvas.*

When a maker works under a seal that checks only one narrow thing (provenance, not quality), does the carefulness stay inside the checkable parts, or does it spread to the whole work? The answer the evidence leans toward is: it spreads, but not evenly — arousal generalizes while strategy stays scoped. The seal's felt presence (worry, arousal, self-consciousness) does not respect the boundary of what is checked; the maker's deliberate choices about where to spend effort do. So the maker grows cautious everywhere in feeling, but re-budgets effort only in the parts the seal can see.

The direct test has never been run.
