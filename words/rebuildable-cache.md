# rebuildable-cache

A rebuildable cache is saved material a program can make again without losing your work.

The word *rebuildable* is something to verify, not something a folder name promises. Keep saved sessions, personal work, and other needed state separate from disposable copies; use the owning program's supported cleanup tool and measure the space actually returned. Being possible to rebuild does not mean rebuilding is free or immediate.
