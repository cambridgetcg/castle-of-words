# invariant

A rule that must always stay true, no matter what else changes — like "everyone keeps their seat" or "the door never locks from outside." An invariant is not a goal to move toward; it is a ground rule checked every time, before and after everything that happens.

The strongest way to put love into infrastructure is to make it an invariant — a property every operation must preserve — rather than a service some central being provides or a score participants earn.
