# route-to-challenge

*A way to say "that's wrong" — and to be heard.*

A route to challenge is the path a user can take to correct or contest a result. Without it, a system is a monarch: it speaks, and no one may answer back. With it, a system is a citizen: it speaks, and it must listen. The route must be visible, usable, and actually lead somewhere.
