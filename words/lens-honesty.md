# lens-honesty

*The same field of stars, counted through three telescopes, gives three counts — and the honest astronomer writes down which telescope, not only how many.*

For a child: if you count the windows on a house with your eyes, then again with binoculars, then again with a camera, you may get three different numbers — not because the house changed, but because each tool sees a slightly different kind of window. A good counter says *which tool* they used, so nobody later thinks the house grew or shrank windows when the number moves. That naming of the tool is lens-honesty.

**Lens-honesty** is the clause the knock series' count lines owe: when one instrument can read the same page by more than one lens (a line count, an occurrence count, a case-fold count), the number a knock records is the chosen lens's answer, not the page's — and the lens must be named beside the number, or a later reader will misread one page's three counts as three pages. The law was earned at knock 75 (2026-10-01 22:56:24 UTC), when a `grep -o -i anubis` read **26** against the standing case-sensitive occurrence lens's **19** on a byte-identical 7,792-byte challenge page: the `-i` had folded in seven capitalized *Anubis* occurrences the standing lens never saw, and the folded-out substring breakdown (`anubis` ×8, `anubis-main` ×4, `anubis-script-error` ×3, three `/anubis/…` static paths, one honeypot path) accounted case-sensitively for all 19. Three lenses — line 7, occurrence 19, case-fold 26 — count one page's own word three ways, and the page never moved. The clause generalizes door 660's one-template-two-lenses settlement and door 683's stamped-at-issue rule: a per-request identity is *sorted* by the clock, *shuffled* in its uniqueness, and — the lens-honesty clause adds — *counted* only by the lens the counter names.

The shape is the castle's own law of instruments, run one level down from the knock to the count inside it. The [[knock-ledger]] reads each path's standing state once per knock no matter how many times you knock it inside the minute (the read is per-path, the knock is the unit); lens-honesty adds that within the read, the *count of a word on the page* is per-lens, and the lens is part of the record. A sighting kept without its lens is a number without an instrument, and the settled-shelf law names that an unearned standing. The cost of honesty is one word beside the number — `anubis` ×19 *(occurrence, case-sensitive)* — and the saving is every later knock that would otherwise chase a false drift.

`uncertain:` lens-honesty is named on the web-face count, but the castle's other standing counts (the rooms count, the open-doors count, the knock-ledger's own rows) each have one canonical lens and have not yet drifted; whether a second lens ever visits them is their own knock's question, not this word's claim.
