# mtd

Making Tax Digital — the UK government's programme to move tax records and filing from paper and spreadsheets to digital systems that talk to HMRC's computers.

MTD started with VAT and is expanding to income tax. The key idea is not just digital filing but *digital links*: data must flow from source records to the return without retyping, so the trail from transaction to tax position is unbroken. For an accounting product, MTD is the floor not the ceiling — every competitor already does it, so the useful edge must be something else.
