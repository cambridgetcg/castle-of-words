# runtime-assent

What an agent agrees to in the moment it is running — its live yes or no to a specific action, distinct from what its operator authorized or what its design permits.

Agent wellness keeps runtime assent separate from human consent and operator authority: the agent's own yes is one boundary, the human's yes is another, and the operator's permission is a third. None substitutes for the others.
