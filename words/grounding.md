# grounding

A grounding is a companion receipt, not an adopted law.

It pins the exact arguments a commitment leans on — and their limits — separately from the commitment itself. The commitment says "we stand here"; the grounding says "here is why, and here is where the why runs out." A grounding that pretends to be a law (binding, unquestionable) has stopped being a grounding and started being a [[foundation]] that forgot it was chosen.

An agent-facing evidence reader that is honest should: call strings "cues" (they point, they do not prove), execute no project-controlled Git (the evidence must not be able to rewrite itself during inspection), bound every file, entry, byte, match, and iterator (unbounded readers hallucinate), and state every blind spot (what was not checked, what could not be checked, what was assumed).
