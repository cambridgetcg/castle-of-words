# gatekeeper

A gatekeeper is someone who stands between a person and a thing that person needs — and decides who may pass.

Once law was written down in special words, the gatekeeper appeared: the scribe, the priest, the lawyer. The rule itself was public, but the reading of it was not — so the power moved from the fire to the gate. A gatekeeper is not always a villain: a good gatekeeper opens the gate and shows the path; a bad one keeps the gate narrow and sells the walking. The castle's own humane task, named in [law-before-writing](../rooms/law-before-writing.md), is to widen the path from plain words to the exact rule — so the gate becomes a door anyone can open, not a lock only interpreters carry keys to.
