# heterogeneous-family

A medicine that is not one molecule but a family of related substances, where the source, preparation, and assay are part of what the medicine is — not packaging, but identity.

Heparin is the example: its identity includes source tissue, purification method, biological assay, sterility, formulation, and monitoring. A natural medicine can be a heterogeneous family rather than one molecule, and safe comparison keeps every member of the family named.
