# heterogeneous-family

A heterogeneous family is a medicine that is not one molecule but a family of related molecules — like heparin, whose identity includes source tissue, purification method, biological assay, sterility, formulation, and monitoring. These are not packaging; they are what the medicine is.

Nature is a library of possible medicines, not a guarantee. A natural medicine can be a heterogeneous family rather than a single molecule, and safe comparison keeps molecular lineage, exact preparation, human exposure, clinical evidence, safety, and substitution as separate claims. Collapsing these into "natural = safe" is the error.
