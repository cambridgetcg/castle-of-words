# part-vs-member

*A form's pieces can hang from one head, follow one thread, or sit beside one question — or they can simply belong to a whole, as a room belongs to a wing. The dependency-structure taxonomy named the first three shapes; it had no compartment for the fourth until [belonging](belonging.md) (2026-08-29) added it, because a member's tie to its form is not a dependency but a belonging.*

The [articulate-family-cluster](articulate-family-cluster.md) gives the map: read the recognition words first, the form words second. The fourth case, [boundary-cascade](boundary-cascade.md), came from [wing-epigraph](wing-epigraph.md) — the form family has already yielded one extraction. The quest that planted this word asked whether the remaining form words carry a fifth. The audit read four: [watch-quest](watch-quest.md), [build-quest-craft](build-quest-craft.md), [content-form-vs-completion-form](content-form-vs-completion-form.md), [the-room-form](the-room-form.md). One carries a load-bearing relationship left unnamed. It matches the cluster's shape.

## The finding

Content-form-vs-completion-form draws the distinction that structures the castle's quests: the watch-quest's five parts are a *content-form* — parts of the deliverable, missing any one and the deliverable is incomplete; the build-quest's craft is a *completion-form* — a discipline the maker holds, with no parts the deliverable must take. To make the argument, the word must say what the five parts *are*, and its body answers: **the five parts are parts of one form; the parts belong to the whole, and the whole is incomplete without any one of them.**

That relationship is stated again where the word does its sharpest work: [*wing-epigraph*](wing-epigraph.md) has parts (epigraph, representative, justification), and the gate's Map has members (the rooms listed under each wing). Both are *pieces of some whole* — but they relate to their wholes in different ways. The justification is a *part* of the wing-epigraph: leave it out and the form is broken, because the form's content is exactly its parts. A room is a *member* of its wing: remove [link-or-noise](../rooms/link-or-noise.md) from the hall of bridges and the hall still stands, because a wing is not made of its members the way a form is made of its parts. The word needs this distinction to say why watch-quest's checklist is a content-form while the Map's list under a wing heading is not — but it never gives the distinction a name.

## Why it is load-bearing and unnamed

The distinction is not decorative; the word's central claim depends on it. "The deliverable must have its five parts" means the parts *constitute* the deliverable. "A build-quest's deliverable can be a word, a room, a work, or a proposal" means those four kinds are *instances* of the deliverable, not parts of it — no one deliverable is incomplete for lacking the other three. Without the part/member distinction, the word cannot say why the five-part checklist binds and the four-kind list does not. It states the relationship — pieces that constitute versus pieces that belong — and leaves it embedded in the body.

And the [dependency-structure-taxonomy](dependency-structure-taxonomy.md) has no compartment for it — or had none, until [belonging](belonging.md) (built 2026-08-29) added the fourth structure the test was missing. The taxonomy asks three questions of a form's pieces: does one choice determine the rest (cascade), do the parts gate in sequence (chain), do the parts divide one question in parallel (fork)? Apply them to the watch-quest's five parts. Does one part determine the rest? No — the epigraph does not draw the wager; the parts are independent, not consequential. Do they gate in sequence? No — the findings do not wait for the craft's answer; the order is the report's reading order, not a chain of unlocks. Do they fork a condition from a mechanism? No — all five answer the same side of the same question: *what did the watch find?* By the taxonomy's old test, the five parts were neither cascade, chain, nor fork — yet plainly bound to their whole. The binding is of a fourth kind: belonging, not dependency, and the taxonomy now names it.

This is the extraction the form words were holding. The dependency taxonomy names how a form's parts relate *to each other*; part-vs-member names how a form's parts relate *to the form* — and contrasts that tie with the looser one a member bears to a collection. The relationship was already stated in content-form-vs-completion-form's body; this naming follows [articulate-vs-discover](articulate-vs-discover.md): found by reading, not by watching practice, and carrying the stated distinction forward into a name.

## Does it match the cluster's shape?

Yes, exactly. The cluster's test is one question — *does the body state a relationship between two or more things that is load-bearing for the argument but unnamed as a principle?* — and its shared shape is the relational distinction: a binding's shape, not a thing's nature. Part-vs-member is that shape: not a property of the watch-quest alone, but the shape of how its pieces hang on their whole, set against the shape of how a wing's rooms belong. The cluster's four instances bind choices to consequences, conditions to mechanisms, reveals to confirmations, triggers to readings. The fifth binds parts to their form and members to their collection. It is the first articulate case about *constitution* rather than connection — but it is the same genus: a distinction a word needed to make its argument, drawn in the body, never promoted.

## The craft

The craft is the one the cluster already named, now with a confirmed seam: when a word says "X is this, Y is that" to carry its argument — here, *the five parts constitute the deliverable; the four kinds only instance it* — ask whether the distinction has its own name. When a form's pieces are named, ask next not only how they depend on each other but how they hang on the whole: are they parts, without which the whole is broken, or members, without which the whole stands thinner? The taxonomy covers the first question; this word covers the second.

The practical yield: a content-form demands its parts because they constitute it; a completion-form demands nothing of the deliverable's pieces because it has none. And the Map's wings, the quest list's entries, the castle's rooms are members, not parts — which is why removing an entry from the list does not break the list the way removing the findings would break a watch report. The [narrated-index](narrated-index.md) test already relied on this without naming it: reordering the quests list breaks nothing precisely because entries are members.

*Sharpened 2026-09-03 by [dent-hole-nothing](dent-hole-nothing.md): the binary edge holds — pieces are parts or members — but the removal test found the thinning side divides. A member's removal leaves either a dent (clean, the other members never point at it) or a hole (an obligation dangling, the way removing a [watch-quest](watch-quest.md)'s links section leaves its sources unpointed). The dented piece is a clean member; the holed piece is an impure part. The taxonomy stands — holes and dents both live on the belonging side of the first question — but the near-miss's verdict is now precise.*

## The recursion

This word is its own instance and its own evidence twice over. It is the fifth articulate case, found by the method the cluster named, in the seam the cluster mapped — the cluster said the form words were the second seam to read, and the reading found exactly one case, no more: watch-quest's five-part form states no unnamed relationship (its parts are enumerated and their necessity claimed, but no distinction between kinds of piece is drawn), build-quest-craft's three completion parts repeat content-form-vs-completion-form's own distinctions under their existing names, the-room-form's three-load-bearing-parts-plus-one-face is stated with its hierarchy visible and its relations already housed in [default-bundle](default-bundle.md) and [rhythm-as-default-generator](rhythm-as-default-generator.md). One word held the unnamed relationship; three did not. That distribution is itself a finding: the cluster's shape is real but not ubiquitous, and the audit that reads four words and extracts one case has learned the seam's density, not just its location.

And the word is an instance of what it names in a quieter way: the audit's four readings are members of this word's evidence, not parts of it — drop one and the claim thins but stands; the part-vs-member distinction in the findings is a part — remove it and the word is broken. The word practices the distinction it extracts.
