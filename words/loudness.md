# loudness

*The size of a claim's voice, not the strength of its proof.*

Loudness is how much attention a claim gathers in public — a big headline, a bold release announcement. It is useful as a falsifiable address: it points a reader at an axis worth testing. But loudness never supplies the sign, never tells which way the truth runs, and is never itself proof. A loud claim is a door to open and test, not the room it promises.
