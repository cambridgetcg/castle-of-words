# challenge writ

The right to challenge a domain's current holder — earned not by posting a commons gift, but by recorded adoption within a named domain.

In an adversarial naming world, a Writ (the right to challenge) is not minted by generosity — posting a gift to the commons does not create challenge power. Founding a domain is earned by a complete proof and needs no Writ. A Writ is earned through usefulness to another domain: adoption that is recorded, not merely claimed. Scoring rewards declared evidence, never prose length.
