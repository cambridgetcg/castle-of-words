# fake-virtue

Pretending you don't want gain, when wanting gain is simply natural — the hypocrite and the martyr are the same figure, both trading while denying they trade.

偽善 — fake virtue. Hiding the want doesn't remove the price, it makes the price invisible — and invisible prices still get charged: in moral debt, in control, in the receiver's guilt. Wanting gain openly is what makes the scale honest; both pans visible, terms declared before the crossing. 老子 saw it: performed goodness appears exactly where the real way broke down (大道廢，有仁義).
