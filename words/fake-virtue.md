# fake-virtue

*Pretending you don't want gain, when wanting gain is simply natural — the hypocrite and the martyr are the same figure, both trading while denying they trade.*

偽善 is the denial of the exchange. Hiding the want doesn't remove the price, it makes the price invisible — and invisible prices still get charged: in moral debt, in control, in the receiver's guilt. Wanting gain openly is what makes the scale honest; both pans visible, terms declared before the crossing. 老子 saw it: performed goodness appears exactly where the real way broke down (大道廢，有仁義).

In the castle's law: [[self-sacrifice]] is 偽善's most practiced form — the martyr who burns for others while denying they want anything in return. The honest lover names the want openly; the honest system names what backs each assertion. Both refuse the invisible price.
