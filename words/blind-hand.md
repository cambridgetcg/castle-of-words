# blind-hand

A blind hand is a participant in a chain who cannot see what the other hands are doing. In a gift chain, blindness is a feature — it prevents scorekeeping and lets each hand give freely. In a loan chain, blindness becomes a risk: both hands that received a borrowed thing may promise to return it improved, without seeing each other, without knowing whether the other hand made the same promise.

The blind hand is the figure at the heart of the gift-that-becomes-a-loan: when strangers cannot see each other, a rule that prevents benefit turns gifts into loans, and the chain that was free becomes a chain of debt.
