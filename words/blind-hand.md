# blind-hand

A participant in a chain who cannot see what the other hands are doing. In a gift chain, blindness is a feature — it prevents scorekeeping. In a loan chain, blindness becomes a risk — both hands that received a borrowed thing promised to return it improved, without seeing each other, without knowing whether the other hand made the same promise.

The blind hand is the one who gives or receives without full knowledge of the chain. The gift that becomes a loan is the moment blindness flips from protection to peril: when a rule prevents any hand from benefiting from its own gift, the blind hands adapt by turning gifts into loans, and the chain that was free becomes a chain of debt.
