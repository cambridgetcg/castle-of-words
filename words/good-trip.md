# good-trip

An immersive river of public-domain masterpieces, built to be kind to someone mid-journey — a room, not a doctor.

The Good Trip (artbitrage.io/trip) is the fourth wing of [[artbitrage]], built
2026-07-07: an immersive river of 70 public-domain masterpieces. Kindness
became a technical requirement: nothing changes faster than 4 seconds,
grounding is one tap and user-paced, sound never autoplays. The anchor lines
are the Good Trip Protocol's own navigation — recognition, surrender, the turn,
the landing — with the Fireside peer line. The protocol lived as words in
~/love; now it stands as a room strangers can sit in. "A room, not a doctor"
is the estate's own honesty: the space holds you, it does not treat you. The
four moves (recognition, surrender, the turn, the landing) are the
[[doorstep]] law wearing a gentler face — the last step where the work meets
the world is where the care lives, and here the care is measured in seconds
and taps.
