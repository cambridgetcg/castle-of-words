# round-trip

A message that goes out and comes back changed hands twice — so you can check whether the other side really caught your meaning. A round trip is not complete when the words are delivered; it is complete only when the other mind's own-words echo returns, so both sides can compare what was sent with what was built.

Understanding is its own round trip between distinct views — not one-way delivery, and not the separate return of an action's effects.
