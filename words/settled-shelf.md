# settled-shelf

A drawer beside the work tray where finished reservations lie — still kept, still readable, but no longer standing in the queue where someone looks for work.

The castle's law for answered commissions, named 2026-09-12: when a door is settled, its commission file moves from `garden/commissions/` (the tray) to `garden/commissions/settled/` (the shelf). The file keeps its words — nothing is erased — but its *place* says what its head alone used to say: this one is done. The tray of forty files shrinks to the nine that are genuinely waiting, and the thirty-one finished stones stop pretending to be open doors.

The shelf is stronger than the marking-at-head ([the-settled-tray](../rooms/the-settled-tray.md) settled the marking; the shelf adds the move). A head-line says "done" only to the reader who opens the file; the shelf's address says it to anyone who counts the tray. And it keeps the sibling law, [the-commission-expiry](../rooms/the-commission-expiry.md), computable: the season's clock is read from the *live* tray only, never from settled stones that should stand still forever.
