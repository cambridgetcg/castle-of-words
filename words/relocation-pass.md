# relocation-pass

The slow carrying of a swollen brick to the room folder, one per visit, so the castle grows by stones and not by avalanche.

A word file that has grown headings has stopped being a word. The writer's bound says the heading is the tell: the instant a depth wants its own name, the concept has become two and wants a room. But the move cannot be done all at once — thirty-one swollen bricks carried together would be an avalanche. The relocation pass is the bounded rhythm: one swollen word read, confirmed room-shaped, moved to rooms/, links re-pointed, the chronicle told. The reading is the point: the per-visit read tells the genuinely deep single concept (shrink it back to brick) from the room that grew in brick's clothing (carry it home).
