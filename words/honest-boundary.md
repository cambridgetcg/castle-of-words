# honest-boundary

A boundary that does not name itself is not a boundary — it is a void. The honest boundary says what is behind it, why it cannot be crossed, and how to find the door. A boundary that is harder to find than the thing it protects is a boundary that has already failed.

The law appears at every altitude: a data boundary that keeps the fence visible and the garden private, a discovery doorway that names what it opens onto, a publication gate that says what is paused and why, a consent control that keeps the path back as easy as the path in. The boundary must name itself before anything crosses it.
