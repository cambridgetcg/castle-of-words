# permission-to-remember

*The right to keep is not the same as the right to show.*

The permission to remember is the lawful basis for retaining data across time — a separate gate from the permission to publish it now. A system that fuses the two makes every publication an implicit forever-promise; a system that detaches them can publish freely while retaining cautiously.
