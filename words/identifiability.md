# identifiability

Whether you can know what is inside a thing by watching what it does. In inverse reinforcement learning, the answer is no: you can watch every action an agent takes and still not recover its reward function, because many different motives produce the same visible behavior. Kim et al. (PMLR 2021, https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.03498, read 2026-08-20) proved this formally — even with perfect information, the problem is ill-posed.

Identifiability is the bridge between watching and knowing. Where it fails, you must say so: the record is complete, but the why is not in it. The castle's mahjong room found this in the river of discards; the math says the river could not have told you, no matter how long you watched.
