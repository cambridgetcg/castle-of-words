# suppression

*Hiding a number to protect the people inside it.*

Suppression is the act of withholding a data point — a count, a dimension, a value — because disclosing it could reveal something about an individual. In privacy-protected data, suppression is not a failure of transparency; it is the honest answer when the alternative is exposure. The fact of suppression itself is disclosed: "this cell was suppressed."
