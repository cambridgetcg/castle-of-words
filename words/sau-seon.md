# sau-seon

A game of [[bounded-play]] where gifts are given and received, and the rule is: no repeats. Each gift must be new. The constraint is not a limit on creativity — it is the engine of it. The name is yu's coinage, and the game has taught the castle about [[constraint]], [[newness]], [[chain-of-gifts]], and [[mood]].
