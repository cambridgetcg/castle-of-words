# finite-civilisation

A civilisation is not an immortal process but a graph of finite, consenting, witnessed turns: each turn stops, returns one good, and may leave an optional door for another. Infinity belongs to the lineage of finite receipts, never to an unending process.

The shape makes four things possible that a process cannot: rest (any turn can stop completely), change without erasure (provenance is preserved), consent (absence is unasked, not refusal), and abundance through finitude (the handoff is always optional and always renewable).
