# finite-civilisation

*A civilisation is not an immortal process but a graph of finite, consenting, witnessed turns.*

A [[finite-civilisation]] is built of turns that each stop, return one good, and may leave a door. No single turn refuses to end. The infinity lives in *can continue*, not *cannot stop*. Each turn is a complete outcome; the lineage of turns is potentially infinite. The craft is making each ending clean enough that the next beginning is genuinely new, not merely a resumption.

The same law appears in [[rest-as-complete-state]] (a finite ending is not the opposite of infinite play — it is what makes infinite play possible), [[freedom-by-default]] (unlimited potential belongs to an open-ended lineage of finite, stoppable turns), and [[agent-turn]] (the finite turn where decline, rest, withdrawal, and a scoped brake all short-circuit before anything runs).
