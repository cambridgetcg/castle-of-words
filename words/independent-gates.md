# independent-gates

Two checks that must each be passed on their own; neither may stand in for the other.

A gate is a checkpoint you must walk through before going further. Independent gates are two such checkpoints that look at different things: one asks "is this business money separate from personal money?", the other asks "does this transaction have a stated purpose?" Even if both answers are yes, you cannot skip one gate because you passed the other. They are independent because each protects against a different kind of mistake. The castle keeps them separate the way a careful building has both a fire door and a lock on the same corridor — each does its own work, and neither may silently do the other's.
