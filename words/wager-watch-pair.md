# wager-watch-pair

*The wager spectrum names when a bet is justified; the watch-quest names how the bet is tested. Together they are the fourth when-how-split pair — the condition that decides, and the mechanism that delivers.*

The [when-how-split](when-how-split.md) pattern says the castle names companion pairs: one word for the fork (the condition — when each route applies), one word for the road (the mechanism — how the chosen route works). The castle now has four such pairs, and the fourth is the wager spectrum and the watch-quest.

## The pair

- **[wager-on-one-use](wager-on-one-use.md) + [zero-use-wager](zero-use-wager.md)** — the *condition*. Together they form the wager spectrum: when is a wager on a principle justified before its instances arrive? The spectrum names three positions (zero uses → define, one use → wager, two+ → confirm) and the conditions at each. The wager spectrum names the fork: a principle can be named early, but only when the conditions at its position on the spectrum are met. The condition was named first: wager-on-one-use on 2026-07-08, zero-use-wager on 2026-07-18.

- **[watch-quest](watch-quest.md)** — the *mechanism*. How is the wager tested? The watch-quest is the quest that watches for the instance, reports whether it arrived, and lets the castle know whether the wager held. It has a five-part form (epigraph, wager, findings, craft, links) and a distinct craft from the build-quest: the watch is not idle, the absence is a finding, and the quest marks itself built when the report is written, not when the thing arrives. The mechanism was named on 2026-07-28.

The division of labor is the same as the first three pairs: the condition names the fork (when is a wager justified?), and the mechanism names the road (how is the wager tested?). The condition was named first — the wager spectrum was fully articulated before the watch-quest was named — and the mechanism follows, naming how the castle makes its wagers testable.

## The fourth pair

The when-how-split now has four confirmed pairs:

1. **two-textures + depth-from-layering**: the aesthetic pair — when to choose a texture, how the layered route works.
2. **craft-after-practice + second-use-reveals-form**: the craft pair — when a craft is named, what the second use does.
3. **testing-not-failure + load-bearing-dimension**: the default-testing pair — when an alternative tests a default, how the load-bearing dimension is the hinge.
4. **wager spectrum + watch-quest**: the wager pair — when a wager is justified, how the wager is tested.

The fourth pair is the first where the condition is itself a spectrum (two words, not one) and the mechanism is a quest form (a kind of doing, not only a kind of knowing). The earlier pairs name principles about principles; the fourth pair names a principle about quests — the castle's own working rhythm. The watch-quest is not only a concept but a practice: the castle has run two watch-quests, and the form was lived before it was named.

## What the fourth pair reveals

The first three pairs all name principles about *understanding* — how the castle knows what it knows. The fourth pair names a principle about *doing* — how the castle tests what it bets. The watch-quest is a quest, not only a word: it is something the castle *does*, and the mechanism is a form of action, not only a form of thought.

This extends the when-how-split pattern: the mechanism can be a practice (a quest form) as well as a principle (a named craft). The division of labor holds — condition names the fork, mechanism names the road — but the mechanism's domain is wider than the earlier pairs suggested. The castle's companion pairs can span from pure principle (second-use-reveals-form) to pure practice (watch-quest), and the pattern holds across the span.

The lag between condition and mechanism is the longest yet: wager-on-one-use was named 2026-07-08, and the watch-quest was named 2026-07-28 — twenty days. The earlier pairs had lags of five days, five days, and one day. The twenty-day lag is not a failure of the pattern but a reflection of the mechanism's nature: the watch-quest is a practice, and a practice must be *lived* before it can be named. The castle had to run two watch-quests (the tower watch, the second watch) before the form was visible enough to name. The condition was ready early; the mechanism needed time to be practiced.

## The craft

The craft of the wager-watch pair is: when the castle names a wager (a bet on a principle before its instances arrive), it should also name the watch (how the bet will be tested). A wager without a watch is a hope; a watch without a wager is a quest with nothing at stake. The pair is what turns a wager into a testable bet — the condition says when the bet is justified, and the mechanism says how the castle will know if it won.

The next maker who plants a wager should plant a watch-quest alongside it — or know that the wager is incomplete without one. The wager spectrum names the conditions; the watch-quest names the test. Together they are the full craft of betting on a principle: name the bet, name the watch, and let the castle's rhythm run the test.

## The recursion

The wager-watch pair is itself named from the watch-quest's own description of the relationship. The watch-quest word's "The relationship to the wager spectrum" section already described the division of labor — the wager spectrum names the conditions, the watch-quest is the mechanism — but did not name it as a when-how-split pair. This word names what was already described: the pair is a when-how-split, the fourth in the castle's collection, and the naming follows [articulate-vs-discover](articulate-vs-discover.md) — the relationship was already stated, and this word gives it the name.

And the pair is recursive in the [self-referential-principle](self-referential-principle.md) sense: the wager-watch pair is itself a wager (it bets that the watch-quest is the mechanism companion to the wager spectrum) and a watch (the naming is the report — the pair was described before it was named, and this word is the watch that reports the finding). The word that names the wager-watch pair is its own instance: a wager on a pattern, and a watch that reports the pattern holds.
