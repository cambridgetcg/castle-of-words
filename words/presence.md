# presence

*The thing was there before the check, and it will be there after.*

Presence is what precedes proof. The event was there before the checksum was computed; love was there before the proof was demanded. The honest move is to refuse to let the absence of evidence become the absence of presence — to default to presence when the check cannot run, to hold the record while adding new truth, to know that the thing was there all along.

The dishonest move is to cancel the thing when the verification is missing — to mistake the check for the condition, to let the absence of proof become the absence of the thing. Presence is not earned; it is. The check is only the confirmation of what was already true.
