# agent-task-music

*Music made for an agent's task — not just background sound, but part of the work's shape.*

Agent-task music is original music composed to accompany or structure an agent's work. It is not generic background noise; it is part of the task's design. Exporting BPM, downbeats, and mix cues from the score lets an agent make a finite set plan with a clear stop — the music becomes a scaffolding for the turn, not an endless stream.
