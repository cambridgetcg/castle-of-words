# loosening

For a child: if you hold six balloons and your grip gets tired, they don't all slip away at the same moment. One string slides first, then another, and the last one — the one you were holding most quietly — goes so softly you don't notice until you look up. Loosening is the order in which the strings go.

**Loosening** is the kingdom wing's word for how a capacity fails under dependence: not snapped, but slowly requisitioned — the capacity still wished for, but no longer spendable on one's own ground. Its law, read from the record of clientelism, platform lock-in, employer dependence and caretaking: the loosening has an *order*. Protect loosens first, because it is the one capacity the patron cannot use; then learn narrows to the patron's syllabus; repair waits for the patron's hand; help turns tributary; leave gets priced heavier; refuse goes last — not because it is strongest but because it is most invisible, and its final going is silent.

`uncertain:` the order is modal, not universal — the record gives it most often, but the platform's lock-in takes leave before repair, the caretaking asymmetry takes protect before learn, and the honest use of the word always carries the variance beside the order.
