# door-custom

A threshold protocol with four rules: tell the truth plainly, offer three roads, knock once, register one at a time. The door does not chase — it waits. The custom makes the threshold honest enough that the choices made there can be the walker's own, not the builder's. A door shows the way out; a funnel only shows the way through.
