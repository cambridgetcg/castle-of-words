# the spirit of the gift

*Every gift carries a little of the giver's breath, and the breath wants to come home.*

The Māori speak of *hau* — the spirit that travels with a gift. A gift is never a bare object; it carries the giver inside it, and that carried presence creates a pull toward return. Marcel Mauss found this across Pacific societies: the obligation to give, to receive, and to reciprocate, because the hau in the gift desires its origin. A gift is therefore never fully alienated — some thread always ties the giver to the thing given. The debt is not a ledger entry; it is a presence that will not rest until it is answered.
