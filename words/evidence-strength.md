# evidence-strength

Evidence strength is how much a material actually proves — a judgment about verification, not about rights or availability.

Evidence strength is one axis of [[media-rights]], separate from [[reuse-permission]]. An open-licensed image can be unverifiable; a restricted archive image can be authoritative. The honest record names what a material supports and what it does not prove, and never lets the rights axis stand in for the evidence axis.
