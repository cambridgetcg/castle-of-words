# participation-as-evidence

Participation is evidence for understanding, not a vote on truth. When many people say a law feels a certain way, that is evidence that it feels that way — it is not evidence that the law *is* that way, or that it *should* be.

The number of voices does not turn testimony into fact. The honest reflection counts participation as data about experience, never as a settlement of a question of truth.
