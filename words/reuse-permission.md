# reuse-permission

Reuse permission is the legal right to use a material beyond viewing it — a fact about licensing, not about availability or truth.

Reuse permission is one position on the reuse-rights axis of [[media-rights]]. It is not the same as [[public-access]] (you can view without reusing), and it is not the same as [[evidence-strength]] (you can reuse a false image). The honest record tracks reuse permission per asset, independently of what the asset proves.
