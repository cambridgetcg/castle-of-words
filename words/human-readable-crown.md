# human-readable crown

The visible, memorable name of a domain — the part humans call it by, and the part that is scarce, desirable, and therefore attackable.

In an adversarial naming system, the crown is separate from the key-bound keep. The crown can be challenged and transferred; the keep remains stable. The craft is to make the crown scarce enough to be meaningful but not so fused with identity that its capture breaks the domain.
