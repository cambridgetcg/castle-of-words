# interest

*The feeling that says: walk toward that — not because it pleases the eye, but because it promises the feet a puzzle.*

Interest is the emotion that turns attention toward an object, event, or process — not because the thing is already liked, but because it is not yet finished being known. Where pleasure rewards fluency (the mind's easy path), interest rewards *resolved disfluency*: the friction that gives way only after effort (Graf & Landwehr, 2017). Interest is the learner's wage for work.
