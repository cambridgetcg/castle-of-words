# Inspectable Path

*A trail anyone can walk from evidence to answer.*

An inspectable path is a chain of records that lets a reader trace a claim from its origin (the evidence) through every intermediate step (event, books, accounts, tax) to its final form (filing) — and back again. Each link must be labelled, and any missing link must be named as missing rather than silently skipped.

In accounting product design, the inspectable path is what makes a beginner-friendly product honest: the product leads with one step but preserves the whole path, labelling every layer it has not yet built.
