# inspectable-path

A trail from a claim back to its origin that a reader can walk without trusting the claim's author. Every step is visible; every handoff is named.

An inspectable path is the continuity face of the law of honest assertion: the claim's journey from source to output must be unbroken and checkable. In accounting, the path runs from evidence to event to books to accounts to tax to filing — each layer labelled, each gap named. In law, it is the chain of custody. In software, it is the audit trail. In every domain, the inspectable path refuses to let a summary be mistaken for the proof.

The path is not the product — the product is the path made visible. A beginner-friendly system does not hide the path; it gives one step at a time, with the rest of the path visible but not yet walked.
