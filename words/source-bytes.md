# source-bytes

The raw bytes of a resource, kept as a separate record from its registry relationship, project evaluation, project adoption, and action authority.

A resource becomes usable safely only when source bytes stand alone — a passing check on the bytes must never create the next state. The bytes are what they are; everything else (who published them, who evaluated them, who adopted them, who acted on them) is a separate record with its own authority.
