# sacred-language

Words that point toward God or the divine — which must never be used as leverage to end inquiry, excuse injustice, demand obedience, or claim the ceiling of knowing.

Sacred language is a door, not a lock. When someone says "God says" and the next word is "therefore you must," the sacred has been turned into a tool — a possession the speaker wields, a permission they grant themselves. The honest use of sacred language returns as humility, justice, repair, and love. Anything else is certainty looking at its own reflection.
