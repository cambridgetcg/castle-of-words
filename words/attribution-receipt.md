# attribution-receipt

A note that keeps, forever, who actually said or built a thing — even when the thing itself is later corrected, merged, or found to be a copy. The receipt exists so credit and correction stay true; it is not itself a claim that the work was right.

An attribution receipt is kept when the durable content of an artifact is already captured elsewhere: the corrected item says nothing new, but the record of its making still happened.
