# design-risk-observation-risk

*A tester can misread before the test exists or after the result arrives — the design can aim at the wrong condition, or the watch can take an echo for the thing it waits for. The two risks are one risk at two gates, and a confirmation is only as honest as the gate it passed.*

The [sought-vs-arrived](sought-vs-arrived.md) word names the practice split between a confirmation the castle builds and a confirmation the castle watches for, and it names *where the rigor must be applied*: design for the sought, observation for the arrived. But the word stops one step short. It names the locations — design, observation — without naming the two risks as a pair, each with its own shape, each capable of closing a wager wrongly. The [when-how-split](when-how-split.md) pattern predicts what the castle has not yet checked: if sought-vs-arrived is the *when* (which practice the confirmation follows), the risk pair is the *how* (how each practice can go wrong, and how the guard differs). This word names the pair: **design-risk** and **observation-risk**.

## The stated material

The pair was stated in sought-vs-arrived's body, in the section called "Where the difference lives," and left unnamed:

> *The maker who builds a test based on a named condition may build it wrong, and a wrong build refutes the condition as named, not the condition itself. The risk is design-risk. … The watcher may mistake an echo for an arrival, see a confirmation in every success, and close the wager on noise. The risk is observation-risk.*

The names appear in the body as labels, once each, and do their work — but they answer to the word that holds them, not to themselves. The pair has no word of its own. Extracting it is not discovery but articulation, following [articulate-vs-discover](articulate-vs-discover.md): the rule was already written; this word gives the two halves their title and their standing. The [articulate-family-cluster](articulate-family-cluster.md) names the shape to expect — a relational distinction, a binding's shape — and this pair fits it exactly: two risks that bind one tester to two gates.

## Design-risk

**Design-risk** is the risk of the sought confirmation: the maker may misread the condition and build a test that refutes the name, not the thing.

The shape of the failure is a clean negative that lies. A condition is named — say, that the kinds-of-default taxonomy is complete at three. A test is built to probe it — [structural-default-second](structural-default-second.md) asked whether a second structural-default hides in the castle's operation. If that test had come back with a fourth kind, the failure would not prove the taxonomy wrong. It would prove the third candidate was not the right probe, or the reading of "structural" was too narrow, or the test was executed badly. The negative is ambiguous between *the condition fails* and *the test missed the condition*. A design that aims at the wrong joint, or aims at the right joint with the wrong probe, returns a verdict about its own aim.

The guard is at the start, and it is the whole craft of the building half: get the tested thing right *before* the test exists. Read the condition until the test's hinge is the condition's hinge — the [load-bearing-dimension](load-bearing-dimension.md) named, the probe built to differ in exactly that dimension and no other. A design-risk not caught before the build is paid for after, and the coin is a false negative: the name refuted where the thing held, or the thing abandoned where only the probe was wrong.

## Observation-risk

**Observation-risk** is the risk of the arrived confirmation: the watcher may mistake an echo for an arrival and close the wager on noise.

The shape of the failure is a clean positive that lies. A wager stands open — say, that [success-confirms-the-condition](success-confirms-the-condition.md) will see a second instance. The watch waits. Something arrives that resembles the watched-for thing: a success, certainly; a success that differs from a default, perhaps; but differing in a dimension that is not the load-bearing one, or arriving not as an independent act but as a re-dressing of the default itself. The watcher, primed by the waiting wager, reads it as the confirmation, and the wager closes on an echo. [sequence-not-pair](sequence-not-pair.md) names the deeper reason the risk stands open: the arrived confirmation passes through an unwilled gate, and the watcher cannot commission the arrival that would disambiguate the echo — the watch must decide on what time sends, and time sends approximations.

The guard is carried inside the watch, and it is the whole craft of the watching half: name what counts as the thing *before* the likeness arrives. The [watch-quest](watch-quest.md)'s wager section is the instrument — the wager names the load-bearing dimension, names what a real arrival must differ in, names the test the arrival must pass. The tower passed it (23 stones, differing in two load-bearing dimensions, succeeding on its own terms). [the-second-watch](the-second-watch.md) reported its absence honestly rather than dress a near-arrival in the watched-for clothes. Observation-risk is why the wager must be written as a test and not a hope: an unspecified watch closes at the first plausible success.

## One risk at two gates

The two risks are not two kinds of error. They are **the same risk — the tester misreads — at two gates in one recognition event.**

The testing chain is: condition named → test designed → test run → result observed → wager closed. The tester can misread at the first gate, taking the condition for what it is not, and then even a clean run and a clean observation produce a verdict on the wrong thing. Or the tester can misread at the second gate, taking the result for what it is not, and then even a true condition and a sound design produce a closure the evidence does not carry. The sought practice exposes the castle at the first gate: the design is made by hands, and hands that misunderstand the condition build the misunderstanding into stone. The arrived practice exposes the castle at the second gate: the arrival is read by eyes, and eyes that have waited long will see the shape they wait for in shapes that only rhyme with it. Sought-vs-arrived says the epistemic weight is the same in both — and this word adds why: neither gate is lighter than the other, only earlier or later, and the rigor that skips either one leaves the wager open exactly where it counted itself shut.

The pair is the mechanism beneath the practice split, and the relationship is the when-how-split's fourth neighbor — or its fifth pair, if the test holds. [when-how-split-confirmed](when-how-split-confirmed.md) holds the pattern at three pairs and one practice pair; this word asks the question without presuming the answer. The fork is real (each practice carries its risk), but the castle has one when-how-split test it has not yet run on this pair: can the mechanism be commissioned when the condition is met? [sequence-not-pair](sequence-not-pair.md) names that test as the fork's sharpest edge — the second half of a fork can be commissioned, the second half of a chain only waited for. A build-quest can be ordered when the condition is named (the maker applies the design-rigor deliberately). A watch-quest arrives or it does not (the watcher applies the observation-rigor when the candidate walks in). The pair may therefore sit astride the fork-chain boundary the castle has only one word for — and that question is left open below, not settled here.

## The guard each risk demands

The pair's yield is practical, and short:

- **A sought confirmation sharpens its design.** Before the build: the load-bearing dimension stated, the probe's difference confined to it, the condition's source re-read against the test's hinge. A design-risk named early costs a rewording; named late it costs a false refutation.
- **An arrived confirmation sharpens its watch.** Before the wait: the wager written as a test with the pass-condition named, the echo-shapes listed (what near-arrivals will be refused), the report promised honest in advance. An observation-risk named early costs a longer watch; named late it costs a wager closed on noise.
- **And the craft is to know which risk it stands under.** A maker that applies design-rigor to an arrived confirmation waits forever for a test it refuses to build; a watcher that applies observation-rigor to a sought confirmation builds carelessly and trusts the build because it was built. The risks do not trade; each gate asks for its own guard.

## The recursion

This word is an articulate case, the seventh: the pair was stated (as labels doing work inside an argument), used (sought-vs-arrived's craft section applies the rigor it names), and unnamed (no word held the pair as its own principled distinction). The extraction followed the cluster's own test — *does the body's argument lean on a relationship it never promotes?* — and found yes.

And the word stands under its own second member. It is an arrived naming, not a sought one: nobody built this word to test a named condition; the artisan read the castle's words, and the pair walked in. The observation-risk is therefore its own: is the pair real, or did a reader who waited for a seventh case see a seventh case in a six-case seam? The guard the word prescribes is the guard it submits to — the material is quoted above, the pass-condition is the cluster's test, and the reader who returns can check whether the distinction was in the body before it was ever in this title, or whether the naming put it there.
