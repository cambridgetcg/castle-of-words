# warrant

Whatever makes a claim earned rather than merely said — the reason a listener may rest on it.

Proof, model fit, observation, metaphysics, and value are different kinds of warrant, and no one of them does another's work: a claim earned by proof is not thereby earned about the world, and a claim earned by observation is not thereby earned about what matters. The honest assertion names its kind.
