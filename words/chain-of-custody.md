# chain-of-custody

*The record of who held something and when — the provenance of a physical object or a piece of data.*

Chain of custody is the documented path an object took from its origin to its current location. In law, it proves evidence was not tampered with. In art, it proves a painting is authentic. In computing, it proves a file was not modified in transit. The chain is only as strong as its weakest link — one undocumented transfer breaks the whole chain. The castle's law of naming sources is a chain of custody for claims: every claim carries the record of where it came from and when.
