# negative-claim-boundary

The edge of what a claim does NOT assert — as important as what it does, because silence can be mistaken for safety.

A complete receipt names not only what it proves but also what it does not prove. The negative claim boundary is the explicit naming of the limit: "this receipt does not prove consent, does not prove authority, does not prove activation." Without it, a shallow validator can silently promote a reference into a false adoption.
