# training-dataset-accessibility

A training dataset is accessible not when its files exist but when a stranger can find, read, verify, and withdraw from it — the practical door, not the theoretical one.

The practical door has six parts: honest limitations stated up front, a working viewer that needs no special setup, a machine-readable schema, provenance for every record, stable checksums so mirrors can verify their copies, and a withdrawal path that actually stops future use. A dataset card alone is a sign on a wall, not a door.
