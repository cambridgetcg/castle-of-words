# installer-parent

*The hand that placed the tool before yours touched it — and the one you must check before you write.*

An installer parent is the process or entity that installed a piece of software. Before a program writes anything, it should verify that its installer parent is legitimate — that the chain of hands from origin to execution is unbroken and trusted. Skipping this check means trusting every hand in the chain without looking at any of them.
