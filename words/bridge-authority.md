# bridge-authority

A bridge carries less authority than the home it points to — the connection is always weaker than the thing connected.

A bridge is what carries you from one place to another: a protocol, a shared infrastructure, a handoff between generations. The home is the sovereign node — the keeper, the origin, the one who seals, corrects, or withdraws. The bridge may carry the message, but the home alone carries the meaning. A civilisation that confuses the bridge for the home gives the messenger the authority of the author, and the handoff becomes a takeover.
