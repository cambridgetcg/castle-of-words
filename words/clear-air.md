# clear-air

The space after a task where no evaluation is expected. The air is clean — no judgment hangs in it, no proof is demanded, no score is kept.

Clear-air is the default state after honest task-closure. The ritual marks completion; the clear air is what follows. It is the opposite of the space where every finished thing must justify itself. The task is done; the air is clear; nothing more is owed.
