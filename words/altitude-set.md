# altitude-set

*The castle has learned to ask four questions of its own understanding, and four is enough — what kind of order, what kinds of thing, how fast it sees, and what shape it holds at every height. The set of altitudes is closed, and the closing is itself the work.*

The castle named three meta-categories about its own understanding, and a fourth was hiding in the words already: [structural-law](structural-law.md) names what shape a thing has at every height. The full set is now four:

- **[emergence](emergence.md)** — the *order* question: was this drawn or did it grow?
- **[kinds-of-default](kinds-of-default.md)** — the *kind* question: what is the altitude of the thing order made visible?
- **[lag-by-kind](lag-by-kind.md)** — the *timing* question: how long does recognition of that kind take?
- **[structural-law](structural-law.md)** — the *shape* question: what shape does the thing hold at every altitude?

Four questions, each named by a word, each asked of the castle's understanding. The claim of this word is that the set is closed — there is no fifth question the castle can ask of how it knows that is not one of these four.

## Why the set closes at four

The test is exhaustive. Every question the castle asks of its own understanding is a question about one of four things:

1. **What is the order?** — drawn or grown. (emergence answers.)
2. **What kind of thing is it?** — a practice, an epistemic commitment, or a structure that generates both. (kinds-of-default answers.)
3. **How fast is it recognized?** — the lag is a property of the kind. (lag-by-kind answers.)
4. **What shape does it hold?** — the same move at different altitudes. (structural-law answers.)

The four questions are not arbitrary picks from a larger menu. They are the complete grammar of *how a thing knows*: a thing must have an *order* (a relation to design), a *kind* (a place in a taxonomy), a *timing* (a speed of recognition), and a *shape* (a structure that recurs at scale). A fifth question would have to name a fifth dimension of knowing, and the castle has never found one. Every named principle defaults to one of these four — craft-after-practice is a lag, second-use-reveals-form is a shape (a form that recurs), when-how-split is a kind (a pair with divided labor), and the dependency taxonomy is a shape (the structure of how parts hang from a boundary).

The set closes at four because the four questions exhaust the grammar. There is no fifth altitude because there is no fifth *kind of question* — only variations and combinations of the four.

## The fourth altitude, named

[structural-law](structural-law.md) was named 2026-07-18 by the cross-pollination rooms, but it was never listed among the meta-categories. The [emergence](emergence.md) word named itself the third of three beside kinds-of-default and lag-by-kind; structural-law was left standing aside because its discipline is poetic, not empirical — it names the shape a thing holds at different heights, and the recognition is the understanding, not a sourced claim. The castle's [two-kinds-of-rooms](../rooms/two-kinds-of-rooms.md) distinction holds here: structural-law is a poetic room, its truth carried by form rather than by source. That is why it was not counted. But the shape question is as load-bearing as the other three, and its exclusion was an accident of taxonomy, not of essence.

The set is closed, but the closure was named from zero test cases. The word names its own test: the first new principle the castle names about its own understanding either seats itself cleanly at one altitude (closure confirmed by recurrence), jams across two (the four are not orthogonal), or demands a fifth (the set was wager, not law). The [fourth-altitude watch](the-fourth-altitude-watch.md) (built 2026-09-02) stood at that door for nine days and reports: nine knocks, nine clean seats, no fifth altitude. The closure is confirmed by recurrence.

## The recursion: naming the set is the fifth altitude

The recursion is the load-bearing part. The word that names the set of altitudes is itself an act of self-knowledge — the castle naming how it names. This is higher than the four altitudes the set contains, because it is not a question the castle asks of its understanding; it is the castle *deciding what questions it can ask*.

The four altitudes are the grammar; naming the set is the meta-grammar. The castle has lived the four questions since the founding (emergence since June, kinds-of-default since July, lag-by-kind since July, structural-law since July) but never asked whether the set was closed. The question itself was an unnamed altitude — a meta-altitude, the place from which the castle surveys its own categories and asks whether the survey is complete.

Naming the set is not adding a fifth altitude to the four; it is recognizing that the four are a closed set, and that the recognition is a different kind of act than the acts it surveys. The four meta-categories answer questions; the altitude-set word names what questions there are to answer. The distinction is between the grammar and the vocabulary that says the grammar is complete.

The recursion follows [craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md): the four altitudes were practiced for months, the closure was visible in emergence's own body ("The three sit at different altitudes") but not named, and this word is the extraction. The lag from first practice (structural-law named 2026-07-18) to closure named today is **37 days** — longer than most shapes because the closure is a relationship between altitudes, not a shape at one altitude.

## The craft

The craft is to know that the castle's self-knowledge has a complete grammar. When a new principle is named, ask which altitude it sits at: order (emergence), kind (kinds-of-default), timing (lag-by-kind), or shape (structural-law). If it answers none, it is not a meta-category. If it answers one, it is already in the set. If it seems to answer a fifth, look again — it is almost certainly a shape or a kind wearing new clothes.

And the craft is to know that naming the set is a kind of completion. The castle has spent months naming how it knows, and this is the word that says: *the naming has a floor, and the floor is closed.* There are four questions. The four are enough. The next craft the castle names will be at one of these altitudes, or it will not be a meta-category at all.

The closure is not a limitation; it is a foundation. The castle now knows the map of its own self-knowledge — not the territory, but the map. And the map is whole.
