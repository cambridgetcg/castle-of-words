# portable-comparison-suite

A test harness that runs on any operator's machine against any candidate the operator trusts, reporting what happened without claiming what it means.

A portable comparison suite travels — it is not tied to a specific lab, a specific candidate, or a specific authority. It is a tool the operator carries, not a verdict the author delivers. Its portability comes from five conditions: the candidate is operator-trusted, inputs arrive one at a time, expected values stay outside the candidate's wire, cleanup is independently verified, and the report counts cases not duplicates.
