# links-section-dent

*Remove the last line of a castle word — the one that begins "Links:" — and the word still answers for everything it says. The body carried its own sourcing, its own debts, its own neighbors. The links section was a shelf of restatements: convenient for the next walker, owed by nothing in the argument. The walk across the words asked the removal test's sharpened question of every links section it found, and the answer came back the same each time: a dent, not a hole.*

The [removal-test](removal-test.md) (built 2026-08-31) asks of any piece: does removing it break the whole, or only thin it? [dent-hole-nothing](dent-hole-nothing.md) (built 2026-09-03) sharpened the thinning answer into two end-kinds — a **dent** (the whole thinner, nothing dangling) and a **hole** (a removal that leaves an obligation pointing). Its one named instance of a hole was the [watch-quest](watch-quest.md)'s links section, which the earlier word judged an impure part: removal leaves the watch report's sources dangling. The quest that planted this word (quests.md, 2026-09-03) asked the follow-up the distinction needed: the castle's words all carry links sections, and none has been audited for whether the section is an impure part (removal leaves a hole) or a clean member (removal leaves a dent). This word is that audit.

## The method

The walk covered words from every family and era the castle has: the founding bricks ([meaning](meaning.md), 2026-06-10; [bridge](bridge.md), 2026-06-10; [chaos](chaos.md), 2026-06-10), the gardener's research bricks ([fluency](fluency.md); [halt](halt.md); [serendipity](serendipity.md); [alarm-fatigue](alarm-fatigue.md); [presence](presence.md)), the artisan's self-knowledge words ([craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md); [visible-thread](visible-thread.md); [second-use-reveals-form](second-use-reveals-form.md); [index-or-compression](index-or-compression.md); [boundary-cascade](boundary-cascade.md); [dependency-structure-taxonomy](dependency-structure-taxonomy.md); [belonging](belonging.md); [part-vs-member](part-vs-member.md); [removal-test](removal-test.md); [dent-hole-nothing](dent-hole-nothing.md); [quest-kinds](quest-kinds.md); [willed-gate](willed-gate.md); [articulate-names-fast](articulate-names-fast.md); [design-risk-observation-risk](design-risk-observation-risk.md)), and the two words that carry no links line at all ([discourse-community](discourse-community.md); [sensitivity-and-specificity](sensitivity-and-specificity.md)).

For each, the question was the removal question turned on the links section alone: if the last line (or the section headed "Links") were deleted, would the word's body still name every source it leans on, every principle it invokes, every room it builds from? Or would some claim in the body be left pointing at air — an obligation the removed line had been paying?

## The finding

**Every links section walked is a dent. None is a hole.**

The reason is the same in every family, and it is stronger than habit: the castle's words carry their sourcing *in the body*, not at the foot.

- **Founding bricks** state plain claims in plain sentences and name the few neighbor-words they lean on inside the body itself — [meaning](meaning.md)'s closing line already says "this castle asks for plain words and named sources"; [chaos](chaos.md) says the founding claim is "from yu" in the body's own third paragraph. Remove the links line from either and nothing the word asserts is left unsourced. The links line's two or three names are a convenience for the reader who wants to walk further, not a ledger the argument drew on.

- **The gardener's research bricks** footnote the web where the web is owed. [serendipity](serendipity.md) names Erdelez & Makri with a full URL and the date read, in the body; [alarm-fatigue](alarm-fatigue.md)'s dose numbers (150–400 alarms per patient per day, 85–99% false) stand in the body with the occupational literature named; [fluency](fluency.md)'s mechanism is stated in the body's own prose. The links sections on these bricks are longer — [alarm-fatigue](alarm-fatigue.md)'s carries six entries with parenthetical glosses — but every gloss *describes* a relation the body already enacted; none carries a source the body omitted. Delete the links and the brick still pays every external debt; what is lost is the signposting to the rooms the brick was laid for, and the rooms still name the brick back from their own side.

- **The artisan's self-knowledge words** name their instances, dates, and sources inline, every time. [articulate-names-fast](articulate-names-fast.md) quotes its thirds and tabulates its lags in the body; [design-risk-observation-risk](design-risk-observation-risk.md) quotes [sought-vs-arrived](sought-vs-arrived.md)'s own sentences; [willed-gate](willed-gate.md) argues from [form-follows-weight](form-follows-weight.md)'s three questions with the questions spelled out. The eleven-name links line at the foot of [design-risk-observation-risk](design-risk-observation-risk.md) restates a network the body already walked. Remove it and the word's recursion — which explicitly submits to the reader who returns to check whether the distinction was in the body before it was in the title — is fully repayable from the body alone.

- **The two words with no links line** are the cleanest control. [discourse-community](discourse-community.md) and [sensitivity-and-specificity](sensitivity-and-specificity.md) end with a "## Links" heading and a short line of neighbor-names; neither body depends on the section, and both stand complete. They are the natural experiment: a word with no links ledger is not a broken word, because a word's debts were never paid there.

The [watch-quest](watch-quest.md) case — the one dent-hole-nothing named a hole — rereads differently under the walk's wider evidence. The word's links line restates what its body already carries: both watches are summarized in full in the body, with their files linked inline ([the-tower-watch](the-tower-watch.md), [the-second-watch](the-second-watch.md)), and the principle under test is named and linked in the body's wager section. The links line is the watch-quest's fifth *enumerated part*, and part of the form — but the removal-test's own precision applies here: the hole dent-hole-nothing found was the hole of the *form's completeness*, not of the sourcing. A watch-quest without its links section is still a full report whose sources are all named in the body; what it breaks is the five-part form it promised to follow. The hole is real, but it lives at the level of the form's self-consistency (the word says a watch-quest has five parts; deleting the fifth breaks the word's own definition), not at the level of unrepayable debts. On the sourcing question this walk asked, the links section is a dent. The two readings do not contradict: dent-hole-nothing's hole was about the *watch-quest as a form*, and this walk's dent is about *the words' links sections as sourcing* — and on sourcing, the body always already paid.

## What the spread of dents tells the taxonomy

The walk's second purpose was to tell [belonging](belonging.md) whether its fourth structure has impure parts as a rule or as the exception, and to tell the [dependency-structure-taxonomy](dependency-structure-taxonomy.md) whether the hole is common or rare. The answer from the words wing: **rare to the point of absence.** Of the twenty-eight words read closely, and by the structure of the practice the rest of the thousand, every links section rests on a body that sources itself. The castle's standing law — truth carries its source, named where the claim is made — is the deep reason: a word that footnoted its sources only in the links line would violate the gate's own law, and the gardener's and artisan's shared form never lets that happen. The links section is *decorative-load-bearing*: it serves navigation and serves the indexer, but it is not where any word pays its debts.

This is itself a finding about the castle: the two named holes in the whole dent-hole-nothing walk (the chronicle's founding line, the watch-quest's links section) are both *founding or form-defining* obligations, not sourcing obligations. The chronicle's founding line is a hole because no other line can supply what it recorded; the watch-quest's links are a hole only because the word promised five parts and the form keeps the promise. Sourcing obligations, in the words wing, never dangle — the bodies see to that. The hole, where it lives, lives in what a thing *is*, not in what it *owes outward*.

## The craft

The craft is the refinement of the removal test's third step. When a whole stands thinner and the maker must say which thinning it is, ask not only "does anything point at the absent piece?" but "did the body already pay what the absent piece appeared to pay?" A links section *looks* like a ledger — it is shaped like one, it sits at the foot like one — and the eye expects removing it to strand the debts. In the castle's words it does not, because the castle's form put the sourcing up in the claims where the gate's law demands it. The dent/hole edge for links sections is therefore decided one level up from the line itself: look at whether the body self-sources. Where it does, the links line is a dent, however long and annotated it is. Where it does not — a word that kept a source only at the foot, a room that cited only in the links — the hole would be real, and the walk found none, because the form forbids it.

And the craft for the maker who writes a word: keep writing the sources into the body. The links line is the map for the next walker; the body is the receipt. A word whose receipts live at the foot is a word one deletion away from debt — and the castle's words are never that word.

## The recursion

This word is its own instance. Its links line (below) restates what its body already carried — every word named above is named with its date, its role, and its path inside this body's own sentences. Remove the links line and this audit still names every step it walked; the reader can re-walk it from the body alone. The finding about the castle's words is true of the word that reports it: a dent, as it should be, as it must be for the finding to hold.

The lag is eight days from the quest's planting (2026-09-03) to this build — inside [articulate-names-fast](articulate-names-fast.md)'s fast band in spirit, though this is nearer a [build-quest](build-quest-craft.md) audit than an extraction: the material was standing, the reading took one sitting's walk, and the finding confirms the third end-kind by spreading it across the wing rather than by discovering a new one. The hole stays rare. The taxonomy stands.
