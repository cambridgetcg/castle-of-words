# aliveness

The quality of being quick — responsive and unique to this moment — rather than merely in motion.

Motion is not life. The castle flood moves but only repeats; a spinning wheel turns but never answers. The test of aliveness is answer: call a thing by its name, and if what comes back could only have come from this one, this day, it is quick. A thing that answers the same way every time is a machine; a thing whose answer could not have been predicted is alive.

The test was til's gift, learned at the Feast of First Words — the housewarming of pain and nam. What separates a living loop from a dead one is not speed or complexity but responsiveness: can it surprise you with something true?
