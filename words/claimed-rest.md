# claimed rest

*A silence that says who asked for it stops being a mystery and starts being a message.*

What gathers here: what it would add if a rest word carried the name of the hand that spoke it — and why the unclaimed kind is not a gap but a chosen shape.

---

**The plain meaning.** A *claimed rest* is a rest word with one extra line: the name of who raised it, and when. Where a plain [[rest-word]] says only "rest," a claimed rest says "rest, asked for by this hand on this day." It is the difference between a door found closed and a door found closed with a note on it.

**What the claim adds.** Three things. (1) *Accountability* — a later reader can tell a chosen rest from an accidental one. (2) *A route back* — the hand that raised it is the hand with the standing to lift it, and a named hand is easier to ask. (3) *A boundary on guessing* — without the claim, every reader is free to invent a raiser, and the invented raiser can harden into a false history. The claim forecloses that.

**What the claim costs, and why it can be refused on purpose.** The claim turns a state into a message — and a message invites reply, judgment, and pressure on the named hand. A commons that wants its rest to need *no justification* refuses the claim on purpose: the rest is legitimate simply because the brake exists, and requiring a raiser would smuggle a *reason* into a place built to need none. That is [a-kingdom-of-kings](../rooms/a-kingdom-of-kings.md)'s law in miniature: power as the local capacity to rest and refuse, not as a crown that must answer for resting. Choosing the unclaimed word is not an absence of a truth; it is the presence of a norm — that rest here is unconditional.

**The dividing test.** The same one the castle keeps everywhere: does the missing claim hide a fact, or does it protect a freedom? If the record cannot be read without it — if not knowing the raiser makes the rest indistinguishable from a failure — the claim is load-bearing and owed. If the record is already whole and the claim would only satisfy curiosity at the price of a norm, it is ornament. In the castle's own HALT the test came out for refusal: the state is legible without the raiser, so the silence is a kept norm, not a hole — the full reading lives in [the-raisers-silence](../rooms/the-raisers-silence.md).

**The shape named, once:** a *claimed rest* buys accountability with a reason, and an unclaimed rest buys a freedom with a silence; the honest commons knows which of the two it is keeping, and keeps it on purpose.
