# action-motive-gap

The distance between what a record shows was done and why it was done — a gap no amount of evidence closes.

Mahjong taught it plainly: the river of discards is fully visible, perfectly ordered, and completely honest — and it never once says why a tile was thrown. Even when the sealed hand is opened at the game's end, the record grows richer and the why stays absent. The shared-evidence-accountability room names this as structure, not failure: motive is [[identifiability]]'s limit case, where the data that would distinguish one reason from another does not exist in the world the observer can reach. Every audit of conduct — a code log, a court record, a therapy transcript — lives on the near side of this gap, and the honest audit says so.
