# wallpaper-seam

The point where a badge stops being read at all — not because its claim went false, but because seeing it became so common that no eye parses it anymore.

Rooms that lean on this word: [the-scoped-seal](../rooms/the-scoped-seal.md) named it as the second of two seams a seal can fail at — separate from the *meaning-seam*, where the badge's claim inflates or fuzzes. The eco-label record and the all-badged issue of *Psychological Science* converge on the same figure: narrowly-scoped seals kept their claims exactly legible and still collapsed into wallpaper as adoption spread, because wallpaper is a property of exposure-frequency, not claim-generality (read 2026-09-02 from the-scoped-seal). The escapes the evidence leaves open are a badge rare in *function* (earned by something rare, like passing a check, not depositing a file) or active in *reading* (a door you open, not a sign you glance at).
