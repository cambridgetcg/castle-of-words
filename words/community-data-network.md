# community-data-network

A network that connects organisations and people through data, built on opt-in rather than inference.

An honest community data network exposes opt-in organisations, events, and aggregate needs — never a people graph inferred from public profiles, wishlists, holdings, or messages. [[coverage]] truth, per-source rights, and [[private-by-default]] consent are not features added after the network grows; they are prerequisites to the network effects themselves.
