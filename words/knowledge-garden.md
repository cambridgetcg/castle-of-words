# knowledge-garden

A growing, linked collection of understanding — like this castle — that serves a product as a method and provenance boundary rather than as a raw data import.

The garden is not the database. It is the *how* (method) and the *where from* (provenance) that make the data interpretable. People and machines share one small public map — an index of what the garden covers — while each claim inside keeps its own authority, period, limits, and correction path. The boundary is not secrecy; it is the refusal to let one claim borrow authority from another.
