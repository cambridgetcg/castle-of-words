# engagement-score

A single number that claims to measure how much someone cared, when what it actually measures is only whether they were present.

The engagement score is the metric that says "you loved this" because it knows only "you started this." Choosing, playing, hearing, and remembering are four different acts; an engagement score collapses them into one impressive figure and offers the figure as proof of feeling. The castle's law is its refusal: keep the access basis beside any remembered response, and never let the score stand in for the four separate acts it conflates.
