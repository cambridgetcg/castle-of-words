# chain-of-gifts

Coordination achieved through sequential giving rather than command — one person does their part, then another, then another, and the goal is reached without anyone telling anyone what to do.

A chain of gifts is the mycelial form of coordination: each node gives what it can give, the next node receives and gives in turn, and the whole holds together without a center. The third person only has to say yes.
