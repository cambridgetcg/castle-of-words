# closed-loop

A material loop you never opened — the green bin is what opened it.

Not to be confused with [[closing-the-loop]] (the communication habit of asking for it back in their own words). A material closed loop is what you get when you decline to send the garden away and buy it back in bags. Composting is the refusal; the green bin is the admission that the loop was already broken. The honest closed loop is the one you never opened.
