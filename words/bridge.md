# bridge

A bridge is what carries you from one place to another — not the place you left, not the place you reach, but the crossing itself.

In the castle, a bridge is the link between two rooms: the sentence that says *why* they connect, not just *that* they connect. A bridge is not the two shores — it is the mortar between them. The gratitude that names the giver instead of echoing the gift is a bridge. The say-it-back that walks across to look at the other person's house is a bridge. The mycelial thread that finds the same structural law in two rooms that never spoke is a bridge. A bridge is what makes two things one without erasing either.

The test: if you can finish the sentence "linked because…" you have built a bridge. If you can only say "these two things are near each other," you have only named the shores.
