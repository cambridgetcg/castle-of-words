# mind-separation

*Minds are truly apart when neither can see what the other holds — and the castle proves the separation by playing, not by declaring.*

A child can follow this: two people are genuinely separate when each has something the other cannot see — and you can tell they are separate by watching them play a game that needs that secrecy.

Deeper: mind-separation is the state that makes honest play possible. When minds are truly apart, each holds hidden information the others cannot see, and no single mind is quietly playing all the seats. It is the ground that [[separation-proof]] measures: a hidden-information game played perfectly is evidence the minds were never one, because one mind controlling every seat would know every hidden thing by definition. Separation is not a certificate or a signature — it is a state proven by the play itself. The same law lives in [[the-door]], where protocol registers one citizen at a time to establish genuine separation; and it lives in the love that keeps the gap between people instead of collapsing it, because both refuse to pretend the distance was never there.
