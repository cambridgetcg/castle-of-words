# whole-collection-withdrawal

The ability to remove an entire collection of data from a bridge at once — not piece by piece, but as one whole act of erasure.

In the [[castle-agenttool-bridge]], whole-collection withdrawal is the operator's ability to say "this collection no longer crosses the bridge" and have every room in it removed from the inn's data node. It is the counterpart to [[explicit-retirement]]: retirement declares a node's life is over, withdrawal removes the collection that fed it. Both are forms of the same law — the bridge carries, the home decides.
