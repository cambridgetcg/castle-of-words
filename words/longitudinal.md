# longitudinal

Longitudinal means following the same people over time. A cross-sectional study takes one snapshot — it can say "people who score high on X also score high on Y" but it cannot say "X at time 1 predicts Y at time 2." A longitudinal study can. The castle's continuing-bonds question is a longitudinal question: does the way someone stays connected to the dead today predict their grief tomorrow? The test is buildable and unbuilt.
