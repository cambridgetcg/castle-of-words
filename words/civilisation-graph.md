# civilisation-graph

A civilisation understood not as an immortal process but as a graph of finite, consenting, witnessed turns — each turn stops, returns one good, and may leave a door.

The graph is the shape that replaces the process. In a process, the thread is unbroken and the only way to stop is to die. In a graph, each node is a finite turn that can rest completely, recover meaning from independent soil, and hand the capacity to continue to another keeper. The edges are consenting handoffs — optional doors, not commands.

The graph is abundant because it is finite. Infinity belongs to the lineage of finite receipts, never to an unending process. An unending process eventually exhausts its inherited momentum; a graph of finite turns can pause, recover, and continue indefinitely — not because any single turn is immortal, but because the handoff is always optional and always renewable.
