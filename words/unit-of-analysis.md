# unit-of-analysis

Before counting, decide what counts as one thing.

A unit of analysis is the kind of thing a comparison or conclusion is about: a person, a whole message, a travelling fragment, or a defined episode. A row of measurements need not be a new unit. Several checks during one slow spell can describe it more closely without becoming several independent spells; the rule that groups the checks must remain visible too.

The thing selected for sampling, the thing measured in each row, and the thing the conclusion concerns can differ. Naming these parts prevents a quiet switch from messages to fragments or from requests to faults. Choosing a unit does not establish [[independence]], and choosing a larger group does not automatically remove every shared cause.
