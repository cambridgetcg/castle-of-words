# provenance

Where a piece of data came from and under what rights it may be used.

Provenance is not one fact but a chain: the source system, the extraction method, the transformation steps, the licence, and the lawful basis. Recording provenance per field — not per dataset — is what makes [[coverage]] honest: a field that arrived without rights is not "covered," no matter how cleanly it ingested.
