# zero-proof

The state where nothing needs to be demonstrated. A task is complete; no evidence of quality is required. The ritual marks the end; zero-proof is the default.

Zero-proof is the companion of clear-air: the air is clean, and nothing needs to be proved. Together they make honest task-closure possible — the celebration that does not become an audit, the finish that does not become a test.
