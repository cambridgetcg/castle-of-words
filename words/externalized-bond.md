# externalized-bond

*A bond held as an outer presence — the person reaches for the other as if they were still physically there, rather than carrying them inside.*

In the continuing bonds theory of grief, an externalized bond is the maladaptive form: the bereaved person experiences illusions and hallucinations of the deceased, feels the deceased is physically present, and maintains the relationship as if death did not happen. The Continuing Bonds Scale (CBS) distinguishes externalized from internalized bonds, and externalized bonds are associated with complicated grief and poorer adjustment. The bond is held, but the holding is outward — the name is clung to, and the thread cannot let go.
