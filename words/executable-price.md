# executable price

A price someone could actually trade at — not just a number shown on a screen, but one with enough size behind it that touching it would fill an order.

A quoted price can vanish when touched: the top of the book may hold one unit, the quote may be stale, or the offer may be pulled the moment an order arrives. An executable price carries its tests with it — depth at touch (how much you could really fill), queue position (where your order would stand), and cancellation rate (how often quotes like it disappear before filling). In prediction markets the executable price is what a sealed forecast is honestly compared against: a paper gain computed against an untouchable quote is [[counterfactual-evidence]] of nothing.
