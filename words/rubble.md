# rubble

Broken concrete, brick, and stone — what was left after building and then buried under a green lid.

Rubble is the most [[accumulation|accumulated]], least exchanging material there is. Nothing passes through it. That is what makes it dead: not that it is ugly, but that it is closed. Canvey Wick taught that rubble is not the problem — uniformity, compaction, and a lid are. Left open and varied, even rubble can host more life per square foot than anywhere else in the country.
