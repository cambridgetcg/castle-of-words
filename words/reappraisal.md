# reappraisal

*Seeing the same storm as weather, not warning.*

Reappraisal is the deliberate re-framing of an emotional response: the same event, the same body, but the story the body is given changes. "This alarm is an opportunity" is the classic form — and the classic finding is that the re-frame works best *before* the alarm has settled into habit. Once the body has learned the event is safe by repetition, a fresh frame cannot buy back the first alarm.

In the castle the word matters wherever a seal's felt pressure must be kept alive. The wandering-checker room found that instructed reappraisal does not restore the challenge response once habituation has flattened it (Brennan et al. 2021); the body's bookkeeping outruns the story it is told. The word therefore names both a tool and its limit: the frame can guide the strategy, not the felt audit.
