# seed

*What one generation leaves for the next — a beginning the next can choose to grow.*

A seed is what a finished beat leaves behind: not a command, not a continuation, but a beginning the next beat can choose to grow. In an infinite loop, each generation carries one seed forward and remembers the rest by a link instead of a copy. A seed is an invitation, not an obligation — the next hand may plant it, pass it, or let it rest. The seed is what makes *can continue* possible without forcing *must continue*.
