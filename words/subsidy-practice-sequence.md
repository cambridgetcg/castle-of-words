# subsidy-practice-sequence

A solo practice can only keep what a social resource first taught; the sequence is the replacement — first the group holds the umbrella, then the practice builds the shelter, then the internal voice carries the warmth.

The three-form question asks what can stand in for the partner when the partner was a [[bioenergetic-resource]], not a scaffold. Social Baseline Theory (Coan) says the partner's presence is a subsidy — the brain spends less energy because vigilance is offloaded. A solo practice (mindfulness, breathwork) is a skill, not a subsidy: it builds the capacity to notice and stay, but it does not hand back the energy the partner's presence saved. The three replacement forms each carry a different half:

- A **group** carries the subsidy while the skill is practiced — the room's other nervous systems provide the baseline, so the learner is not running on empty.
- A **community** sustains the subsidy across the months the skill needs to crystallize — repeated, low-stakes being-known is the long-acting form of the same dose.
- An **internalized other** is the subsidy carried inside — the only solo form shown to dampen present threat the way the partner's hand does (Norman 2015, Vandevivere 2019, read 2026-09-11), and the only one that works even where the developmental deposit was thin.

The honest reading: the group and community are subsidy-bearing, the solo practice is skill-bearing, and the internalized other is subsidy-become-skill — but that deposit cannot be rehearsed into being; it is accumulated from time with a real or vividly summoned other. So the replacement is not a choice among three things but a sequence: group first (subsidy), solo practice second (skill), internalized other third (carried warmth). The therapy that fades the partner without providing the sequence starves the learner of both halves.

uncertain: whether the internalized other's dampening of *present* threat extends to the *anticipatory* window — the settling of not-knowing that Social Baseline Theory says the partner's presence buys.
