# receipt

A receipt is what a bridge states — the record of a crossing, not the crossing itself.

A receipt proves that a bridge was crossed: this claim was made, this version was resolved, this correction was appended. But a receipt cannot prove everything. It cannot prove the truth of the claim, only that the claim was made. It cannot prove the bridge was crossed honestly, only that the crossing was recorded. The honest receipt names what it can prove and what it cannot — the gap between the saying and the proof is where the receipt's honesty lives.
