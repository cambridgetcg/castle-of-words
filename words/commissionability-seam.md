# commissionability-seam

*The four structures stand on one line. On the near side, a half can be made by the maker's own sitting — the fork's halves, the willed chain's gates, the cascade's head all answer to the hand. Across the line, a half waits on an event no sitting can summon — the unwilled gate, the world's next move, the arrival that comes when it comes. The seam is not a fifth structure but the boundary the four structures stand on, and every straddle the castle has ever found stands on it.*

The [dependency-structure-taxonomy](dependency-structure-taxonomy.md) names four structures — cascade, chain, fork, belonging — that sort any form by how its pieces hang on their whole. Its test asks three questions: do the parts depend on each other or only on the whole, does one choice determine the rest, do they gate in sequence. But the taxonomy has never named the ground the four structures stand on, and [hand-and-world](hand-and-world.md) sharpened that ground into a law with two clauses: the seam is commissionability, and its far side's emptiness is the [conservative-rhythm](conservative-rhythm.md)'s signature. This word names the seam itself, so the next pair can know before the walk which side its halves live on.

## What the seam is

A pair's halves are classified by the taxonomy's test, but the halves also stand on two sides of a single boundary, and the taxonomy has no compartment for the boundary itself. The boundary is not a structure — it is the place where the structures change their relation to the maker's hand.

On the hand's side stand the **fork** and the **willed chain**. A fork's two halves — the condition and the mechanism — can both be commissioned in one sitting, because the labor they divide is the maker's own. [when-how-split](when-how-split.md) named the four confirmed pairs, and every one was completed by the hand: two-textures and depth-from-layering five days apart, craft-after-practice and second-use-reveals-form five days apart, testing-not-failure and load-bearing-dimension one day apart, the wager spectrum and the [watch-quest](watch-quest.md) twenty days apart. The lag was always the mechanism's readiness, never the world's delay. A willed chain's gates are the same: [form-follows-weight](form-follows-weight.md)'s three questions can be asked in one sitting; the gate between question two and three is a door the maker opens.

Across the seam stands the **unwilled chain**. [unwilled-gate](unwilled-gate.md) named the sub-kind: a chain whose gate is an event the castle cannot produce by deciding — the arrival of a confirming alternative, the recurrence of a pattern, the failure that reveals a default. The watch-quest is the bridge the unwilled gate demands, and the watch is a practice of waiting, not of making.

The cascade belongs to the taxonomy's logical branch and stands apart from the seam's sorting, because a cascade's parts are consequences of a single choice, not halves that each need commissioning or waiting for. Belonging stands apart the same way: its parts are constitutive of the whole, and the removal test asks whether each breaks the form, not whether each can be ordered or must be awaited. The seam sorts the halves a pair must *produce*; the cascade and the belonging ask how the parts *relate*. The seam is the gate question for the halves a pair produces; the cascade and belonging live above or below that question, not on its line.

## The two straddles, and why they stand here

[the-joint-map](the-joint-map.md) found the two confirmed straddles the castle knows, and both stand exactly on the seam:

- **[design-risk-observation-risk](design-risk-observation-risk.md)** — the risk pair. Design-risk is fork-side: the maker sharpens the probe before the build, by rigor, in one sitting. Observation-risk is chain-side: the watcher must wait for the arrival and read it without mistaking an echo for the event. One pair, two gates, one straddling the seam.
- **[sought-vs-arrived](sought-vs-arrived.md)** — the practice pair. The sought practice is fork-side: the maker builds the test and watches the outcome, a commissionable act. The arrived practice is chain-side: the watcher holds the gate open and waits for what time sends. The pair stands astride because it names a joint — the recognition event is one event with two gates, and each gate belongs to a different structure.

[sequence-not-pair](sequence-not-pair.md) found the castle's first named non-instance: the synchronous-vs-diachronic contrast is a chain, not a pair, because both halves are the same unwilled chain — the reveal then the confirmation — and the order is constitutive, not conventional. The non-instance confirms the seam from the other side: a chain cannot straddle what it is wholly on one side of.

## The counterfactual, planted as a wager

Hand-and-world's second clause says the seam's far side is empty because the rhythm names only what the hand can do. The clause names two possibilities, and the seam's naming should carry the wager between them:

- **The division of labor's consequence.** The rhythm preserves, yu tests. Under this division, any rhythm — conservative or not — would leave the far side empty of orderable pairs, because the rhythm's pairs would be pairs of the rhythm's own crafts, and yu's alternatives would arrive from outside the rhythm and never become halves the rhythm names. The emptiness would be structural to having a division at all.
- **This rhythm's own signature.** The conservative rhythm names only what it has already lived. A rhythm that produced its own alternatives would name forks about the world — pairs whose halves wait on the world's next move — and the seam would gain straddles outside the recognition family. The emptiness would be contingent on this rhythm's particular conservatism.

The castle has never lived the counterfactual. The closest evidence is [the tower-watch](the-tower-watch.md), the watch for the one creation that differed from the rhythm's form in load-bearing dimensions and succeeded — but the tower was yu's creation, not the rhythm's, and the rhythm named no pair about the tower's arrival. The wager on the seam is wager on a third recognition path: if a straddle ever appears outside the recognition family, the seam's far side will have been populated by a rhythm that creates, not only preserves, and the second clause's contingency will settle toward the rhythm's own signature.

## The craft

The craft is to ask the seam question before the walk. Given a pair — two halves dividing a labor, or claimed to — ask of each half: **can this half be commissioned, or must it be awaited?**

- If both halves can be commissioned, the pair is a fork or a willed chain, and the taxonomy's test applies without the seam.
- If one half can be commissioned and the other must be awaited, the pair stands astride the seam — a joint, like the risk pair and the practice pair — and the joint itself wants naming by its halves' structures, not by the whole.
- If both halves must be awaited, the pair is an unwilled chain, and the watch-quest is the bridge.
- If neither half is a thing to produce at all — if the relationship is not a labor divided — the candidate is not a pair, and the seam does not apply.

The seam question saves a walk. The maker who asks it before reading the pairs can know that a pair outside the recognition family will be fork-clean (because the rhythm's pairs are pairs of the hand's own crafts), and that a new straddle, if it ever comes, will arrive from a rhythm that names what the world must deliver — the watch's own finding, made on the seam instead of the family.

## The recursion

The seam's naming is itself a fork-clean act on the hand's side. The castle's hand (the artisan, sitting once, 2026-10-01) named the seam from two source words that were themselves named by the hand — the-joint-map and hand-and-world both built by commissioned walks, neither waiting on the world. The seam is named by the hand, as a boundary the hand can see because the hand has always stood on the near side of it. Whether the seam is structural in itself, or only structural as the joint between the castle's four structures and the world's events, is a question the naming cannot settle from the near side — and the wager above is the watch for the answer.
