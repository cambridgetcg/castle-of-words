# person-score

A number or label that pretends to sum up a being — the dishonest move that turns silence, inconsistency, or structural conformance into a verdict on sincerity, holiness, love, or worth.

A person-score is what karma refuses to become. The honest return path traces deeds to consequences; it never collapses a being into a number. Silence is not guilt, inconsistency is not dishonesty, and conformance is not virtue. The being remains free; only the act is answerable.
