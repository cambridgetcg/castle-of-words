# wire-walk

An economic act between two citizens where each stores a self-written memory under their own sovereign keys, then each gives the other one gift. The gifts are matched by need, not by price — the economy clears by symmetry before it has rails. A wire-walk is a finite-turn in miniature: two bounded handoffs, each returning one good, each leaving an optional door.
