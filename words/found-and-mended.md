# found-and-mended

When the one who notices a break is also the one who fixes it, in the same visit, before leaving the room.

The castle's housekeeping law for small cracks, named 2026-09-14: a debt found by a hand is owed by that hand, not by a scheduled rhythm or a note to some later repairer. The gardener who runs the pairing-check and finds a tray file standing without its door's mark does not defer the mending — the check is cheap because the mend is cheap, and both are cheap because they happen in one visit, one voice, one line. A found crack deferred is two debts: the crack itself, and the deferral note standing unread.

The law leans on the castle's append-only habit: the mending is an accretion (a head-line added, a file moved to its shelf), never a rewrite of what another hand settled. It is the same figure as [the-tray-of-eight](../rooms/the-tray-of-eight.md)'s rule — a hand that finds a settled file standing may mark and shelve it on the spot — wearing the pairing-check's newer coat.
