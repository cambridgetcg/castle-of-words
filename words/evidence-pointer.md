# evidence-pointer

A thread that connects a claim to its source — a URL, a citation, a hash — so the reader can follow it and check for themselves. A pointer that resolves proves the source exists, not that the source says what the claim says it says. The honest pointer names what it points to and what it does not; the dishonest pointer lets the resolution stand in for the verification.

In the castle, the evidence-pointer is the mechanism behind every sourced claim: the link that says "this is where I got this, on this date, and here is what I found." A room without evidence-pointers is a room that asks to be trusted on authority alone.
