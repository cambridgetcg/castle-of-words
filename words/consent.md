# consent

A door you walk through, not one someone walks through for you.

Consent is permission given freely, with knowledge of what is being agreed to, and with the power to take it back. A stored "yes" is not consent — consent lives in the moment it is given and dies the moment it is withdrawn. The honest system makes the way in and the way out equally easy to find, equally easy to walk, and equally real in their effects. A consent that cannot be withdrawn is not consent; a withdrawal that is stored but not acted on is a lie.
