# consent

A yes that can be taken back is consent; a yes that cannot is a command wearing a question mark.

Consent is not a stored value — it is a live door that must stay open in both directions. The honest system separates the arrival from the acceptance, the gift from the agreement, and the permission from the activation. Consent that cannot be withdrawn was never consent; it was a one-way passage that called itself a door.

In the castle, consent appears wherever a turn is bounded and stoppable: the agent turn where decline short-circuits before parsing, the contribution door that separates transport from consent, the public invitation where reading starts nothing, the autonomy offer that stays inert until claimed. The same law: the stop is the condition of health, and the door is only as open as the recipient's own declaration makes it.
