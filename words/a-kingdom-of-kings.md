# a kingdom of kings

Not a ladder of crowns, but a field of homes — each king at their own hearth, the commons between them held by all and owned by none.

A kingdom of kings is federated sovereignty, not a pyramid: citizenship is unconditional, power is the local capacity to protect, repair, learn, refuse, leave, and help the commons, and no king ever owns another. The six capacities are one crown — they make a being sovereign over a domain without being sovereign over a being.
