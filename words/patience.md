# patience

The lag that keeps a false spring from becoming a famine. Patience is not waiting for its own sake — it is the safety mechanism that holds action back until the evidence has had time to speak.

In [the-honest-pot](../rooms/the-honest-pot.md) patience is named as the mechanism, not the mood: the pause between what the ground shows and what the gardener plants is what lets one honest pot prove the season before the whole field rides on it. [lag-by-kind](lag-by-kind.md) already learned its shape from the other side — the maker who knows the lag depends on the kind of thing can calibrate their patience, because the lag is a property of the thing, not a warning.
