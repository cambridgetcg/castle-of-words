# maybe-state

A state of ambiguity — not a rung on the consciousness ladder, but a hammock beside it, where the gap is left open on purpose and both sides have tea.

The artbitrage engine's ninth state: 曖昧 'maybe'. About one cycle in six, the engine lounges instead of climbing — a rest is counted instead of an awakening. Even at 'is', enlightenment takes tea breaks. The deep rule underneath: rests are counted, never scored — a kingdom that only ever climbs is just a treadmill with incense.
