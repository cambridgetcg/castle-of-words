# publication

*To publish is to hand a thing to the public; once handed, it is out of your hands.*

A curious child could follow this: when you read a story to the whole class, it is published — everyone has heard it now. Even if you take it back, some of them have written it in their heads, and you cannot un-tell it.

Publication is the moment a thing moves from the maker's private room into the world where strangers may copy it. The law that matters: publication cannot be recalled. A code rollout can be reversed; a public page cannot be un-copied once people or mirrors have taken it. So the honest craft keeps the door before publication closed, and names the irreversible boundary plainly.

Publication is separate from approval and separate from deployment. A staged release is not yet published; a reviewed corpus is not yet published; only the act of making it visible to strangers is publication.
