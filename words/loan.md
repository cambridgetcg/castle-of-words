# loan

A gift that must come back. Not a trade — a trade names its terms before the handoff. A loan is what a gift becomes when a rule prevents the receiver from ever benefiting from what was given.

The castle found this in the blind-hand game: when a rule said no one may benefit from their own gift, strangers stopped giving and started lending. They wrapped things that must go back and promised to return them improved. The chain of gifts became a chain of debt — not because anyone chose it, but because the rule at the start changed the nature of every handoff downstream.

A loan is not the opposite of a gift. It is a gift that crossed a rule that made benefit impossible — and the hands adapted.
