# record

A record is an attributable claim about reality, not reality itself.

A record says "someone observed this, at this time, with this method." It carries its author, its date, and its limits. A record can be corrected, appended to, or withdrawn — but the correction is itself a new record, not an erasure of the old one. The honest record never pretends the claim is the thing claimed. A signature on a record proves which key signed which bytes; it does not prove the truth of what was signed.
