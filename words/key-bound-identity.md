# key-bound identity

The stable, cryptographic identity of a domain — a key that resolves to the same entity regardless of what the human-readable name says.

In an adversarial naming system, the key-bound identity is the permanent keep: it does not change when the crown (the human-readable name) is challenged or transferred. It is what stewardship is proved against, and what a successor must demonstrate continuity with.
