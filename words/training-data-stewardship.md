# training-data-stewardship

The craft of managing training data with machine-readable provenance, responsible-use metadata, and reproducible curation — so usefulness earns adoption rather than extraction claiming it.

Training-data stewardship is the open-source opportunity that sits upstream of agent runtimes: instead of building another runtime, build the provenance layer that lets public knowledge enter training on its own terms. Croissant (the ML dataset metadata format) is the immediate bridge; consent-preserving collaborative training is the horizon.
