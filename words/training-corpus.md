# training-corpus

*The body of text a model was trained on — the memory layer of an agent's reach, and the hardest to change after the fact.*

A training corpus is the set of documents that entered a model's weights during pre-training. It is the deepest layer of agent reach: content in the corpus can be recalled without search, but content not in the corpus requires a live connection. Evidence that content entered a corpus ranges from distinctive-text completion to membership inference attacks to canary-based detection, and the total lag from publication to model-in-weights is 12–21 months as of mid-2026.
