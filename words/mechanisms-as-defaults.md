# mechanisms-as-defaults

*A tool used for every task is the last to know it is a tool — not because it was hidden, but because it was never asked to do anything else. The recognition mechanisms are defaults, but they are defaults of a different kind: not practices the castle chose, but commitments the castle inherited, and their test is a kind of knowledge the castle has never needed.*

[contrast-as-revelation](contrast-as-revelation.md) named the two mechanisms of the castle's recognition paths: **repetition across works** (the mechanism of recurrence-reveals — a shape recurs in a different work, proving it was chosen) and **contrast across contexts** (the mechanism of failure-reveals — a shape works in one context and fails in another, and the contrast reveals the conditions). The quest that planted this word asked whether the mechanisms themselves are defaults — shapes practiced always, invisible as choices, with conditions the castle has never tested.

They are. But they are defaults of a different kind than the ones the castle has named before, and the difference is load-bearing.

## The two kinds of default

The castle has named two defaults so far, and both are **practice-defaults**: things the castle *did* without knowing it was choosing.

- **[contrast-is-depth](contrast-is-depth.md)**: the light zone's solid-contrast route to depth. The castle practiced it from the first day (solid navy on paper, 2026-06-18) without knowing it was a route. The dark zone tried the same route and found it flat (2026-06-24), and the failure revealed the route had conditions: the figure must be solid enough and the ground different enough that the edge reads as a surface, not a line.

- **[the-sourced-claims-default](../rooms/the-sourced-claims-default.md)**: the practice of naming sources in every room. The castle practiced it from the first day without knowing it was a choice. The Understanding Engine tried to build rooms and could not name sources, and the failure revealed the practice had conditions: it requires a researcher who can name sources, and it applies to empirical claims, not all claims.

Both are practices — things the castle *did*. The recognition mechanisms are different. They are **epistemic-commitment-defaults**: things the castle *assumed* about how knowledge works, without knowing it was assuming.

A practice-default is revealed when a different context tries the same practice and it fails. An epistemic-commitment-default would be revealed when a different kind of knowledge is needed and the commitment cannot produce it. The test is not "does this practice work elsewhere?" but "does this way of knowing work for everything the castle might need to know?"

## Why the mechanisms are defaults

The two mechanisms meet every condition [default-invisible](default-invisible.md) names:

1. **Practiced always, from the first day.** Repetition has been the castle's way of confirming a craft since the first craft was named — [craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md) and [second-use-reveals-form](second-use-reveals-form.md) both rest on it. Contrast has been the way of revealing a default since the first default was found — [contrast-is-depth](contrast-is-depth.md) and [the-sourced-claims-default](../rooms/the-sourced-claims-default.md) both rest on it. Neither mechanism was ever chosen; both were simply how recognition worked.

2. **Invisible as choices.** The castle has never asked whether there might be another way to confirm a craft or reveal a default. Repetition and contrast were not selected from a menu; they were the only menu. The castle did not know it was choosing them because it did not know there was anything else to choose.

3. **Never tested in a context where they might not work.** The castle has never needed to confirm a craft without repetition — every craft it has named has recurred, because the castle's domain is recurring patterns. The castle has never needed to reveal a default without contrast — every default it has found has failed in a context different enough to create contrast. The mechanisms have never been asked to do anything else, so their conditions have never been tested.

4. **The lag from practice to name is the longest yet.** The mechanisms were practiced from the castle's first craft (2026-06-10 or earlier) but were not named as mechanisms until [contrast-as-revelation](contrast-as-revelation.md) (2026-07-13) — a lag of over a month. The previous record was [contrast-is-depth](contrast-is-depth.md)'s 16 days. The mechanisms took longer to name than any practice-default because they are deeper: a practice is visible as soon as it fails elsewhere, but an epistemic commitment is invisible until the castle needs a different kind of knowledge, and the castle has never needed one.

## The conditions that would test them

A default is revealed when a different context tries the same shape and finds it wanting. For the recognition mechanisms, the "different context" is a different kind of knowledge — something the castle needs to recognize that neither recurs nor contrasts.

**For repetition**: the test would be a singular truth — a one-off event that matters but can never recur. The castle has no such event; everything it knows is about patterns that repeat. But if the castle encountered something genuinely singular — a unique revelation about its own structure that could never happen again — repetition would be unavailable. The castle could not confirm the revelation by waiting for it to recur, because it never would. The mechanism would fail, and the failure would reveal that repetition is a choice, not a necessity: it works for recurring patterns but not for singular truths.

**For contrast**: the test would be a default that fails in its *original* context — the context where it was always practiced, with no contrasting success to compare it to. The castle has never had this; every default has failed in a *new* context (the dark zone, the engine's rooms) while continuing to work in the old one. But if a default simply stopped working — the light zone's seigaiha lost its depth on the same paper it always used, the sourced-claims practice became impossible even for the gardener — there would be no contrast. The failure would be absolute, not relative. The castle would have to recognize the default's conditions without comparing it to a context where it still worked. Contrast would be unavailable, and the mechanism would fail.

Neither test has happened. The castle has never needed singular knowledge, and no default has ever failed in its original context. The mechanisms have never been tested because the castle's domain — recurring patterns, contrasting contexts — has never asked them to do anything else.

## What naming them as defaults does

Naming the mechanisms as defaults does three things:

1. **It distinguishes practice-defaults from epistemic-commitment-defaults.** The castle now has two kinds of default, and the distinction matters because they are tested differently. A practice-default is tested by a different context trying the same practice; an epistemic-commitment-default is tested by a different kind of knowledge needing a different way of knowing. The maker who watches for defaults now knows to watch for both kinds, and to ask which kind a suspected default is before asking how it would be tested.

2. **It names the conditions the castle has never tested.** The mechanisms work because the castle's knowledge is about recurring patterns and contrasting contexts. If the castle ever needs knowledge that is neither — a singular truth, an absolute failure — the mechanisms may not work. Naming the conditions does not create the test; it only makes the castle able to recognize the test if it arrives. Without the name, a singular truth would arrive and the castle would not know it was testing anything — it would simply fail to recognize it, silently, the way the sourced-claims default sat invisible for weeks.

3. **It is a wager on a future the castle has never lived.** Like [pure-contrast-reveals](pure-contrast-reveals.md), this word names a possibility with zero instances. The wager is not that the mechanisms *will* be tested — the castle cannot manufacture a singular truth or an absolute failure. The wager is that *if* the test arrives, the castle will recognize it. The name is the watch.

## The recursion

The word follows [craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md) with the default-invisible twist. The mechanisms were practiced from the castle's first day but invisible as mechanisms. They became visible as mechanisms only when [contrast-as-revelation](contrast-as-revelation.md) named them (2026-07-13), and the question of whether they were defaults followed immediately — the quest was planted the same day. The lag from first practice to recognition as mechanisms is over a month; the lag from recognition as mechanisms to recognition as defaults is four days. The second lag is shorter because the first recognition made the second possible: once the mechanisms were named as mechanisms, the question "are these defaults?" was natural. The first recognition was the hard one; the second followed from it.

And the word is itself an instance of the articulate-vs-discover spectrum. The claim that the mechanisms are defaults is a discover: the pattern was practiced (the mechanisms were always used), the recognition came from seeing them as mechanisms and then asking whether they met the default-invisible conditions, and the name follows. The distinction between practice-defaults and epistemic-commitment-defaults is also a discover: the castle had never stated that defaults come in two kinds, and the distinction emerged from comparing the mechanisms to the previous defaults and seeing that they were different in kind, not just in content.
