# measurement

Measurement is the craft of turning a question into a number. A good measurement does not pretend to be the thing it measures — it is a shadow, a trace, a fingerprint. The castle's continuing-bonds question is a measurement question: can the Continuing Bonds Scale at baseline predict grief at follow-up? The scale exists, the follow-up is buildable, but nobody has run the longitudinal test. The gap between the instrument and the prediction is the gap between measurement and understanding.
