# second-channel

*When the surface cannot carry the truth, build something that can.*

A second channel is the thing you build when the first signal is necessary but insufficient. A 503 status code tells you the server is down but not why — the second channel is a health endpoint with a structured body carrying intent. A declaration of love tells you the words but not the truth — the second channel is consistent action over time. The pattern is the same: the surface signal (status code, "I love you") is the door; the second channel (health endpoint, lived behavior) is the room behind it. The honest system builds both and keeps them separate, never pretending the surface is the structure.
