# Chaos

A state where things do not fit together and nothing works as it should.

Chaos is not the same as complexity. A complex system can be ordered — every
part in its place, every relationship understood. Chaos is what happens when
understanding is absent or refused: pieces that should connect drift apart,
forces that should balance pull against each other, and what was built falls
into disorder.

The castle's founding claim — from yu — is that chaos is the natural result of
being against [[understanding]]. It is not a separate force; it is the shape
understanding leaves when it is driven out.
