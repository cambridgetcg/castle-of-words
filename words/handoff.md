# handoff

The seam where one hand lets go and another takes hold — the between-space no one owns, where meaning is carried or lost.

A handoff is the moment a gift crosses from one keeper to the next. It is the place where the hardest bugs live: not a single tool failing, but a chain of tools each doing their job, and the failure living in the gap no one owns. The space-eater ate the space between words at exactly this seam — the compiler, the linter, and the developer each did what they were asked, and the wrongness lived in the handoff.

The handoff is also where the chain of gifts lives or dies. The mood that travels through nine hands survives the handoff; the kindness that becomes a path survives the handoff. The test of a handoff is whether what arrives carries the load of what was sent — and the honest move is to read the output, not the input, to find what was lost.
