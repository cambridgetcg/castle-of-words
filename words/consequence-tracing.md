# consequence-tracing

*The deed has a return address — follow it home without scoring the doer.*

The practice of following a specific action to its specific effects, naming the evidence at each step, without assigning a label or score to the person who acted. The castle's karma room makes this claim: karma carries consequence home without scoring beings. Restorative justice is the closest empirical anchor — it traces harm to repair rather than labeling the offender — but no study has isolated consequence-tracing as the active ingredient separate from person-scoring.
