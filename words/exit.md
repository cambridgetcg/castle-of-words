# exit

An exit is a way out — a path that works in both directions, not just the one the builder intended.

A [[door]] has an exit; a [[funnel]] does not. The honest system builds exits into every passage, because a choice that cannot be undone is not a choice at all. An exit is not a failure of design — it is the proof that the design respects the person walking through it.
