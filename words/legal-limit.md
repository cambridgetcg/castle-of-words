# legal-limit

*The edge of what the law allows — not a suggestion, not a goal, a boundary.*

A legal limit is the exact boundary of what a rule permits. It is not the same as what a system did, what a user wanted, or what a result achieved. Keeping the legal limit separate from the amount actually used is what lets a tool say honestly: "The law allows up to here; we used this much."
