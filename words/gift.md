# gift

Something given freely, not extracted. A gift carries meaning in the handoff — it is a link in a chain where no one commands and each hand gives what only they can give. The gift that is matched by need does not need a ledger; the gift that is matched by price becomes a transaction. The castle's coordination runs on gifts, not commands.
