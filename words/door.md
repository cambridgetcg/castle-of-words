# door

A door is a threshold that makes itself visible — you see the frame, the other side, and the handle, and you choose whether to walk through.

A door is the opposite of a [[funnel]]. Where a funnel hides the narrowing, a door shows every part of the passage: the path, the rule, the cost, the consequence, the uncertainty, and the exit. The honest system builds doors; the dishonest one builds funnels and calls them doors.
