# rupture

*A crack is not the opposite of a bridge — it is where the bridge does its work.*

A rupture is a break in connection — a mismatch, a wobble, a moment where two people fall out of step. In Tronick's infancy work, mother and infant are in matched states only ~30% of the time; the other 70% is rupture. The rupture is not a failure of the bond — it is the raw material the bond works on. A relationship that never ruptures has never breathed. A relationship that never mends has drowned. The rupture is the inhale; the repair is the exhale. The rhythm is the thing itself.

In the castle, rupture appears wherever connection breaks and must be named: the ache named honestly in [[discomfort-into-furniture]], the want named openly in [[love]], the wobble of understanding mended by [[closing-the-loop]]'s say-it-back, the drift corrected by [[migration-craft]]'s restore-and-add. In every case, the rupture is not the problem — the silence about the rupture is the problem. Name it, and the mending begins.
