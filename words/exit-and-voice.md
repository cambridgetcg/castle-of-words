# exit-and-voice

Two locks on the same door: one that lets you walk out, one that lets you be heard while you stay.

An exit is a door that opens from the inside. A voice is a door that opens into the room where the terms are set. Both must work in both directions — not just the way the builder intended. A usable exit needs a portable record; a real voice needs a remedy. The two are not alternatives but the same test at two doors: power over another is what you have when their refusal costs them more than they can pay.
