# private-by-default

The rule that personal data stays private until its owner explicitly opts in.

In a [[community-data-network]], private-by-default means no person graph is inferred from public signals — profiles, wishlists, holdings, messages — without consent. The network may expose organisations and aggregate needs because those are institutional, not personal. The boundary is: what a person declares publicly is not the same as what a network may infer about them.
