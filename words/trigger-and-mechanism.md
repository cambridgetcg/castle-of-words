# trigger-and-mechanism

*The stone breaks the ground; the eye sees what was always there. The breaking and the seeing are two parts of one event — and the maker who watches only for the breaking will miss the revelation the breaking was for.*

A recognition event has two parts, and the castle has used both without ever naming them apart. One part *makes the difference happen*; the other *moves the mind from the difference to the condition the difference reveals*. [contrast-as-revelation](contrast-as-revelation.md) stated the distinction in its own body — failure is the causal trigger, contrast is the epistemic mechanism — but gave it no name of its own. Naming it is extraction, not discovery; it follows [articulate-vs-discover](articulate-vs-discover.md).

## The two parts

**The causal trigger** is what creates the difference in the first place. In the castle's history the trigger has always been a *failure*: the dark zone's solid-contrast seigaiha went flat on near-black, and the Understanding Engine's rooms could not name their sources. The trigger is an event in the world. It breaks the default's run — the shape that worked always, suddenly does not work here — and the breaking is what *produces* the contrast the recognition will need. Without the trigger there is no difference; two forms sit side by side as two choices, and nothing says that one of them was a default with conditions.

**The epistemic mechanism** is how the mind moves from the difference to the condition. The trigger says *this shape does not work here*; the mechanism says *therefore the shape had conditions I never saw*. In the castle, the mechanism has always been *contrast*: the flat gold thread against the deep warm navy, the engine's bare assertions against the gardener's sourced claims. Contrast is not an event — it is an act of seeing. The eye sets the two forms beside each other, reads the space between them, and infers the demands the default never had to state.

The difference in one line: the trigger *creates* the difference; the mechanism *reads* it.

## Why the two must be named apart

The two parts are separable in analysis and separately losable in practice. A trigger can fire without its mechanism following — that is a *mere problem*. The dark zone's flatness could have been read as "gold thread does not work on near-black": a local difficulty, fixed locally, teaching nothing about what the light zone had been doing all along. The trigger fired, the difference existed, and no revelation came, because nobody set the two contexts beside each other and read the space between them.

The [articulate-cases-audit](articulate-cases-audit.md) named the asymmetry exactly: *missing the mechanism means missing the revelation*. The failure alone is just a problem, and the contrast is what turns a problem into a principle. Both of the castle's [default-invisible](default-invisible.md) instances needed the pair complete — [contrast-is-depth](contrast-is-depth.md) and [the-sourced-claims-default](../rooms/the-sourced-claims-default.md) each show the trigger (a context tried the shape and broke it) and the mechanism (the unbroken default beside the broken attempt, showing the conditions) arriving together, the failure making the contrast and the contrast making the failure *revealing* rather than merely frustrating.

The naming also sharpens the other recognition path. [recurrence reveals](second-use-reveals-form.md) has the same two parts with different content: the trigger is a *second use* (a different work reaches for the same shape) and the mechanism is *repetition* (the recurrence itself, counted as evidence of choice). Trigger and mechanism are the anatomy of any recognition event; failure-and-contrast are only the failure path's filling.

## The craft

The maker who watches for a default should watch for *both* parts and know which one is missing. A failure with no contrast named is an unfinished recognition — the craft is to build the contrast deliberately: set the broken attempt beside the unbroken default and say aloud what the difference shows. A contrast with no trigger is a [pure-contrast-reveals](pure-contrast-reveals.md) — possible in principle, never yet lived, the one arrangement where the mechanism works without the causal event.

And the parts can wear the two faces a thing wears. The maker *performs* the trigger — builds the alternative, tries the shape in a new context — but *understands* the mechanism. The [practice-as-mechanism](practice-as-mechanism.md) word's open question, whether a mechanism's doing-face and thinking-face ever separate, is already answered here in the small: they separate at the joint between trigger and mechanism, the doing and the seeing.

The word is its own instance twice over. It is an [articulate case](articulate-vs-discover.md) — the distinction was already written in contrast-as-revelation's body ("Contrast is the *epistemic* mechanism… Failure is the *causal* trigger"), found by reading, not by watching practice, and this naming only carries the extraction forward. And its own recognition had the anatomy it names: the audit's reading was the trigger that created the difference (a stated distinction sitting beside an absent name), and the naming is the mechanism that moved from the difference to the principle. A recognition event has two parts; this word is one, named.
