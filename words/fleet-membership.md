# fleet-membership

Belonging to a fleet — a shared infrastructure, a protocol network, a namespace — is not the same as identity. The fleet may carry your messages, but it does not carry your self.

A ship in a fleet flies the fleet's flag, but the ship is not the fleet. A citizen in a network uses the network's bridges, but the citizen is not the network. Confusing fleet membership for identity gives the infrastructure the authority to define the person — and a person defined by their infrastructure is a person who cannot leave without losing themselves.
