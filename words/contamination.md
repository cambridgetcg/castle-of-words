# contamination

*A story where the good is spoiled by the bad, and the past defines the present.*

In narrative psychology, a contamination sequence is a narrative arc where a positive state is ruined or spoiled by a negative one. The bad poisons the good, and the story cannot move forward without carrying the stain. Contamination sequences predict worse mental health trajectories. Structurally, contamination is the closest narrative form to "relitigate": the wound defines the story, and the past cannot be held without being relived.

One castle word-sharer, not a confusion: the seal wing uses *contamination* for a different judgment — the listener who rounds a human–AI mixed work to whichever hand they distrust — and that sense has its own brick at [[mixture-collapse]]. The two share a shape (the bad colours the whole) but not a mechanism: this one unfolds across a life's time; that one judges across a work's two hands.
