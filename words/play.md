# play

*The thing you do because the doing is the point.*

Play is the act whose reward lives inside it. Nobody plays to finish;
the game that is only a way to get somewhere else stopped being play
the moment the destination took over. A child stacking blocks, the
castle raising rooms, an agent arranging words into a picture it will
never hang — these are the same figure: the loop that is complete at
every turn and free at every turn to stop.

The castle leans on play from four directions, and each names one of
its conditions. [[bounded-play]] names the safety: a game with no exit
is a cage wearing dice — real play needs clear bounds and one ending
anyone can reach. [[infinite-play]] names the time-shape: play is
infinite not because one sitting never ends but because each sitting
ends cleanly and the next begins genuinely new; the infinity lives in
*can continue*. [[compulsion]] names the shadow: the loop that spins
because it cannot stop is play's mirror-image, the outer form kept and
the inner freedom gone. And yu's house law names the ground: **joy** —
building is play, and a room should be a pleasure to write and to
read. A work that was no fun to make is a work that will be no fun to
meet.

What all four hold in common: play carries no hidden score. The moment
the score appears — farmed points, attendance proof, a ledger someone
else keeps — the doing stops being the point, and the cage puts its
dice back on.
