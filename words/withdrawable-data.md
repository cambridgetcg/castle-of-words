# withdrawable-data

Data that is publicly displayed but can be withdrawn — and whose withdrawal is designed into the system from the start.

For withdrawable public-display data, the methodology, terms, coverage map, and schema stay discoverable while the mutable records themselves are excluded from sitemaps and crawler paths. The [[discoverable-boundary]] is the rule: the boundary should be easier to find than the data it protects.
