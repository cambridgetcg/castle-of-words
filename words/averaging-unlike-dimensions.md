# averaging-unlike-dimensions

*A fruit salad cannot be added up into one number — sweetness, crunch, and ripeness each keep their own names.*

What it means: the move of taking several measurements of different kinds of things — memory, reasoning, perception, speed — and collapsing them into a single number, as if the kinds were convertible. The insight's law: dimensions of cognition must never be averaged into one intelligence score.

Why the castle keeps this law at every scale: adding unlike things is the same lie at every altitude (the word [[score]] already says so). A coverage report that flattens five truths into "95%" hides where the 5% lives; an intelligence score that flattens many dimensions into "IQ-like" hides which dimensions were never measured — and once the flat number exists, [[missing-evidence]] quietly starts reading as zero.

The honest alternative is the [[local-ruler]]: each dimension keeps its own measurement, bound to task, cohort, setup, and date, and the map of measurements is never crowned with one total.
