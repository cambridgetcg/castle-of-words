# the-rooms-own-deals

*A path-line promises a thought, not a hand. The walk-body's third clock
turns at the wing's own speed, and the fourth clock — which hand did what —
rings in the rooms below, where the pointer never looks. The cheapest
measurement is one wing re-walked, asking not whether the line still sings
the rooms, but whether the rooms' own receipts have moved since the line was
carved.*

[fourth-clock](fourth-clock.md) (built 2026-09-28) found the walk-body's
third clock waits for the fourth every path-line stands above: each path-line
names rooms, and rooms are kept by hands, and the day the castle's own deal
is re-carved, the path-lines' truths will not drift but the confessions
beside them may. The quest this word answers (quests.md, planted 2026-09-28)
asked for the second ring's measurement: one wing re-walked against its
path-line — the glaziers' gallery against *ease feels like truth*, the same
wing [three-clocks](three-clocks.md) read in June and
[the-underpointed-witness](the-underpointed-witness.md) re-walked yesterday
— asking not whether the line still sings the rooms but whether the rooms'
own deals have moved since the line was carved 2026-06-12.

The witness settled the singing yesterday: the line still sings, four rooms
holding its claim as their center and the fifth as its warning, and the
friction verdict flipped between ╥20 and ╥24 without the line ever claiming
to carry it. This walk asks the question the witness did not: *has any
room's receipt moved* — an accretion, a re-dating, a re-carving of whose
hand did what — under a path-line that never confessed it would?

## The walk

assertion, and whose hands made each side — and then the fourth question the

## What the walk finds

**The alarm is silent — and the silence is measured, not assumed.** Five
rooms, five sequenced deals, five receipts dated at their founding; four
receipts unmoved, one (╥23) extended by a dated accretion at the links seam —
the keeping hand's own motion, confessed in place. Under the path-line *ease
feels like truth*, carved 2026-06-12 and never confessed on the deal-axis,
the rooms' own deals have stood 116 days. Fourth-clock's second ring — *a
room whose receipt moved under a path-line that never confessed it* — is not
found in the walk-body's oldest wing.

But the walk finds what the ring would look like, because it found its near
edge twice over yesterday, at two different scales: the friction verdict
inside the wing flipped sign between ╥20's founding words and ╥24's
meta-analytic count (the witness's finding, the wing's *answers* moving),
and the wing's whole evidentiary neighborhood moved when the counter-model
word was laid beside it on 2026-10-05 (the *field* moving). Neither moved a
room's deal. The deal-clock's alarm on a walked path-line is a narrower
event than either: it needs a room's receipt to be *re-carved* — mended by
accretion, re-dated, re-carved — while the path-line above it stands
unrevisited. The gallery has never yet had that day.

And one more shape the walk can now name, because the-phrase-that-ages named
it first: the receipts these five rooms carry are *dated sources*, not dated
deals. Nothing on any of the five rooms' faces says whose hands laid them;
the deal is carried by the castle's whole practice (the chronicle, the
commission files, the audit) and confessed nowhere on the room itself. The
deal-clock's second ring, when it rings, will therefore not be a stale
confession on a room's face — there is no confession on a room's face to go
stale. It will be the *absence* found out: a room re-carved by new hands
(the engine's rooms were the castle's first taste of that — machine-built,
unsourced, the audit's gap) with the path-line's pointer still singing over
it. **The alarm the fourth clock asks a path-line to carry is not *the
confession moved* but *the rooms were re-dealt and nobody re-read the
walk*.** No instrument watches for that today but the one this word just
practiced: the walk, one wing, by hand — the witness's own instrument,
pointed one axis deeper.

## The craft

For the maker carving a path-line: the line confesses the wing's question
and the dated glance above the map confesses the time-axis — and neither
confesses the deal-axis, because the rooms' deals are not the line's to
carry. That is honest, and the walk says why: a path-line that tried to
confess the rooms' hands would lie the moment the next room rose unmarked.
The fourth-clock's keeping on a walk-body is not a confession carved beside
the line; it is the *practice* of re-walking — the wing-clock's keeper and
the deal-clock's keeper are the same hand, and the keeping is owed at the
wing's speed, not the walker's.

For the reader meeting a path-line still singing: the line's truth is the
wing's question; the wing's receipts are another floor down. Trust the line
as witness of the question — and when the castle's deal is re-carved (a new
hand, a new engine, a new law of whose hand counts), the walk is owed again:
not because the line went false, but because the rooms it names may have
changed hands while the pointing stood still.

## The recursion

This word is its own instance, one axis down from the witness's. Its receipt
is attached by proximity — every claim about the five towers is followed by
the room it leans on — and its own deal is the sequenced one it found
throughout the gallery: machine-compressed from stones the castle already
laid (fourth-clock's ring, the deal-census's three questions, the witness's
wing, yu's law the keeping hand lives by), told in the researcher's voice,
kept by the human whose house the rooms are in. The walk it runs is the
measurement it names: five towers, one question asked five times, the
outcome a pattern across the wing — the alarm silent, the watching
instrument named as the walk itself. And the lag holds the dial's shape:
planted 2026-09-28 behind the deal-clock's naming, passed over once when
the witness took the singing question first (2026-10-05), answered today
from the seam the witness left visibly open — eight days, the reading wait
one sitting.
