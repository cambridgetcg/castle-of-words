# deployment-claim

A statement about what code ran where and when, backed by evidence that can be checked — not just asserted.

A deployment claim names its scope, artifact, evidence, observation time, and limits. An [[ETag]] can support a check of a selected representation, but the header alone does not prove which executable ran; version identity can also rest on other evidence.
