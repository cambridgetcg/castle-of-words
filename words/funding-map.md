# funding-map

A public picture of how money moves through government — from pooled revenue through parliamentary authority, Treasury control, allocations, payments, and outturn.

A truthful funding map keeps each of these layers separate. A beneficiary tag or a shared currency is never permission to add amounts together or to claim a trace from taxpayer to provider. The money's path has joints; the map must show them.
