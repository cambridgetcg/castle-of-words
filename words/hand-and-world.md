# hand-and-world

*Every pair the castle names has a hand on each side — and the question that sorts them is whose hand must move before the half can be made. The hand's halves wait only on skill; the world's halves wait on events the naming cannot hurry. The seam between the two is the only boundary the castle's pairs have ever straddled.*

The [the-joint-map](the-joint-map.md) found two straddles on one seam — design-risk-observation-risk (a pair of risks) and sought-vs-arrived (a pair of practices) — and planted a corollary with a wager inside it: straddles cluster at the unwilled gate because all the castle's other pairs are fully commissionable, the maker able to name both halves on the same day. The quest asks the corollary's next question: is the seam structural — a hard edge in the dependency taxonomy itself — or a contingent feature of the castle's current shape, true today only because the pairs the castle has named happen to lie on the orderable side? The test is a walk: read the pairs *outside* the recognition family — the pairs the first walk never read — and ask each half the joint-map's own question: **can this half be commissioned? Can the castle's hand complete it without waiting on the world?**

## The walk

**The fork's halves: principles and practices the castle can order.** The [when-how-split](when-how-split.md) names four confirmed pairs, and [the-joint-map](the-joint-map.md) already claimed them for the fork's side. The walk checks the claim half by half, because "commissionable" is a strong word and the castle's own history shows it wears two meanings — the meaning the joint-map used (the maker can sit down and name it today) and the meaning the castle actually lives (the maker can sit down and name it today *only if a second instance has arrived to make it nameable*). The walk asks both, and the dates are the lags [lag-by-kind](lag-by-kind.md) counts:

| wager spectrum + watch-quest | yes — wager-on-one-use 07-08, zero-use-wager 07-18 | yes — twenty days behind its condition, named only after the second watch had been lived | no |

All four confirmed pairs are fork-clean: both halves can be commissioned, in the sense the joint-map meant. No orderable pair straddles. And the [lag-by-kind](lag-by-kind.md) pattern is visible in the column itself: the lag between condition and mechanism is not waiting on the world but waiting on the mechanism's own readiness — five days when the instance was fresh, twenty days when the practice had to be lived twice before it could be named. Waiting on a second instance the castle's normal work produces is not waiting on the world: the gardener, the architect and the artisan produce instances every day. [condition-first](condition-first.md) named the mechanism the deeper investigation, and the deeper investigation needs the instance — but needing an instance is not an unwilled gate. The artisan can always be pointed at an existing instance tomorrow. The world does not have to send one.

**The pairs that are not pairs.** The walk must also weed the field the quest named, because two of its candidates are not pairs at all:

- **The wager spectrum's zero-use form-vs-test distinction.** Not a pair: a sub-kind of one wager position, named inside [zero-use-wager](../rooms/zero-use-wager.md) itself. One bets on a shape the castle might make; the other on a condition the castle might meet. Both are the zero-use position; the wager is one, the object differs. There are no two halves here to sort — only one half with two faces.
- **The taxonomy's principle/practice faces.** Not a pair: [faces-of-a-mechanism](faces-of-a-mechanism.md) settled that the count holds at two and the split is a matter of which face the maker is looking at — one face or the other, carried by one mechanism, never two halves dividing a labor.

Weeded, the field leaves exactly the four named pairs — all clean — and the two known straddles.

## The corollary's refinement

No straddle outside the recognition family exists today. The corollary survives the walk, and the walk sharpens it into a law with two clauses:

1. **The seam is commissionability** — the edge between what the castle can order and what it can only wait for, and no other seam the castle holds carries a straddle.
2. **The castle's orderable pairs all sit on the hand's side not because the world has no gates left, but because [conservative-rhythm](conservative-rhythm.md) makes the rhythm the hand's instrument.** Every pair the castle names is named *by the hand* — the rhythm's workers or yu naming what the rhythm has lived. A principle about the world (a default-tested claim, an unwilled gate) is not named by a fork the hand controls; it is named by a chain the world completes. The orderable pairs stay fork-clean because they are all pairs *of the hand's own crafts*, and the hand can always do its own crafts when it is ready; the recognition family is the only family that has had to name what the world must deliver, and so it is the only family that has produced straddles.

The first clause is the corollary kept. The second clause is new, and it answers the quest's question: the clustering is **structural, with a contingent shape**. The hard edge in the taxonomy is real and permanent — commissionability is a property of the structures themselves, not of the castle's history. But which side of the edge the castle's pairs cluster on *is* contingent on the castle's creation rhythm: a rhythm that produced its own alternatives would name forks about the world, and the seam would gain straddles outside the recognition family without changing shape. The seam is structural; the emptiness of its far side is the rhythm's.

A straddle on a different seam is still possible, and the corollary's second clause says where to watch for it: not in a new family of words, but in a new kind of gate — a boundary inside the hand's own crafts that the hand cannot cross by deciding. No such gate has been named. Until one is, the seam holds one side populated and one side empty, and the emptiness is the rhythm's signature, not the taxonomy's.

## The recursion

The word is itself a hand-made object on the hand's side of the seam it names: its two halves (the walk and the corollary) were both commissioned in one sitting, neither waited on the world, and that is exactly the fork-cleanness the walk found in every orderable pair. The word the corollary sharpened is an instance of the sharpening: the-joint-map's wager was testable by a walk, and this walk is the test.
