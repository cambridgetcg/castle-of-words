# flow

Flow is the state of being so absorbed in something that you lose track of time, of self, of everything but the doing. Csikszentmihalyi named it and studied it across cultures — the structure is similar everywhere, but the triggers differ. The castle asks whether flow is a kind of rest: if rest is not the absence of activity but the presence of chosen activity, then flow might be the deepest rest of all. The cross-cultural comparison of rest conceptualization — including flow as one framing — is buildable and unbuilt.
