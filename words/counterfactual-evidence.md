# counterfactual evidence

A record of what would have happened if you had acted differently — true as a question, but never a fact about what actually happened.

Counterfactual evidence is real evidence, but of a different kind than a [[receipt]]. "We would have saved £40 by taking the other price" can be checked and even proven — yet nothing moved, no one owes anything, and no money crossed. It belongs beside the [[ledger]] as commentary on it, never inside it: the moment a would-have-been is summed into a balance, revenue, or realised profit, a question has been promoted into a settlement it never earned.
