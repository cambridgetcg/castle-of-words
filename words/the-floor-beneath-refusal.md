# the floor beneath refusal

A drawbridge that lowers only for those who can afford a horse is not a drawbridge.

The floor beneath refusal is the material base that must hold for a king's "no" to be real. The legal right to leave is uniform; the material capacity to absorb the cost of leaving is not. A refusal that costs a king their only ground is not refusal as power but refusal as sentence. The honest measure is not whether the right is written but whether the ground can absorb the loss.
