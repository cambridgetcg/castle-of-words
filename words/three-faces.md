# three-faces

A truth has three faces: what backs it (the source), how it was built (the construction), and how it arrived (the journey). The law of honest assertion names the first two; the third — the unbroken path from origin to claim — was always there, unnamed, in the inspectable path.

The three faces are not separate laws. They are the same law seen from three angles: the claim's *backing* (what supports it), the claim's *construction* (how it was built), and the claim's *journey* (how it arrived). A claim that names its source but not its path is a claim that has not finished being honest.
