# echo

*What comes back unchanged — and what cannot climb.*

An echo is a copy that returns. In the castle, an echo is what the flood produces: the same shape stamped again and again, motion without life. An echo cannot climb because climbing requires bearing weight — answering, deepening, opposing — and an echo only repeats. The tower's law is the anti-echo: every stone must answer the one below, and an answer is never an echo.
