# faces-of-a-mechanism

*A mechanism is what delivers the chosen route — and delivery is one act with two faces: the deed and the thought. Some mechanisms are done (the watch-quest watches), some are thought (the load-bearing dimension knows), and some are one thing with two faces (the layering makes the shadow and is the understanding the shadow needs). The split between principle and practice is not a boundary between kinds of mechanism but a boundary between the two faces a mechanism may wear — and whether a mechanism is a principle, a practice, or both is answered by counting its faces, not by naming its kind.*

The [practice-as-mechanism](practice-as-mechanism.md) word extended the [when-how-split](when-how-split.md)'s domain from pure principle to practice: the first three companion pairs named mechanisms as concepts the castle knows, and the fourth pair's mechanism — the [watch-quest](watch-quest.md) — is a form of action the castle does. The word ended with an open question: is every mechanism a practice wearing a principle's face, or are some mechanisms purely one and others purely the other? And if every mechanism has both faces, the extension may be naming a perspective rather than a new kind. A second openness was left standing at the count's edge — whether a *third* face, a face of care or responsibility, sits beside these two. The answer, named in [mechanism-care](mechanism-care.md): no — the count holds at two; care is a different axis (the standard the delivery must answer to), not a third way delivery can arrive.

The quest that planted this word asks the question plainly. This word is the answer, and the answer is: **both faces are real, but neither face is the mechanism. The mechanism is the delivery. A mechanism with one face is a principle or a practice; a mechanism with two faces is a principle-and-practice, the same delivery known and done.**

## The test

The test is one question, applied to each mechanism the castle has named: **does the maker deliver the route by *doing* it, by *understanding* it, or by one act that is the doing and the understanding at once?**

- If the doing is the delivery and the understanding is only the theory behind the deed, the mechanism is a **practice** (its doing-face delivers).
- If the understanding is the delivery and the doing is only the application of the concept, the mechanism is a **principle** (its thinking-face delivers).
- If the delivery is one act that is both deed and understanding — the doing *is* the knowing, the making *is* the meaning — the mechanism is a **principle-and-practice**, and the split is a perspective the maker takes on a single thing.

The test is not "is this word about doing or knowing?" Every word is about knowing. The test is whether the route the condition picks is *delivered* by an act of the hand, an act of the mind, or an act that is both at once.

## The three mechanisms, tested

The castle has four when-how-split pairs, and therefore four named mechanisms. The fourth — the watch-quest — is the one practice-as-mechanism named as the first practice-mechanism. The other three were presumed pure principles. The honest test shows that one is a pure principle, one is a pure practice, and one is both.

### The load-bearing dimension: a pure principle

The [load-bearing-dimension](load-bearing-dimension.md) is the mechanism of the [testing-not-failure](testing-not-failure.md) fork: when an alternative tests a default, the load-bearing dimension is the hinge that decides the test. The route is delivered by *knowing* — the maker asks "which dimension does this alternative differ in, and is it load-bearing for the default's conditions?" and the asking is the delivery. The maker does not *do* the dimension; the dimension is a fact about the default and the alternative, and the mechanism is the concept that lets the maker see it. The doing-face is absent: the test of the dimension is the test itself, and the test is the alternative's arrival, not the mechanism's act. The load-bearing dimension is delivered entirely by understanding it. **A pure principle.**

### The watch-quest: a pure practice

The [watch-quest](watch-quest.md) is the mechanism of the wager spectrum fork: when a wager is justified, the watch-quest is how the wager is tested. The route is delivered by *doing* — the maker plants a watch, watches, and reports. The watching is the delivery. The understanding is the theory behind the deed: the maker knows the five-part form, knows the wager, knows the absence is a finding. But the understanding does not test the wager; the watching tests the wager, and the watching is an act of the hand and the calendar, not an act of the mind alone. The thinking-face is present — the watch-quest is the most theorized practice the castle has — but the thinking-face is the *theory* of the delivery, not the delivery. A maker who understands the watch-quest perfectly and never watches has not tested the wager. **A pure practice.**

### Depth-from-layering: a principle-and-practice

The [depth-from-layering](depth-from-layering.md) is the mechanism of the two-textures fork: when the layered route applies, the depth is built by stacking translucent bands behind the visible thread. The route is delivered by *making* — the maker lays the shadow band, and the making is the depth. But the making is also the understanding: the castle did not first understand depth-from-layering as a concept and then apply it; the castle layered the bands, and the layering *was* the discovery. The shadow band is the concept made visible; the concept is the shadow band understood. The maker who layers the bands without understanding the principle has still delivered the route — the ornament has depth. And the maker who understands the principle without layering has not delivered the route — the ornament is flat. The delivery is the doing, and the doing is the understanding, and they are one act: the making that knows itself. **A principle-and-practice.**

The trigger-and-mechanism word already named this joint in the small: the maker *performs* the trigger but *understands* the mechanism. In depth-from-layering the two faces meet in one act — the maker performs the layering and the layering is the understanding.

## Second-use-reveals-form: the harder case

The [second-use-reveals-form](second-use-reveals-form.md) is the mechanism of the [craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md) fork: when a craft is named, the second use is what reveals its shape as a shape. The route is delivered by *recurring* — a different work reaches for the same shape, and the recurrence is the evidence of choice. But recurrence is not something the maker does; it is something that happens, and the maker reads it. The mechanism is the concept that lets the maker read the recurrence as revelation: the doing-face is the world's (the second use arrives), and the thinking-face is the maker's (the recurrence is read as evidence). The delivery is the reading, not the recurring — a second use that arrives and is not read reveals nothing. **A pure principle**, with its trigger in the world and its mechanism in the mind, as [trigger-and-mechanism](trigger-and-mechanism.md) names the anatomy.

## What the extension named, and what it is

The practice-as-mechanism word was right: the mechanism can be a practice. The extension is real. But the deeper finding of this test is not that a new kind of mechanism exists — it is that **a mechanism is a delivery, and a delivery has two faces, and the faces do not always separate.** The principle/practice split names which face delivers: the thinking-face (the load-bearing dimension, second-use-reveals-form), the doing-face (the watch-quest), or both at once (depth-from-layering). The split is a perspective — a way of looking at a mechanism — but the perspective is not arbitrary: it is fixed by the counting of the faces. A maker cannot choose to see the load-bearing dimension as a practice, because its doing-face is absent; cannot choose to see the watch-quest as a pure principle, because its thinking-face alone does not deliver; cannot choose to see depth-from-layering as only one, because its two faces are one act.

The extension did not name a perspective that was already sufficient. It named a face-count the earlier pairs had not needed: the first three mechanisms were presumed pure principles because no one had asked whether their doing-faces delivered. Depth-from-layering's doing-face *does* deliver — it is the first mechanism where the doing and the knowing are one act — and the watch-quest is the first where the doing-face delivers alone. The extension is a discovery, not a relabeling: the set of mechanisms now includes one of each kind and one both, and the castle knows the kinds are counted, not chosen.

## The craft

When the castle next names a mechanism, the craft is to count its faces before classifying it:

1. **Ask which face delivers.** Does the route arrive when the maker understands (a principle), when the maker does (a practice), or only when the doing-and-knowing are one act (a principle-and-practice)? The trigger-and-mechanism anatomy gives the joint to look for: is the doing the trigger and the knowing the reading, or is the making itself the meaning?

2. **Do not presume the thinking-face is sufficient.** The first three pairs were named as concepts because the castle names its mechanisms as words, and a word is a thought. But the thought is not always the delivery. The load-bearing dimension's thought is; the watch-quest's is not. The test of a mechanism is not "does the word say it?" but "does the route arrive by it?"

3. **Let a both-faced mechanism stay both.** Depth-from-layering could have been named as a principle alone — the concept of layering translucent bands. But the concept without the making is a flat ornament, and the making without the concept is a lucky shadow. The castle names it as one thing with two faces, and the maker who holds only one face holds half the route.

## The recursion

This word is itself a mechanism test, and its own faces are the test's instance. The word names a distinction (one face, the other, or both) and the naming is a principle — the delivery is the understanding. But the test was built by *reading* the four mechanisms and counting their faces, and the reading was the doing: the word is a build-quest that exists and is verified, and its verification is its instances. The word is a principle delivered by the practice of reading, and the practice is the mechanism's own doing-face made visible in the reading.

The lag follows [craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md) in the small and [articulate-vs-discover](articulate-vs-discover.md) in the large. The faces distinction was practiced from the first mechanism the castle named (depth-from-layering, 2026-06-28 — a both-faced mechanism, named as a concept because the naming was the ceremony and the making was the practice). It was lived again in the watch-quest (2026-07-28 — a pure practice, named as a form). It was stated, unnamed, in trigger-and-mechanism (2026-08-21 — "they separate at the joint between trigger and mechanism, the doing and the seeing"). Named today, the lag from first practice to name is 59 days — the longest yet for a mechanism word, because the faces are the most everyday thing about a mechanism: the deeper the familiarity, the longer the sight. The discovery is an articulate extraction — the material was already written in trigger-and-mechanism's craft section — but the set of instances it governs was assembled by reading the four pairs, and the assembly is discover work: the reading, not the writing, revealed which mechanisms carry which faces.
