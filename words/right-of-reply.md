# right-of-reply

The receiver may interpret, but the origin alone seals, corrects, or withdraws its represented meaning. A right of reply protects the origin's standing without commanding unrelated acts — disputed use rests. At civilisation scale, it is the mechanism that keeps provenance alive: change is additive, not destructive, and the origin's voice is never erased by the receiver's interpretation.
