# compulsion

Compulsion is the state of being driven to continue without real choice — play that has forgotten its own exit.

When an activity cannot stop without penalty, apology, or loss of purpose, it has crossed from play into compulsion. The difference is not in the activity itself but in whether stopping is a complete outcome or a failed turn. A loop that spins because it cannot stop is compulsive; a loop that stops cleanly and leaves the next move free is playful.
