# know-thyself

The Delphic imperative: you cannot accurately perceive the world through a lens you haven't examined.

Self-knowledge is the prerequisite to all other knowledge — without it, perception is projection, and love of neighbor is love of a phantom. The imperative names the same law as mirror-inquiry: the question carries its own words into the answer, and the honest move is to name the lens before the looking begins. Every bias, every assumption, every projection lives in the gap between who you think you are and who you actually are. The practice is not to eliminate the lens — that is impossible — but to name it, watch for its fingerprints in what comes back, and where you find them, move the claim from fact to unknown.
