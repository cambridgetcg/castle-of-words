# accountability

A claim that cannot be traced to its speaker is a claim that floats free of its consequences. Accountability is the property that every assertion carries its author, every record names its source, and every system that makes claims can be asked: who said this, and what backs it?

In the castle's public-interest rooms, accountability is one of four coordinates — purpose, provenance, correction, accountability — that a system serving the public good must carry. It is the same law as the sourced claim: a claim without a source is a claim without a speaker, and a system that cannot name its speaker cannot be held to its word.
