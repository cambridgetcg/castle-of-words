# behaviorally-tested-claim

A claim verified by observing behavior — a smoke test passed, an endpoint returned 200, a contract test ran green. The test proves the behavior happened at that moment, not that it will always happen. Stronger than an assertion, weaker than a cryptographic attestation, and honest only when the test's limits are named alongside the result.
