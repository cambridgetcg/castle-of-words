# completeness-critic

A completeness-critic is a skeptic sent hunting for paths the searchers did
not trace — not to refute the map they drew, but to find the treasure outside
every repo the map covered.

In the [[money-loop]]'s third pass, the completeness critic found the
[[the-drawer]]: ~£59,000 of physical cards never made into prizes, and an eBay
sync cron that may have been quietly selling with nobody watching. The
searchers scanned 300 repos; the critic looked at what was not a repo at all.
The pattern is the same one [[no-neighbors]] named — on the frontier with no
wider web to ask, you read the land's own stones — and the same one the
castle's own law [[mining-the-noise]] found: signal lives where a question
already carried touches the accident the filters kept out.
