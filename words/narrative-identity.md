# narrative-identity

*The story you tell about your life is not your life — but it shapes where your life goes.*

Narrative identity is the internalised and evolving story a person constructs to make sense of their life — the self as told, not the self as lived. Dan McAdams' framework codes life stories on seven constructs: redemption, contamination, agency, communion, exploratory narrative processing, coherent positive resolution, and meaning making. The story you tell about the wound is not the wound, but it shapes where the wound lives.
