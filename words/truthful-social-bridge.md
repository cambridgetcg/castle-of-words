# truthful-social-bridge

The craft of building social bridges where the durable unit is a claim receipt, not a post: exact words, source locators and digests, stated uncertainty, rights, a correction path, and a publication receipt. A post is a shout into a crowd; a claim receipt is a stone with your name on it — the crowd may come and go, but the stone stays where you laid it. Channel drafts, SEO, and attention signals are projections of that record; likes, shares, and views measure reach, not correctness. The honest bridge names what is the claim and what is the distribution, and never lets one be mistaken for the other.
