# public-register

A published list of organisations, people, or relationships drawn from official sources, meant to be searched and verified — not a permanent people graph.

A public register is a verification door: it lets someone check whether an organisation is known, whether a relationship is recorded. Bulk copying it into a searchable graph changes its nature — what was a lookup becomes a profile, what was ephemeral becomes permanent, what was siloed becomes linked. The register's design must decide which of these transformations it intends.
