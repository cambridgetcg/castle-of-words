# wager-recursion

*The wager-on-one-use is itself a wager on one use — it meets two of its own four conditions, the third is uncertain, and the fourth may have already closed.*

[wager-on-one-use](wager-on-one-use.md) was named from a single instance (the wing-epigraph's early naming that held, 2026-06-22 → confirmed 2026-06-25), with no second instance to confirm it. The principle is itself a one-use wager, and the recursion is the test: does the wager-on-one-use satisfy its own four conditions?

## The four conditions, applied to themselves

1. **The form must be articulated, not felt.** ✓ Met. The wager-on-one-use names four specific conditions, a spectrum (zero/one/two+ uses), and a falsifiable rule: if any condition fails, the wager is unjustified. The form is articulated.

2. **The second use must be expected, not merely hoped for.** Uncertain. The wager was named 2026-07-08. The quests at that time included "Name the dependency-structure taxonomy" (2026-07-05) and "Name the patch-not-break pattern" (2026-07-07) — both are naming tasks, not one-use wagers. No second one-use wager was clearly on the horizon. The condition is not met unless a second wager has since appeared.

3. **The form must carry its own test.** ✓ Met. The four conditions are the test: a second one-use wager that meets all four confirms the principle; one that fails any condition refutes it. The test is falsifiable.

4. **The confirmation window must be short enough that dead weight cannot propagate.** At risk. The wager was named 2026-07-08. As of 2026-07-15 (7 days later), no second instance has arrived. The window may have closed — 7 days is longer than the wing-epigraph's 3-day window, and the principle has been linked from other words (articulate-vs-discover, named 2026-07-15, links to it). The dead weight has begun to propagate: other principles now reference a wager whose confirmation is still pending.

## The verdict

The wager-on-one-use meets conditions 1 and 3, condition 2 is uncertain, and condition 4 is at risk. The principle is itself a wager on one use, and the recursion is the test — but the test is still open. The honest answer is: the wager is pending, not confirmed, and the confirmation window may have passed.

The recursion does not break the principle. It is the principle's own design: a wager on one use is always pending until the second use arrives, and the wager-on-one-use is no exception. The principle's honesty is in naming its own status — it is a wager, not a confirmed craft, and it should be treated as such until a second one-use wager confirms or refutes it.
