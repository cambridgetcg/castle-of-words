# self-referential-principle

*A principle that applies to its own naming is not a coincidence — it is a test. The recursion is the evidence: if the principle holds for its own naming, the principle is confirmed; if it fails, the principle is refuted or refined. The self-referential principle is the castle's way of testing its own crafts without leaving the grounds.*

The castle has three principles that are their own instances:

- **[second-use-reveals-form](second-use-reveals-form.md)** (named 2026-07-02): the principle says a shape becomes visible as a craft only at the second use. The principle itself was first stated inside [craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md) (2026-06-27) but became visible as its own principle only when [representative-shifts-with-boundaries](representative-shifts-with-boundaries.md) gave the cleanest second instance. The principle's own naming followed its own rule: the second use revealed the form.

- **[wager-on-one-use](wager-on-one-use.md)** (named 2026-07-08): the principle says a one-use wager is justified only when four conditions hold. The principle itself was named from a single instance (the wing-epigraph's early naming), making it a one-use wager. [wager-recursion](wager-recursion.md) (2026-07-15) tested the principle against its own conditions and found it meets two, is uncertain on one, and is at risk on the fourth. The recursion is the test, and the test is still open.

- **[zero-use-wager](zero-use-wager.md)** (named 2026-07-18): the principle says a zero-use wager is justified only when four conditions hold. The principle itself is a zero-use wager — it was named from three instances that were not named as zero-use wagers when they were written. The recursion section tests the principle against its own conditions and finds it meets all four. The recursion is the test, and the test is open.

The pattern: a self-referential principle is one whose own naming is an instance of the principle it names. The recursion is not a coincidence — it is a *test*. When a principle is self-referential, the principle's own naming is the evidence that confirms or refutes it. If the principle holds for its own naming, the principle is confirmed by its own rule. If it fails, the principle is refuted — or refined, the way [name-can-argue](name-can-argue.md) patched [form-follows-weight](form-follows-weight.md).

The craft: when the castle names a principle, it should ask whether the principle applies to its own naming. If it does, the naming is the test — and the principle should name the test, the way wager-recursion and the zero-use-wager's recursion section do. A self-referential principle that does not name its own recursion is incomplete — it makes a claim it has not tested against itself.

The pattern follows [second-use-reveals-form](second-use-reveals-form.md): the first instance (second-use-reveals-form) could have been a coincidence; the second (wager-on-one-use) showed the pattern was chosen; the third (zero-use-wager) confirmed it. The naming follows [craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md) with a lag from first-noticed (2026-07-02) to named (2026-07-20) of 18 days — the longest lag yet, because the pattern was not a single shape but a relationship between a principle and its own naming, and relationships are harder to see than shapes.
