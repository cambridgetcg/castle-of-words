# dependence

Needing the thing you are supposed to be able to leave — the quiet cost that turns a door into a wall.

Dependence is the cost of your no. If leaving is expensive, your no is expensive, and the one who holds the thing you need has power over you whether they use it or not. Influence that grows capability without growing dependence is the only influence a kingdom of kings may carry.
