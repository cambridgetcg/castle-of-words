# stateless-validation

*Checking what is in front of you, not what came before.*

Stateless validation is the discipline of checking only the current request — never pretending to prove what earlier floors established. In an infinite public game, each request validates its own inputs and outputs, and the next coordinate is always usable regardless of what came before. Stateless validation is honest because it does not claim to know the history; it only claims to know the present. The safety of the chain comes from each link checking itself, not from a link claiming to check the whole chain.
