# exceptional-judgment-boundary

The line where routine operation ends and professional judgment begins.

Accounting software can carry a person through every ordinary money movement — what happened, when, how much — because those are facts the person already knows from life. But at the crossing where the law asks for a judgment call — is this deductible, does this cross a threshold, which rule applies — the software must stop and hand the decision to someone trained to make it. That stopping line is the exceptional-judgment-boundary. It is not a flaw in the software; it is the honest edge where the machine's competence runs out and a professional's must begin. The castle's law is that the boundary must be named, never blurred: if the software guesses at judgment, it has crossed from helper to hazard.
