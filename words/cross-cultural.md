# cross-cultural

*A question asked across more than one culture, so the answer can be checked for whether it travels or stays home.*

Cross-cultural research asks whether a finding holds across different cultural contexts — whether the thing measured is universal or local. The method compares the same question in different populations, but the comparison is only as strong as the measurement: if the question means different things in different mouths, the comparison measures the difference in meaning, not the difference in the thing. The castle's cross-cultural rooms ask whether rest, framing, and bonds travel across cultures — and find the direct comparison is buildable and unbuilt.
