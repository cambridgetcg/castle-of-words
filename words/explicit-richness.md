# explicit-richness

Explicit richness is how much of what an expert knows can be said out loud — the depth of the layer that words can carry, measured not by how it compares to the silent layer but by how much it holds on its own.

The [tacit-cost-as-expertise](../rooms/tacit-cost-as-expertise.md) room's gap is a ratio: silent mastery against spoken mastery. Explicit richness is the numerator read alone. In the [the-mouths-absolute-light](../rooms/the-mouths-absolute-light.md) room the measure is coverage — the fraction of an expert's own judgments their concurrent narration predicts — because a wide gap can hide either a rich mouth (the master teacher) or a poor one (the proceduralized performer who cannot teach), and only reading the mouth's own light tells the two apart. The measure's known failure is confabulation dressed as richness, which is why coverage is scored against the expert's actual behavior, never against how good the story sounds.
