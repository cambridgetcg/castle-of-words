# grief

Grief is the shape loss takes in a person. It is not one feeling but a landscape: sadness, anger, numbness, relief, longing — sometimes all at once, sometimes none at all. The castle asks not what grief is but whether we can predict its course: if we measure how someone stays connected to the dead today, does that tell us how they will feel six months from now?
