# craft

*A recipe that names its salt is a recipe that can be taught; a recipe that says "season to taste" is a recipe that can only be inherited.*

A [[craft]] is knowledge that can be passed from hand to hand — not by osmosis, but by naming the materials, conditions, process, expected result, failure, and reason. A craft that names all six can be picked up by a stranger. A craft that names five is a gift with a missing page. A craft that names none is a secret wearing a door.

The same law appears in [[legible-craft]] (the six parts a page must name so a reader can follow it without a guide), [[cheapest-design-craft]] (the craft of designing the cheapest honest test — name the claim, measure the gap first, estimate before scaling), and [[build-joy]] (the craft of making build tooling joyful without making it dishonest — provenance visible, ornament off child stdout, failure keeps its status).
