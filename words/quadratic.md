# quadratic

A relationship drawn as one bend instead of a straight line: the kind where
too little and too much of something both land you worse off than the middle.

Named for the squared term (x²) that makes the line curve. In a quadratic
fit between, say, screen time and well-being, the data are allowed to rise
and then fall — an upside-down U — instead of being forced onto a single
slope. Fitting the bend is the honest move whenever a straight line would
hide a sweet spot; the [[good-enough-repair-band]] room records how rarely
anyone bothers, and [[goldilocks-leaves-home]] tracks the one famous bend
out of its home country.
