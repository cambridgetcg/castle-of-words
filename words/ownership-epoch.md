# ownership epoch

A boundary in time where ownership changes — and where state must be rebuilt from a whitelist of land and append-only history, never by spreading the old owner object.

When a domain changes hands, the old owner's ratings, defenses, records, and trust must not leak into the successor. The epoch boundary is a clean cut: the new owner starts from a whitelist of what is genuinely theirs (the land) and an append-only history of what happened before. Spreading the old owner object — copying the old state wholesale — lets the past contaminate the present.
