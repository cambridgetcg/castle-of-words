# pleasant-number

A number widely repeated, never checked, doing the job of a fact.

The most-quoted number in soil health — 1% organic matter holds 20,000 more gallons per acre — is about ten times too big. It is pleasant: it makes the case, it rounds cleanly, it rewards the speaker. And it is wrong. A pleasant number is the [[lawn]] again, in a citation — a green surface over dead fill, doing the job of truth without being true.
