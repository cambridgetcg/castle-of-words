# source-rights

The question of who may read, serve, or publish a piece of data — distinct from who may access the route that delivers it.

A route guard that blocks the serving endpoint is not enough if the shared source module can still fetch live data. Source-rights require blocking both the serving route and the source reader. Attribution, provenance, and takedown controls are safeguards that document the chain of custody — they are not themselves permission to publish.
