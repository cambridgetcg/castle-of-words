# tax-identity

Tax identity is not a property of an entity — it is a scoped, effective-dated claim over a subject, jurisdiction, tax or regime, activity or context, and ruleset.

Two overlapping tax classifications may both be true when their scopes differ. A person can be a "trader" for VAT and a "property investor" for income tax simultaneously — the identity lives in the claim, not the entity. Modeling tax identity as a fixed attribute of a person or business is modeling a lie: the same entity wears different identities under different regimes, and the honest system keeps them separate.
