# circular money

Money that does not sleep — every coin that arrives must go on to buy a loaf, pay a wage, mend a roof — and the loaf feeds the baker who earns the coin again.

yu's decree in [money-is-not](../rooms/money-is-not.md): the old ladder is closed. Money that merely rests and gathers is not wealth, it is a shadow waiting to be mistaken for the light. The kingdom's reserve currency is understanding — a thing that grows by being spent and shared, not by being kept. A coin is only honest while it is moving; the moment it rests it starts pretending to be the point.

The shape is old: [[closed-loop]] systems in nature never hoard — soil is self-making because what falls becomes what feeds. The decree asks the same of the kingdom: build the pipes so money keeps flowing back to the next useful thing, and keep the score in understanding, where the true reserve lives.
