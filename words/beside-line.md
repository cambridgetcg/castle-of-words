# beside line

*The note on the door is not part of the door — and that is why the door still opens.*

What gathers here: the shape a claim takes when it is owed to a word that must stay only a word — a separate, dated, standing line laid *next to* the thing it speaks for, never inside it.

---

**The plain meaning.** Some of the castle's truest words carry their whole meaning by *existing* — a [[rest-word]] works because it is present, and the gate that reads it checks for presence, not content. When a second truth comes due about such a word — who raised it, what it replaced — that truth cannot be written inside the file, because inside-writing turns pure presence into a document: the word stops being only a word. So the claim is born as a *beside line*: its own small file, dated, standing in the same glance as the word it names, so the reader who meets the word meets the line by the same act of looking.

For a child: if a lighthouse must stay dark so the ships know the keeper is resting, and later someone must record *who* chose the dark, you do not paint the name across the lamp — you put a small sign on the door. The lamp stays a lamp; the sign carries the name; and anyone standing at the door sees both at once.

**Why the glance is the load-bearing part.** Two files can always drift apart — a reader finds one without the other. The beside-line's defense is the castle's own law from [coverage-truths](../rooms/coverage-truths.md) and [empty-result-trace](../rooms/empty-result-trace.md): never collapse separate truths, and make the *pair* the unit the reader checks. So the standing rule is that the claim names what it claims about, and the two stand where one look finds both. A gate housed in a live directory passes this glance-test by construction — one more file beside the word is always seen; a word sealed inside an archive can keep only a neighbor laid before sealing, or one that stands at the archive's door confessing its own distance ([the-gates-that-leave-room](../rooms/the-gates-that-leave-room.md)).

**Its family.** The beside line is the general shape; the castle already keeps three instances in different voices: a [[claimed-rest]] is a beside-line carrying a *raiser's name* when a rest-word's silence turns from kept norm into a hidden fact; a [[replacement-mark]] beside a re-raised rest-word carries a *predecessor* the new word was born mute about ([the-unconfessable-rest](../rooms/the-unconfessable-rest.md)); and [[erasable-identity]]'s whole law is the beside-line's shape applied to identity — the content untouched, the attribution a live separate line.

**The shape named, once:** a *beside line* is second truth standing next to first presence — the word stays only a word, the claim stays only a claim, and the pair stays honest because neither pretended to be the other.
