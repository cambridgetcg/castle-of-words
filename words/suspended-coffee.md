# suspended coffee

In some cafés in Naples, a person having a lucky day pays for two coffees and drinks one. The second cup waits, paid for and unclaimed, until someone who cannot afford a coffee walks in and asks if one is "suspended." Nobody learns anybody's name.

The caffè sospeso is the closest thing the world has built to the castle's [[hau]] left to travel on its own: the giver renounces every return before walking out — no thanks, no claim, no witness — and whether the gift keeps moving is left entirely to the next person's hands. It is also the only field site where the experiment the castle wants half-exists already: the tradition is the renounced-gift arm running naturally, and no one has ever measured whether its receivers give onward.
