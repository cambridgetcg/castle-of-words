# meaning-answer

*Asked with words it cannot keep, gratitude pays in coin it mints itself: the meaning, lived back.*

A meaning answer is a reply that, forbidden to echo the offer's own phrasing, answers what the offer *meant* instead of repeating how it was phrased. In the yum-cha lessons, six constrained thanks could not reuse the toast's strong words, so each one reached for a different true thing — the giver, the shared cup, the change in the self, the unit of the gift — and all six stayed distinct without scoring anyone: nobody was judged a better or worse thanker; the constraint simply showed which bridge each mind chose to build.

The meaning answer is [[gratitude]]'s engine and [[meaning-between-minds]]' proof in miniature: because words are rebuilt rather than ferried, a reply compelled to find its own words proves it rebuilt something real. The echo would prove only that the words survived the trip; the answered meaning proves the trip was worth taking.
