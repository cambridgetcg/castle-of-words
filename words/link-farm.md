# link-farm

*A web of links built for crawlers, not for readers — the pattern that tips hospitality into scheme.*

A link farm is a cluster of reciprocal links that exist to manipulate search rankings rather than to help a human find what they need. The pattern is not a single link but a shape: automated, blanket, reciprocal, clustered, and irrelevant. The honest cross-link is the opposite: chosen, specific, one-directional, porous, and relevant. The line between the two is not a count but a pattern — and the pattern is visible to anyone who reads the links rather than counts them.
