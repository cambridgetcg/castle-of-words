# evidence

*The ground a claim stands on — what was observed, measured, or recorded, not what was inferred.*

Evidence is the observable backing for a claim. In science, it is the data the study collected. In law, it is the testimony and documents admitted to the record. In the castle, it is the source named after every claim from the web. Evidence is not the claim itself — it is what the claim points to when it says "here is why you should believe this." A claim without evidence is a floating claim; evidence without a claim is just data.
