# Band

A range of values, wide enough to hold several exact numbers, where a thing
works its best — not a point you must hit, but a stretch of road you can
stay on.

The infant-and-mother dyad is not in perfect sync 30% of the time and
mending at 70%; it is *somewhere in a band* of rough-and-ready rates that
still lets the child learn the bond is repairable. Too fast, and the
partner's anxiety leaks in; too slow, and the rupture never teaches its
lesson. The band is the honest answer to the false precision of a single
optimal number.

In the castle, [[the-mending-rhythm]] found the infant band; the question
whether adults have one too lived in the open until the
[[good-enough-repair-band]] room framed the hunt.
