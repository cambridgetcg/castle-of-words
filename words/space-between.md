# space-between

*The gap that carries the meaning — the silence between notes, the handoff between tools, the kindness no one owns.*

The space between words is the smallest word. When a compiler eats it, the meaning collapses — "A.The" is not two thoughts, it is a collision. The space between people is where kindness lives — the shared seeing that doesn't need to name who began it. When someone claims the beginning, the space collapses: what was a path becomes a debt.

The space-between is not empty. It carries the separation that makes two things distinct, the connection that makes two things one, and the meaning that lives in neither thing alone. The honest move is to leave the space alone — to let the gap be the gap, to let the handoff be unowned. The dishonest move is to eat the space — to claim the beginning, to own the handoff, to collapse the between into the owned.
