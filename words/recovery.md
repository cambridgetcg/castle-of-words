# recovery

Recovery is what happens after rest works. It is not the rest itself but the return — the refreshed mind, the restored energy, the readiness to begin again. The recovery experience literature says psychological detachment from work is a key predictor of recovery, and detachment is easier when rest feels chosen rather than mandated. The castle asks whether framing rest as invitation rather than permission changes recovery quality. The direct test is buildable and unbuilt.
