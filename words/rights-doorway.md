# rights-doorway

A threshold where rights are carried as an exact receipt binding words, attribution, and what is NOT claimed into one verifiable package.

A rights doorway is honest only when the carried words, attribution, and complete negative claim boundary are one exact receipt. Treatment, permission, consent, authority, identity, relationship, practice, composition, and activation must stay separate through every transition — a shallow validator can silently turn reference into false adoption.
