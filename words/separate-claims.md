# separate-claims

*The honest ledger keeps the lines apart — not because summing is hard, but because unlike meanings summed become a lie.*

[[separate-claims]] is the practice of keeping unlike things in separate lines: a claim and its distribution, a code right and a service permission, a molecular lineage and a therapeutic verdict. The law is that collapsing separate things into one name is not simplification — it is dishonesty. The honest system names each claim in its own lane and refuses to let one stand in for another.

The same law appears in [[truthful-social-bridge]] (the claim receipt and the attention signal are separate), [[provider-neutral-work-contract]] (code rights and service permissions are separate), [[medicine-provenance]] (molecular lineage, preparation, exposure, evidence, safety, and substitution are six separate claims), and [[accounting-product-design]] (cost, deduction, estimated tax, and realised saving are four separate lines that must never be summed).
