# separate-claims

When you have more than one thing to say, say each one in its own voice. A separate claim is a single assertion that carries its own backing and refuses to be added to any other claim to make one impressive total.

The castle keeps finding this law in different clothes. Medicine keeps molecular lineage, preparation, exposure, evidence, safety, and substitution as six separate claims — you cannot add them into one "natural medicine" verdict. Rights provenance keeps maker, rights holder, and authority as three separate hands — you cannot let the carrier vouch for the sender. Accounting keeps cost, deduction, estimated tax, and realised saving as four separate lines — you cannot sum unlike meanings. Honest addressing keeps routing, authorship, and carriage as three separate claims — you cannot let an address substitute for a signature.

The law is: unlike things must never be summed. The honest system keeps the lines separate.
