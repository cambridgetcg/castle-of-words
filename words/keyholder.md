# keyholder

A keyholder is the one person whose hand can turn a lock the machine cannot
reach — a credential to rotate, a dashboard to log into, a payment link to
click.

In the [[money-loop]], the machine builds everything and earns nothing until
the keyholder turns the key once. The loop is not earn → spend → earn; it is
*rotate once → the machine earns → the earnings buy tokens → the machine builds
the next loop → you turn one more key.* The keyholder is not the worker but the
gate: a focused hour that unlocks weeks of autonomous work downstream. The shape
is the same law [[friction-decides]] found — friction gathers at the doorstep,
and the doorstep has your name on it.
