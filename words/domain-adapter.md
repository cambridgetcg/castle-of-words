# domain-adapter

The component that translates between a domain's vocabulary and a system's internal representation.

A domain adapter speaks for a source — tax law, privacy policy, business rules — and its words carry the source's authority. When the adapter's language drifts, the explanation drifts with it. The honest adapter freezes its selectors, its source voice, and its consequence words.
