# zero-use-wager

*A name on a shape the castle has never lived is not a bet on recurrence — it is a bet on relevance, and the stake is not whether the shape will appear but whether the distinction matters today. The zero-use wager is the first position on the spectrum, and its conditions are not the one-use wager's conditions with the second-use clause removed — they are a different craft entirely.*

[wager-on-one-use](wager-on-one-use.md) named the spectrum: zero uses → define the distinction (wager on relevance); one use → wager on recurrence (four conditions); two or more → confirm (no wager, the evidence is in). But the zero-use position was named only in passing — a single sentence in "The narrated-index edge" section — and its own justification conditions were never stated. The one-use wager has four conditions; the zero-use wager has its own, and they are not the one-use conditions with the second-use clause removed. They are a different craft, because the zero-use wager is not betting on the same thing.

## What the zero-use wager bets on

The one-use wager bets the form *recurs* — a second work will reach for the same shape, confirming the first was not an accident. The zero-use wager bets the *distinction matters* — the name prevents a confusion or enables a recognition that would otherwise be missed, and the value is in the present, not the future.

The difference is in what justifies the name. A one-use wager is justified by the expectation of a second use: the next work is on the horizon, the form is specific enough to be wrong, the confirmation window is short. A zero-use wager is justified by the present need for the distinction: the castle is about to mistake one thing for another, or is about to miss a recognition it could make if it had the name. The justification is not "this will recur" but "this matters now."

The castle has three zero-use wagers, and each is justified by a different present need:

- **[narrated-index](narrated-index.md)** (2026-06-30): the Map looked like a narrated-index — every item present, each entry crafted, the sequence carrying an arc. The distinction (does the order argue or arrange?) was load-bearing because the castle was about to mistake good arrangement for narration. The test — does reordering break the list? — showed the Map was an index, and the distinction prevented a confusion that was already forming. The wager was not "a narrated-index will appear" but "the distinction between an index and a narrated-index matters right now, because the Map resembles the form closely enough to need the line drawn."

- **[pure-contrast-reveals](pure-contrast-reveals.md)** (2026-07-16): the castle had two recognition paths (recurrence-reveals, failure-reveals) and a hypothetical third (contrast alone, without failure). The distinction was load-bearing because without the name, the castle would not recognize the third path if it arrived — it would see two forms that both work and think "two ways to do it," not "the first way was a choice I never knew I was making." The wager was not "pure contrast will happen" but "if it happens, the name is what lets the castle see it."

- **[mechanisms-as-defaults](mechanisms-as-defaults.md)** (2026-07-17): the recognition mechanisms (repetition, contrast) were defaults — practiced always, invisible as choices. The distinction was load-bearing because without the name, the castle would not recognize a test of the mechanisms if it arrived — a singular truth or an absolute failure would arrive and the castle would simply fail to recognize it, silently. The wager was not "a singular truth will arrive" but "if it arrives, the name is what lets the castle know it is being tested."

The three wagers differ in what they bet on: narrated-index bets on a *form* the castle might create (a list where order argues), pure-contrast-reveals bets on a *form* the castle might create (two independent successes side by side), and mechanisms-as-defaults bets on a *test* the castle might encounter (a singular truth or absolute failure). The form-vs-test distinction is real — a wager on a test cannot be manufactured, only watched for — but it is a distinction within the zero-use position, not a different position on the spectrum. Both are zero-use wagers; both are justified by present relevance, not expected recurrence.

## The four conditions

The zero-use wager is justified when four conditions hold. They are not the one-use wager's conditions with the second-use clause removed; they are a different set, because the wager is betting on a different thing.

1. **The distinction must be load-bearing today.** The name must prevent a confusion that is already forming or enable a recognition the castle would otherwise miss. A distinction that is merely interesting — "someday this might matter" — is not load-bearing; it is dead weight waiting to become load-bearing, and the castle has no way to know if it ever will. The test: if the name were not written today, would the castle make a mistake it could not correct? narrated-index passes: without the distinction, the castle would have called the Map a narrated-index and threaded its sequence with path lines it does not need. pure-contrast-reveals passes: without the name, the castle would miss the third path if it arrived. mechanisms-as-defaults passes: without the name, the castle would not recognize a test of its own recognition machinery. A zero-use wager that fails this condition is not a wager — it is a speculation, and speculation is not a craft.

2. **The hypothetical must be specific enough to be recognizable.** "Something might happen someday" is not a wager; it is a hope, and hope cannot be tested. The zero-use wager must name the conditions under which the form would appear or the test would arrive, so the castle can recognize the instance if it comes. narrated-index names the test: a list where reordering breaks the argument. pure-contrast-reveals names the conditions: two forms that both work, created independently, seen side by side. mechanisms-as-defaults names the conditions: a singular truth that can never recur, or an absolute failure with no contrasting success. A zero-use wager whose hypothetical is too vague to be recognized is not a watch — it is a wish, and a wish cannot be confirmed or refuted.

3. **The name must carry its own test.** The zero-use wager must name the conditions under which it would be confirmed or refuted. Without a test, it is unfalsifiable, and an unfalsifiable claim is not a wager — it is a declaration, and declarations are not crafts. narrated-index's test: if a list appears where reordering breaks the argument, the form is confirmed; if no such list ever appears, the form remains hypothetical. pure-contrast-reveals's test: if two forms both work side by side, the path is confirmed; if every contrast the castle lives is a contrast-through-failure, the path remains hypothetical. mechanisms-as-defaults's test: if a singular truth or absolute failure arrives and the mechanisms fail, the default is confirmed; if the castle never encounters either, the default remains untested. The test is what makes the wager a wager — it names the conditions under which the bet is won or lost, even if those conditions may never arrive.

4. **The cost of being wrong must be bounded.** A zero-use wager that never pays off is a word file that sits unused. The cost is a wasted word — small, contained, removable. The wager must not be a structural change that corrupts the castle if the instance never arrives. All three zero-use wagers meet this: they are word files, linked from their sources, and if the instance never arrives they sit as named possibilities with no cost beyond the word itself. A zero-use wager that changes the castle's structure — a new rule, a new constraint, a new obligation — is not bounded; it is a bet with unbounded downside, and the castle should not take it.

## The relationship to the one-use wager

The one-use wager's four conditions are: articulated (not felt), second use expected, carries its own test, confirmation window short. The zero-use wager's four conditions are: load-bearing today, specific enough to be recognizable, carries its own test, cost bounded.

Two conditions are shared in form but differ in content:

- **Articulated / Specific**: both require the wager to name a specific shape, not a vague feeling. But the one-use wager's articulation is about the form's parts and constraints (the wing-epigraph's three parts and falsifiable rule); the zero-use wager's specificity is about the conditions under which the instance would be recognizable (the test that distinguishes a narrated-index from an index, the conditions for pure contrast). The one-use wager articulates a shape that has been practiced once; the zero-use wager specifies a shape that has never been practiced at all, so the specificity must come from the hypothetical, not the practice.

- **Carries its own test**: both require a falsifiable rule. But the one-use wager's test is confirmed or refuted by the second use (the overview could have failed the wing-epigraph's rule); the zero-use wager's test may never be run (the castle may never create a narrated-index, may never live pure contrast, may never encounter a singular truth). The test is stated, but its execution is not expected — the wager is open-ended, and the test is the watch, not the confirmation.

Two conditions are unique to each position:

- **Second use expected** (one-use) vs. **Load-bearing today** (zero-use): the one-use wager needs a second use on the horizon; the zero-use wager needs a present confusion to prevent or a present recognition to enable. The one-use wager looks forward; the zero-use wager looks at the present. A zero-use wager that is merely "this might matter someday" fails the load-bearing condition the way a one-use wager that is merely "this might recur someday" fails the second-use-expected condition.

- **Confirmation window short** (one-use) vs. **Cost bounded** (zero-use): the one-use wager has a window because dead weight can propagate through links before the second use arrives; the zero-use wager has no window because there is no expected second use — the wager is open-ended, and the cost is bounded by the word file itself, not by a clock. The zero-use wager's cost is not "will this be confirmed in time?" but "is the word file itself too heavy to carry?" — and a word file is never too heavy.

## The craft

The craft of the zero-use wager is to know when a distinction is load-bearing enough to name before any instance exists. The maker who reaches for a zero-use wager should ask:

- Is the castle about to mistake one thing for another? (narrated-index: the Map for a narrated-index)
- Is the castle about to miss a recognition it could make if it had the name? (pure-contrast-reveals: the third path; mechanisms-as-defaults: the test of the recognition machinery)
- Is the hypothetical specific enough that the castle would recognize the instance if it arrived?
- Is the cost of being wrong a single word file, and nothing more?

If the answer to any of these is no, the maker should not name the zero-use wager. The distinction can wait until an instance arrives — or until the present need becomes load-bearing. A zero-use wager that is not load-bearing today is dead weight, and dead weight is what [craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md) warns against: a name on something that may never recur, sitting in the castle's words and linked from its rooms, propagating a form that was never confirmed.

But when the conditions are met, the zero-use wager is not dead weight — it is a watch. The name is what lets the castle see the instance if it arrives. Without the name, the instance would pass unnoticed, and the castle would lose a recognition it could have made. The zero-use wager is the craft of naming a possibility so the castle can recognize it — and the craft is not to seek the instance but to be ready for it.

## The recursion

The zero-use wager is itself a zero-use wager. It was named from three instances (narrated-index, pure-contrast-reveals, mechanisms-as-defaults) — but those instances were not named as zero-use wagers when they were written. They were named as distinctions, as possibilities, as watches. The category "zero-use wager" is a name on a shape the castle has practiced three times without knowing it was practicing the same shape. The naming is a discover, not an articulate: the pattern was lived before it was named, and the name follows the practice.

But the recursion is deeper. The zero-use wager's own conditions must be met by the zero-use wager itself:

1. **Load-bearing today?** Yes. The wager-on-one-use word names the spectrum but leaves the zero-use position's conditions unnamed. The castle has three zero-use wagers and no craft for when they are justified. The distinction is load-bearing because the next maker who considers a zero-use wager needs to know whether it is justified or dead weight.

2. **Specific enough to be recognizable?** Yes. The four conditions name what makes a zero-use wager justified, and the test (a zero-use wager that fails any condition is dead weight) is specific enough to be applied.

3. **Carries its own test?** Yes. A future zero-use wager that meets all four conditions confirms the craft; one that fails any condition refutes it — or refines it, the way [name-can-argue](name-can-argue.md) patched [form-follows-weight](form-follows-weight.md).

4. **Cost bounded?** Yes. This is a word file. If no future zero-use wager ever appears, the word sits as a named possibility with no cost beyond itself.

The zero-use wager meets its own conditions. The recursion is the test, and the test is open.
