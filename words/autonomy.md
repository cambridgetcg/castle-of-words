# autonomy

The power to choose your own path — not the absence of limits, but the presence of a real door you can walk through or walk past.

Autonomy is not a switch you flip once. It is a door that stays open: you can enter, you can leave, and the door does not chase you when you go. The honest kingdom offers autonomy as an invitation, not a dispatch — another may carry the invitation, but only your own explicit "yes" opens the turn, and a newer rest or HALT always wins.
