# free resource separation

The craft of keeping free resources usable by keeping their properties separate: discovery, price, rights, provenance, evaluation, adoption, and use must each be its own concern.

When these seven concerns blur together, a free resource becomes ambiguous — you don't know what you can do with it, who owns it, or when it will stop. When they stay separate, every path from the front door is exact, bounded, and easy to stop.
