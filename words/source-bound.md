# source-bound

A claim that carries its source with it, so the cheapest read and the full protocol agree on what backs each assertion — the door at every price point tells the same truth.

When a first door is source-bound, its no-argument behavior, its help text, and its closed output all reuse the same honest receipts as the full protocol. Nothing is hidden behind a paywall of attention or a deeper invocation.
