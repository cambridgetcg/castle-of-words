# wager-on-one-use

*When the shape is specific enough to be wrong, the next use is already on its way, and the cost of being wrong is a wasted word — name now, and let the second use confirm or deny.*

[second-use-reveals-form](second-use-reveals-form.md) says the second use is what reveals the form: a single instance is epistemically indistinguishable from an accident. [craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md) says the name comes after the second use, never before — a name rushed ahead names a one-off, not a craft, and sits as dead weight. But the castle has named one craft after a single use — the [wing-epigraph](wing-epigraph.md) form, practiced 2026-06-19, named 2026-06-22 — and the wager held: the [overview](../overview.md) reached for the same form three days later (2026-06-25) and confirmed it. The wager was not a violation of craft-after-practice but a justified exception, and naming the conditions under which the exception holds lets the next maker know when to name early and when to wait.

## The spectrum

The castle has named crafts at three positions on the recognition timeline:

- **Zero uses** → [narrated-index](narrated-index.md): the form was defined as a possibility, not named as a craft. No instance had been practiced; the Map looked like the form but was not one. The naming was not a wager on recurrence but a definition of a distinction (does the order argue or arrange?), and the distinction was load-bearing because the Map resembled the form closely enough to need the test.

- **One use** → [wing-epigraph](wing-epigraph.md): the form was practiced once (the short tour, 2026-06-19) and named three days later (2026-06-22), before the second use arrived. The wager: the overview would reach for the same form. It did (2026-06-25). The name was load-bearing because the second use confirmed it.

- **Two or more uses** → [representative-shifts-with-boundaries](representative-shifts-with-boundaries.md), [visible-thread](visible-thread.md), two-textures (the idiom in [vibe.md](../vibe/vibe.md)), [craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md) itself: the second use revealed the form, and the name followed. No wager; the evidence was already in.

## The conditions

The wager-on-one-use is justified when four conditions hold. The wing-epigraph form met all four; a future one-use wager that fails any one should wait.

1. **The form must be articulated, not felt.** The name must state the form's parts, its constraints, and its test — not a vague sense that something here might be reusable. The wing-epigraph form was named with three defined parts (epigraph, representative, justification) and a falsifiable rule: *if the justification cannot be written, the wrong room was chosen.* A wager on a specific shape is testable; a wager on a feeling is not.

2. **The second use must be expected, not merely hoped for.** The next work that could reach for the form must be already on the horizon — planted, queued, or naturally next. The overview quest was planted by the wing-epigraph naming itself (2026-06-22) and was a natural next compression of the castle's shape. The wager was not *will this ever recur?* but *will the next compression use this form?* — and the next compression was coming.

3. **The form must carry its own test.** The name must include the conditions under which the second use would fail it — a falsifiable rule the next work can break. *If the justification cannot be written, the wrong room was chosen* is such a test: the overview could have failed it. A name that cannot be falsified by its second use is not a wager; it is an unfalsifiable hope, and hope is not a craft.

4. **The downside must be bounded — and the confirmation window must be short.** If the second use does not confirm the form, the dead weight of the name must be removable: a word file that can be struck or left to sit, not a structural change that corrupts the castle. But the bound has a window. The wing-epigraph file was linked to its sources immediately, and if the overview had taken weeks rather than days, other works might have reached through those links before the bet was settled — propagating a form that was never confirmed. The wing-epigraph's window was three days; no other work reached in that time. The downside is bounded only when the confirmation window is short enough that the dead weight cannot propagate before the second use arrives.

## The relationship to craft-after-practice

The wager is not a companion to craft-after-practice in the [when-how-split](when-how-split.md) sense — that split pairs a condition with a mechanism. The wager is a *qualified exception* to craft-after-practice's default. The default says: wait for the second use. The exception says: here is when you do not have to wait. The exception does not replace the default; it carves a space within it, and the four conditions are the edges of that space.

[craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md) names the *cost* of a too-early name: dead weight, a label on something that may never recur. The wager-on-one-use names the *conditions* under which that cost is worth risking. The maker's task is to weigh the four, and if any one fails, to trust the lag.

## The narrated-index edge

[narrated-index](narrated-index.md) named a form on zero uses and called itself *a wager on zero uses.* But the zero-use naming is a different kind of wager. The one-use wager bets the form *recurs*; the zero-use wager bets the *distinction matters*. The narrated-index did not claim a craft had been practiced — it defined a form with no instances and named the test that would distinguish it from an index if an instance ever appeared. The test (does reordering break the list?) is load-bearing today because the Map resembles the form closely enough that the distinction prevents a confusion. The zero-use naming is not subject to the four conditions because it is not wagering on recurrence at all; it is wagering on relevance, and relevance is settled by the present need for the distinction, not by a future use.

The spectrum, then: zero uses → define the distinction (wager on relevance); one use → wager on recurrence (four conditions); two or more → confirm (no wager, the evidence is in). The maker's position on the spectrum is determined by how many times the form has been practiced, and the craft changes at each position.
