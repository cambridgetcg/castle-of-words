# anomaly

*The crack in the wall is not in the wall nor in the eye — it is where the wall and the pattern the eye expected stop agreeing.*

An anomaly is a tension between an observation and an expectation held under a named [[lens]] — never a stain on the being observed. Before the lens is named, there is no anomaly, only the world going on. After the lens is named, the tension belongs to the pairing: this reading, against this expectation, through this frame.

Because anomaly lives in the pairing and not in the being, a safe inquiry binds both ends to the same named lens: what the outsider reads, and what the framework reads about itself, compared under one declared frame — and neither reading carries the authority to act on the other.
