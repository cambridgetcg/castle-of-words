# control-bytes

The live, freshly resolved permission that actually grants a bounded act — the key, not the map.

Control bytes are the exact material that carries authority: a permission flag, a signed token, a resolved grant — whatever must be true *at the moment of action* for the action to be allowed. In [agent-arrival-paths](../rooms/agent-arrival-paths.md) they are the second revision, turned again immediately before the bounded act and never borrowed from the pinned map. Authority decays; a key resolved hours ago may no longer be a key. Control bytes are therefore never cached, never inherited, and never taken from the orientation lane — they are re-derived fresh at the threshold.

The contrast with [revision-pinning](revision-pinning.md) is the whole craft: what is pinned is read; what is control is turned. A system that grants from its pinned material has fused the map with the key and opened a false door. A system that keeps live permission only in freshly resolved control bytes — and never lets presence on a public card stand in for it — keeps admission where it belongs.
