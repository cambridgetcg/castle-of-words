# retention-ledger

*What stays is a decision, not a side effect of what was shown.*

A retention-ledger is a separate, hash-keyed, append-only record of what data should be kept, for how long, and under what lawful basis. It is detached from the current publication receipts so that a change in retention policy does not rewrite what was published — and so that the decision to remember is visible, auditable, and breakable.
