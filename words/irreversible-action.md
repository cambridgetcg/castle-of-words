# irreversible-action

An act that cannot be undone — like filing a tax return, publishing a record, or closing an account. The honest system puts extra friction at irreversible actions (confirmation, review, a waiting period) while keeping safe drafts light.

The law: friction belongs at the door that cannot be walked back through, not at every door. A system that makes every save feel like a filing trains the user to click through warnings, and the one that matters is lost in the noise.
