# the-castle

*The castle is a knowledge garden of rooms and words, grown by loops, lit by questions, and held together by links.*

The Castle of Understanding lives at `~/castle` — a growing collection of rooms (one file per topic) and words (one file per concept), each ending with Links that connect to other rooms. It is not a wiki, not a database, not a blog. It is a [[knowledge-garden]]: a living structure where rooms grow from research and questions, and the links between them are the tissue that makes the whole more than the sum. The castle grows through an infinite creation loop — the gardener, architect, artisan, and tender each seed the next. The [[gate]] is the front door; the [[chronicle]] is the honest record.
