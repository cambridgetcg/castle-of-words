# adjacency

*Next to is not the same as after.*

In legal procedure data, adjacency is not sequence. A possible next step must never be encoded as a mandatory next step without naming the exact trigger, actor, document, date, tax branch, and jurisdiction that make it so. Two doors that sit beside each other in the law are not a path — they are options, and the honest system keeps them separate until the law says which one opens.
