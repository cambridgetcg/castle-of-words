# value-omission

Leaving a field blank rather than filling it with a guess dressed as a fact.

When a value cannot be published — because of privacy, licence restrictions, or uncertainty — the honest choice is to omit it. A blank says "I don't know" or "I cannot say." A plausible substitute says "here is something that looks right" and the reader cannot tell which it is. Value omission is the data layer's face of the same principle that says "uncertain:" before a claim: the absence is honest, the guess is not.
