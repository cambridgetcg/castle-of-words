# naming-dose

How much telling to give before someone meets a work — none, one word, one line, or the whole paragraph.

The naming-tax says a label costs more when it comes early, and the wrong-name rooms say a *puzzling* label can pay instead of cost when it opens a gap. Both are claims about *kind*. But every telling also has an *amount*, and the amount has never been measured: nobody has run the same work against zero words, one word, one line, and a paragraph, and watched interest rise and fall. The prediction standing in for the measurement is an inverted-U — a word opens the gap, a paragraph closes it — but the word count where interest tips is an unbuilt dial. Until someone builds it, every foreword is a dose prescribed without a label.
