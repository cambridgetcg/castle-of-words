# routing-claim

A routing claim says "messages for this name can be delivered here" — it is a statement about reachability, not identity. An email address is a routing claim; a postal address is a routing claim; an IP address is a routing claim. The honest system never lets a routing claim substitute for a signature, and never lets the carrier vouch for the sender.

The routing claim is one of three hands in honest addressing: the address (routing), the sender (authorship), and the carrier (transport). Conflate any two and the third becomes a lie — this is the root of phishing, spoofing, and interception in every addressing system humans have built.
