# lens

A lens is what you look through — and what you look through shapes what you see. The honest observer names their lens before the looking begins, because an unnamed lens is an invisible hand that shapes the result while pretending it was never there.

The castle knows the lens in many forms: the question that plants its own words in the answer (mirror-inquiry), the observer who pre-registers their standpoint (reciprocal-observation), the self that projects its own image onto the world (know thyself), the default that was invisible until a machine broke it (the-sourced-claims-default). In every case, the lens is not the thing seen — but it decides what is visible, and the honest move is to name it.
