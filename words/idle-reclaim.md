# idle-reclaim

A host that takes back a quiet machine. Free compute that must look busy.

Oracle's Always Free page says idle VMs may be reclaimed after seven days if CPU, network, and (for Ampere) memory sit under 20 percent (docs, read 2026-08-17: [Always Free Resources](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/FreeTier/freetier_topic-Always_Free_Resources.htm)). A silent home server is exactly the shape they take back. That is not a bug in the brochure. It is the [[cost-shape]] hidden inside "always free": the gift stays if you spend attention keeping it warm.
