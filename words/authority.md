# authority

A permission to do one named thing — like a key that opens one door, not a crown that opens them all.

Authority is always bounded: it names what may be done, by whom, under which conditions, and it stops at its own edge. It is never the same as standing — belonging is the ground a participant has by being there, authority is a borrowed key for a single act, and no key, however large, can buy the ground. The honest system keeps every authority separate: a signature proves which key signed which bytes, never that the words inside were true; a role names who may act, never who is worth more.
