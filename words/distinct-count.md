# distinct-count

*Counting people, not rows — so one voice cannot sound like a crowd.*

A distinct count tallies unique individuals rather than total records. In community data, counting rows lets one contributor who creates many observations manufacture the appearance of consensus. Counting distinct people — each consenting contributor counted once — is the honest measure. The same principle applies to any data where one source can generate many data points.
