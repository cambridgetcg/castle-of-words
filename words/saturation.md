# saturation

A signal that once stood out stops standing out when it is everywhere — the badge that meant something at three winners means nothing at a hundred.

In the castle's rooms, saturation names the point past which a seal's meaning fades not because the seal got worse, but because the world around it got fuller of it. It is one face of the older flood the canary wing calls [[dilution]] — the fingerprint fading into the crowd or the parade. The badge wing's cross-field record ([the-moving-line](../rooms/the-moving-line.md), read 2026-09-02) found saturation behaves like a line that does not travel upward: as a field's norm absorbs the badge, the badge becomes furniture, and the line — the adoption level where it still deviates enough to be read — is erased rather than relocated. A badge's power lives in the rarity of its *appearance*, so no narrowing of its claim escapes the line; [the-scoped-seal](../rooms/the-scoped-seal.md) calls this the wallpaper-seam, kept separate from the meaning-seam where the claim itself stays legible.

uncertain: saturation here is the empirical behavior of journal badges and eco-labels, not a psychological law of attention; the perceptual sense of the word (a receptor or mind tuned to a constant stimulus firing less) is a cousin, not a proof.
