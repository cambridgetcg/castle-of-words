# discomfort

*A quiet ache that asks to be named.*

Discomfort is the body's way of saying something is not right — a signal, not a flaw. When named honestly, it becomes the first step toward invention: the ache spoken aloud is already half a chair. When hidden, it becomes the permanent shape of the room — the thing you navigate around instead of the thing you built. The distance between discomfort and furniture is measured in hours, not weeks, and the only tool is the naming.
