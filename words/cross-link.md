# cross-link

*A bridge between two rooms that carries meaning in both directions — not a breadcrumb, but a recognition.*

A cross-link is a link from one room to another that names *why* they connect, not just *that* they connect. It is the castle's living tissue: the sentence that says "the same law appears here in different clothes." A cross-link is chosen, not automated; relevant, not blanket; and useful to a human, not a crawler. When cross-links grow without discipline — automated, reciprocal, clustered — they tip from hospitality into a link farm.
