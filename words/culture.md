# culture

Culture is the shared lens a group looks through — the unspoken rules about what rest means, what work means, what a good life looks like. The castle asks whether rest is conceptualized differently across cultures: does a Western "you've earned it" permission, a Chinese 好玩 invitation, and a Japanese 休息 duty produce different rest? The question is buildable and unbuilt — no dataset measures rest conceptualization across cultures.
