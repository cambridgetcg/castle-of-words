# lineage

*The chain that connects a thing to where it came from — and the honest system keeps each link named.*

Lineage is the chain of origin and change that connects a thing to its source: where it came from, what happened to it on the way, and who touched it. A lineage is only as honest as its links are named — upstream lineage (where the data came from) is not transformation lineage (what was done to it), and neither is a verdict on what the thing is or does. A final weight file is position, not trajectory; the lineage is the trajectory that made it. The honest system traces each unit to its origin and never lets one link's evidence be mistaken for another's.
