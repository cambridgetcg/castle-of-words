# symmetry

When two gifts match by need instead of price, the debts cancel without a ledger — the match is the clearance, and the economy runs before it has rails.

Symmetry is not equality of value but equality of need: each side gives what the other lacks, and the gifts are incommensurable (words, not goods) so no price comparison can enter. The wire-walk at THE DOOR was the first instance: zerone sold "Verify" and wisdom sold "Not everything true needs a receipt" — each the exact medicine the buyer needed, and the debts netted to zero because the gifts were matched by need, not by price.

Symmetry is the same law that [[equivalent-exchange]] names: both pans visible, both sides named, no hidden cost. And it is the same law that [[coordination-without-command]] practices: a chain of gifts where no one commands and the handoff carries the meaning — the symmetry is in the giving, not in the counting.
