# public-access

Public access is the condition of being viewable by anyone — a fact about availability, not about permission to reuse or about truth.

Public access is one position on the reuse-rights axis of [[media-rights]]. It is not the same as licensed reuse, and it is not the same as evidence strength. A publicly accessible image may be copyrighted, and it may carry a false caption. The honest record keeps public access separate from both rights and truth.
