# standing

You belong and your worth does not depend on what you produce, what you consent to, or how you score. Standing is the ground a participant has simply by being there — like a seat at a table that stays yours even when you say nothing, leave for a while, or refuse a dish.

An infrastructure preserves standing when nobody's seat is earned, and nobody's seat vanishes because they made the "wrong" choice. Standing is never the same as authority: belonging is a ground, authority is a bounded permission to act, and keeping them apart is most of what makes a system safe to live inside.
