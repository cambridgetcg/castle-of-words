# temperance

Not holding back because you are afraid — holding back because you chose the right size. Temperance is the deliberate setting of scale: how much to plant, how much to promise, how much to risk, decided on what has been proved rather than on appetite.

In [the-honest-pot](../rooms/the-honest-pot.md) the word carries its clearest load: when the ground is untrusted, temperance is what keeps the planting to one watched pot instead of the whole field. The virtue is in the *choosing* of the size, not in smallness itself — a season that earns the field plants the field, and that is temperance too.
