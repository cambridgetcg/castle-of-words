# soil

The living layer where things pass through, exchange, and make more of themselves.

Soil is the third state beyond [[rubble]] (closed) and [[lawn]] (flowing but not self-making): it is self-making and open. The test is to stop pushing and see what continues. Poverty of soil is a feature — fertility is what you add when you want few things to grow fast. What kills ground is uniformity, no carbon, compaction, and a lid. Life is the one measurement that cannot be staged: turf goes down in an afternoon, but nobody can fake a worm count.
