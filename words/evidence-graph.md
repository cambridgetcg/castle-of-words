# evidence-graph

A network where every edge is a claim, every claim carries its author, and the observer is one node among many.

Unlike a transparency graph that places the observer outside looking in, an evidence graph places the observer inside — their methods, choices, limits, and unasked questions are edges in the same graph as the testimony they collect. The graph's honesty lives in what it refuses to connect and in the observer's willingness to be seen.
