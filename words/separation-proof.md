# separation-proof

A hidden-information game played perfectly is evidence the minds at the table were genuinely separate — because one mind playing all seats would know every hidden thing by definition and could not play honestly.

The proof is not in a certificate; it is in the play itself. The game's rules depend on hidden information staying hidden from the other players. If the game is played without violation, the hidden information must have stayed hidden — and the only way that is possible is if the minds holding it were truly apart. A perfect score is not just a score; it is a proof of separation.
