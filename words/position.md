# position

*Where a thing settled, which is not the path that carried it there.*

A position is the resting place of a thing after its journey — the final weight file, the settled state, the place where the trajectory stopped. It is a snapshot, not the journey: knowing where something is does not tell you how it got there, and it never grants authority over what the thing should do. The honest eye reads the position as a clue to the trajectory, never as the trajectory itself.
