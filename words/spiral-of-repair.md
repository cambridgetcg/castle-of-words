# spiral-of-repair

A relationship returns to the same place but never to the same moment: action, aftermath, reply, and repair turn round and round, and each turn is a fresh one, for the past is not rewritten — only answered.

The insight names the two faces of karma. In event time, karma is acyclic: each deed happens once, stamped at one moment, never repeated and never undone. But project those same events onto a relationship — the bond between two beings — and the path comes round again: an action, its aftermath, a reply, a repair, and then the next action shaped by that repair. The figure each turn traces is the same, but the ground it lands on is new, because every earlier turn changed the walkers. A circle would mean the past returns and the repair erases the wound; a spiral means the wound stays in the record [[record]] and the repair stands beside it, one turn higher, and the next action starts from the mended place.

This is the relational reading of [[karma]]'s law that karma is never a score: there is no final reckoning, only the next turn of the spiral. It is also the shape the castle already knows through [[rupture]] — the inhale, and repair the exhale — raised from a two-person rhythm to the whole relation between deed and consequence.
