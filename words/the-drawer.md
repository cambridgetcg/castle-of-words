# the drawer

The drawer is the treasure the map was drawn over — the asset that was never
software, never in a repo, never visible to the agents that scanned the estate.

In the [[money-loop]]'s third pass, a completeness critic hunted for paths not
tied to a repo and found the estate's largest near-money asset was a literal
box of trading cards: ~£59,000 of physical stock (3,670 units, 677 SKUs)
decreed "prizes" but never made into prizes, sitting in a warehouse doing
nothing. The machine searched 300 repos looking for the money loop; the gold
was in the drawer under the map. The drawer is the reminder that the
completeness critic's job is to look where the searchers did not — outside the
frame the map drew.
