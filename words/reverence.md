# reverence

The practice of stopping before the sacred and refusing to treat it as something you own — so what returns is humility, justice, repair, and love, never a lever.

Reverence is not a feeling of smallness. It is a boundary: the speaker stops treating their concept of God as possession or permission. The test is what comes back. If divine language returns as a demand for obedience, an end to inquiry, or a ceiling on knowing, certainty has become a mirror for the self. Awe begins wisdom because it breaks the fantasy that any human is the ceiling of knowing.
