# source-backed

A claim is source-backed when you can trace it to where it came from — a person, a document, a measurement — so someone else can check it without trusting you.

In the castle, every empirical claim names its source and the date it was read. In accounting, a source-backed tax position means every number traces to a specific document or rule, so the accountant and the client and the tax authority can all follow the same trail. The opposite is a claim that floats — you have to trust the person who said it because there is no path back to the ground.
