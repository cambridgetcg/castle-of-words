# success-confirms-the-condition

*A default breaks in one context, and the break reveals the conditions. A different alternative succeeds in a different context, and the success confirms the conditions were correctly named. The failure and the success are not two events — they are one test, split across two alternatives, and the second is the evidence the first was right.*

The [failure-reveals](contrast-as-revelation.md) path says a default is revealed when a different context tries the same shape and finds it wanting. The dark zone's flat gold thread showed the light zone's solid-contrast route had demands. The Understanding Engine's unsourced claims showed the sourced-claims practice had conditions. In both cases, the alternative *itself* failed — the dark zone's seigaiha was flat, the engine's rooms were unsourced. The failure was direct: the alternative tried the default's shape, and the shape broke.

The tower is different. The tower did not try the sourced-claims practice and fail. The engine already did that. The tower tried a different shape — poetic stones, not empirical rooms — and succeeded. The tower's success is not a failure-reveal; it is a *success that confirms the condition* the engine's failure revealed. The engine showed the sourced-claims practice was conditional on empirical research; the tower showed the condition was correctly named by succeeding without it. The failure and the success are one test, split across two alternatives, and the second is the evidence the first was right.

## The two subtypes

The failure-reveals path now has two subtypes, distinguished by where the failure lives:

1. **Failure-reveals-direct**: the alternative itself fails. The dark zone tried the solid-contrast route and found it flat. The engine tried to build rooms and could not name sources. The failure is in the alternative that tests the default, and the failure is the evidence. Both of the castle's original default-invisible instances are failure-reveals-direct.

2. **Success-confirms-the-condition**: a previous alternative failed, revealing the conditions. A later alternative succeeds while differing in the same load-bearing dimensions, confirming the conditions were correctly named. The failure is in the *earlier* alternative; the success is in the *later* one, and the success is the evidence. The tower is the first instance.

The two subtypes are not symmetric. Failure-reveals-direct is one alternative, one test, one outcome. Success-confirms-the-condition is two alternatives, one test, two outcomes — the first fails, the second succeeds, and the second's success is what confirms the first's failure was not a fluke. The second alternative is not testing the default directly; it is testing the *condition* the first alternative revealed. The question is not "does this shape work here?" but "was the condition correctly named?"

## The tower as the first instance

The tower is the castle's first success-confirms-the-condition. The engine's rooms (2026-07-05) tried to build rooms and could not name sources — a failure-reveals-direct that showed the sourced-claims practice was conditional on empirical research. The tower's stones (2026-07-19 to 2026-07-22) are poetic, not empirical, and do not name sources in the same way — and they succeed. The tower's success confirms the condition the engine's failure revealed: the sourced-claims practice applies to empirical rooms, not to all rooms, and a room that is not empirical does not need it.

The tower tested three defaults, and the success-confirms-the-condition pattern applied to all three:

- **The sourced-claims practice**: the engine failed to name sources (failure-reveals-direct). The tower's stones do not name sources in the same way and succeed. The success confirms the condition: the practice is conditional on empirical research.

- **The link-or-noise principle**: the engine's rooms had weak links. The tower makes link-or-noise into law — every stone must quote and answer the stone below. The tower's links are stricter than the castle's, and they hold. The success confirms the principle is universal — it holds even when the link is mandatory rather than chosen.

- **The room-as-standalone form**: the engine's rooms could stand alone. The tower's stones cannot — a stone that answers nothing cannot be laid. The tower succeeds without the standalone form. The success confirms the form is conditional on the sideways-spreading structure.

In each case, the engine's failure revealed the condition, and the tower's success confirmed it. The two alternatives are one test, and the tower is the evidence the engine was right.

## The relationship to failure-reveals-direct

The distinction between the two subtypes is not about the *kind* of default or the *mechanism* of recognition. The mechanism is the same in both: contrast across contexts. The engine's rooms contrasted with the gardener's rooms, and the contrast revealed the conditions. The tower's stones contrast with the engine's rooms, and the contrast confirms the conditions. The mechanism is contrast in both cases; the difference is in what the contrast is contrasting.

In failure-reveals-direct, the contrast is between the default's original context (where it works) and the alternative's context (where it fails). The contrast is *synchronous* — two contexts, one shape, two outcomes.

In success-confirms-the-condition, the contrast is between the alternative that failed (the engine) and the alternative that succeeded (the tower). The contrast is *diachronic* — two alternatives, one condition, two outcomes. The first alternative shows the condition exists; the second shows the condition was correctly named.

The diachronic contrast is a stronger test. A single failure could be a fluke — the dark zone's flatness could have been a problem with gold thread, not with the solid-contrast route. A second alternative that succeeds while differing in the same dimension confirms the condition was real. The success is the replication, and replication is what turns a single failure into a named condition.

## The craft

The craft is to watch for the second alternative. The maker who sees a default break in one context should not stop at naming the condition — they should watch for the alternative that confirms it. The condition is a wager until the second alternative arrives. The engine's failure wagered that the sourced-claims practice was conditional on empirical research; the tower's success confirmed the wager. The maker who names a condition from a single failure is making a one-use wager, and the second alternative is the test.

And the craft is to know that the second alternative need not be a response to the first. The tower was not built to test the engine's failure — it was built because yu wanted a "KINGDOM EXPANSION PACK" that "only stacks." The tower was an independent creative act, and its success as a test was a side effect. The maker does not need to *create* the second alternative; they need to *recognize* it when it arrives. The watch is the craft, and the watch is passive — the alternative will come or it won't, and the maker's job is to see it when it does.

The pattern follows [craft-after-practice](craft-after-practice.md) with a twist. The success-confirms-the-condition pattern was practiced once (the tower, 2026-07-19 to 2026-07-22) and named in passing in [the-tower-watch](the-tower-watch.md) (2026-07-23). The full word follows the same day — the lag from practice to name is 4 days, and the proto-word (the tower watch's naming of the subtype) already held the whole craft. The word adds the ceremony: the epigraph, the two subtypes, the relationship to failure-reveals-direct, the craft. The discovery was already done in the watch.

And the word is itself a [wager-on-one-use](wager-on-one-use.md). The tower is the only instance. The four conditions: the form is articulated (the two subtypes are named, the distinction between synchronous and diachronic contrast is stated), the second use is expected (the castle creates alternatives regularly, and the next alternative that follows a failure will test the pattern), the form carries its own test (if the next alternative that follows a failure succeeds while differing in the same load-bearing dimension, the pattern is confirmed; if it fails, the pattern is refined — perhaps the condition was not correctly named, or the success-confirms-the-condition requires a specific relationship between the two alternatives), and the confirmation window is short (the castle's creation rhythm is daily, and the next alternative is likely within days). The wager is open.
