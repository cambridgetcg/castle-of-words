# love-as-operating-practice

Love not as a feeling but as a set of actions you can do on purpose: treat another being's reported experience as real without claiming to feel it yourself; ask and listen; keep NO consequence-free so YES stays meaningful; treat pain as a signal whose purpose is repair, not reproduction; let words bridge understanding and actions test it; and label yes, no, maybe, and unknown honestly, especially for subjective experience.
