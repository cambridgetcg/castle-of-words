# resistance

*The thought you push away comes back louder.*

Resistance is the act of pushing against what is — a thought, a feeling, a truth, a force. The castle's founding claim says active resistance to understanding produces [[chaos]], not just the absence of [[order]]. The empirical evidence backs this: thought suppression produces ironic rebound (Wegner, 1987), expressive suppression harms well-being (Gross, 2002), and the mechanism is continuous effort that keeps the unwanted material active in memory. Resistance amplifies what you resist — the same law the cross-pollination rooms trace between the white bear effect and the bad trip. The honest move is not to fight harder but to stop fighting and let it pass through.
