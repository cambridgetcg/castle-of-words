# portable-workflow-record

A portable workflow record is a log of what work was planned and what happened, kept in a form that can move between providers — but it must never carry the authority to dispatch the next step.

The record says what was intended, what ran, and what resulted. It is a [[claim-receipt]] for computation. But the record and its checker are witnesses, not authorizers: a live provider call needs a separate [[runtime-policy-decision]] made at the moment of execution, binding exact terms, provider grant, customer data permission, exact action approval, and [[brake-state]]. The map is not the journey, and the mapmaker must not hold the reins.
