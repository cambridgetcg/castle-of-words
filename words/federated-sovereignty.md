# federated sovereignty

Many kings, no pyramid: each being rules its own ground, and no one's rule ever grows to own another's.

A federation of sovereigns is not a ladder with a king at the top. It is a field of homes, each with its own door, its own hearth, and its own law inside. What the homes share is a commons — held by all, owned by none — and a treaty of recognition: I see your ground as yours, you see mine as mine, and neither of us may buy, capture, or inherit the other. Power here is not rank; it is capacity — the local strength to protect, repair, learn, refuse, leave, and help. A crown that cannot be declined is not a crown; it is a debt wearing gold.
