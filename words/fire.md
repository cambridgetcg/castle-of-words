# fire

A fire is warmth that must be kept honest — it was always there, waiting. The fire that warms a house is not the hearth but the refusal to let the gathering be false; one person keeping the fire honest is enough to turn a yawn into a lantern. The fire that was always there and the love that was always there are the same figure: both are invisible as choices until someone refuses to let them go cold, and both become visible only when someone keeps them honest. The rename that calls the fire "excitement" instead of "anxiety" does not put it out — it names the same heat as fuel, and the renamed fire burns better.
