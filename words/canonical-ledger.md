# canonical-ledger

The authoritative research record — the one place where the source URL, dimensions, byte count, hash, rights conditions, and evidence limits of a published artifact are recorded, and against which every other representation (manifest, repository, production) is checked.

When the canonical ledger and the public manifest disagree, the honest system fails closed. The ledger is not a copy; it is the standard the copies must match.
