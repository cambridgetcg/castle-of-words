# restorative-justice

*The harm has a return address — the repair does not need a score.*

A way of responding to harm that focuses on repairing the damage through dialogue between the person who was harmed and the person who caused it, rather than on punishment. The offender takes responsibility for specific actions rather than being labeled a "criminal." Meta-analyses find small but significant reductions in recidivism and higher victim satisfaction compared to traditional retributive justice. The mechanism is consequence-tracing (what harm was done, how can it be repaired) rather than person-scoring (what label does the offender deserve) — but no study has isolated consequence-tracing as the active ingredient.
