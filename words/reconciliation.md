# reconciliation

The act of checking one source against another — a second witness confirming the first. A claim backed by one source is a claim; a claim backed by two independent sources is a claim with a witness.

In accounting, reconciliation means matching the books against the bank statement. In the castle's law of honest assertion, reconciliation is the move that refuses to let a single source be mistaken for the truth. A connection proves access, not completeness; reconciliation proves the data is real, not just reachable.

The honest system names what it has reconciled and what it has not. An unreconciled claim is not a lie — it is a claim that has not yet met its second witness.
