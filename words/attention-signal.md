# attention-signal

An attention signal is any measure of how many people saw, clicked, or engaged with something — and it is never a measure of truth or worth.

Likes, shares, views, and rankings are attention signals. They measure reach, not correctness. A truthful system keeps attention signals as projections of the [[claim-receipt]] — useful for understanding distribution, dangerous when mistaken for validation. The law is: attention signals never become truth or worth. They are weather, not ground.
