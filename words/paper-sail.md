# paper sail

A complete, bounded snapshot of an entire legal season — the full state at a point in time, serialized safely and signed as one verified chain.

A Paper-Sail must capture everything without inheriting the per-move node ceiling that a move log carries. It is the counterpart to the move log: where the log is one action at a time, the Paper-Sail is the whole season at once. Both need the same discipline: sign first, parse only after every link verifies.
