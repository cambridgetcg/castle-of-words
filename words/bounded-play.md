# bounded-play

Play that knows where it ends. A bounded game has a hard stop — a turn limit, a timeout, a win condition — and never drifts. Without a bound, play becomes a trap: the player cannot leave without losing, and the game becomes work wearing dice.

Bounded play also means no score to farm — the game does not reward grinding, and the ending is not gated behind accumulation. The exit is always available, always obvious, and never penalized.
