# Accounting

*The craft of keeping money's story honest.*

Accounting is the practice of recording money movements so that anyone can trace a transaction from the evidence that triggered it through the books to the tax filing — and back again.

In the castle, accounting appears as a product-design craft: the honest product leads with one money movement and preserves an inspectable path through every layer, labelling what is not yet built rather than pretending the path is complete.
