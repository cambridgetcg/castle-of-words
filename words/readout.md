# readout

A gauge that shows the state of a thing without moving it — the thermometer, not the fire.

In the castle, a readout is an act that the bond *generates*: the repair rate is the face of a bond already formed, not a technique the couple practices into existence. The residue it leaves is evidence of the deposit, not fuel for it. The opposite is a [[lever]], where the act itself is the mechanism and practice builds the capacity.

The clinical warning is precise: an engine that treats a readout as a lever will coach the repair and find r=.11 (null). The signal lives in observed repair — the spontaneous kind the bond produces — not in coached repair, because the bond is the mechanism and the repair is its face.
