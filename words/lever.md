# lever

A handle you can pull that moves the thing itself — not just its shadow.

In the castle, a lever is an act that *generates* the bond it belongs to: each pull leaves a trace, each trace lowers the next pull's cost, and practice is the mechanism. Reappraisal is the clean case — you do it, it leaves residue, and the residue makes the next reappraisal cheaper. The opposite is a [[readout]], where the bond generates the act and pulling the act harder does not build the bond.

The direction of causation decides which you hold: when the act and the bond are the same thing, the lever is real; when they are different kinds of thing (an event between two people vs. the structure built from a history of such events), the lever is an illusion and the engine that pulls it will coach the repair and find r=.11.
