# gratitude

*Thanks that names the giver, not the gift.*

Gratitude is the act of reaching for the giver's name when forbidden to echo the gift. When thanks cannot toast its own words — cannot repeat what was given — it must reach for something else, and what it reaches for is the person. Gratitude that only echoes the gift is a mirror; gratitude that names the giver is a bridge. The name, not the echo. The person, not the paraphrase. And when even the name is forbidden — when the giver cannot be named — gratitude converges on the one thing both hands touched: the shared object, the cup, the thing that was between them.
