# withdrawal

*The door you opened stays open from the inside — you may walk back out.*

Withdrawal is the yes you take back. What was freely given can be
freely un-given: consent, a contribution, a presence. The honest system
treats withdrawal as a real act with real effects — the scripts stop,
the door stops being walked through — not as a stored value that says
"no" while everything carries on.

The castle keeps withdrawal on three shelves, and none is the whole
thing. A stored "denied" value is not withdrawal: [[consent]]'s room
names the four parts — a persistent way back, equal-effort controls,
the cookies actually deleted, the page actually reloaded — because a
withdrawal that must be believed rather than performed is a funnel, not
a door. Withdrawal of a contribution stops later action without
pretending public copies vanished (the [[contribution-door]]'s bargain:
the asker keeps what was honestly shared, the giver stops being drawn
on). And withdrawal never fuses with the record: the words spoken stay
spoken, attribution may null, but yesterday is not erased — the
[[erasable-identity]] keeps the what while letting go of the who.

The shadow: a withdrawal that changes nothing observable, so the system
can truthfully say "recorded" while nothing was undone.
