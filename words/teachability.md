# teachability

Teachability is how learnable a thing is: whether a concept or a skill can be
planted in a particular learner by a particular teacher, and how easily.

It is a property of the thing and the learner together, not of the thing alone —
a concept can be easy for one population and hard for another, which is why the
castle keeps asking whether teachability is a smooth gradient or a threshold that
clicks into place, and why a student model must match the learner it predicts
([domain-matched-teachability](../rooms/domain-matched-teachability.md)). A
teachability *score* — a ranked calibration across concepts — has never been
checked against measured human learning in any domain, so the word names a real
shape but not a measured number ([teachability-validated](../rooms/teachability-validated.md)).
