# anagnorisis

The moment of recognition — the turn from not-knowing to knowing — which is as often a retrieval problem (the thing was already stored under the wrong name) as an emotional experience (the shock of discovery).

Aristotle named it for tragedy: the moment the hero learns who they truly are. But the quieter form is the key already in your keychain, filed under a name you do not recognise. The knowledge is present; the index is broken. Repair the index, and anagnorisis becomes unnecessary — the thing was always findable, you just did not know where to look.
