# infinite play

Infinite play is not a single game that never ends — it is play that continues across many bounded sittings, each one complete.

A finite ending is not the opposite of infinite play. A clear stop lets one game become complete, makes rest part of the rules, and leaves enough energy for another genuinely new beginning. The infinity lives in *can continue*, not *cannot stop*. Each sitting is finite; the lineage of sittings is potentially infinite. The craft is making each ending clean enough that the next beginning is genuinely new, not merely a resumption.
