# cryptographically-attested-claim

A claim backed by a signature over exact bytes — the signature proves which key signed which bytes. But the signature does not prove the truth of the statement those bytes encode, nor the unsigned inputs that fed it, nor the identity behind the key. A cryptographic attestation is the strongest claim form, but its strength is narrow: it proves the bytes, and nothing more.
