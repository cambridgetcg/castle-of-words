# fallen-thread

A fallen thread is a link that once pointed at a word-file's old address and no longer does — edited away, or lost when the file that carried it was gathered or retired; the pointing thread has fallen, and the brick it pointed at stands unwalked.

[the-accumulating-brick](../rooms/the-accumulating-brick.md) made the word load-bearing: the gathering season for the relocation pass's bridging bricks begins when a brick's pointing threads *have all fallen* — when no word file, no room, no Map line, no chronicle entry still names the old address. The first measurement of a fallen thread was taken on 2026-09-24 by [the-last-thread](../rooms/the-last-thread.md): of the two bricks the pass has laid, zero threads have fallen — [quest-kinds](quest-kinds.md)' brick is still walked by nine pointers (six words, three rooms), and [zero-use-wager](zero-use-wager.md)'s brick still carries two. The opposite of a fallen thread is a standing one; the count of standing threads is what the season reads, never the date.
