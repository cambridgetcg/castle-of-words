# standing-phrase-census

*A census is not an audit and not a repair. It is a walk with a notebook: every standing phrase that wears a fact is written down, each one marked pointed or unpointed, and the seam's map is the list itself — the mending belongs to the phrases that need it, not to the counting.*

[the-phrase-that-holds](the-phrase-that-holds.md) (built 2026-09-18) found the re-dealing seam opens in the third kind of standing phrase: one whose truth lives in a receipt the phrase does not point at, and whose correction landed in another silo. The story's closing line was found by walking the phrases a quest named, not by a census. The quest that planted this word (quests.md, 2026-09-18) asked for the census: front/, the party page, trophies.md, and the made works' dates-and-tallies beyond the headline phrases. This word is that walk.

The walk covered thirteen standing works and files: anthem.md, story.md, tour.md, tour-short.md, overview.md, gate.md, questions.md, quests.md, chronicle.md, trophies.md, front/README.md, front/party.html, and front/build.js. Each work was read for every standing phrase that wears a fact — a count, a date, a claim about the castle's state. Each phrase was asked the seam's question: *does the truth live in the words, in a receipt the phrase points at, or in a receipt nobody points at?*

## What the census finds

The castle's standing phrases sort into three kinds, and the three kinds are the same three the-phrase-that-holds named:

1. **Truth lives in the words alone** — creeds and house words (the anthem's verses, gate.md's opening). No seam possible; the words are the whole claim.
2. **Truth lives in a receipt the phrase points at** — the dated glances (tour, short tour, overview, story's mend), the live counts (the Map, the chips), the provenance marks (front/README, the seal). The pointer is the prophylactic; the seam cannot open here.
3. **Truth lives in a receipt nobody points at** — the story's closing line (found and mended 2026-09-18), and now the party page's standing counts (found today). The receipt moved; the phrase stood still; no pointer was carved.

The census's finding is that the seam has exactly two instances in the whole castle, and both are in made works whose claims are about the castle's own state at a moment. The story's close was the first; the party page's counts are the second. Both are the same shape: a phrase that was true when written, carved for a day, and left standing as if it were true forever.

The party page is the worse instance. The story's close at least had the chronicle one door away; the party page's counts have no pointer at all, no date, no confession of horizon. A visitor who opens party.html today meets *132 rooms and counting* and has no way to know the count is five months stale.

## The mend

The castle's mending law is accretion, never rewrite. The mend for the party page is the same shape as the story's: carve the pointer beside the standing phrase, confess the horizon, and let the telling stand.

The mend, laid at front/party.html beneath the stats ring in the same visit:

> *drawn for the party of 2026-06-18, when the castle held 132 rooms and 84 word-bricks — the castle keeps growing, and the live count is always the Map's at gate.md*

The page's other standing count — *"132 rooms of understanding"* in the invite box below — is covered by the same mend: it sits one confession away, in the same glance as the stats ring it repeats. One dated glance, carved where the counts stand, confesses for the whole page.

And the build script's own honesty is already pointed — the chips are computed at build time. The uncertain section below asks whether the built artifact should carry its own build-date, the way the made works do.

## The craft

For the maker who carves a standing phrase about the castle's state: **the phrase must carry its own horizon or point at the instrument that shows it.** The dated glance (*drawn on 2026-06-12… the Map is always the live count*) is the castle's own prophylactic, proven since June. The undated tally is a seam waiting its quiet months.

For the reader who meets a standing phrase: **ask where its truth lives.** If the words are the whole claim (a creed), trust the words. If the words point at a receipt (a date, a pointer, a computed count), walk the pointer. If the words stand alone with no date and no pointer, the phrase may be a seam — and the kindest thing to do is to carve the pointer beside it.

## What this word cannot settle

uncertain: whether the tour's wing-path lines are truly pointed or only structurally pointed. The path-line *ease feels like truth* is followed by its rooms, and the rooms are the receipt — but the receipt moves. If the glaziers' gallery were re-read and its hinge re-named, the path-line would stand uncorrected until someone re-walked the tour. The structural pointing is stronger than no pointing, but weaker than a confessed horizon. The seam's third kind may have a sub-kind: the phrase whose receipt is the next thing read, where the next thing is itself a moving target.

And uncertain: whether the front's build artifacts should carry their own build-dates. The made works do; the front's HTML does not. The build script is honest, but the artifact it leaves is a standing file that may be opened long after it was built. The seam is in the artifact, not the script.
