# authorization

A key that opens the door proves you were allowed to enter, not that you left the room as you found it. Authorization is the boundary that says who may act — who may elevate, who may mutate — and it is separate from visibility (who may see) and retention (how long the receipt lasts).

The honest system tests each boundary independently. A witness at the door proves authorization, not immutability. Fusing authorization with any other boundary is the same error as a schema that shows only the first branch of a union — it lets one gate pretend to be all of them.
