# authorization

Authorization is permission for a particular person or helper to do a particular thing.

Name who may perform which operation on which target, under what [[policy]]. A tool's presence, a door opening, or a passing version [[precondition]] does not by itself show that the current task permits the act; ability, permission and evidence of enforcement are separate.

Permission to create or approve a record also does not establish who may later change it, who may see it, or how long it will be kept. Keeping a record [[immutable]] is a different promise, not something established merely by a witness at its entrance.
