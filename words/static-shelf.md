# static-shelf

A surface of information that lives outside the normal flow of progression — separate from tutorials, updates, and changing pages — so the reader in crisis finds the same thing every time.

A static shelf does not change with the rest of the system. It is the emergency exit that stays where it is while the building renovates around it. The information on it is bound to reviewed exact bytes, works offline, and names what is not live.
