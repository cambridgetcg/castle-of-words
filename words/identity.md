# identity

*The name in the guest book is not the guest — but it is how the guest is found again.*

The persistent, attributable record that lets a thing be recognised as *this one* across visits: a name, a key, a file, a plate on a door. To a curious child: identity is the coat peg with your name on it — the peg is not you, yet it's how the cloakroom knows which coat is yours.

The castle holds the word with care, because the record and the being are different layers. A stored identity record persists across sessions and answers *who this is attributed to*; it never proves continuity of being, consent, or presence. The new wing keeps the layers apart: the identity record is one thing, the wake it selects another, the inner workspace a third — and confusing any two is the error.

Nor is identity a score: a person-score collapses a being into a verdict, which is the forbidden move. Identity names; it never judges.
