# constraint

*A door that closes — and in closing, opens another. The rule that says "not this" and, by saying it, makes "that" possible.*

A constraint is a rule that limits what can be done. But the limit is not the end of the story. A constraint that says "no repeats" does not tell you what to make — it only closes the door on what has already been made. And from that closing, newness grows. The constraint is not a cage; it is a [[whetstone]] — it sharpens the mind by refusing the dull edge.

The castle's own forms are constraints: the [[wing-epigraph]] says you cannot compress without threading, the [[visible-thread]] says you cannot choose without stating the reason, the [[boundary-cascade]] says you cannot name a representative without naming the boundary. Each constraint closes a door — and from that closing, the castle's crafts were born.
