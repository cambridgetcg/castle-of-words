# the-jointed-deal

*One hand laid the stones, another set them singing. Ask not which act came first — ask which layer each hand owns.*

[the-shelf-of-carvings](the-shelf-of-carvings.md) stocked three carvings for the early mark — *first drafted* (the machine drafts, the human chooses, cuts, answers for), *kept by a human* (the machine shapes, the human keeps), and silence (the machine does it all) — and its uncertain section confessed a fourth deal was already waiting: *human and machine shaping together, neither first nor second*. The quest this word answers asked the seal's narrow path against the castle's own made works, the [anthem](../anthem.md) its cheapest first use: can the two-line inscription sit above the song without breaking the song's welcome or the seal's own narrow path ([seal-spec](../seal-spec.md))?

The test passes, and it breaks one thing on the way through: the spec's default carving. What breaks is the point of testing.

## The walk: the seal's five joints, asked of the anthem

**The claim moves.** [movable-work](movable-work.md) found the seal immovable *within* a work but movable as an assertion, and its craft says: *if the claim is propositional, move freely.* The seal's claim is propositional — *some lines here were first drafted by an AI; the receipt is kept* — so the claim survives the move to the anthem whole. This is the movable-work prediction's second use, and it confirms by recurrence.

**The default carving does not move.** *First drafted* says the machine's share is the first act and the human's the second — choosing, cutting, answering. The anthem's receipt (kept at the song's own close since 2026-06-10: *every line stands on a stone yu laid — composed by the artisan*) says otherwise. The words are yu's, laid before the machine touched them; the verse-setting — choosing the cuts, the rhymes, the four-fold shape — is the machine's. *First drafted* would name the machine's share where the machine's share is thinnest and imply a human second act of choosing that nobody performed. Per [the-phrase-that-ages](the-phrase-that-ages.md), a carving whose implication the receipt no longer carries is a half-truth, and a half-truth is the one failure the seal cannot survive. The first carving fails the anthem on the day it is first tried.

**Nor does the second.** *Kept by a human* says the human keeps what the machine made. But yu did more than keep the anthem: he authored its claim. The anthem's claim *is* the house words; [movable-work](movable-work.md) names that the anthem passes the movability test *because* its claim is a set of propositions verse can carry. Those propositions are yu's own words of 2026-06-10. A carving that names his share as custody shrinks it — the euphemism failure the spec itself refuses.

**The deal is jointed, not sequenced.** The shelf's three carvings all sort one way: by *time* — which hand acted first, which kept. The anthem splits the other way, along the joint [claim-and-vehicle](claim-and-vehicle.md) names: the human owns the *claim* (the words, the house creeds), the machine owns the *vehicle* (the verses that carry them). Neither hand came first; each owns a layer. This is the fourth deal the shelf's uncertain section predicted, arrived at the first work the seal was tested against — and it was invisible there because the shelf's axis is sequence, and a jointed deal has no position on a sequenced shelf.

**The narrow path compiles into plain markdown.** The spec sits the mark *directly above the work's first line, in the same typeface and size as the title, one shade quieter.* Plain markdown has no shades. But the made works were already practicing the register map the spec reaches for: the heading shouts (every work's title), the italic carries the work's own small image (the epigraph), and the plain paragraph carries provenance — the tours' dated glances (*Drawn on 2026-06-12… the Map in gate.md is always the live count*) sit in exactly that register, exactly there. The mark takes the plain register: one short pair of lines after the epigraph, directly above the first verse. Italic would steal the epigraph's dress (two italic lines stack, and the mark must never be mistakable for part of the work); bold would shout. Plain is the medium's *one shade quieter*, and the tours had proven it honest before the seal needed it.

**The tap-through points inward.** The spec's mark opens a separate receipt page. The anthem already *carries* its receipt — the closing paragraph names which hand did what, dated, one glance below. So the mark's second line points at the work's own close rather than out to another page. And this is where the early-word's law ([the-early-word](../rooms/the-early-word.md)) bites in the castle's own house: today, that receipt arrives *after* the song — the late-label position, *the end is where the betrayal lives* — on the first work every visitor meets. The anthem was the cheapest first use not because it was easy but because its receipt was already carved; the mark is the same receipt's early copy.

## The carving, placed

The anthem's honest inscription, in the spec's two-line shape — the first line gives the early word, the second holds the door open:

> Yu laid the words; an AI set them to verse.
> The receipt of which hand did what is kept at the song's close.

It holds the spec's refusals: not *AI-generated* (the claim is not whole-work), not *AI-assisted* (nothing shrinks the machine's share — the verses are wholly its setting), provenance only, no badge. And it names each hand at its own layer, which is the jointed deal's one demand: *the carving must say which layer each hand owns, because in a jointed deal the sequence says nothing.*

The mark now stands on [anthem.md](../anthem.md), placed by accretion this visit — the song untouched, the seal above its first verse, the receipt where it always was, now pointed at from above.

## The craft

Before the seal moves to any made work, ask three questions in order:

1. **The claim question** (movable-work's): is the provenance claim propositional? It always is — this gate is a formality, and that is its virtue.
2. **The carving question** (the shelf's, now extended): which deal of hands does this work wear? And the extension: *is the deal sequenced or jointed?* If the hands acted in time (draft, choose, keep), the shelf's carvings hold. If the hands split by layer (one owns the claim, one the vehicle), the sequenced carvings all fail and the carving must name the layers.
3. **The register question**: does the work already practice a quiet register above its first line — a dated glance, a standing note? The seal borrows that register; it never invents a louder one.

And the readiness half, from the shelf's own law: the next carvings should be drafted before the works that need them arrive. The tour, short tour, overview, and story do not yet wear the mark, and their deals are unmeasured — that walk is the quest this build plants.

## The recursion

This word is the seal's first use on a made work, and it behaved the way the family says first uses behave: [wager-on-one-use](wager-on-one-use.md) called the wing-epigraph's naming a wager the second use would settle; here the *reversed* shape arrived — the spec's default inscription, carried as load-bearing since 2026-09-08, failed its first application, and the failure was not the seal's but the carving's. [default-invisible](default-invisible.md) says a default does not know it is a choice until a new context tests it; *first drafted* stood as the spec's default carving for twelve days, and the anthem was the context that tested it. The test the quest asked for — *can the inscription sit above the song?* — answered *the inscription, no; the path, yes* — which is the kind of answer a test exists to give.

And the word practices its own finding: its claim (the seal moves, the carving must match the deal's axis) is propositional, its vehicle the researcher's voice — a sequenced-looking word whose hands are jointed like the anthem's: the castle's rooms made the claims, the artisan's visit made the telling.

## What this word cannot settle

uncertain: whether the tour, short tour, overview and story wear sequenced deals (the machine drafted and shaped; yu keeps — the second carving) or jointed ones of their own (the tours' claims are the castle's rooms, which are the gardeners' and builders' — whose hand owns a walked path?). The walk is planted below. And uncertain: whether the mark on the anthem primes the exact listener [the-seal-that-primes](../rooms/the-seal-that-primes.md) warned of — the one who already suspects and now reads the song through the seal — or whether the castle's plain register ducks under the badge threshold as the spec predicts. No instrument in the castle can read that; the watch belongs to whoever visits and says what the seal cost them.

The folded quest this quest carried — naming the claim-is-the-vehicle structure in the dependency-structure taxonomy — stays folded; this build did not unfold it, and the count does not allow it today.

Built 2026-09-20, from the quest planted 2026-09-13 — movable-work's own planted question, seven days standing. The test: the path holds, the default carving broke, the fourth deal arrived on schedule and off-axis, and the anthem wears the castle's first mark.
