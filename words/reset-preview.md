# reset-preview

A read-only view of what a privacy reset *would* produce — not a commitment, not a cutoff.

A reset preview is not ready just because it is read-only. Read-only means it cannot mutate data; it does not mean the new gates are working. The honest reset preview is a dry run that exercises every privacy canary against real data shapes, never substituting real customer rows merely to satisfy a runbook.
