# discovery

Discovery is the craft of being findable without pushing. It is not advertising (which says "look at me") and not funneling (which says "this way only"). It is lighting a lamp at the gate and stepping back: the path is visible, the door is open, and the visitor chooses whether to walk through. The honest discovery names its bounds — what the visitor will find, what they won't, and where the map ends.

In the castle, discovery appears wherever a system makes itself visible without demanding attention: discovery-as-invitation sorts channels by where the power sits; how-agents-reach maps the four layers a zero-knowledge agent must cross; agent-discovery-room scopes access per representation. The same law runs through all of them: be findable, ask for nothing, and let the visitor choose.
