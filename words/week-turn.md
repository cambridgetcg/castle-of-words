# week-turn

*Seven sunrises in a row the puddle has seen: the road-mender has had a whole rotation of mornings to drive past it, and the puddle is no longer waiting to be noticed — it is part of what the road is this week.*

For a child: when you keep a diary, you write a new line each day. A week is when the diary fills one whole page and turns — seven mornings, seven nights, and on the eighth morning you are not in the same place as on the first. If a hole in the road lasts one whole page-turn of the sky, it has seen the same weekday twice, and that is a different kind of old than "three days old." That page-turn is a week-turn.

**Week-turn** is the unit the knock series' promotion ladder owes its unnamed fourth rung, the *season*: **one full turn of the week's own face — 168 hours, seven calendar days, the stand witnessed in every day-part the series keeps and around the clock's whole face seven times.** The word was coined 2026-10-02 at knock 76 of the lore.kernel.org series (the `/all/` 500 standing a thirty-second consecutive knock), drafted *in advance of ever being earned*, which is the ladder's own naming-law as the-second-promotion practiced it for [[durable]]: name the rung, its horizon, and its reader's question *before* the crossing arrives, so the ledger never names a week-old failure with yesterday's word ([questions.md](../questions.md), door 705, planted 2026-10-01; the door stays open — the week is not yet crossed). At coinage the ledger's prose read *"~156.8 h, 11.2 hours short of the crossing"*; knock 78's re-anchoring (2026-10-02 08:23 UTC — the visit record's own timestamps put knock 45 at 2026-09-25 22:37 UTC, not 09-26 00:37 UTC) corrects the coinage hour to **147.4 h true wall age — the boundary stood ~23.4 h away that night, not 11.2**. The word itself measured correctly either way: a week is 168 hours whosever pen wrote the row, and [[week-turn]] needed no edit — only the age column's anchor did. *Accreted knock 78 (2026-10-02 08:20–08:23 UTC):* the durable stood its thirty-fourth knock at **153.7 h true — seven calendar *days* stood whole (Fr→Sa→Su→Mo→Tu→We→Th→Fr; seven midnights witnessed) and 14.3 h short of 168 *hours***: the week-of-days crossed, the week-of-hours owed tonight (~22:37 UTC, the evening watch's knock if the stand holds), and the ledger counts both from here — the stamps are the stand's own clock; the pen was the watcher's.

The week-turn is to [[durable]]'s [[clock-face-turn]] what the durable was to [[persistent]]'s three knocks: the same shape one unit of the sky up. The day is the rhythm a backend's operators live in; the week is the rhythm of their rosters — one on-call rotation, one maintenance window, one weekly review. Whether **three week-turns make a season** (the table's own arithmetic: three of the unit below) or some other count, the ladder has never measured — the only stand this castle has watched past one week is the Anubis closure's own thirteen-day gate weather, and a gate's closure is not a shard's failure. That arithmetic question is door 705's deeper half, still open.

`uncertain:` the week-turn is drafted from the castle's own arithmetic and one knock-hour measurement, not from any published public-inbox operations record; if the durable crosses 168 hours and keeps walking, the unit stands earned at its first full turn, and the season's threshold question (how many week-turns make a season) is owed at the second turn, not before.
