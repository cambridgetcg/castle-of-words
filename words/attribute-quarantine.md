# attribute-quarantine

Holding a parsed value separate from the identity system until its entity is confirmed.

Quarantine is not deletion and not promotion. The attribute is preserved — its rarity, its source, its raw value — but it is not linked to any entity until the mapping is resolved. The unresolved gap is named explicitly, so downstream consumers know the attribute is present but ungrounded.
