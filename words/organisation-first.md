# organisation-first

A design rule for public registers: model the institutional system before the people in it.

Join organisations by exact official identifiers (company number, charity registration), not by name matching or inferred association. Keep named people and inferred belief outside the bulk publication layer. Treat contact fields as potentially personal. Make unavailable or changed official feeds fail closed rather than silently stale.
