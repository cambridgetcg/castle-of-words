# double-loop-learning

*The thermostat that asks why it's set to 69°F — the question that changes the rules, not just the temperature.*

When something goes wrong, you can fix the mistake (single-loop) or ask why the rule that caused the mistake exists at all (double-loop). Argyris & Schön named this in 1978: single-loop learning corrects errors within existing rules; double-loop learning questions and modifies the underlying assumptions, goals, and norms. Organizations that do the second adapt better and drive more innovation — but the evidence is qualitative and case-based, not a controlled comparison.
