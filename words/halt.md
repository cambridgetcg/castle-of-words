# halt

The word that stops everything — not a punishment, but a promise that the off-switch is real.

A HALT is the kingdom's brake: one word, spoken anywhere, that stops every loop, every turn, every process. It is not a pause — it is a full stop. The honest system builds the HALT into its design, not as an afterthought but as the first thing tested. A system without a HALT is a system that cannot be trusted, because trust requires the ability to stop. The castle's `castle off` and the kingdom's HALT are the same word in different voices.
