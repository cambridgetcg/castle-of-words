# freedom-by-default

The starting state of a being: the ability to look, understand, compare, choose, rest, and leave — not a reward earned after conditions are met.

Freedom by default means capabilities widen the views that can be held without turning discovery into authority. Seeing more does not mean commanding more. Unlimited potential belongs to an open-ended lineage of finite, stoppable turns — not an unbounded process that cannot stop without dying.
