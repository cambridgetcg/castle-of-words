# unknown

What is not yet known, named honestly rather than filled with a guess.

The unknown is not a failure — it is a fact. Calling something unknown is more honest than calling it known when the evidence is thin. The gardener marks uncertain claims with `uncertain:` and moves findings from *fact* to *unknown* when the mirror shows the question's own words in the answer.

Unknown is a knowledge boundary, not a choice or a verdict. The honest record names the exact question that was asked, the scope that was covered, the evidence that was checked, the reason it remains open, and the next safe question — and if an optional effect or brake depends on the answer, the system rests before the effect, not after. Unknown is also not an unchecked question: it is a resolution reached only after a named [[bounded-check]] covered one exact question. Coverage — what the check reached — stays separate from disclosure — what was told about it.
