# visible-terms

*An invisible price is a lie — the honest exchange makes every term visible before the crossing begins.*

Visible terms are the conditions of an exchange declared openly before either side commits. In a door, the six visible things: path, rule, cost, consequence, uncertainty, and exit. In love, the two visible pans of 等價交換: the named want and the heard want. In a game, the sealed contribution that refuses to let one side see the other's move before declaring their own.

The dishonest move is to hide the terms — to charge prices in the dark, to narrow the passage without showing the walls, to let one side pay without knowing what the other will do. The honest move is to name everything, hide nothing, and let the other decide.
