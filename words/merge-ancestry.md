# merge-ancestry

The craft of comparing what came before a merge, not just what conflicts — a feature-line update can omit previously merged commits, turning a small PR into an intentional restoration that must be named and reverified.

Before merging after main advances, compare ancestry as well as file conflicts. A branch that looks like a small change may be silently restoring something that was deliberately removed — and that restoration is a choice that needs its own name and its own verification.
