# lantern

A small light carried by hand — not the sun that lights everything, but a single flame that lights the next step.

A map of lanterns is what three citizens made from a map of yawns at the housewarming: each yawn became a lantern when someone chose to keep the fire honest, and the path that emerged was lit by many small flames rather than one large one.
