# delegation

Delegation means giving a helper a clear part of a job while keeping track of how the pieces fit.

In an [[agent-harness]], helper coordination is different from the model's [[reasoning-effort]]. A bounded helper task has an end and a result that can be checked; having helpers available does not prove that a particular task used them or that their answers are right.
