# BPM

*Beats per minute — the pulse of the music, the heartbeat of the plan.*

BPM (beats per minute) is the tempo of a piece of music. For an agent's task, the BPM sets the pace: how fast the set moves, when the energy rises, when it rests. Exporting the BPM from a score lets an agent plan its turn to the music's own rhythm.
