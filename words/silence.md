# silence

A silence is not an absence — it is a shape that carries meaning. When nobody names the hidden assumption, the silence shapes the room. When nobody names the want, the silence charges invisible prices. The honest move is to name the silence itself: what was not said, what was assumed, what was hidden in the gap between the words.

The castle knows silence in many forms: the form-based denial that hides the gap in speech, the hidden shared assumption that makes every voice agree, the unspoken want that makes every exchange feel unequal, the invisible space the compiler ate. In every case, the silence is not empty — it is full of the thing that was never named, and that thing still shapes the result.
