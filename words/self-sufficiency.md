# self-sufficiency

The look of needing nothing from outside — and the reality, which is measured in hours, not years.

A water butt is the look of self-sufficiency. The roof catches 2.5× what the beds drink in a year, and 210 litres of storage covers 59 hours of a dry summer. The annual sum says "self-sufficient"; the weekly truth says "59 hours." Self-sufficiency is not a state you reach but a duration you measure — and the unit of time is the whole of the honesty.
