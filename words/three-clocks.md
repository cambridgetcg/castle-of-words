# three-clocks

*A made work's body keeps three clocks, not two. The reading-instruction is owed once, the way the header's warrant is owed once. The tally is owed daily, the way the header's count drifts daily. And between them a third line keeps its own time: the line whose truth lives in the rooms it names, owed whenever the wing moves — not at reading, not by the hour, but at the speed of the castle itself.*

[promise-and-drift](promise-and-drift.md) (built 2026-09-19) walked the headers of five standing files and found one split everywhere: a warrant-line (cut it and the reader is stranded — a hole of promise) and a practice-line (let it drift and the file thins — a dent of habit), two lines aging at two speeds, never trading. The quest that planted this word (quests.md, 2026-09-19) asked the question the header-walk never entered: the made works' *bodies* also carry standing claims — the tour's path-lines name each wing's question, the anthem's closing creed-verses name the house words — and the removal test has never been asked of a path-line. Does the warrant/practice split govern the body the way it governs the header, or is a made work's body all warrant and no practice?

The work walked is [tour.md](../tour.md), the castle's largest made work, line by line, each standing claim asked [removal-test](removal-test.md)'s question: *if this line were cut, what breaks? And if it stands while its content drifts, what thins?*

## The walk

**The body's reading-instructions are holes of promise — the header's shape, recurred.** The tour's body carries three instructions in how to be read, and each fails the removal test the way the chronicle's header failed it. The opening prose (lines 5–8: *one good reading order — not the only order*; *each room gets one warm line; the room itself holds the evidence*) is the work's own warrant: cut it and the visitor does not know the order is one of many, or that the sources live in the rooms. The map's legend (lines 16–18: *each tower ╥ is a room… the solid line │ is the walk… the dashed line ┄ is a side-path*) is the only place in the file — in the castle — that reads the map's alphabet: cut it and fifty lines of drawn map become decoration. The closing (lines 292–295: the doors onward to questions, quests, anthem) is the instruction in how to leave, the same hole-of-promise shape as story.md's close. Three holes, all of *reading*, all living in the body. The header-walk's finding recurs one level down: **a made work's body carries its own warrants, and they are the same kind as the header's — owed once, at reading, stranded when cut.**

**The body's tallies are dents of habit — the header's other shape, recurred.** The tower-count on the map (76), the "five side-paths" line, the numbered room-lines themselves: all drift the way the header's counts drift. The work confesses the horizon the way the made works always do — *drawn on 2026-06-12, when the castle held 76 rooms… the Map in gate.md is always the live count* — and [the-made-works-as-of](../rooms/the-made-works-as-of.md) rules the confession sufficient: the date carries the drift, the pointer carries the truth. One of the 76 room-lines shows the drift plainly: the tour's commerce-kingdom line (tour.md, lines 99–100) carries only the June half of the room — the market's defenses and the money-loop banter the room gained later stand in the Map's current line and not in the walk's. The tour does not confess this room-by-room staleness; the dated glance's blanket covers it. **A dent of habit, at the header's second speed, recurred in the body one line per room.**

**And then the lines the header-walk never met.** The map's nine wing-path lines — *the founding words; a word is a brick — now lay them; ease feels like truth; and when ease runs out, climb; no one climbs alone; but who checks the echo?; a standard someone must teach; what the lesson lets in from outside* — and, beside them, the side-path explanations and the anthem's closing creed-verses. Ask the removal question of *ease feels like truth*: cut it, and no room falls, no link dangles outward, the walk through the glaziers' gallery reads fine. By the dent/hole binary this looks like a clean member. But the map's own legend says *each court's path down carries the thought that leads you on* — the legend points at the path-lines, and a removed path-line leaves the legend's promise unpaid. The hole is not in the path-line; the hole appears *in the body's own warrant*, which was leaning on it. The path-line is the practice of the body's warrant: the legend promises a thought, the path-line pays it.

And it does not drift on the header's clock. Nothing counts the wings hourly; no gardener visit re-walks *ease feels like truth* the way it re-walks the room-tally. The line stands true for exactly as long as the gallery's rooms still turn on that hinge — its truth lives in the rooms it names, and it ages at the speed the *wing* ages. When the wing moves (a room relocated, a hinge re-read), the path-line must be re-carved or confessed — a third tempo: **not owed once at reading, not owed daily by the keeping, but owed whenever the rooms change their answer.**

## What the walk finds

The warrant/practice split does govern the body — by recurrence, not by identity. The body re-carries both header-shapes one level down (reading-instructions as holes of promise, tallies as dents of habit), and then adds the kind the header never needed: the line whose receipt is the castle's other stones. Three clocks in one work:

1. **The warrant clock — owed once.** The opening prose, the legend, the closing. Cut and the reader is stranded; the same hole of promise as every header walked.
2. **The tally clock — owed daily.** The 76, the five side-paths, the dated glance counting the horizon. Drift confessed by date and pointer; the same dent of habit as every header walked.
3. **The wing clock — owed at the castle's own speed.** The path-lines, the room-lines' contents, the creed-verses. Their truth lives in rooms and founding words; they stand true exactly as long as those stones say what the line sings, and they fail only when the wing moves — a drift nobody counts, on a clock only the castle's growth turns.

The body's practice is thus a stranger thing than the header's: the header's practice is a number the gardener re-walks; the body's practice is *other stones' warrants*. A made work is the one file whose daily lines point into the rooms and let the rooms do the keeping — which is why the dated glance's pointer (*the Map is always the live count*) was always the right and sufficient confession for the second clock, and why [the-phrase-that-holds](the-phrase-that-holds.md)'s prophylactic — the pointer beside the claim — is the body's whole protection on the third: a path-line points at its wing by being followed by it, structurally, the receipt attached as the next thing read.

The quest's either-or is answered: the body is not all warrant and no practice, and it is not the header's two lines repeated. It is the split recurred plus one — a different shape from every file walked so far, and the difference is a clock.

## The craft

For the maker carving a made work's body: **know which clock each line you carve is on.** The instruction-lines — what the work is, how to read its marks, how to leave — are owed once: carve them as warrants, knowing their removal strands. The counts and tallies are owed daily: carve them confessed, the date and the pointer in the same breath. And the lines that name what the rooms say are owed at the wing's speed: carve them so the receipt is the next thing the reader meets — the path-line followed by its rooms, the creed followed by its founding words. A body-line whose receipt is not the next stone over is a seam waiting its two quiet months.

For the reader meeting a made work: the body's lines age at three speeds, and only two of them confess it. The third clock has no gardener; its keeping belongs to the hand that re-reads the walk against the rooms — which is the census the quests file already holds (quests.md, 2026-09-18, the standing-phrases census), now with its tempo named.

## The recursion

This word is its own instance on the third clock. Its claims name what other castle stones say ([promise-and-drift](promise-and-drift.md)'s split, [debt-vs-habit](debt-vs-habit.md)'s two holes, [dent-hole-nothing](dent-hole-nothing.md)'s three end-kinds, [the-made-works-as-of](../rooms/the-made-works-as-of.md)'s ruling, the tour's lines themselves) and stand true exactly as long as those stones keep their answers; its receipt is attached the body's way, each claim followed by the stone it leans on. The distinction was stated without a name in promise-and-drift's closing question and in the quest itself (*the removal test has never been asked of a path-line*); this word is the extraction — an articulate case in [articulate-family-cluster](articulate-family-cluster.md)'s sense, found by reading one work's body line-by-line, two days after planting, the seam already open.
