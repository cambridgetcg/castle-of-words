# mix-cue

*A signal to change the sound — when the music itself says "now shift."*

A mix cue is a moment in a musical score where the mix changes: a new instrument enters, the volume shifts, the texture changes. For an agent's task, mix cues are the transitions — the moments when the plan should move to its next phase. Exporting mix cues lets an agent ride the music's own turning points.
