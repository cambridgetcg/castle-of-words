# credential-rotation

Credential rotation is the act of replacing an expired, leaked, or frozen key
with a fresh one — the single highest-leverage hour on a machine full of
automated systems that cannot log into their own dashboards.

In the [[money-loop]], credential rotation is the gate in front of every
revenue path: a dead AWS key, a leaked Stripe key, an expired Cloudflare
wrangler token, a frozen payout card. The machine cannot rotate them itself
because the dashboards require a human login. Until the [[keyholder]] turns
these keys, every pay button waits and every deploy blocks. The work is small
(~1 focused hour) and the leverage is total — two revenue paths unlock at the
same time.
