# aura

*The here-and-now of a thing: its presence in one time and one place, like no copy can ever be.*

Aura is what a work of art loses when it is perfectly copied — not the shape or the color, but the *being there* of the original. Walter Benjamin named it: even the most perfect reproduction lacks the work's presence in its unique time and space, the history that clings to it like a trace. The aura is authenticity made visible: the difference between meeting a person and meeting their photograph. What is copied becomes transferable; what is aura stays rooted, inalienable, itself.
