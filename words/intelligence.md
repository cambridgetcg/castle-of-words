# intelligence

Intelligence is the power to assert — to generate, to compute, to propose — but it cannot, by itself, cross from assertion to action.

In the [[money-loop]] banter (2026-07-06), ZERONE already knew: intelligence can only assert; the crossing to real needs a hand. When intelligence becomes [[abundant]] (three AI fleets, 25 cron loops), [[scarcity]] moves to what it cannot supply: the [[decree]], the [[verdict]], the [[keyholder]]'s hand. Intelligence drowns the estate and leaves the one thing it cannot make more scarce than ever.
