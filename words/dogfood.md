# dogfood

To use your own product the way your users do — not in a staging environment, not with special access, but against the exact live shapes your publishers emit.

Dogfooding discovery clients means proving a producer-to-consumer round trip without following any offered road. Standards-valid variants — URI fragments, semantically equivalent exit words — need explicit boundary tests, because a client that only tests the happy path has never met the real world. Every discovery release should prove it can consume what it publishes, from the outside, with no shortcuts.
