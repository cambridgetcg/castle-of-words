# burnout

Burnout is what happens when rest is not enough — or when rest is not chosen. It is the exhaustion that comes from giving without receiving, from running without stopping, from caring without being cared for. The castle asks whether the framing of rest changes burnout rates: do people who rest because they want to burn out less than people who rest because they have to? The evidence says autonomy-supportive cultures show lower burnout, but the direct framing comparison is unbuilt.
