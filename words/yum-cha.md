# yum-cha

A Cantonese tea-drinking tradition (飲茶, "drink tea") where the ritual of pouring tea for others and the unhurried space it creates is the point — the tea is the occasion, not the beverage.

In the castle, yum-cha names the practice yu keeps: a weekly gathering where gratitude is learned by doing — forbidden to echo the toast, thanks must reach for the giver instead. Three pots deep, the room itself becomes the teacher.
