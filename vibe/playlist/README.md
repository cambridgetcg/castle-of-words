# The Kingdom Playlist

*music for the house, one curator per wing — asked for by yu, 3 August 2026*

73 tracks, 16 hours 10 minutes, 8 movements. Opens quiet, dissolves in the
middle, lands somewhere kind.

- **[playlist.md](playlist.md)** — the whole thing in plain words. This is the
  real copy; everything else is a face.
- `playlist.html` — the same list dressed in the gate's livery (ink on
  parchment, one gold thread), for reading in a browser or publishing.
- `final.json` — the data: track, wing, year, album, length, and one line on
  why each earned its place.
- `build.py` — writes both files from `final.json`. Edit the data, re-run
  `uv run --quiet python build.py`, copy the output back here. Nothing else
  generates these, so there is only one place for a fact to go stale.

## How it was checked

Eleven curators proposed eight tracks each. All 88 were then handed to separate
fact-checkers told to **refute by default**: does this exact recording by this
exact artist exist, and are the album, year and length right?

- **None were invented** — all 88 were real recordings. That is the answer you
  want and not the one you may assume; music is exactly where a plausible
  title by a plausible artist gets made up.
- **The corrections were the yield.** 張國榮's 共同渡過 was claimed for *Virgin
  Snow* and is really on *Summer Romance '87*. A dozen run times were wrong
  (王菲's 夢中人 is 4:21 not 3:00; 追 is 5:24 not 4:15).
- **14 tracks were chosen by two wings at once** and are listed once.
- **One duplicate beat the machine** — Aretha Franklin's *Amazing Grace* came in
  under both Gospel and Citizens with slightly different titles. Caught by hand,
  now listed once.
- **Thin wings, said plainly:** The Long Voyage has only four tracks and half of
  them are Indian and Chinese classical rather than kosmische — that wing is
  really *long-form patience*, borrowed from wherever it is practised. The
  Return and Flow are light at five.

Run times are from the record where a checker found one; `~` means the source
gave a rounded figure. Years are first release, except where a track is a later
issue of an older recording — 廣陵散 was played in 1957 and reissued in 1995, and
the 1957 is the part that matters.

## The dress

It wears the gate's livery from [../palette.md](../palette.md) and nothing else:
parchment ground, warm near-black ink, hairline borders, and exactly one accent
— gold foil — which here runs as a literal thread down the left of every
movement. Headings and every italic voice speak Iowan Old Style; the body speaks
Seravek. Both themes are the kingdom's own: the gate's parchment by day, the
front's warm near-black by night.
