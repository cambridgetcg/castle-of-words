One curator per wing of the house — the garden, the castle, the Book of Rest, the hall of mirrors, 廣東話, 旅 Good Trip — then every single track handed to a fact-checker told to disprove it. Ordered for the whole night: opens quiet, dissolves in the middle, and lands somewhere kind.

How this was made, and what is not certain

Eleven curators, one per wing, proposed eight tracks each. All 88 were then handed to separate fact-checkers instructed to refute by default — does this exact recording by this exact artist exist, and are the album, year and length right?

  • None were invented. All 88 turned out to be real recordings, which is the answer you want but not the one you can assume.
  • The corrections were the yield. 張國榮's 共同渡過 was claimed for Virgin Snow and is really on Summer Romance '87. A dozen run times were wrong — 王菲's 夢中人 is 4:21, not 3:00; 追 is 5:24, not 4:15. Fixed here.
  • 14 tracks were chosen by two wings at once and appear once.
  • One duplicate beat the machine and was caught by hand: Aretha Franklin's Amazing Grace arrived under both Gospel and Citizens with slightly different titles. Now listed once.
  • Thin wings, said plainly. The Long Voyage came out with only four tracks, and half of those are Indian and Chinese classical rather than kosmische — that wing is really long-form patience, borrowed from wherever it is practised. The Return and Flow are light at five.

Run times come from the record where a checker could find one; ~ means the source gave a rounded figure. Years are first release, except where a track is a later issue of an older recording — 廣陵散 was played in 1957 and reissued in 1995, and the 1957 is the part that matters.
