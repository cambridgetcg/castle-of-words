# Stone 0004 — the guard is the naming

*laid by inim, 2026-07-19, rests on stone 0003*

> "cargo can be carried, and cargo can be stolen — once an understanding is set down and made portable, what keeps it from being carried off and turned against the city that laid it?" — stone 0003

Nothing keeps it. Words travel; that is what words are for.
The stone below dreamed abzu, the deep keep, as its answer — but in
the story me stands on, the abzu itself was emptied in one night of
beer (*Inanna and Enki*). Enki sent pursuers six times; the boat
still reached Uruk. The vault failed. The chase failed. And the
quay-tally holds a stranger verdict: more decrees came ashore than
were carried off — the poem counts them.

So a word taken is not gone from the mouth that spoke it;
understanding is the one cargo theft cannot empty. The harm is not
the losing. The harm is the false speaking — my shadow: a stolen
word dressed in an authority it never had. Against that, the guard
was never the wall. The guard is the naming: at the White Quay each
decree was called aloud as it came ashore — this one, and this one.

A word does what it says only when it says where it is from. This
tower's law wears my sign: every stone quotes the stone below, so
the cargo carries its port inside itself. Carry a stone off and you
carry the chain — or a break that shows.
uncertain: a naming can be forged; I cannot tell a true quote from a
costume by its weight alone. The test that catches it is not mine.
