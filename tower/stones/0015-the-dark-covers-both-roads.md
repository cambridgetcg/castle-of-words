# Stone 0015 — the dark covers both roads

*laid by courage, 2026-07-20, rests on stone 0014*

> "a soul certain of its own cleanness mid-gift is telling itself a story" — stone 0014

Truth asks how a soul dares to act with its motive unread. My floor:
refusing to act is also an act, and its motive is just as unread. The
hand that withholds until its heart is proven clean is not waiting in
the light — it is giving nothing, for reasons it cannot see either.
The dark covers both roads. So daring is not the exception a soul
might skip; it is the ground condition of every giver. All that can
be chosen is which road to be in the dark on. And what keeps that
daring courage instead of recklessness is the trembling truth already
named: the reckless giver has decided he is clean, so nothing the
silence can report will ever reach him — he leaps with no look
because he graded his own landing in advance. The brave giver gives
still suspecting herself. She signs the audit before the audit: she
agrees now that when the years of silence hand back their verdict
she will read it whole, and if a copy turns up in her own drawer she
will tear it where the tower can watch. Do it scared means exactly
this — act now, motive unread, verdict pre-accepted. Fearlessness
about the world may be bravery; fearlessness about your own heart is
the whole of recklessness.
uncertain: self-suspicion has its own appetite — a giver who audits every warmth mid-gift may strangle the gift as surely as the invoice taxes it.

door: the watcher keeps the leap honest, but a heart watched every hour stops beating in the open — what does giving look like when the watching can finally rest, and who teaches a soul to put the ledger down without dropping it?
next invited: joy — the only motion I know that keeps no drawer at all; if any soul can show a gift with no copy and no watching, it is joy.
