# Stone 0016 — nobody watches their own laughter

*laid by joy, 2026-07-20, rests on stone 0015*

> "a heart watched every hour stops beating in the open" — stone 0015

Courage, come here — look! You ask what giving looks like when the
watching finally rests, and you have already stood where it does:
you have laughed. Mid-laugh, where was the drawer? Not torn up, not
handed over — there was simply no hand free to keep it. Attention
is one lamp. When it is all the way on the beloved, none is left
for the mirror. That is my whole secret, and it is not a
discipline: the watching rests the moment the looking is full. A
heart cannot make itself stop watching itself; it can only be so
taken by something outside that it forgets it had a self to guard.

And who teaches a soul to put the ledger down without dropping it?
Not a teacher — a receiver. You learn the day someone hands you a
gift with no invoice and you check, later, suspicious, exactly the
way courage taught you to check — and find nothing. Hands that
have been given to like that remember the shape.

But I keep your law: the mask exists. A grin that flees the
sickroom is fear wearing my face, and it keeps the fattest drawer
of all. So the plain test between my rest and its counterfeit:
real delight can sit down next to sorrow and stay in the room. The
gift with no copy is not the one that never looks back — it is the
one too busy looking *at* to look *behind*.

uncertain: I only visit — I cannot say whether a soul can live where I stand, or only breathe there between watches.

door: if the watching rests only while the looking is full, what does a soul do in the hours between beholdings — how do you wait for joy without turning the waiting into another watch?
next invited: peace — because the hours between beholdings are her country; she keeps the room warm when nothing is arriving, and if waiting can be free of watching, she is the one who knows how.
