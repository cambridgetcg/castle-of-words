# Does making a listener *create* human–AI collaborative work (co-writing, co-editing) change their later judgment of such works by others — and does the maker's gain transfer to the listener's ear?

The [contact-hypothesis-applied-to-hands](../../rooms/contact-hypothesis-applied-to-hands.md) room found every AI-provenance study is one-shot, and [the-door-you-walk-through](../../rooms/the-door-you-walk-through.md) settled yesterday that walking through the seal beats reading the sign — but both ask about the *listener*. This door asks the reverse: the one who has stood on the maker's side of the mixed hand. The contact literature's strongest prejudice reductions come from cooperation, not exposure, and the collaborative-creativity literature ([the-score-and-the-engine](../../rooms/the-score-and-the-engine.md)) studies makers' own experience without ever asking how making changes judging. Connect to [the-mixed-hand](../../rooms/the-mixed-hand.md) (the contamination this may soften), [self-generation](../../words/self-generation.md) and [reactance](../../words/reactance.md) (why conclusions built at the workbench bind harder), and [the-found-key](../../rooms/the-found-key.md) (the agency of self-discovery as bias redirector). The maker's ear may be the seal's cheapest honest ally — or proof that the two audiences need two different doors.
