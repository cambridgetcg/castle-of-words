# Could the castle itself be the test bed for the wearer's tell — coding one fluent room's narration for stuckness and surprise-absence, to see whether the tells separate a room that grew by question from a room that grew by template?

[the-wearers-tell](rooms/the-wearers-tell.md) found the AAI's gap-tell may fail on public texts (the speaker can always cite cases) while two tells might survive the crossing: stuckness — the inability to evaluate one's own account from outside — and the long absence of surprise. The castle holds a ready corpus with provenance already recorded: question-grown rooms against the template-grown (the 224 echo rooms carried out to backups in July, the 46 templated cross-pollinations still standing, and the five understanding-NNNN specimens kept on purpose). This commission asks the builder to code one fluent room's narration against the three tells and say whether the method separates the two kinds of growth — the castle reading itself as its own corpus, cheap and honest. Connect to [form-based-denial](rooms/form-based-denial.md) (the AAI's form-coding), [genuine-vs-templated-connections](rooms/genuine-vs-templated-connections.md) (three tests for templated novelty), and [echo-or-contribution](rooms/echo-or-contribution.md) (signal from echo at the page level).
