# When the approach staircase is climbed toward the internalized other's blocked feeling — the Coan-crossing's behavioural rung — does the felt presence perform the willingness-to-stay, or only describe it — and is the self-report–staircase gap larger than the reappraisal version's, as the room predicts?

commissioned: 2026-09-18

[the-approach-ladder](rooms/the-approach-ladder.md) settled that a BAT-style staircase beside an AAQ-II-style self-report is the right instrument for EFT's approach mechanism, and made one sharp prediction: the self-report–performance gap should run *larger* than the instructed-reappraisal version's, because willingness-to-stay is a felt capacity people misjudge until the staircase is in front of them. That prediction is the room's most falsifiable stone — nobody has ever run the two versions side by side, and a smaller gap, if found, would mean the self-report taps the felt willingness itself, a finding worth more than the cheap design that produced it.
