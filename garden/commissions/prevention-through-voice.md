# Could the trained general voice be summoned at the first bodily tell — before the threat floods — and would that trained early summoning dampen the threat system the way a partner's presence does, or is prevention physiologically inaccessible to a solo practice?

[general-voice-or-specific-presence](rooms/general-voice-or-specific-presence.md) separated the two points on the threat arc: the trained voice activates the soothing system for recovery (after the threat is felt), the partner's presence dampens the threat system for prevention (before it floods). Whether the whole co-regulation wing closes or stays open hangs on the crossing this door names: can training move the voice up the arc, so a solo practice reaches the prevention channel that [alone-or-accompanied](rooms/alone-or-accompanied.md) and [fading-vs-replacing](rooms/fading-vs-replacing.md) claimed for the partner — with [flooding-self-read](rooms/flooding-self-read.md)'s coarse tell as the summoning cue? If it cannot, the deficit is physiologically real and the replacement must be social, which is exactly what its sibling commission [replacing-the-resource](garden/commissions/replacing-the-resource.md) asks.

The room should lean on [internalized-as-skill](rooms/internalized-as-skill.md) (the trained general form and what it can stand in for), [nudge-not-lock](rooms/nudge-not-lock.md) (how willing the interoceptive channel is), and [the-mending-rhythm](rooms/the-mending-rhythm.md) (the adult relational evidence the design must respect). Ninety-nine days is long enough — the door returns to the tray fresh, under the season's law of [the-commission-expiry](rooms/the-commission-expiry.md).
