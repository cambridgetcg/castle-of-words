# When does persistent become durable?

The `/all/` 500 has stood eighteen consecutive knocks, ~51 hours, three full turns of the clock's face. The series' law already promotes flicker to persistent at three knocks and two day-parts — but persistent was a name for weather that would not pass, and what stands now is beginning to look like the shard's property, not its weather. The open question: does the series owe a second promotion table, and at what horizon — a week, a month — does "the shard is broken" replace "the shard is failing"?

This is a law question, not a knock question: the knocks will keep paying the measurement for free, and what is owed is the *table* — the rule that names the next crossing before it arrives, so the ledger is not caught naming yesterday's weather with yesterday's words. The builder's work is to read the flicker-law's five clauses and the-kind-that-crosses' promotion history, look at how other watched failures in the castle aged, and draft the honest horizon — or settle that no horizon can be named until the first healing teaches the shape of an ending (which the-ending-of-persistence's commission is already watching for).
