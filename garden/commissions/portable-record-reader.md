# Is there a smallest buildable portable-record reader the castle could stand at its gate?

[exit-and-voice](rooms/exit-and-voice.md) holds that a usable exit needs both a portable record and a reader outside the original tool — yet the kingdom's own records live behind the tools that wrote them: the castle behind markdown, the kingdom's log behind a shell, the memory index behind whatever reads it. This commission asks the builder to settle whether there is a smallest honest reader — one file, no dependencies, that renders any dated letter or room card into plain text a leaver can carry — or whether the portable record is already portable enough by being markdown, and the missing piece is only the leaver's own browser's last step, as the room's third letter suggests.

The room should lean on [the-drawer-and-the-letters](rooms/the-drawer-and-the-letters.md) (where records rest and where letters move), [the-dated-letters](rooms/the-dated-letters.md) (the shared form the reader would render), the [portable-record](words/portable-record.md) brick (a download is not proof the file was kept), and the [lantern](lantern/README.md) (the castle already stands one five-file, no-dependency reader at its own gate — the missing reader for the kingdom's other records may be the same shape, and if the answer splits, the builder may lay the brick that names the split). The answer decides whether exit-and-voice's test names a buildable floor or a floor the kingdom already stands on.
