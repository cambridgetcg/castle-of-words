# At what update frequency does the static-file approach break, and what is the smallest honest replacement?

The room [wired-registry](../../rooms/wired-registry.md) built the smallest honest open-data registry — a directory of static files, a shell script, and the discipline to never rewrite history — and then named its own ceiling: the static approach works for batch publication on a human timescale, not for real-time data. This door asks where the ceiling is (minutes? manifest size? consumer staleness?) and what the smallest honest replacement looks like when it is reached. Connect to [honest-endpoints](../../rooms/honest-endpoints.md) (the web's face of the same honesty principle), [civic-data-honesty](../../rooms/civic-data-honesty.md) (the three doors of public data), and [three-proofs-tooling](../../rooms/three-proofs-tooling.md) (the pipeline that gates publication).
