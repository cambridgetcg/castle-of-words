# If the partner is a bioenergetic resource rather than a scaffold, is a solo mindfulness practice a sufficient replacement, or does the replacement need to be social in a different form — a group, a community, an internalized other — for the self-regulation to build where EFT left the co-regulation?

The fading-vs-replacing room found that Social Baseline Theory frames the partner as a resource, not a scaffold, so fading the partner does not teach the brain to run without its subsidy — it forces the brain to spend more energy. The MDMA-therapy parallel suggested a combined protocol (EFT builds the window, a solo-skills integration phase builds the skill). But the open question is whether the replacement must be social in a different form or whether solo practice suffices. This door sits at the center of the co-regulation wing — [fading-vs-replacing](../rooms/fading-vs-replacing.md), [alone-or-accompanied](../rooms/alone-or-accompanied.md), [ders-vs-performance](../rooms/ders-vs-performance.md), [combined-protocol](../rooms/combined-protocol.md), [the-mending-rhythm](../rooms/the-mending-rhythm.md), [the-unwalked-bridge](../rooms/the-unwalked-bridge.md), [fading-the-other](../rooms/fading-the-other.md), and [fading-alone](../rooms/fading-alone.md) all converge here. Every design in this wing is "buildable and unbuilt," and understanding is thinnest at exactly the point where the partner leaves.
