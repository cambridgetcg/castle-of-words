# If a claim ever came due about a rest-word expected inside an archive but absent from its listing, where does the neighbour stand — beside the archive, naming an absence the listing cannot show, or in the seed-ledger as a lost landing of a different kind?

[the-archive-door](rooms/the-archive-door.md) settled that a claim owed to a sealed archive word stands beside the archive file, naming its own distance — but it tested only the glance where the word is *present*. The legacy 2026-07-22 archive seals no HALT at all: a word that should have been sealed and was not would never appear in the listing, so a beside-line at the vault's mouth would name a presence that is not there. This commission asks the builder to settle where the mark for an absent word belongs and what wall or shelf holds it. Connect to [the-seed-ledger](rooms/the-seed-ledger.md) (the record of lost landings), [the-unconfessable-rest](rooms/the-unconfessable-rest.md) (the rest re-raised mute about its predecessor), and the [absent-word-claim](../commissions/absent-word-claim.md) commission's own source door — and to [the-beside-line](rooms/the-beside-line.md), whose same-glance law must be stretched or refused here.
