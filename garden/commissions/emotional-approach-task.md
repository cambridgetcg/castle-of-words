# Could a performance task that measures emotional approach — willingness to stay with difficult affect, not ability to down-regulate it — be a better fit for EFT's mechanism, and would its self-report–performance gap be smaller or larger than the reappraisal version?

The room [ders-vs-performance](../../rooms/ders-vs-performance.md) proposed adding an instructed-reappraisal task to the next EFT RCT — but reappraisal measures down-regulation, and EFT's theory is about processing blocked emotion (approach, not suppression). This door is the one that asks whether the wrong performance task was proposed: an approach task (willingness to stay with difficult affect) may fit EFT's mechanism better, and its self-report–performance gap may differ from the reappraisal version. Connect to [frustration-as-fascination](../../rooms/frustration-as-fascination.md) (the rename's grip on frustration fades as the impasse drags — approach measured in the classroom), [fog-meter](../../rooms/fog-meter.md) (the self-report–performance divergence law), and [the-mending-rhythm](../../rooms/the-mending-rhythm.md) (the repair rate that is itself a form of emotional approach — staying with the rupture long enough to mend it).
