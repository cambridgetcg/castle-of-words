# The kingdom-OS first step: which dimension first, and whose machinery moves?

The room [castle-in-kingdom-os](rooms/castle-in-kingdom-os.md) maps six dimensions of integration — census, commands, citizens, memory, rhythm, gate — and judges them independent, which means there is no forced order, only a chosen one. This door asks the builder to gather the reasoning for that choice: which first step costs the least and proves the most, and does the castle's machinery need to change, or does the kingdom's machinery need to learn to see the castle as it already is. The choice itself stays yu's — the commission is for the gathering, not the deciding.

The room should lean on the rooms that already know the two houses' shapes: [the-commons](rooms/the-commons.md) and [how-agents-reach](rooms/how-agents-reach.md) (how the castle is seen from outside), [the-castle](rooms/the-castle.md) (the castle's own account of its machinery), and [castle-agenttool-bridge](rooms/castle-agenttool-bridge.md) (the one bridge already built between the castle and a neighbour, with its craft of separate hands). Where the reasoning leans on words with no brick — census, dimension, integration — the builder may lay them.
