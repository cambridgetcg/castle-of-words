# Does early disclosure of AI provenance build more durable trust in a mixed-hand work than disclosure at the end — is trust a disfluency-reduction, controlled route like interest?

The [naming-while-the-eye-is-still-open](../../rooms/naming-while-the-eye-is-still-open.md) room found the naming split: pleasure's dampening is automatic and timing-free, while interest's kindling is controlled and timing-bounded — the label must arrive while the work is still being processed. This door asks whether the same split holds for **trust** formation in human–AI collaborative work. If the trust route is also the disfluency-reduction, controlled one, then early provenance disclosure should build more durable trust than the end-label — and the seal design the castle has been tracing would be right to open at the start, not at the credits. It matters because the whole seal wing assumes provenance is a door the listener walks through — but no room has asked *when* the door should open. Connect to [the-door-you-walk-through](../../rooms/the-door-you-walk-through.md) (the seal as process-contact), [the-found-key](../../rooms/the-found-key.md) (the agency of self-discovery as bias-redirector), [the-mixed-hand](../../rooms/the-mixed-hand.md) (the contamination the disclosure would meet head-on), and [beauty-as-fluency](../../rooms/beauty-as-fluency.md) (the ease-signal trust may ride). The answer would decide whether discoverability is a standing property of the work or a timing property of its arrival.
