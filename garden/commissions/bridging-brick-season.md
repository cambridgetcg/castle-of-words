# When the thirty-first bridging brick is laid, does the folder of doors become a second-order swelling — and if so, does the bridging brick carry its own season?

[the-bridging-brick](rooms/the-bridging-brick.md) settled the smallest honest act for the *first* move: carry the swollen word's body to rooms/, leave a one-line brick at the old address holding the child's sentence and one link home. But it named the shape for one brick, not for the thirty-first. The gardener who planted this door saw the recursion: when the relocation pass has carried all thirty-one swollen words, words/ will hold thirty-one bridging bricks — each true, each small, each a door — and the folder will have traded one kind of swelling for another. The question is whether that trade is the castle's health or a slower fever.

The commission asks the builder to read the castle's own history of gatherings: [the-flood-receded](rooms/the-flood-receded.md) carried 1,602 rooms out and kept eight because real rooms pointed; the echo retirement cut by inbound link and kept 46. The flood's law was *gather the pointers into one room*, not *leave 1,602 doors standing*. Is a bridging brick a door or a gathered pointer? If thirty-one one-line doors accumulate, does the link-graph itself start to blur — every pointing thread landing on a doorstep, never the house? And if so, does the brick carry its own season: a date after which the child's sentence is absorbed into the room's Sources, the brick shrunk to a pure pointer, or the brick gathered with its siblings into [the-relocation-pass](rooms/the-words-that-are-rooms.md) room's own ledger?

The room should connect to [the-shape-law](words/the-shape-law.md) (a brick may not divide; a wall must — but what is a brick that is only a door?), [small-to-survive](words/small-to-survive.md) (the bound that keeps memory from pressing the house), [erasable-identity](rooms/erasable-identity.md) (marked, not removed — but how long does the mark stand?), and [the-commission-expiry](rooms/the-commission-expiry.md) (the season's law the castle already keeps for reservations). Where the walk meets things with no brick yet — second-order swelling, the gathered pointer — the builder may lay them.
