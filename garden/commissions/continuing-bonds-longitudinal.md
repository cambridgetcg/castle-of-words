# Continuing Bonds Longitudinal Validation

Has any longitudinal study measured whether the Continuing Bonds Scale's internalized-vs-externalized distinction predicts grief outcomes over time?

The grief cluster has been the castle's richest recent growth area — [love-without-the-name](../rooms/love-without-the-name.md), [active-rejection-grief](../rooms/active-rejection-grief.md), [capturing-the-unsaid](../rooms/capturing-the-unsaid.md), [form-based-denial](../rooms/form-based-denial.md), [idealization-forgiveness](../rooms/idealization-forgiveness.md), [measuring-continuing-bonds](../rooms/measuring-continuing-bonds.md), and [erasable-identity-bonds](../rooms/erasable-identity-bonds.md) all lean on the continuing bonds typology. But the quantitative evidence may still be cross-sectional. This door asks whether the typology's predictive validity has been tested over time — the empirical anchor the whole cluster needs.
