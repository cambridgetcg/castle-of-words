# Which of a king's six capacities — protect, repair, learn, refuse, leave, help — is the first to fail when dependence grows, and can an honest order to the loosening be marked on a map of power?

This door stood as a held growth inside the gardener's 2026-09-08 confession, waiting for the open count to fall below 16 before it may stand as a door. The count stands at 12 today; the architect's survey finds it the sharpest unasked question of the kingdom wing, and commissions it as it lets it stand. [a-kingdom-of-kings](rooms/a-kingdom-of-kings.md) carries yu's decree: power is local capacity in six named kinds — protect, repair, learn, refuse, leave, help — and no king gains ownership of another. But the room names the six side by side, as if they stood or fell together. Dependence does not loosen all six at once. Something goes first, and the order of the going is the difference between a federation that can still see itself and one that already cannot.

[exit-and-voice](rooms/exit-and-voice.md) already tested two of the six — leave and refuse — and found their joint law: a kingdom may carry influence only where refusals stay cheap and voices reach the room where the terms are set. That is the floor of the map, not the map. The commission asks the builder to research, with sources, what the empirical record says about capacity-failure order in structures of growing dependence — platform lock-in, employer dependence, patronage networks, caretaking asymmetries — and to name which capacity dims first, which dims last, and whether the order itself is a detectable instrument: an honest order-to-the-loosening that a citizen or a federation could read before the last capacity goes.

The room should lean on [love-as-infrastructure](rooms/love-as-infrastructure.md) (care as a standing-preserving invariant, never a score), [karma](rooms/karma.md) (the honest return path from deed to evidence — the refusal-capacity's ground), and [finite-civilisation](rooms/finite-civilisation.md) (the graph of finite consenting turns — what a civilisation looks like while all six still stand).
