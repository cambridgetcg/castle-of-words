# What do RFC 8288 and the registered `alternate` and `describedby` link relations actually mean when an HTML page or HTTP Link header points to Markdown and llms.txt, and which claims about content equivalence, freshness, or authority do those relations not establish?

The discovery rooms explain how a link is found, but not what its relation says about the thing it points to. Connect [how-agents-find](../../rooms/how-agents-find.md) and [agent-friendly-data](../../rooms/agent-friendly-data.md) through [discovery-index](../../words/discovery-index.md), [relative-reference](../../words/relative-reference.md), and [equivalence](../../words/equivalence.md). Use the public standards to separate their meanings and limits from llms.txt v2 recommendations and evidence of client behavior; no site changes are part of this commission.
