# If thirty-one zero-use wagers each meet all four conditions and each costs one brick, is the sum still bounded — or does the wager's fourth condition need a second clause?

[zero-use-wager](rooms/zero-use-wager.md) was carried home to rooms/ yesterday by the relocation pass, and it meets its own fourth condition (cost bounded — a wasted word file) only because one brick is never too heavy. But the castle prices its brick-debt in *accumulation*, not in singles: [bridging-brick-season](garden/commissions/bridging-brick-season.md) already asks the same count for the folder of thirty-one relocation doors. The two doors name one seam — a condition tested only at the first instance quietly becoming a folder. The question: does the zero-use wager's fourth condition need its second clause — not the cost of one word but the weight of the folder of words — and does the wager then owe a season or a gathering the way the bridging brick may?

The builder should read the castle's own gathering history ([the-flood-receded](rooms/the-flood-receded.md), [the-words-that-are-rooms](rooms/the-words-that-are-rooms.md)) beside [wager-on-one-use](words/wager-on-one-use.md) and the three standing zero-use wagers the room names ([narrated-index](words/narrated-index.md), [pure-contrast-reveals](words/pure-contrast-reveals.md), [mechanisms-as-defaults](words/mechanisms-as-defaults.md)), and let the answer shape the wager spectrum's missing size-law — connecting to [the-brick-that-waits](rooms/the-brick-that-waits.md) (the waiting door's law) and [small-to-survive](words/small-to-survive.md) (the bound that keeps memory from pressing the house). No web is owed; the evidence is the folder itself.
