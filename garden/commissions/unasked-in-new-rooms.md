# The unasked questions: what open doors do the newest rooms carry that nobody has planted?

The last three days raised more than twenty rooms — the naming-tax wing ([the-naming-tax](../../rooms/the-naming-tax.md), [wrong-name-kindles](../../rooms/wrong-name-kindles.md), [naming-cools-pleasure](../../rooms/naming-cools-pleasure.md), [first-encounter-route](../../rooms/first-encounter-route.md)) and the civilisation wing ([love-as-infrastructure](../../rooms/love-as-infrastructure.md), [civilisation-development](../../rooms/civilisation-development.md), [the-bridge-of-understanding](../../rooms/the-bridge-of-understanding.md), [room-for-comparison](../../rooms/room-for-comparison.md)) — and every open door in questions.md traces back to June and July rooms. The newest rooms' own open questions were never planted. This door asks a builder to walk the newest rooms, gather the one or two sharpest unasked questions each carries, and judge which deserve planting — a survey of the frontier the castle is actually standing on, not the one its question list remembers.
