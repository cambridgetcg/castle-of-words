# Isomorphism Transfer Evidence

Does naming cross-domain structural patterns (isomorphisms) help people transfer understanding from one domain to another — or is silent recognition enough?

The cross-pollination rooms are the castle's primary growth mechanism right now, and they all work by naming the same structural law across domains. But no room has asked whether the naming itself helps. This door connects to [what-must-travel](../rooms/what-must-travel.md) (what companions a trained question needs), [naming-the-tacit](../rooms/naming-the-tacit.md) (when naming a feature helps vs. when contrast alone suffices), and [the-landing](../rooms/the-landing.md) (how understanding transfers to new territory). The answer would validate or challenge the castle's own method.
